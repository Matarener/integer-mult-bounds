# Independent audit of the no-axial three-mark floor

21 October 2026. PASS for the stated `12+n` theorem and the interface to the

## Written argument checked

Three ordinary canonical marks in distinct paired roles have six actual ports
in six distinct triads. Disjoint indicator supports make `C` injective and
prevent cancellation between the coordinates of a single query `C a`.
Consequently a query with at least two nonzero marked coordinates touches two
complete paired roles; their binary union has dimension at least eight.

Deleting reader entries outside the six output ports preserves the complete
endpoint, since each corresponding output column was zero. Whole-group tariffs
are monotone under this deletion. Count `n` only after deleting zero outer
products from this restricted factorization. Added null cancellations outside
the target cannot inflate this `n` and invalidate the bound.

If one remaining term reads `k` of the six ports, the selected triads span at
least `k+2` dimensions: triad dimensions cover k=1; pair/triple facts cover k=2,3;
pigeonhole gives a complete paired role at k=4 and two at k=5,6. The source
annihilator has dimension at most one in the nondegenerate nine-dimensional
binary space. Hence the full pairing rectangle has rank at least `k+1`.

`A R=C^T` forces every selected actual reader to use at least two query columns,
since no single non-axial query spans a marked unit vector. Distinct reader
ports occupy distinct triads, so their incidences are counted exactly by the
sum of the per-term k values. That sum is at least twelve. Therefore the
restricted invoice, and thus the original invoice, is at least `12+n`.
This closes every restricted n>=6. It does not by itself close n<=5.

## Independently reconstructed finite geometry

`independent.py` reads only the same two foundational SHA256-pinned inputs.
It imports no producer. It represents each physical space by its complete
annihilator as a512-bit set, intersects these sets, and derives ranks from
cardinality. No Gaussian elimination or producer receipt is used.

All666 triad pairs have ranks `{4:2,5:59,6:605}`. The independent calculation
checks all7770 triad triples, whose ranks are `{6:31,7:900,8:4431,9:2408}`.
Thus every triple actually has rank at least six, stronger than the rank-five
premise needed in the proof. The producer's smaller argument checks the70
extensions of the two rank-four pairs; that is sufficient because any triple
of rank at most four would contain such a pair and violate its extension check.

The inputs have53 ordinary two-copy marks and18 distinct paired roles. Each
role consists of two mutually orthogonal orthonormal triads and has dimension
and canonical self-tariff six. All153 role pairs have ranks `{8:11,9:142}`.
These checks establish the finite premises without pricing decoder candidates.

## Scope that must remain visible

The source queries must lie in the three selected canonical marks' span and
have at least two nonzero marked coordinates. The marks must be ordinary,
two-copy, and from distinct paired roles. Axial queries, exterior source
directions, repeated roles, residual-bearing or singleton targets, and changed
allowed-free endpoints are not covered. The count n is the restricted active
count. The seven-query signed gluing is a corollary only after invoking the
retained <=5 theorem for its possible lower-active-count specializations.
