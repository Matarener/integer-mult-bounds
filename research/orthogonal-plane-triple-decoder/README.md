# A rank-135 fitting matrix on all 336 alternating-plane ports

This contribution was produced by Nick Petalas. It is a finite exact
construction with a scoped rank and tariff proof. No new kappa is claimed.

For the 336 nondegenerate alternating planes in the even subspace of F2^7,
the explicit partition in [CERTIFICATE.json](CERTIFICATE.json) gives a
fitting matrix of exact scalar rank **135**. This is 33 below the rank-168
minimum of the spectral family reported in Ali Romman's
[PR #5624](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5624).
That predecessor result is cited, not audited or replayed here.

The new factorization has 84 terms of binary pairing tariff six, 33 of
tariff four and 18 of tariff two. Its complete additive full-plane COPY
mass is **672**, the same as the predecessor's cited constructions. Thus
the rank improvement does not create a COPY surplus or establish a better
recursive invoice. The changed fitting matrix advances #5624's second
open question; it does not settle the question of a tariff below 672.

## Construction and proof

Let V be the even-weight subspace of F2^7, with the standard binary dot
product. It has dimension six and its form is nondegenerate alternating.
Indeed its orthogonal complement in F2^7 is the line generated by the odd
all-ones vector, which does not lie in V. The ports are the nondegenerate
two-dimensional subspaces E of V. There are 63 choices of a nonzero first
vector and 32 choices of a second vector pairing to one. Each plane is
counted by its six ordered bases, giving 63*32/6 = 336 ports.

A fitting matrix has diagonal one and entry zero between distinct ports
whose planes are not orthogonal. Entries between orthogonal planes are
free. This is precisely the one-scalar-per-plane convention of #5624.
It differs from the two-scalar-port convention in the older 56-plane
full-plane tariff lineage.

The certificate indexes planes by the lexicographically sorted triples of
their three nonzero seven-bit vectors. Each vector is the integer with
those seven coordinate bits. It partitions the ports into 84 orthogonal
triples, 33 orthogonal pairs and 18 singletons. For each block B, let u_B
be its 0-1 indicator vector and set

    F = sum_B u_B u_B^T.

Every port occurs once, so F has unit diagonal. An off-diagonal one occurs
only within an orthogonal block, proving the fitting condition. Reordering
by blocks makes F the direct sum of 84 all-ones 3-by-3 matrices, 33 all-ones
2-by-2 matrices and 18 unit matrices. Each block has scalar rank one.
Equivalently the displayed factorization proves rank at most 135, while
one representative of each block gives an identity minor of order 135.
Thus rank F = 135 over every scalar field, in particular over Q and C.
This is an upper bound for the unrestricted fitting minimum, not its value.

The tariff of a term u v^T is the binary pairing rank between the spans of
the complete planes in its two supports, as in the credited full-plane
contract. For a block of k pairwise-orthogonal nondegenerate planes, their
sum is an orthogonal direct sum of dimension 2k, with nondegenerate form.
Both supports of u_B u_B^T span this same space, so its tariff is exactly
2k. Hence the complete tariff is

    84*6 + 33*4 + 18*2 = 672 = 2*336.

No coefficient, source support or reader support is omitted from that
declared additive tariff. This remains an algebraic tariff, not a paid
physical COPY program. A clique in this geometry has at most three planes,
so this particular partition family needs at least 112 blocks. Attainment
of 112, optimality of 135 and a global fitting-rank lower bound are unproved.

## Deterministic reproduction and evidence

Use CPython 3.12 with its standard library, from the worktree root:

```sh
/Users/nick/git/integer-mult-bounds/.venv/bin/python3.12 -I -B -W error research/orthogonal-plane-triple-decoder/verify.py
/Users/nick/git/integer-mult-bounds/.venv/bin/python3.12 -I -O -B -W error research/orthogonal-plane-triple-decoder/verify.py
/Users/nick/git/integer-mult-bounds/.venv/bin/python3.12 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/orthogonal-plane-triple-decoder
/Users/nick/git/integer-mult-bounds/.venv/bin/python3.12 -B scripts/check_research_commit.py --package research/orthogonal-plane-triple-decoder --check-source-history
```

Another CPython 3.12 interpreter may replace the absolute local path. Both
registered checks have 120-second limits. The direct verifier prints a
generated SHA-256 receipt binding all six package files, then
`PASS_ORTHOGONAL_PLANE_TRIPLE_DECODER rank=135 copy_mass=672`. It writes
nothing and needs no Git, network, solver or predecessor executable.

Local CPython 3.12.15 on macOS 27.0.1 arm64 passed both registered commands.
The separately timed direct checks took 0.435 and 0.431 seconds in normal
and optimized mode. Three actual optimized CLI corruptions also reject
missing coverage, a doubled factorization coefficient and a lowered tariff.
These are verification timings, not a performance comparison. Hosted CI is
reported separately in the PR body.

The construction is reproducible without optimization. Visit orthogonal
triples in lexicographic order and keep each disjoint triple. Then visit
remaining ports in ascending order and pair a port with its least available
orthogonal neighbor, leaving the others as singletons. The verifier checks
the stored partition against this recipe and separately verifies its actual
mathematics.

The complete checks cover both the vector-pair enumeration and an independent
RREF enumeration of all 651 binary two-spaces, retaining the same 336
nondegenerate planes. They check every block, all 112,896 fitting entries,
all 18,225 entries of the identity minor, the literal binary rank of F and
every term's span and Gram rank. Eight controls reject missing or duplicated
coverage, a nonorthogonal pair, a Boolean or out-of-range index, a repeated
index, a four-member block and an empty block. Checks use exceptions and
remain active under optimized Python.

The advance CLAIM targeted a full 112-triple partition. A bounded
backtracking attempt returned no cover; a 45-second
full-partition integer-program attempt also returned no feasible witness.
Neither run proves infeasibility. The first deterministic greedy construction
then gave the strict rank improvement above, and further search stopped.
The published result is the explicit 135-block witness, not a search failure
or a claim of optimality. Search software is unnecessary for its replay.

## Limits and implementation decisions

The finite geometry, fitting matrix and its additive support tariff are the
entire claim. Native preparation, physical acquisition and delivery, dirty
restoration, ownership, compiler closure, routing, precision, stock, BIT
compatibility and the complete outer caller remain unpriced. No supplier
admission, selected-frontier change or complete multiplication theorem is
supplied. Independent human and formal proof-kernel review are unclaimed.
No Lean, Docker, selected cold replay or predecessor scientific replay was run.

The verifier has no recursion, subprocess or variable-size search. Loops have
fixed domains of at most 336 ports, 651 RREF planes, 336 blocks, seven address
bits and 336 rank pivots. Certificate bytes are capped at 1 MiB before parsing.
The incidence matrix has at most 336^2 entries and matrix construction uses
at most 336^3 scalar products. Every function is below 60 physical lines.
Warning-strict normal and optimized executions provide runtime diagnostics.
Ruff was unavailable in the local environment; source compilation passed.

Power-of-ten rule 3 has a managed-runtime deviation. Python allocates integer,
list, tuple, set and JSON objects during this offline calculation. Fixed
domains bound the mathematical tables above; each receipt file is streamed
with a 1 MiB cap and released before the next. There is no steady service,
shared mutable state or persistent resource. Larger geometries would require
new explicit capacities and a new verification budget. No repository-wide
analysis configuration is changed for this isolated standard-library checker.

   admissible triples as an exact-cover instance and publish a literal witness
   if one is found. The bounded searches above leave existence unresolved.
2. Can a changed fitting matrix or factorization achieve COPY mass below 672?
   Start with one exact factorization and every literal source/reader pairing
   rank. The present rank reduction leaves that tariff question open, and
