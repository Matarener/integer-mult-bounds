# Current-word scientific metadata repair

An independent native audit found that the original producer used
`dict(f, ...)` to retain predecessor fields after changing the prefix.
The literal words, phases, selected recipes and inverses passed that
audit, but the retained predecessor science fields were not current
evidence. This was a producer error.

All 13 pre-repair top-level files, including the old manifest and
receipts, were preserved byte-for-byte in
`before-metadata-repair-20261019T130921Z/`. Its
`PRESERVED-BYTES.json` records their hashes. Those copies are historical
evidence and retain the rejected metadata.

The repaired producer constructs an explicit allowlist schema,
`folded-fusion-current-word-v2`. It removes the inherited H/L/core,
P2-code, physical-prefix matrix/inverse and reader-description fields.
The immutable predecessor is retained as an explicit source reference.
Current prefix/tail/complete histograms, masses, margins, event counts
and all four original-owner/current-carrier inverse coefficients are
recomputed from the actual current prefix and its literal inverse.

| Current artifact | q | Prefix margin | t<-V native arity/f |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| SIMPLIFIED | 2 | 6 | 6 |
| SIMPLIFIED | 3 | 8 | 8 |
| THREE | 2 | 0 | 6 |
| THREE | 3 | 2 | 8 |

THREE moves the rank-6 U recovery into an original rank-2 prefix site,
so its prefix margin is `2q-4`. SIMPLIFIED has margin `2q+2`.
Both complete masses remain stock+8.

Each retained N/J family is checked against the actual operator. All
native definitions are recompiled, embedded and compared against each
current event's signed tableau, rank, direction and exact tensor-phase
exponent. The recipe pool contains precisely the forward/inverse used
keys. Replacement and concatenation events are constructed from their
new operators; no prior event's native pointer is copied into them.

`REPAIR-BYTE-VALIDATION.json` records that:

* normal and Python -O producers have byte-identical complete word
  artifacts;
* every prefix, tail, forward, full and serialized inverse event list
  equals its pre-repair counterpart;
* every retained recipe definition equals its pre-repair definition.

Normal and Python -O final verifiers pass all original columns, exact
F_k J endpoints, literal phase families and both inverse orders.
Each fixture has seven rejecting controls, including restored stale
prefix margin, restored stale tV arity and an old source-J recipe
pointer on the replaced fresh query. The last control reaches the
actual embedded-operator mismatch. Receipts are `VERIFICATION.json`
and `VERIFICATION-OPTIMIZED.json`.

This repair introduces no new search, endpoint, caller word, selected
native recipe or mathematical gain. The root's later rank-only q2
mass76 word remains a separate result. Owner, precision, physical tape,
all-size implementation and useful-supplier obligations remain as
stated in PROOF.md. Publication remains outside this local repair.
