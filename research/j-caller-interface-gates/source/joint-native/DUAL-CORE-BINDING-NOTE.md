# Literal paid and folded dual cores

`DUAL-CORE-BINDING.json` independently binds the changed core to the
caller's pinned physical prefix at q2 and q3. It replays both original
owner inverse rows directly from every literal forward event, including
all public-column coefficients. Every unary definition circuit is
recompiled with the frozen exact Clifford implementation; every actual
native rank and all four f-dependent Gaussian phase residues are
checked. The caller's X-character/formula implementation is not imported.

Both named variants have

    K=[[Q1,0],[Q1-P2tilde,P2tilde]],
    H=diag(Du,Dv) Swap K.

The paid variant has `P2tilde=JB2` and one extra early rank-2f J
continuation. The folded variant has **P2tilde=B2**. They are different
physical prefixes and must not share a source-code label. In the folded
word the single J-valued stage2 postfanout is delayed into the next
already paid source query through

    J(b1+Jt)=Jb1-t.

The intervening `t-=c1` bridge is also delayed until after that native
read. Thus the exact original-column matrix changes as specified, but
the extra J continuation is absent. This is a real cross-cut fusion
outside the separately exposed continuation tariff in
`DUAL-CORE-PROOF.md`.

For both variants, the complete original-owner joint inverse pivot
block in the actual physical FJ gauge is

    t<-U: 0,
    t<-V: F J Q1^-1 Dv^-1 F^-1,
    k<-U: F J P2tilde^-1 Du^-1 F^-1,
    k<-V: F J (Q1^-1-P2tilde^-1) Dv^-1 F^-1.

The first nonzero coefficient has native arity **2qf** for every q,f.
The lower-left sign `Q1^-1-P2tilde^-1` is mandatory; its reversed sign
fails the complete noncommutative original-column inverse check.

The paid prefix has margin `2q-2` and hence retains a support gap of at
least2. The folded prefix has margin **2q**, so the same unfactored
coefficient proves only total complete native mass at least stock.
Its exact q2/q3 native prefixes are respectively34/61 rank-2f calls,
with full-output stock72/128 and margins4/6. No positive native
deficit is implied by deleting the paid continuation.

Under equal-volume full-bank native child normalization and the parent's
full-scan transfer premises, even mass neutrality fails the strict
complete child moment at any fixed component b>0. Every admitted child
fraction x in(0,1] satisfies `x^(1-b)>=x`, and at least the prefix native
children have x<1, making the sum strictly larger than stock/m. This
statement also follows asymptotically when the support tariff has an
o(f) additive scan allowance. It does not cover unequal child volumes,
cross-cut query fusion elsewhere in the word, or a new owner/target
contract.

The actual all-input, all-phase binding here is finite q2/q3. The
written rank/core/complement identities hold for every q satisfying the
stated later H/L interface; the caller separately supplies its all-q
local folding and reader equations. This package does not turn the two
finite physical serializations into an all-q physical implementation.
Selected-native lowering of the *complete* derivative tail belongs to
the caller's separate receipt. No improved kappa or full supplier is
claimed.
