## Independent owner-cover counting check

The two residual literal gate keys are `(add,6,8)` with multiplicity4Gamma
and `(add,8,6)` with multiplicityGamma. The independent rational checker
verifies the counting inequalities used to cover them from736 paid owner-basis
monomial calls per owner. It does not assume the identity owner's gate word is
representative.

Fix an arbitrary9-by-9 head matrix A. Complete nine full orthonormal vectors
by choosing their64-bit tails in order. At step i=0,...,8 the required tail
parity and i pairings are i+1 independent linear constraints, provided the
previous tails together with the64-bit all-ones vector are independent.
There are exactly `2^(63-i)` solutions; at most `2^(i+1)` belong to the
forbidden span. The number of good completions is therefore at least

```
2^531 product_(i=0..8)(1-2^(2i-62)) > 2^530.
```

The number of possible full orthonormal9-frames is at most2^612 by the
analogous73-bit constraints. The uniform orthogonal group has constant fibres
over its orbit. To justify that every counted completion belongs to this
orbit, fix the characteristic all-ones vector omega73 and pass to its
nondegenerate72-dimensional alternating orthogonal complement. The independent
vectors `e_i+omega73` and `v_i+omega73` have identical alternating Gram
matrices. The symplectic extension theorem extends this subspace isometry;
extending it by identity on omega73 gives the required orthogonal matrix.
This is an ordinary written linear-algebra proof, not formal-kernel evidence.
Consequently each head has group probability greater than2^-82, at least half
the uniform9-by-9 binary-head probability.

For source `binary_word`, an invertible leading6-by-6 block together with
Schur bits `(6,6)=(8,6)=1` forces `(add,8,6)`. An invertible leading8-by-8
block, bit6 of its inverse times column8 equal to1, and the final Schur pivot
bit1 force `(add,6,8)`. In each case the two constrained bits are independent
uniform bits conditional on the invertible leading block. The probability of
an invertible k-by-k binary matrix is `product_(j=1..k)(1-2^-j)>1/4` for
k=6,8. Each forcing event therefore has uniform-head probability>1/16 and
orthogonal-cover probability>1/32. Inversion preserves uniform owner coverage.
Thus736 paid owner calls supply more than23Gamma occurrences of each key,
which covers the respective4Gamma and Gamma deficits. NONCHILD.md must still
bind those736 calls and literal geometry keys to the actual paid program; this
count alone would not discharge that source obligation.
