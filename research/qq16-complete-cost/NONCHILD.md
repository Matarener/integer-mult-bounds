## Boundary and exact source partition

Compare frozen D and original-arithmetic B over the same original root entry
through public return. The stronger B* adjustment is applied separately. Work
inside complete external original C calls is excluded from this nonchild bill.
It must be counted by the C-body bound exactly once.

`nonchild.census` builds an explicit occurrence injection between the literal
forward/backward compiled original and clean-core instruction lists. The key
removes only the raw source event number; it retains opcode, target/source,
scalar literal, monomial recipe hash, inverse flag and child rank. Every old
and new occurrence appears exactly once in the paired, removed or added sets.
The injection is a cost matching, not a proposed reordering of machine work.
#4166's semantic refinement supplies the actual order and endpoint. #4172's
successful opacity/history result supplies equality of the cost of matched
closed primitive invocations at their common geometry.

The complete removed list is:

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Centre wrapper monomial dispatch |16|16|32|
| External rank-eight child |8|8|16|
| Private copy dispatch |8|0|8|
| Unit subtract scalar dispatch |0|6|6|
| Control-only marker |1|1|2|

The new clean cores add 404 and 402 scalar dispatches respectively. At least six
of their literal keys are `('add', -1, 1)`, so six new unit subtract calls match
the six removed ones. Targets are different where necessary; complete packet
extent, signed component width, Gaussian count and actual scalar recipe agree.
The physical engine's loops and restored borrowed ports depend on these values,
not the fixed role selector. Both calls are on successful admitted inputs.
They have no fitted or Ordinary dependency. Control-only markers perform no
source tape action and retain their separately compiled control obligation.

All other root source events, including both bridge legs and the complete

owner keys, 704 selected slots, eight private roles and full bank geometry.
Their gather/scatter/commit, group entry/exit, private initialization and final
zero checks match. Zero private values and structural extents at those checks
are part of #4166's successful endpoint proof. Do not cancel a private-state
check from a bare count of instructions.

## Local monomial bases and exact gate atoms

For each monomial, split the actual `MonomialEngine.run` at its source call
sites into:

1. descriptor/layout setup and complete input copy;
2. one `signed_phase.scan`;
3. one complete `single_column_rows` or `compact_gate` per compiled gate;
4. complete output append/clear, closure and caller `transfer`/marker erasure.

The sum of items 1, 2 and 4 is the **monomial base**. At equal complete geometry
its R/W/M price does not depend on the recipe or inverse flag. In particular,
`Recipe.phase` reads all 73 coordinate bits for every column even if its linear
or quadratic coefficients vanish. Those coefficients affect only finite
control arithmetic. The negative `emit_component` branch and positive
`copy_bits` branch have the same source read/append/step/countdown schedule.
Inverse mode moves the phase scan from before to after the gates; it does not
alter its action count on successful inputs. All source numerical admission
and borrowed-value obligations remain in force.

A gate atom is keyed by its exact `(kind,target,source)` and full packet
geometry, including the 73 vector widths, prefixes, unused rows, front/back
and spectator fields, columns, K, G, residue, physical H_b and coefficient
count. `compact_gate` passes precisely the corresponding source/target/late
selector to the same closed Compact/Binary/Ordinary pipeline. It does not pass
the surrounding recipe or inverse flag. Inverse traversal reverses the gate
word and leaves each self-inverse gate selector unchanged.

For this comparison, all added producer/service primitive backends must use
the same admitted compiled q, profile, programs and capacities as B's pipeline.
This is an explicit compatibility domain, not a consequence of the callable
name `ordinary_factory`. Successful closed marker phases and six-arena state
relations are the inherited #4172 boundary. Count scratch and packet roles
begin at the same canonical head and structural lengths. Every gate atom
includes its completed Binary/native descendants and output disposal; those
costs are not added again after matching.

The 32 removed centre wrappers contain exactly **736 gates**. The added
producer line calls and service intrinsic right/left wrappers contribute
**7,150 fixed gates** over 800 monomial bases, where the 64 producer owner-basis
calls are represented by the identity owner for this fixed-gate count. That
identity sample supplies no general-owner gate credit. The exact pointwise
deficits are only:

    add(target=6, source=8): 4 per owner,
    add(target=8, source=6): 1 per owner.

All other 731 removed gates have a literal same-selector injection into the
added fixed words. The separate uniform-cover theorem supplies the remaining
five using the actual 736 owner-basis calls per cover owner: 64 producer and
672 service. Half are inverse traversals; this reverses order only. The literal
`cover.unrank` rows, with zero offset/phase, are compiled as `literal(g)` in
both entry and exit owner views. The same object `g` is used; even a proved
transpose/inverse convention would require an explicit cover bijection.

Match 32 removed bases against 32 of the 800 added bases. This does not count
the 64 producer owner bases twice: their gate contribution is counted in the
owner sum, while their base contribution appears only once in the 800. The
additional 672 service owner bases are nonnegative and can be discarded.
There is no assumption that a whole rank-eight wrapper costs the same as a
rank-one wrapper. The matching occurs only at the source base and exact gate
interfaces just defined.

## A positive subset of the complete new bank work

After matching the two old core groups, D has 41 additional non-map groups
and 12286/51 map groups per Gamma on the complete parent bank. The service
has 2022 groups per Gamma on a bank of B_bank/32. Each successful actual
`BankPackets.gather` reads and steps across every bank bit and then rewinds
the borrowed source. Consequently it contributes at least `(2B,0,2B)` in
R/W/M, without counting Counter work or selected-packet appends.

Additionally, the subsequent `scatter` appends exactly one output bit for
every bit in the full bank, whether selected or unselected. Its B output
writes are disjoint from the gather source reads/moves.

The sum of these **candidate-only source intervals** is therefore at least

    R >= (281593/408) Gamma B_bank,
    W >= (281593/816) Gamma B_bank,
    M >= (281593/408) Gamma B_bank,
    R+W+M >= (1407965/816) Gamma B_bank.

This is not the old diagnostic-volume subtraction relabelled as an action
bound. The paired original two group cycles cancel by the source contract;
each listed remaining gather is an actual nonnegative added interval. All other
scatter actions, commit, selected field copies, Counter work, 448-family acquisition,
encoded compiler, assembly, extraction, borrowed comparisons, publication,
service disposal and fences are additional nonnegative intervals. No omitted
candidate cost can make this lower bound smaller.

The complete service census remains 1696 monomials, 3484 direct scalar calls,
176 exchange-internal negations, 48 private zero calls and 336 children per
Gamma. Internal negations are included in the exchange and never charged
again. Whole family V=Gamma*v and B_bank=14336V already contain Gamma.

## Conservative upper debit for the remaining removed small intervals

Let v be the full per-owner packet extent and L the complete Count-length
envelope used in CBODY.md, enlarged if required to dominate parent and child
preparation descriptor lengths. The header width is the actual baseline
h_B=2685. All Count bodies in these intervals have fewer than 4L binary digits.
The inherited binary Count source gives a conservative 256L componentwise
price for linear operations and 4096L² for product. The exact full-countdown
amortization is proved and calibrated in CBODY.md; it must not be replaced by
one logarithmic scan charge for every payload bit.

`Machine.copy_step` consists of `packet_extent` (one product and multiplication
by two), an exact `require_zero` scan and preserved `copy_symbols`, then Count
retirement. The complete packet scans are linear in v after countdown
amortization. Including all delimiters, rewinds and zero tests, the bound

    U_copy = 2^20 (v+1+L^4)

holds separately in each action coordinate. There are fewer than 64 Count/
Counter setup operations in this source interval; each is bounded by 4096L²,
and payload/read/write/move traffic is below 128v+128. Their sum is below the
stated envelope for L>=16. This includes copying into the existing v-bit
private zero packet, rather than an uncharged blank destination.

One removed original external child **shell** consists of `prepare_child`,
B's `enter_child` excluding `native_child.run`, and completed reconnection.
It includes width/tail arithmetic, after-field product/addition, the current
payload preserved copy, fourteen descriptor preserved copies, header write,
primitive-boundary tests, header read, full returned output transfer into the
old target, marker erasure, all fifteen capsule clears and final header clear.
It has fewer than 128 Count/setup/descriptor operations and fewer than 128v
payload actions per coordinate, plus fewer than 32h_B header actions. Thus

    U_shell = 2^24 (v+1+L^4+h_B)

is a conservative componentwise bound. Complete C.run, including all of its
descendants, is explicitly excluded and debited separately. This is B's
external shell, never A's expensive embedded/parking shell. These deliberately
loose coefficients follow a source loop envelope; the sign proof uses upper
bounds only on work that is removed.

Accordingly the total remaining small negative debit is at most
`8 U_copy + 16 U_shell` in each coordinate. Six scalar calls were already
matched and must not be subtracted again. The fixed original engine
construction is common. B's additional controller setup/terminal retirement
may conservatively be debited by its entire inherited 66496-action bound;
all additional D construction/inventory retirement is nonnegative. The matched
original backend's 703-write cold/warm potential cancels once globally.
Finite rebinding/control costs and historical backing remain separate.

## Controls and limits

The executable checks reject partial-cover denominators, wrong service-bank
volume, Boolean cardinalities and negative removed upper prices. The source
occurrence injection detects duplicated/dropped events independently of rank
histograms. The integrated verifier must additionally bind the full gate
cover certificate and geometry-specific C-body upper bound before claiming a
sign. A failure of the owner-gate theorem leaves precisely the five named
Compact atoms unresolved; this file does not replace their prices by zero.

Actual reduced phase-pair calibrations execute all four unit phases at H_b=4,8,17
with nonconstant signed input (3,-2); their R/W/M counts agree phase by phase.
Actual BankPackets.gather calibrations use 6,48,90-bit banks with multiple
prefix/unused-row cases and preserve complete borrowed banks and heads. These
are primitive calibrations, not production O73 execution.
