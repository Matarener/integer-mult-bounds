# Rational scalar overwrites do not remove the sector workspace field

For every `s>=1` and `r>=1`, the formal Boolean-sector perturbation map
`x_(b,a) -> (1+q E_a/2)x_(b,a)` requires at least one owned field, even with
arbitrary rational scalar overwrites and free ordinary linear mixing. Any
finite number of dirty borrowed fields may be used, provided they are exactly
restored. The minimum is exactly one, using the cited PR #5658 construction
for the upper bound.

This is a scoped algebraic proof with finite corroboration. The new result is
the lower bound in the enlarged alphabet. It resolves the scalar-overwrite
workspace question explicitly left open in [Ali Romman's PR #5658](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5658).
The predecessor's route, cost counts and one-field upper bound are cited,
not independently replayed or audited here.

Ordinary instructions are matrices over the algebra generated by the commuting
involutions `J_0=q^2,J_1,...,J_(s-1)`. Native queries multiply one field by
`q` or `q^-1`. The paired evaluations `q=1` and `q=-1` agree on every
ordinary coefficient. Thus every zero-owned-field program with nonsingular
paired outputs has determinant ratio `+1` or `-1`; the target's ratio is `3^r`. [PROOF.md](PROOF.md)
gives the complete argument and exact instruction boundary.

## Limits

The theorem covers fixed finite linear words in the formal faithful-sector
algebra. It excludes nonlinear or adaptive instructions, discarded borrowed
outputs and ordinary primitives with additional `q` dependence. Arbitrary
rational scales are allowed, including nonunits such as `3/2`. The argument
does not extend the predecessor's `r+1` CLEAR lower bound to that alphabet.
No physical native realization, complete caller, precision/tape tariff,
supplier admission or new kappa follows. The selected frontier is unchanged.
