## 2. Gaussian rank is subadditive

Write the binary symplectic tableau of a Clifford U in X|Z block form. Its Gaussian grade is the rank of its upper-right block B_U. This equals the Lagrangian distance `d(Z,U Z)`. Scalar phase and ordinary monomial factors do not change this grade. The retained compiler realizes a grade-r word with one rank-r Gaussian request and positive finite ordinary factors.

For two Cliffords U and V, the upper-right block of their product is

`B_(UV)=A_U B_V+B_U D_V`.

Therefore

`r(UV)<=r(U)+r(V)`.

No invertibility of an individual block is needed. For coframes A and B, symplectic invariance gives

`r(B A^-1)=d(A^-1 Z,B^-1 Z)`.

The inverse images matter. Forward-image distances can give a different answer, as the complete small-matrix controls show.

For nonnegative integers r_j and `0<t<=1`, subadditivity of x^t gives

`(sum_j r_j)^t<=sum_j r_j^t`.

For `0<t<1`, the two-term statement follows by scaling to a+b=1 and using `x^t>=x` on [0,1]; induction gives the finite sum. The t=1 case is equality.

## 3. Persistent field paths pay their endpoints

Let one persistent original visit coframes `A0,A1,...,Am`. Its transport words are `Uj=Aj A_(j-1)^-1`. Their product telescopes:

`Um ... U1=Am A0^-1`.

Let k be the exact Gaussian grade of this two-sided endpoint. Combining the two inequalities above proves

`sum_j r(Uj)^t >= k^t`.

This is a statement about the declared physical transport path, not a claim that the intermediate logical payload never changes. Scalar contacts can change that payload. They do not change the chart or interrupt its path. Every charged query is assigned to exactly one field, so the inequalities can be summed without double counting.

Each independent helper is credited with precisely this endpoint grade k, not its final frame dimension. Hence its paid path covers at least its own changed productive moment. A rank-zero helper has no positive productive credit. Arbitrarily changing the helper coframes cannot defeat the inequality, because the credit changes with them.

The argument remains valid after splitting a physical transport into several paid children and ordinary factors: rank subadditivity applies to their actual product. It also remains valid after joining adjacent pure transports. Neither representation reduces their total t-moment below the endpoint grade. Tensoring all charts to width f multiplies every grade by f and both sides by f^t.

For a complete supplier in which every productive original obeys this same contract, summation gives `P_t>=T_t`. That conditional statement rules out a strict complete moment contraction for this class. It is not a claim that an arbitrary multiplication algorithm or existing outer caller belongs to the class.

## 4. Exact application to the proposed local word

Both source directions contain 632 original fields: 224 main fields and 408 independent helpers. The separate scalar replay gives `(X,Y,h)->(X,Y+X,h)` in the forward direction and `(X,Y,h)->(X-Y,Y,h)` in the backward direction when every native generator is identity. The retained source interpreter separately checks the full free-group endpoints.

Every actual scalar contact uses its declared common frame. All 240 warps in each direction preserve frame dimension, so the proposal replaces them by identity between the same dimension coframe. Every original path is continuous through all proper transports. Scalar CLEAR and COPY target only the owned centre, and nonzero scalar SCALE does not discard an original. Every original reaches its literal final frame. All 448 entry/exit main normalizers per direction are present.

The required main coframes are `H0=I`, `H1=F_L`, `H8=F_(L-perp)` and `H9=F_full`, up to the proposal's actual ordinary boundary factors. The reference line is nonisotropic and complementary to its hyperplane. There are 112 main paths from H1 to H9 and 112 from H0 to H8. Each endpoint grade is eight. Thus the main paths cost at least `224*8^t`.

The literal owned centre has eight transports. Every one is between dimensions zero and eight. The proposal fixes those physical coframes to H0 and H8, so each grade is exactly eight. Centre clear resets do not remove these eight specified queries. They add `8*8^t`. All other centre, ordinary, scalar, route, coefficient and adapter costs are nonnegative.

Subtracting the exact changed helper credits yields

`P_t-T_helpers,t >= (224+8)*8^t = 232*8^t`.

At t=1, the deficit is at least 1856. Every helper grade is at most nine, so `T_helpers,t<=408*9^t`. Whenever T is nonzero,

`P_t/T >= 1+(232/408)*(8/9)^t >= 1+232/459`.

The final inequality uses `0<t<=1` and `0<8/9<1`. Thus the paid local moment is at least about 50.54 percent above its helper credit for every coframe choice in the contract. A scan of partial Fourier subsets cannot produce a favorable standalone helper-versus-paid comparison.

This does not prove that every larger caller fails. Such a caller must supply its actual main productive credit and all external paid work, while respecting these local costs. If its full physical word also has only continuous identity-native coframe transports, Section 3 gives the complete obstruction. No such membership claim is made here for an unexamined outer caller.

## 6. Consequence and boundary

The proposed operator construction is retained as valid under its stated phase-exact/native premises. Its helper-versus-paid local invoice cannot be favorable. A useful successor must retain nonidentity logical native action, change the scalar/ownership/zero-state contract with a complete proof, or give a caller that actually funds the explicit residual. Repeating the coframe search while keeping the same contract cannot evade this bound.

No selected frontier is changed. No new physical supplier, complete outer admission, rounded implementation, human acceptance, formal-kernel proof or global multiplication lower bound is asserted.
