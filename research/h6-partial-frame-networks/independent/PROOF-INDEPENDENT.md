## Ordinary inputs and private interfaces

indeterminate input. In particular, the centres and compensation readers are

A private forward episode is the explicitly assumed clean COPY/descent/scatter/
ERASE interface. It copies centre c, scatters coefficients q_i into targets y_i,
and does not change c or its copy before erasure. Its ordinary operator is

`y_i <- y_i + q_i c`.

The separate private dual episode gathers `p=sum_i q_i y_i` from a cleared

the unchanged readout. Its ordinary operator is the inverse transpose of the

no arbitrary private entering value is included in this interface.
