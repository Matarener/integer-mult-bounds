## What is new in this package and what is reused

The preprint supplies the spectral-concentration architecture, deformation, tapered recovery and immutable descriptions. Their general mechanisms are credited to OpenAI and its cited antecedents. The current additional application is the exact four-phase Gaussian spectral bridge, its explicit denominator-R ordinary word and lower-precision bulk, together with the ramified Gaussian Hermite presentation, normalized mode budget, joint jet filtering and complete query/endpoint interfaces.

The physical sum acquisition at role 146 and its two sibling acquisitions are already PR 5802 results. Their use here illustrates the compiler and gives an exact affine-parameter packet; it is not a fourth acquisition or an unreported κ gain. The ordinary tensor source already contains a recursive exact Gaussian transform; B1-B4 provide a different exact decomposition, not a claim that the transform was previously unknown.

The paper's global contraction theorem has not been specialized to a faster multiplier. The explicit exceptional response, native source implementation, complete paid/target histogram, strict guards and compatible BIT/outer endpoint remain necessary for such a later claim. These are clearly separated from the positive constructors already proved; none is hidden inside an assumed favorable gadget.
