## Principles and bounded deviations

Build the Lever changed a hand classification into two exact rerunnable
incidence/field checkers. Prove It Works required real optimized CLI faults,
final committed-byte/source-history checks and Git-free committed-export
replays. The exact source and export hashes are preserved in the session
receipts before disposable output cleanup. No selected construction is edited.

Power of Ten inspections cover the three Python scripts. Their call graphs
are acyclic and contain no recursion or dynamic source generation. Incidence
extensions have at most 21 candidate blocks on six old points, at most three
selected blocks and at most 7! canonical labelings. Primary type sets contain
at most 24 entries. Independent line enumeration has 99 candidate lines and
an enforced 200,000-state/worklist ceiling; the actual census has 8,390 states.
Field arithmetic has four or eight fixed bit steps. Elimination has at most
seven rows and three columns. Rank tables have 128 entries and loops over at
most 35 triples, 21 pairs, 21 marked cases or 5,040 permutations. Reads are
capped at 128KiB. The fault suite copies at most 32 package files and executes
18 bounded subprocesses, each with a 60-second timeout. Registered commands
have a 120-second timeout. All scientific invariants use explicit exceptions,
including under optimization. No function exceeds 60 formatted lines.

Rule 3 deviation: Python/JSON/sets and arbitrary-size integers allocate during
execution. The standalone processes have finite input, collection, state and
subprocess bounds above; there is no service or growing input stream. The
capacity-fault control exercises oversized input and private copies use
context-managed cleanup. A larger point or file domain requires a new capacity
review, rather than silently increasing these bounds.

Rule 9 deviation: the independent rank helper selects one of two fixed local
determinant functions, and finite standard-library iterator callbacks allocate
internally. Both targets are exercised on every positive replay; neither is
caller-supplied. No mutable shared state or arbitrary runtime dispatch exists.

Private copies require regular sources, stable source inode/device, new owned
regular destinations with different inode/device, complete writes and exact
hash equality. There is no metadata/mode copying or chmod. All mutations occur
inside the exclusively created temporary directory. Two local copy controls reject existing destinations and symlink sources. Source bytes are compared
after the entire suite; the temporary directory is removed on every exit.

Focused Ruff formatting and E4,E7,E9,F,B,UP,SIM,I diagnostics run on all three
scripts, with Python warnings as errors. Optional annotation/docstring policy
for a supported library is outside this isolated executable package. No
warning suppression is added. A separate Ruff `--select ALL` inventory reports
177 annotation, public-docstring, naming, literal-constant, CLI-print and
standalone-script policy findings; its full output is retained in the session.
The inventory also flags the fixed local subprocess call with explicit
positive/negative status checks, the bounded seven-case fault dispatch and one
formatted long line. Those calls and branches were inspected above; no
suppression is added. The all-rule inventory is not a passing gate or a claim
of universal Ruff compliance. The replay manifest makes the scientific and
fault checks recurring repository checks.

## Limits

No Lean, Docker, full selected-supplier replay, native supplier execution,
physical invoice or independent human/formal proof-kernel audit was run.
Finite classification covers seven simple points and two marks only. Six
terms can have ranks (4,4); more marked targets need different transfer
conditions. No broader obstruction or new kappa is claimed.
