## Uniform payment for records and stopping tests

I use actual complete records, not numerical labels, in the following
absorption. With x=log2 p, (1) implies, for each fixed k>=0,

    p^k/r <= 2^(kx-bx^2) <= 2^(k^2/(4b)).            (3)

The upper bound on log2 r and the retained descriptor contract give
address/key length A<=A0 p. The compact source charges

    O(V+M A^6+delta_bad M A(R+A)), R=2hr,

where h is the working component width and M is its record count. On the
fast branch delta_bad<=5/(128p^3). Thus A^6/R<=A0^6 2^(9/b), and
delta_bad A(1+A/R) is uniformly bounded. I also pay the phase invoice
O(V+M A^2) through (3). The same inequalities cover the retained polynomial
preparation and descriptor backing. They use no exact equality r=2^(Theta(p/d)).
The coefficient count remains unchanged through child descent, so I may
apply them at each actual record-bearing node.

The new stopping test computes min(e^(10^6),g) by the retained binary
power algorithm. It has 20 fixed iterations, 19 squares and seven result
products, or 26 capped-product calls. Stored values stay at most g;
nontrivial temporary products stay below g^2. With ell=bitlength(g),
I pay O(ell^2) using the retained bitwise Horner multiplication. On
root-generated nodes, component_bits>=p, V_node>=2p, and g<=p. The bound
(1+log2 p)^2<=13p therefore pays this test within O(V_node). It closes
its scratch before child descent and adds no tape role or Gaussian operation.
An arbitrary huge-root-width toy descriptor with tiny components does not
satisfy this time-domain argument.

## Conditional single-bit exposure on a global tensor

Let S=(s_1,...,s_g) be distinct address positions of a complete dyadic
tensor. Choose a complete gK target span, with spaced selected positions
T=(rho,rho+K,...,rho+(g-1)K); in the root convention take right=0 and
left equal to the remaining address width. Require that the full span
fits and that the actual record contract survives its placement.

Define a coordinate permutation P by sending s_i to t_i, then mapping
T\S bijectively onto S\T, and fixing the complement of S union T.
It moves at most 2g positions. For coefficient arrays,

    P^-1 (H0_T tensor I) P = H0_S tensor I.           (4)

A coordinate permutation of the complete tensor leaves each target chunk
a complete binary field. The unselected coordinates may come from different
original axes. A fixed coarse-label microbox generally lacks that closure.
I prove (4) without assuming a free payload permutation.

If I supply the pinned arbitrary-support router contract, each direction
costs O(V g^tau polylog p), including its own prescribed record grouping
and restoration. I pay its additive poly(p) setup through (3), since the
complete input volume includes the actual r coefficients. I then obtain
the conditional global cost

    O(V (g^alpha log p + g^tau polylog p))
      = O(V g^alpha polylog p).

I retain the router's logarithmic factor; tau<alpha alone does not absorb
an arbitrary polylog p into the single log p in (2), uniformly in g.
I invoke the retained conditional routing theorem here. exposure.py verifies
finite address and linear-algebra certificates; it is not an executable fast
physical router. I did not find such an adapter in the inspected source tree.

Nick Petalas proves a complementary obstruction in
[PR3581](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/blob/282915e57cf71d7a6f0087fbb6ae03345a0b33d4/research/isolated-axis-exposure/PROOF.md).
For an isolated-axis hard family in this domain, he needs g completed
equal-contiguous-block B1 exchanges and Theta(Vg) time when each call
materializes a distinct full-packet output. He permits intermediate spectator
movement but restricts the operation alphabet to those exchanges. The retained
arbitrary-support router also uses controlled rotations, carry-dependent
updates and sparse repair. I retain that richer conditional premise; I do not
derive its cost from a list of individual completed B1 swaps. I allow wider
support in router intermediate states and impose the 2g bound on the final
coordinate permutation.

If actual complete records are absent, a separately paid untouched-suffix
construction is necessary. Grouping ceil(log2 p)^2 genuinely untouched
complete bits would supply (1), but a declared or fixed suffix does not.
I do not silently infer that input contract or drop its exposure/restoration bill.
The tensor compiler's raw H also needs its supplied 2^g scaling and extra
O(g) guard; this package returns normalized H0.

I do not extend (2) beyond its g cap or inherit the modern outer headline.
Larger selected sets need batching or a different root contract, with a
fresh complete outer ledger. I leave support-router implementation, short-
record placement, outer error/packing integration and independent scientific
review open. I leave the selected frontier unchanged.
