### Scalar gauges need return only at real observations

The next step tracks a scalar gauge between each old value and its emitted

\[
z_d\leftarrow z_d+c\frac{g_s}{g_d}z_s.
\]

gauges and subsequent ADD coefficients. Uniform scalar factors commute with the retained linear native
warps; the exact native matrices, lenders, entry frames and exit frames are

## 6. A concrete objective for the next κ regime

F(a)=\sum_r N_r r^{1-a}-322560\cdot73^{1-a}.

### The fixed construction conserves complete raw rank mass

The moment inequality alone permits changes that the retained geometry does
not. Each current local provider has raw paid mass equal to **1,856 plus its
hosted helper widths**: THREE has \(5157=1856+3301\), and FOUR has
\(5156=1856+3300\). Every original phase axis is either hosted or charged in
the residual owner child. Consequently

\[
\sum_r rN_r
=17,418,240+1856\cdot3240
=23,431,680.
\]

This identity is tied to the retained monotone native endpoint and observation
contract and the original phase-axis pool. The owner receipt independently
reconstructs 10,694,150 hosted dimensions and 6,724,090 residual idle
dimensions, whose sum is the fixed phase mass. Changing helper widths
transfers rank mass between the local word and the residual owner. In
particular, FOUR's local mass reduction of one is repaid by one residual-owner
dimension. The actual mixed improvement conserves complete raw rank mass.

This explains a systematic obstacle. A pure net rank-3-to-rank-2 decrement
would save nearly one unit of moment, but would violate that invariant. The
budget retains the arithmetic of 1,080 such decrements, one per three calls,
only as a price yardstick for a **broader construction with a changed
contract**. It is not an admissible fixed-geometry frame refit. Omitting the
compensating owner charge would produce a false gain.

Within the retained contract, the useful freedom is the distribution of paid
ranks. With \(H_r=r^{1-a}\),
\(H_r=r-a r\log r+O(a^2)\). A mass-preserving replacement cancels the leading
linear terms, so its benefit is much smaller than a raw dimension decrement.
Scalar simplification alone does not change that distribution.

### Synthesize a mass-preserving receiver batch

The preceding rank-batch construction supplies a compatible objective. Let \(s\) count
the nonzero receiver rows, \(B\) be the full physically available update
matrix, and \(d,t\) be the first and total frame-rank increases. Such a
physically available receiver batch can save

\[
(s-\operatorname{rank}B)(H_d+H_{t-d}-H_t)
\]

before added costs. A second realizability check is essential: the replacement
cannot consume more paid contacts of any rank than the actual invoice has.
The retained \(d=4,t=9\) fixture saves \(H_4+H_5-H_9\) per deficiency, but
each local provider has only **20 rank-5 contacts**. Its 32-row eager schedule
needs 32. Even granting every rank-5 contact in the complete supplier,
including idle-owner contacts, the total 66,425 is below the 86,402 deficiencies
needed to clear the target by that replacement alone. A favorable isolated
price therefore cannot be substituted into the current invoice at that scale.

The new `receiver-target/` fixture adapts the same exact dirty-receiver matrix
to **\(d=4,t=8\)**. Each deficiency replaces two rank-4 contacts with one
rank-8 contact. Raw mass is conserved because \(4+4=8\), and its unit saving is

\[
\delta=2H_4-H_8
\approx0.00964711959408832006286902720055105.
\]

The exact rational budget requires at least **30 useful rank deficiencies per
call** before additional paid costs; 29 are insufficient. The adapted
**32-row, rank-two** fixture supplies exactly 30. Its eager profile is
\(\{4:64\}\), while its compiled profile is \(\{4:4,8:30\}\); both have raw
mass 256. The 64 eager rank-4 contacts fit the necessary count in every
provider: THREE has 102 and FOUR has 99.

At the target the balanced fixture would save approximately
**0.289413587822649602 per call**, leaving approximately
**0.002633807140394665 per call** beyond the present deficit. With zero other
added paid costs, a batch in 3,211 suitably selected calls would suffice;
3,210 would not. Applying it formally in all calls changes complete rank-4
count to 132,810 and rank-8 count to 123,120, with all other counts unchanged.
Every count remains nonnegative, complete raw mass remains 23,431,680, and
the target complex moment is negative by approximately 8.533535134878714
before extra costs. These are exact interval checks in `BUDGET.json` and its
independent review; decimals here are rounded for readability.

This is a more suitable synthesis objective because it survives both raw-mass
and paid-inventory checks. Those are necessary conditions, not a physical
cohort. The interval must truly have total rank increase eight. Truncating a
rank-nine interval and omitting its remaining rank-one continuation would
create a false saving unless that continuation were included in the complete
comparison.

The new fixture also replaces the generic Euclidean mixing program with a
much smaller structured basis. Its original update rows have the form

\[
B_j=p+q_jv,\qquad p=(1,0,1),\quad v=(0,1,2),\quad q_j=j\bmod5-2.
\]

First apply \(Y_1\leftarrow Y_1-Y_0\), then
\(Y_0\leftarrow Y_0+2Y_1\). Their update rows become \(v\) and \(p\),
respectively. For each remaining receiver subtract \(Y_0+q_jY_1\), omitting
the second ADD when \(q_j=0\). Only two mixed receivers now carry source
updates. Undo the literal mixing word after all receivers reach their shared
exit frame.

This uses **56 entry ADDs, 56 inverse ADDs and four source additions**, or
**116 scalar instructions** instead of the generic compiler's 737. The
mixing norm remains eight, while the inverse norm improves from 64 to **four**.
Entry prefix/suffix norms are both eight; exit prefix/suffix norms are both
four, improving the generic exit prefix bound of 156. No additional bank is
used. This is a concrete improvement to the isolated receiver template; its
paid histogram is unchanged, and the 621 scalar deletions are separate from
the admitted providers' 1,372 deletions.
