## Result and model

The proposed change raises the helper entrance from `E=span(414,51)` to
`P=span(392,37,22)`, formally reducing ordinary hosted width from 7 to 6.
`P` is the unique three-dimensional extension inside the inherited first
producer frame. Other starts, terminals, native operators and target contracts
are retained.

- Keeping the two original release slots gives a short frame path carrying
  direction 22 into a forbidden target terminal. An independently built least
  constraint graph finds exactly four failing target terminals. This rules out
  every monotone scalar-frame refit of that unchanged contact word.
- A changed word deletes those releases, reconstructs the original dirty value
  at the cut before source B1752, and releases it through ten exact dyadic
  additions. It restores the complete original current-state prefix and passes
  full arbitrary-dirty endpoint/dual/cleanup checks. It still violates frames.
  A carrier-frame invariant rules out any scalar-only block at that cut that
  restores the required receiver coefficient and retains the subsequent
  consumer. The proof allows different scalar factorizations and independent
  dirty scratch; it does not assume the ten-gate recipe is optimal.

## Scope and limits

The artifact combines exact finite checks of two pinned words with written
implications for arbitrary monotone scalar frames and arbitrary scalar-only
late blocks under the declared boundary assumptions. Complete dirty endpoint
checks include 632 live inputs and 91 independent buffer inputs. A finite exact
operator computation, a written universal implication on that finite interface,
a formal-kernel proof and full supplier admission are distinct.

The original positive construction target is not achieved. Changed consumers,
new native maps, different producer or target contracts, different entrance
subspaces outside the inherited producer frame, and other cuts remain open.
No actual local paid profile is admitted for a frame-infeasible candidate.
Native Gaussian phase, chronological owners, source windows, precision/tape,
all-size and outer transfer are not discharged. No selected-frontier change,
unconditional multiplication theorem, independent human review or proof-kernel
acceptance is claimed.
