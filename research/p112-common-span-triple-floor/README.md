# A common span forces the four-term triple tariff

For three alternating-half indicators in an even binary-orthonormal partition,
assume the two largest complete groups have the same binary span V and size d.
Let the third group have size e<=d. Over every coefficient field, every exact
at-most-four-term replacement has actual complete-plane rectangular Gram bill
at least **2d+e**. Arbitrary ambient factors and external directions are allowed.
The original three terms attain the bound.

The new lemma gives bill **at least 3d** for both residual non-Fano families,
VVV and MMM, in characteristic other than two. The four reader annihilators
lie in distinct joint atoms of two coordinate decompositions of V. Those
atoms form a direct sum, so their dimensions sum to at most d. This replaces
a dimension-bounded geometry census under the stated common-span premise.
[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) combines the lemma with the inherited seven-point
classification and selective transfers to prove the full four-term statement.

This is a scoped obstruction. Differing group spans remain open. No cheaper
decoder, physical supplier, new kappa or selected-frontier change is claimed.
Independent human and formal proof-kernel review are unclaimed.
