## 2. A strict positive moment gain throughout the target interval

For 0<C<=351/100000 put t=1-C. Define the target-minus-paid difference between the two complete local suppliers by

    E(C)=201-78*2^t+9*3^t-27*4^t+12*5^t
              -18*6^t-36*7^t+42*8^t.

**Theorem 1.** E(C)>74 C>0 on this entire interval.

**Proof.** At C=0, the two equal rank-mass changes give E(0)=0. Its derivative is

    E'(C)=156*2^(-C) log 2+108*4^(-C) log 4
          +108*6^(-C) log 6+252*7^(-C) log 7
          -27*3^(-C) log 3-60*5^(-C) log 5
          -336*8^(-C) log 8.

The elementary logarithm bounds used below are

    log 2>69/100, log 4>138/100,
    log 6>169/100, log 7>19/10,
    log 3<11/10, log 5<33/20, log 8<21/10.

For completeness, integrate the geometric series in log((1+x)/(1-x)). At x=1/3, positive truncation and a geometric remainder give 69/100<log 2<7/10. At x=1/2, they give 1<log 3<11/10. Hence log 6>169/100 and log 4>138/100. Also

    log 5=2 log 2+log(1+1/4)<7/5+1/4=33/20,
    log 7=3 log 2+log(1-1/8)>207/100-1/7>19/10.

Here log(1+x)<x and log(1-x)>-x/(1-x) follow by integration. Finally log 8<21/10. For every k<=8 in a positive term,

    k^(-C)>=1-C log 8>99/100.

The sum of the positive derivative terms is therefore greater than

    (99/100)[156*(69/100)+108*(138/100)
                         +108*(169/100)+252*(19/10)]
      =(99/100)*918.

The absolute sum of its negative terms is below

    27*(11/10)+60*(33/20)+336*(21/10)=8343/10.

Consequently E'(C)>74, with strict rational margin. Integrating from zero proves the claim. No fractional moment has been numerically evaluated. QED.

The result is a strict improvement of a complete actual local supplier. It is not an isolated edge saving. It does not assert that this supplier alone is a complete multiplication algorithm.

## 3. Higher-moment changes with all new children charged

Use the same C interval, t=1-C and s=t+1/5.

**Lemma 2.** The productive t-moment increases by more than 47, and the paid s-moment increases by less than 120:

    delta T_t>47,
    delta P_s<120.

The second inequality is an upper bound, not a claim that the paid higher moment decreases.

**Proof.** Rewrite

    delta T_t=36(8^t-7^t)+6(8^t-6^t).

On [6,8], the derivative t x^(t-1) exceeds (99/100)^2>98/100, since t>99/100 and 8^(-C)>99/100. Integration gives delta T_t>47.

For the paid bound, 7/6<s<6/5. The following rational comparisons follow by raising positive quantities to the indicated integer powers:

    2^(6/5)<7/3, 3^(7/6)>7/2,
    4^(6/5)<16/3, 5^(7/6)>13/2, 6^(6/5)<26/3.

The complete changed paid profile therefore gives

    delta P_s
      =-201+78*2^s-9*3^s+27*4^s-12*5^s+12*6^s
      <-201+78*(7/3)-9*(7/2)+27*(16/3)
                               -12*(13/2)+12*(26/3)
      =239/2<120.

All twelve new H6 jobs appear in this inequality. QED.

## 4. Literal substitution into a compatible complete caller

**Theorem 3.** Take any explicitly supplied finite caller using n>0 occurrences of the old restricted-C supplier through its actual 56 A / 56 C ports. Replace those occurrences by the new supplier, leaving the caller's other actual operations and its port choices unchanged. The resulting complete program has exactly the old physical main endpoint and independent-input count. Its complete signed moment improves by n E(C)>74n C.

If at this same tested C the old complete caller has source tail guards

    gamma_old<31/32, delta_old<19/20,

with cutoff c>=17, target floor b unchanged, exponent u=1/5 and the old source completion convention, then the new complete caller has

    gamma_new<49/50<1,
    delta_new<delta_old<1.

Any old nonnegative first-moment margin becomes strictly positive. This is a constructive replacement theorem on an actual caller supplied as input; it does not assume the existence of a missing profitable caller in order to claim multiplication κ.

**Proof.** The local main maps and port partition are unchanged, in each actual orientation. Each source invocation retains every arbitrary original helper, with its explicit new two-sided positive matcher. Their banks remain disjoint and independent. Literal replacement therefore preserves the whole main composition. The changed productive helper operators are exactly the new Gaussian jobs, with every old job debited. Summing local paid and productive inventories gives n E(C).

Let T be the old whole productive t-moment. The old local helper rank mass is 3066 and its largest rank is eight, so its t-moment exceeds 3000. Hence T>3000n. Lemma 2 gives

    T_new>T+47n,
    P_new,s<P_old,s+120n.

Since c^(1/5)>=17^(1/5)>7/4, the old gamma guard implies

    gamma_new<[(31/32)T+(480/7)n]/(T+47n)<49/50.

The last rational expression decreases as T/n increases; substitute 3000 and multiply positive denominators. The independent productive-field count is unchanged, while the denominator in the completion ratio increases, so delta strictly decreases. The strict moment statement follows from Theorem 1. QED.

For n=0 the caller is unchanged and there is no strict improvement claim. Additional B-provider calls, idles, queries, scalar boundaries, gauge corrections and all other original jobs remain in the total caller invoice. They are not omitted by this theorem.
