The changed parameter is replica count: fixed T8 instead of PR3465's T16. Source words, nine-stage per-replica events and native interfaces remain unchanged. Chronology is the exact projection of PR3413 onto replicas 0 through 7. The candidate dictionary combines the inherited PR3465 patterns with active patterns from a chronology-free T8 relaxation; the latter is a necessary-cost screen, not an owner construction.
