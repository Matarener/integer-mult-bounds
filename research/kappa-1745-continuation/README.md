## What the wider geometry and controller work decides

### Fixed complex slots cannot be repaired by new orthonormal bases

The twenty idle slots of the retained complex scalar graph have independent
first-order perturbation matrices over F2: their64-by-20 column matrix has
rank 20. An explicit left inverse checks all 400 entries. A second independent
dense-matrix implementation reproduces those columns and the left-inverse
certificate.

Working to second order in `Z[i]/(i−1)^3` strengthens this finite rank fact.
First order fixes the XOR vector of every slot's frame. Second order fixes
its quadratic Fourier phase up to linear translations. Polarization then
fixes its orthogonal projector, and therefore its rank. Thus simultaneous
changes to arbitrary orthonormal frames cannot improve the rank histogram
while keeping this normalized local/scalar graph and these twenty slots.
Ordinary translations and scalar signs do not remove the quadratic condition.

The theorem is scoped to those slots and that graph. New slots, a changed
local circuit, new shear coefficients or a genuine nonlinear Fourier
correction are outside it. Such a correction must have an actual native
implementation and a price. A quadratic phase cannot be declared a free
translation. See [complex/SLOT-PROJECTOR-RIGIDITY.md](complex/SLOT-PROJECTOR-RIGIDITY.md).

A legal changed-slot control was also constructed: move any subset of six
initial residual phases to after the terminal exchange on their original
streams. All 4,864 exact matrix comparisons pass. It has **zero tariff gain**:
the residuals remain on distinct streams and the rank inventory is unchanged.

### Four-pair controller retiming does not supply the target

The integral paired-bridge search now quotients the entire equal-clock GL
group, rather than a bounded list of short bridges. In the model with four
top/bottom pairs, one cross-shear call at each of nine stages, ordinary
monotone pair flags and final signed exchange, the exhaustive F2 orbit
certificate requires at least nine idle segments. A separate rank argument,
valid over any field even for independent top/bottom bridges, shows that one
idle segment cannot span more than six of the nine calls.

There are 88 unordered nine-segment partitions of total advance27 with each
part at most6. Exact packing duals price all of them after discarding
chronological eligibility and physical-axis constraints. This favors a
candidate construction, so failure is a useful obstruction.

For the original BIT local word, every partition has root below 0.001740157.
More relevantly, a fresh dual evaluation of the **final achieved word** still
rejects 0.001745 with normalized moment excess greater than
`0.00001154684213864184749812173160`, even after omitting fallback cost.
For the legitimate complex local word and its actual mixed helper widths,
every partition fails both 0.001745 and the rounded upper test 0.001744.
Each complex receipt checks 322,344 rational slot-pattern inequalities; the
saved duals have a separate standard-library verifier.

These floors apply to integral dual bridges `diag(A,A^(-transpose))` and the
stated clock law. Dyadic bridges with even denominators and independent
top/bottom bridge matrices are outside the orbit proof. The broader rank-span
lemma does not by itself prove the nine-segment floor for those classes.

The most favorable relaxed complex partition, `(1,1,1,2,2,3,5,6,6)`, was also
tested for actual controller existence. The exact directed orbit search finds
no F2 controller for that partition; the existing partition passes as a
positive control. A single five-interface topology was then searched with
genuinely rational bridge parameters and with independent top/bottom maps.
No exact controller was obtained. The latter numerical residual decreased
only while coefficient sizes and condition numbers grew sharply; all exact
rational reconstructions remained nonzero. Those traces are a failed
construction attempt, not a universal impossibility theorem.

See [bit-geometry/CLOCK-MODEL-PROOF.md](bit-geometry/CLOCK-MODEL-PROOF.md) and
[complex-controller/README.md](complex-controller/README.md) for the model,
proof reductions, finite certificates and limits.

### Larger pair counts: an optimistic possibility that fails actual bins

We also changed the pair count instead of further optimizing four-pair
controllers. A cap-only relaxation for five through eight pairs excludes the
unchanged legitimate complex word from the target. For BIT, that first
relaxation is favorable enough to survive, so the actual five-pair B5 recipe
was priced next.

Its slot inventory is `{7:888,14:444,28:444,42:444,56:444}` per replica. All 4,807
width8 helper occurrences fit, but too much residual mass remains in the
rank 56 slots. An exact dual proves that even optimal fractional packing
fails 0.001745. The displayed fractional profile that is optimal at the target,
including the required halving split55+1, has a root below
`0.001703894945366569` for the final BIT word; this displayed root is not a
separately certified universal B5 ceiling. The target excess is greater than
`0.000116548329359407220126130486`.
This closes that actual B5 recipe without spending effort on a native caller
whose most favorable slot price already fails. It does not exclude different
five-pair controller topologies or changed local words and helper widths.

## What remains: the next tasks that can still matter

The evidence supports retaining the improved BIT word and directing the next
construction effort at the complex branch. The following are distinct ways
to change assumptions that survived the checks above.

Before closing this continuation, we transferred the successful BIT birth
idea to the legitimate signed complex word. All 204 exact commutation
candidates, 25 feasible last-write prefix reconstructions and 7 feasible
first-observer reconstructions tie the original rank profile after joint
frame optimization. The seven observer cases involve a subset of the same
25 helpers; the earlier 15 multi-frame cases are also a subset, so these
counts are not added as distinct helpers. The optimization uses 43base.
A fresh signed free-warp replay checks the neutral 19-birth schedule on both
43base and 43extra, with all 632 live input columns and all 91 zero buffers
restored. It needs no new dirty correction and gives no tariff gain.
This is a finite greedy-basis screen, not an exhaustive search over rational
networks. See [complex-birth/README.md](complex-birth/README.md) and its
source-bound [review](complex-birth/REVIEW.json).

1. **Change the complex helper production and observation interface.** The
   useful BIT improvement came from permitting unobserved prefixes and dirty
   responses to change, then rebuilding compensation. The analogous complex
   task must work with signed rational coefficients, wrapped helpers and
   actual native projectors. A complete candidate needs both gauge variants,
   arbitrary dirty restoration, all captures and a regenerated complete
   invoice. A SOURCE-only identity does not establish that dirty interface.
2. **Obtain actual helper-width reductions.** Six ideal width7 BIT helpers,
   each removing one local H1 with no extra adapters, pass the earlier exact
   optimistic mixed-width budget. That calculation uses the preceding
   local word and gives a sufficient target, rather than a newly computed
   minimum for the final improved word. It does not supply six admissible
   helpers. A new screen on the final
   word finds no zero D columns and rejects all 366 relevant first-contact
   intersection classes for a direct common nonroot entrance. Later cuts,
   SOURCE donors and coordinated graph changes remain outside that screen.
3. **Change the flag law, helper splitting or phase chronology together with
   the local word.** The four-pair pricing ceiling applies to the retained
   clock law and whole helpers. Interleaved or split helpers, different
   SOURCE/TARGET endpoints and new synchronized phases require new native
   contracts. A common phase commutes through scalar shears, but moving the
   two active streams' local-call phases into it must include the resulting
   entrance, exit and inverse-transform costs.
4. **Construct a genuinely different controller topology.** Nonintegral
   dual maps or independent primal/dual maps are not ruled out globally.
   The failed numerical topology is now documented so it need not be
   rediscovered. A successor must supply an exact finite scalar identity,
   a legal flag chronology and the actual helper-slot inventory before a
   favorable moment can be treated as feasible.

The size of the complex deficit is useful for deciding whether a candidate
can matter. On the retained valid complex owners, the target requires a
weighted tariff saving of at least `0.2939066968326756` per local call. As a
price equivalent, 87 extra independent `H1+H2→H3` fusions fail and 88 pass.
Those are hypothetical occurrences, not discovered rewrites. Concentrating
all idle rank mass at rank 48 has an optimistic root near 0.001745669; the
actual width and controller duals show why that concentration is unavailable
in the retained integral four-pair model.

The next admissible improvement must therefore supply a changed finite
object with enough paid headroom. Another favorable rank histogram, a small
floating-point matrix residual or an unspecified frame map does not close
the remaining construction obligation.

## Reproduction and source history

Run from the repository root with Python 3.11 or later and assertions enabled.
The principal checkers use the standard library. Optional discovery programs
use NumPy/SciPy to nominate candidates or packing duals; admission uses exact
checks. The portable wrapper writes only in a new output directory and
replays fixed-output tools in an isolated copy:

```sh
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/verify.py --output /absolute/new/kappa1745-replay
```

Its validation receipt distinguishes fresh core checks from authenticated
reuse of the complete native/global/controlled-physical receipts. To repeat
those expensive executions as well, add `--full-native`. Discovery searches
are not silently rerun by the core wrapper; their exact commands and coverage
are in the individual scope READMEs.

The optional controller review is available with
`--complex-controller-review` and requires installed NumPy/SciPy imports.
The separate signed complex birth review and its dependencies are documented
in that scope's README; the default portable core does not rerun its frame
optimization searches.

The final word can also be reconstructed and independently integrated:

```sh
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-word/early_birth_prefixes.py --out /absolute/new/local-word
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/integrate_bit_checkpoint.py --word research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-word/early-birth-prefixes/WORD.json.gz --records research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-word/early-birth-prefixes/records/249-records.bin --out /absolute/new/full-bit-invoice
```
