## Why the nonroot entrance candidates fail

The predecessor prefix report propagated an upper space for the proposed
helper but did not check every prescribed SOURCE line against the same
graph. This package backpropagates endpoint and capture caps through the
entire changed A/B graph over Q, then checks every initial SOURCE space.

The exact scope is both orientations of `(444,473)` and `(468,477)`, at cuts
immediately before and after every affected A contact, plus both endpoints.
The current composed word has 66 such graphs. All 29 graphs with a nonzero
proposed helper upper space violate a SOURCE entrance. For example, the
`p=444,q=473,cut=234` graph requires SOURCE57's start vector
`(0,0,0,0,1,-1,-1,1)` to be annihilated by `(0,0,0,0,1,0,0,0)`, whose pairing
is one. This is an exact subspace contradiction, independent of any finite
catalogue of root frames.

The artifact lists a violating vector and annihilator for every SOURCE
contradiction. It does not exclude other basis changes, changes to the
SOURCE interface, dynamic role exchange, coordinated early rerouting, or
interleaving A, captures and B. In particular, this fixed-scalar-graph
obstruction does not automatically transfer to the new SOURCE-resynthesized
word and its changed D matrix.

## The expanded controller search and its check

Each equal-clock group is represented by its paired top and bottom row
spaces. This quotients the entire GL group at that clock; selecting a
physical pair requires only `(2^k-1)2^(k-1)` choices. A rank potential then
forces every call after the first to lower one diagonal-block rank. The K4
search reaches 12,264 final states, takes about 219 seconds, and establishes
the nine-segment minimum. It uses no idle-jump bound.

`verify_clock_reductions.py` independently enumerates all 20,335 GL bases
in dimensions one through four and checks that their physical pair orbits
are exactly the search's splits. It also extracts the frozen B4 recipe,
replays its integer identity, derives its nine idle advances, and rejects
19 omitted-event / wrong-dual-sign controls. This validates the quotient
mechanism and source binding; it is not a second full K4 enumeration.

The source profile and width inventory remain unchanged in the economic
screen. Every eligible controller has at least nine segments with total
advance 27 and maximum six. The 88 unordered nine-part partitions are
priced after discarding chronological owner eligibility and physical-axis
constraints. That makes the bound favorable to candidate constructions.

For BIT, one scalar rational packing dual per partition suffices. For
complex, the solver proposes six helper dual prices, which are rounded to
rationals and repaired by evaluating **all 3,663 integer slot patterns**
against exact rational tariffs. Each complete receipt therefore checks
322,344 exact inequalities. No numerical solver's success or optimality flag
is used as mathematical lower-bound evidence.

The best relaxed complex partition is `(1,1,1,2,2,3,5,6,6)`. The separate
controller track has excluded that target profile in its full GF2 clock-orbit
traceback. Its numerical dyadic and independent-bridge target topologies
degenerated and supplied no actual controller. Those numerical failures are
not a universal rational-controller exclusion. The economic optimum in this
package therefore remains a favorable relaxation, with no native witness.

`verify_packing_duals.py` independently generates the integer slot patterns
with a Cartesian product and checks the two saved complex receipts using
only the standard library. All **644688 exact dual inequalities** pass.
Inflating a tight slot price by one is rejected in both receipts.

## Larger pair counts

The generalized coverage law for K pairs is
`L=2K+1`, `m=1+sL`, and `W=2Kv`, where BIT uses `(s,v)=(7,111)` and the
legitimate complex word uses `(8,112)`. With total helper width `w` per call,
the remaining idle rank mass is
`2vs(K-1)L-Lw`. Subtracting it and the complete local rank mass from `mW`
gives deficit `96K-63` for BIT and `96K-64` for complex.

`larger_pair_budget.py` grants every idle rank unit the cheapest legally
allowed rank `isqrt((m*m-1)//2)`, with arbitrary fractional multiplicities.
This drops all slot, clock, owner and fallback restrictions. Exact rational
18-decimal root brackets give the following strict upper endpoints:

|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | `0.001920639184053587` | `0.001725428467972880` |
| 6 | `0.001868396142548621` | `0.001686480587619301` |

| 8 | `0.001754795417086374` | `0.001590836558407824` |

All four complex cases fail the target even under this broad relaxation.
The favorable BIT signs are not constructions; they motivate the next
actual-bin test.

For BIT K5, the script extracts and replays the frozen B5 recipe as a literal
10 by 10 integer identity, then derives its exact idle slots
`{7:888,14:444,28:444,42:444,56:444}`. Their whole-width8 capacity is 7104,
above the demand of 4807. An exact rational dual checks every permissible

all rank14,28,42 slots, fill `811/7` of the rank56 slots completely, and leave
`2297/7` rank56 slots empty. The exact paid-child guard splits each empty
rank56 remainder into ranks55 and1, producing
`{7:888,6:444,4:444,2:444,55:2297/7,1:2297/7}`.

With the final local word, this profile has root below
`0.001703894945366569`; its target normalized excess exceeds
`0.000116548329359407220126130486`. Integer owner counts and chronology can
only make this favorable relaxation harder. No B5 host template or physical
construction is claimed. The cap-only BIT K6 through K8 signs remain merely
surviving relaxations; no new controller search on them was run.

The preceding adaptive architecture certificate already excluded the
legitimate complex word's K3 and all K>=5 in the narrower fixed paired-chain
native-bin family. The cap-only K5 through K8 test here uses fewer hypotheses
and is separately recomputed; neither claim is silently extended to changed

## Reproduce

Run from the repository root with assertions enabled. The following core
replays need only Python's standard library. They may be run in an isolated
copy of the source bundle to preserve the archived result files.

```sh
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/geometry_budget.py
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/controller_clock_orbits.py --pairs 4 --idle-budget 6 --max-states 2000000 --output research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/controller-orbits/K4-replay.json
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/verify_clock_reductions.py
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/verify_packing_duals.py
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/prefix_rational_feasibility.py --out research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/prefix-rational-replay
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/review_bit_checkpoint.py
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/review_retimed444.py
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/review_final_bit_word.py
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/reprice_achieved_clock.py
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/final_entrance_closure.py
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/larger_pair_budget.py
```

The prefix output directory must be fresh. Script and immutable input
hashes are embedded in the receipts. For the K4 replay, ignore only the
top-level `seconds` and `logs[*].seconds` runtime fields when comparing JSON;
retain all states, counters, witnesses and source pins. All other core
receipts are deterministic. The saved K4 receipt remains the source-bound
input to its independent quotient check; a separate replay filename avoids
changing that input's hash.

Regenerating the complex proposal prices is optional and uses NumPy / SciPy:

```sh
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/complex_clock_packing.py
python3 -B research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/complex_clock_packing.py --parameter .001744 --output research/kappa-1745-continuation/bit-geometry/COMPLEX-CLOCK-CEILING.json
```

The certificate verifier above is sufficient to validate those saved duals
without either library. `REPLAY-INPUTS.json` supplies the source inventory and
SHA256 pins for packaging the core commands and their read-only dependencies.

## Open next questions

1. **Construct several admissible width7 helpers or a larger word saving.**
   The six-unit budget is sufficient only if the initial line and full dirty
   cleanup genuinely remove the stated cost. Coordinated early SOURCE
   rerouting is outside both the unchanged entrance proof and the two-pair
   prefix obstruction; the word track owns that construction attempt.
2. **Construct an attainable complex controller and combine it with a new
   word.** Any favorable partition needs a clock-orbit traceback with its
   full advance histogram, an exact lift, and genuine projector matching.
   Fractional owner counts alone do not supply a native template.
3. **Change the bridge or flag model.** Independent top / bottom networks,
   rational bridges without an invertible reduction mod2, or a different
   coverage law escape this finite ceiling. They need their own complete
   scalar identity, native frame semantics and invoice. A mere dimension
   match cannot replace equality of address projectors.
