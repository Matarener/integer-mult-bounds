## Fixed-slot rigidity includes genuinely new orthonormal bases

We expanded the numerical search first: for one standard axis in each later
8D coordinate block, enumerate every six-of-twenty placement, including slots
that did not originally contain that axis, and permit final translation signs.
All **310,080 placements** were evaluated using exact bounded integer matrices.
For every block, only the original placement survives even the first tested
mode. The second mode provides a positive consistency control. This bounded
census alone would not exclude simultaneous nonstandard-basis changes.

`slot_projector_rigidity.py` supplies that stronger, explicitly scoped result.
It derives the twenty idle-slot perturbation matrices from the literal
eight-stream scalar graph. They have rank 20 over F2, certified by an explicit
left inverse with all 400 entries checked. In the ring
`Z[i]/(i-1)^3`, the first-order equations force every slot's XOR vector to stay
fixed. The second-order equations force every slot's quadratic phase form to
stay fixed up to translations. Its polarization determines the orthogonal
projector of the frame, which in turn determines the frame's rank.

Thus **simultaneous replacements by arbitrary orthonormal frames cannot alter
any slot rank** when the normalized local/scalar graph and these twenty slots
are retained. Arbitrary inserted translations and scalar sign controls cannot
cancel the quadratic coefficients. With the hosted widths fixed, the residual
rank profile cannot improve.

The proof carefully separates this conclusion from the weaker first-order
test. The frames `{e1,e2,e3}` and `{e1+e2+e3}` have the same XOR and different
ranks. They are rejected at second order: their projectors and quadratic
Fourier phases differ. The proof and exact matrices are in
`SLOT-PROJECTOR-RIGIDITY.md` and `SLOT-PROJECTOR-RIGIDITY.json`.

## A legal new-slot construction, and why it does not improve the bill

first scalar observation. Pull their product F to the input. Since the original
complete operator O is one convolution times the identity on all eight streams,

to new slots after the terminal exchange on their original streams.

## What remains open after these tests

A useful positive candidate must change an assumption that survives these
checks: the normalized local/scalar graph, the productive/source/target
interface, helper widths, or the actual recursive work attached to new slots.
A nonlinear Fourier correction is also outside the theorem, but its native

A common phase on all eight streams commutes through every scalar shear.

phase packet: the two active streams' local-call phases must be transferred
with their entry/exit contracts and all replacement children included. The
present fixed-word/fixed-slot methods do not provide that construction.

The target remains open outside the precise scopes above. This package is not
