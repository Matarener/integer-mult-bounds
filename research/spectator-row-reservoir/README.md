# Complete spectator rows remove the active-axis stock restriction

**Constructive conditional interface theorem, with an exact reference. No new κ.**

A global Hadamard supplier can take its row prefix and compact controls from complete spectator coordinates. It can complete every selected axis in one outer invocation, with one padding by less than two, and restore the original coarse layout before the next diagonal. The prefix may be much larger than the active width.

Using the retained SUPPORT-ROUTER sparse extension, the construction maps the h complete K-bit active neighborhoods to their destination slots, then completes that map on their union. Only **2hK coordinate positions** can move, regardless of the row-prefix length or gather distance. The retained paid support router therefore preserves the supplier's exponent. This is a concrete replacement for active-axis reservation and deferred completion, with the full layout and cost proof in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md).

For total simultaneous stock

$$\log_2Q=O(h^\nu p^\zeta\operatorname{polylog}p),$$

at width h=Θ(p^x), the new fit test is **νx+ζ<1**, using the ambient Θ(p)-bit address. It requires no stock cutoff h≥p^(ζ/(1−ν)) and no individual-axis fallback caused by row reservation. Existing coordinates supply the rows; there is no 2^R volume multiplier.

## Significance for researchers

This opens a larger supplier/representation contract than allocating rows inside the active axes. For hypothetical completed U=B=.02 suppliers with guard exponent ρ=3/2 and stock h^(1/2)p^(3/5) polylog(p), active-axis allocation would require β>6/5. The spectator construction instead gives a passing **κ=.011 interface**, with global stock power 217/240<1 and grouped stock power 157/240<1. The stronger finite suppliers remain missing; the example is a sufficient target for their implementation, not a new .011 multiplier.

More generally, put M=min(U,B,1/32), and

$$E=\min(1,1/\rho,(1-\zeta)/\nu)$$

for ν>0; omit the last term for ν=0 and require ζ<1. The changed wrapper's 60 strict exponent predicates admit rational witnesses exactly when `E>1/2` and `OLD<κ<ME`. All nine source outer savings remain unchanged. [Nick Petalas](https://github.com/petalas)'s [PR5984](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5984) supplies the unchanged-ledger result; the new contribution is the constructive ambient stock interface.
