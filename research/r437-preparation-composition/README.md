# Compose two preparation improvements on actual pooled R437 sources

This package joins ToikY's #4025 shared radix traversal with #4011 chronological
Callback admission and independently compiles #4010's actual pooled source
contexts. It measures a complete named interval: Context construction, full
next-directory production, both first-prefix read/validate and read/child-program
phases, and disposal of every owned result and Context.

The new composition applies radix sharing to Context.high_count as well as the
directory producer. Both variants exhaust validation and retain every ordered
Count multiplication. The interval saves **8190 validated radix traversals**
worth **4,283,370 reads, 778,050 writes and 4,553,640 moves** of source residue
Words in each dimension64/cap1 case. No tape or numerical word is added.
The chronological adapter separately saves two complete admissions; the four
original/candidate combinations prove exact additive savings without cross terms.

| Actual-source fixture | Original total source actions | Composed total | Reduction |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| h=0, one old physical prefix | 11,709,798 | 2,023,876 | 82.716% |
| h=2, two duplicate old physical prefixes | 21,090,379 | 10,356,737 | 50.894% |

Totals sum separately recorded record-Tape, residue-Word and index-Word reads,
writes and unit moves. They are source-operation counts, not wall-clock or a
physical bit-machine tariff. RESULT.json preserves all lanes and phase invoices.
At h=2 the record lane alone drops 7,331,381 to 6,247,715 actions, and numerical
peak remains 4097 additional residue Words and 16 index Words (including Context
and frame controls). Created tapes and backing slots agree in all four modes.

The current source is **native IDLE896352, rank14, Q=6512**; the old history also
uses **common COPY1603, rank7**. Actual Specifications compilation checks the low
identity contexts over q=3. The IDLE is fallback, and its actual plan starts with
a UNIT child. Legal generic histories reach current width2048 and produce a
width32 child. Active inputs enter at canonical head1; unused spectators also test head2.
Every borrowed byte/head, spectator Word, input Count and numeric
ownership set is restored after complete disposal. The first prefix is cleared
and reread between validation and extension, matching the real caller boundaries.

These are complete dimension64 high cubes at cap1 (modulus3). They do not execute
a nontrivial 3^4096 cube. Source compilation is real; top-level selected-history
reachability and a full physical CALL/RETURN are not established. Geometry,
child_extents, transport/gather/scatter, handoff copies, coefficient evaluations,
control/allocator tariffs, original-width stock and remaining Pi terms stay
explicit. There is no new kappa or selected-frontier change.

## Evidence and reproduction

Run `python3 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/r437-preparation-composition`.
The package is self-contained: its authenticated 154-file materialized pooled export preserves
all frozen notices and complete loader inventory; exact #4025 sources and the
#4011 adapter are retained with original hashes. PREDECESSORS.json and
POOLED-SOURCES.json bind bytes and upstream heads. Shared/frozen files are never
edited. The checker independently compiles both source specifications, then runs
four modes for each fixture. An independent serializer checks every returned child byte and its width32 extent. The independent invoice checker imports no runtime.
Eleven controls include two actual same-output losing compositions: leaving
Context unoptimized and silently skipping validation. Checks remain active under
optimized Python. This is AI-assisted reviewable source evidence, not independent
human acceptance or formal kernel verification.
