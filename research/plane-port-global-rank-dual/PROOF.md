# Proof of the global bound

## Object

The 112 selected ports carry binary labels `q_p` in `F_2^9`. The retained PR1825 matching
(`research/paired-plane-port-matching-seed6c563a/CERTIFICATE.json`) pairs them into 56 planes
`E_i = span(q_a, q_b)`. Every plane is nondegenerate: its 2 x 2 Gram matrix over `F_2` has rank 2.
Two planes are *orthogonal* when every vector of one is orthogonal to every vector of the other;
109 of the 1,540 plane pairs are orthogonal.

A **decoder** over a field `F` is a finite family of rank-one terms `u_k v_k^T` with
`u_k, v_k in F^112` and `M = sum_k u_k v_k^T`, satisfying the strict scalar mask of PR1889:

- `M_pp = 1` for every port;
- `M_pq = 0` when `p != q` and the planes of `p` and `q` are equal or non-orthogonal;
- `M_pq` is free when the planes of `p` and `q` are orthogonal.

`F` is arbitrary: `Q` with any denominators (in particular even ones), `R`, `C`, or a finite field.

**Tariff** (the contract of #4365, unchanged). Let `A_k` and `R_k` be the sets of planes that contain a
port where `u_k`, respectively `v_k`, is nonzero, and `W(X) = span(E_i : i in X)`. The term costs

    t_k = rank over F_2 of the dot-product pairing between W(A_k) and W(R_k)
        = dim W(A_k) - dim(W(A_k) meet W(R_k)^perp).

It depends only on the plane supports. Charges add: the decoder costs `sum_k t_k`.

## Theorem

For every field `F` and every decoder,

    sum_k t_k  >=  18233853 / 131072  =  139.1132...,

hence `sum_k t_k >= 140`, because the cost is an integer.

## Proof

A **clique** is a set `Q` of pairwise non-orthogonal planes. Write `P(Q)` for its `2|Q|` ports.

**Lemma 1 (identity block).** For every clique `Q`, at least `2|Q|` terms touch `Q` on both sides,
that is, have `A_k` meeting `Q` and `R_k` meeting `Q`.

*Proof.* Every pair of distinct ports in `P(Q)` lies in one plane or in two non-orthogonal planes, so
the mask fixes `M` restricted to `P(Q)` to the identity `I_{2|Q|}`. A term that misses `P(Q)` on
one side restricts to zero there. Hence `I_{2|Q|}` is a sum of the restrictions of the touching
terms, each of rank at most one, and their number is at least `2|Q|`. This holds over every field.
Lean: `V4.identity_block_needs_terms` in [lean/V4Duality.lean](lean/V4Duality.lean). Its hypothesis
is the identity block on a port set `S`; its conclusion counts the terms nonzero on `S` on both
sides.

**Lemma 2 (weak duality).** Let `w_Q >= 0` be weights on cliques such that every term satisfies

    sum over cliques Q touched by term k on both sides of w_Q  <=  t_k.            (F)

Then `sum_Q w_Q * 2|Q| <= sum_k t_k`.

*Proof.* Swap the order of summation and use Lemma 1. Lean: `V4.weak_duality`.

**Lemma 3 (finite check).** Condition (F) holds for every possible term if, for every ordered pair
`(A, R)` of subspaces generated by nonempty plane sets,

    sum over cliques Q with  in(A) meets Q  and  in(R) meets Q  of w_Q  <=  t(A, R),       (F')

where `in(A)` is the set of planes contained in `A`.

*Proof.* For a term with nonempty supports put `A = W(A_k)`, `R = W(R_k)`. Then `A_k` is contained in
`in(A)`, so every clique touched by the term is counted in (F'), and `t_k = t(A, R)`. A term with an
empty side costs 0 and touches no clique.

**The certificate.** [CERTIFICATE.json](CERTIFICATE.json) lists 443 cliques, of sizes up to 24,
with integer weights `w_Q / 2^20`. [verify.py](verify.py) recomputes the planes from the pinned
archive and matching, enumerates all 39,087 subspaces generated by plane sets, computes their
tariffs, checks that every column is a clique and checks (F') on all `39,087^2 = 1,527,793,569`
ordered pairs in exact integer arithmetic. The minimum slack is 0, attained on 1,732 pairs. The
bound is `sum_Q w_Q 2|Q| / 2^20 = 18233853/131072`.

Lemmas 1-3 and the certificate give the theorem.

## Where the certificate came from

The weights are a rounded-down optimal dual of a clique linear programme solved by column
generation (separation over all ordered span pairs, on Kaggle). Only the rounded certificate
matters for the proof. We do not know the optimum of the full clique programme. A floating-point
pricing estimate from the same run suggests it is at most about 167; that estimate is not
certified here.

## What the proof does not use

- No coefficient restriction: no integrality, no parity, no bound on denominators or term counts.
- No symmetry `u_k = v_k` and no positivity.
- Free mask entries are never used; only identity blocks on cliques.

It also leaves out everything outside the #4365 contract: native realization, hosting, routing and
any non-additive or concave pricing.
