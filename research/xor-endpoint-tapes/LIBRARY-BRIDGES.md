# Linked-library and predecessor bridges

The shared source catalogue and authoritative DAG were queried with bounded
packets. Retrieval is not a fresh kernel check or an exhaustive novelty
search. Exact inspected source hashes and declaration names are stored in
LIBRARY-BRIDGES.json; no absolute local user path is needed by the package.

| Inspected source | Reused concept and exact limit |
|---|---|
| `Mathproof.External.IntegerMultBounds20261008.Matrix.CommutingPhases` | `translate`, `xor_translation_commutes` and `raw_phase_translation_commutes` supply the exact XOR operator/commutation precedent. The new proof specializes to bounded h-bit records and implements the resulting permutation on tapes. |
| Same module, `gauge_phase_noncommuting_witness` | Prevents assuming a deferred translation passes every native gauge. The new correction is applied at an explicit endpoint and does not use free global relabeling. |

The bounded source packet used 4 queries with at most 12 hits per query.
The directly relevant translation hit was inspected in the canonical source.
Queries with no hits do not exclude related declarations under other names.
Historical/source-catalog DAG entries remain labelled as such; no new typed
port, compatible combined import environment or kernel/transitive-axiom
certificate is claimed for the informal machine proof.

The concrete sufficient result consists of the finite transition construction,
exact travel formulas, dirty-scratch restoration, norm-preserving endpoint
composition and source-bound bills. XOR permutation, three-swap restoration
and sequential tape compilation themselves are established techniques.
