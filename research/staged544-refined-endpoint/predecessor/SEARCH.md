# Bounded new-word searches and scope changes

These exploratory searches ran on the exact current PR #3734 words before
settling on the finite analytic certificate. They are not the research result,
exhaustive architectural exclusions, or κ gains. Private artifact hashes in
SEARCH-HASHES.json preserve the local checkpoints without pretending they
are independently replayable public certificates.

* Exact commuting B/restore retiming used our prior MathProof implementation
  as a template. The accelerated current-word scan evaluated 4,982 moves,
  with minimal moved frames, retained other frames and convergence back to
  the unchanged suffix; no favorable paid-profile delta was found.
* An adjacent compensated-commutator screen evaluated 101 eligible three-register
  rewrites, each with its explicitly paid extra ADD; none improved the bill.
* A sparse rational prefix-kernel search used fixed-prefix identities and
  actual scalar/warp constraints over the 408 live helper columns. It found
  666 two-donor relations plus selected sparse basis solutions at the stated
  first-producer cuts. Those are sufficient algebraic candidates, not valid
  physical words by themselves. Other cut choices, more general non-scalar
  eigenvectors and changed native interfaces are outside the search.
* One changed helper 472 word passed all 723-column forward/dual/reverse and
  cleanup-boundary checks for the base provider. It changed the local bill by
  `15H1 − 9H2 − 2H3 + 2H4` and the inventory by one width-8 helper becoming
  width 7. Its new local profile was `{1:2455, 2:716, 3:222, 4:104, 5:20, 8:8}`.
  A fresh exact dual over all 2,551 patterns, using that actual new inventory,
  is feasible and strictly rejects the current target even before chronology.
  Thus this one candidate cannot beat the current endpoint in that model;
  its extra provider and full native admission were not pursued.
* Coordinate and adjacent-node-pair frame changes, with endpoints and native
  warp contracts fixed, produced no lower bill on that candidate. A subsequent
  4,973-move retiming screen and 102 compensated-commutator cases also found no
  favorable delta. These do not exhaust simultaneous graph/contract changes.

The discriminating decision was to stop investing in a new allocation or
native admission of that losing candidate. The submitted positive result is
instead the actual current-profile real moment certificate and its tiny strict
conditional endpoint refinement. No failed search is relabelled as a universal
obstruction or a new mechanism.
