## Rank-distance lemma

For a Clifford with symplectic matrix `[[A,B],[C,D]]`, let r be rank(B).
For products, `B(GH)=A_G B_H+B_G D_H`, so `r(GH)<=r(G)+r(H)`.
The inverse has the same B rank. From `W=L U^-1 R`,

    L=W R^-1 U,  R=U L^-1 W,

and therefore `r(U)+r(W)>=|r(L)-r(R)|`.

Exact Gaussian Bruhat decompositions

    L=LL F_a LR,   R=RL F_b RR

give the attaining choice `U=RL LR`. It is a physical monomial, and

    L U^-1 R=LL F_a F_b RR.

Canonical native C chirps overlap on the first min(a,b) axes; each
overlap squares *exactly* to Pauli X. The remaining arity is |a-b|.
The Gaussian phases belong in the literal monomial adapters and the
recorded whole-field unit, as checked for the two actual witnesses.
This is an all-parameter Clifford lemma at a fixed isolated two-site
interface. Attainment uses the stated phase-complete Gaussian native
Bruhat factorization; it does not extend the admitted scalar/phase
alphabet beyond that model. It does not make the actual q2/q3 transport
an all-q caller.
