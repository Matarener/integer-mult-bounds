prefix mass66. Its complete six-by-six arbitrary-current-input matrix has

certifies row support; it is not advertised as a complete new cleanup.

For a replacement whose total FULL-BANK native macroarity is R_f and binary
ADD/accumulation count is S_f, maximum row support gives R_f+S_f>=6f(q2)
or8f(q3). These exact inequalities permit finite-f savings paid by ADDs;
with S_f bounded independently of f they force normalized native mass at
least6/8, reaching stock after the fixed prefix. Arbitrary extra dirty banks
restored and arbitrary monomial/scalar coupling topology are allowed by this
support proof. No clean or correlated input promise is used.

All arrays here have the same full bank volume. A runtime corollary needs
each ADD scan to cost a fixed positive multiple of that volume. Partial,
striped or fused-volume query models are not automatically priced by the
unweighted histogram and need a separate weighted proof. Growing O(f) ADD
counts are a conditional escape from the bounded-scan conclusion, not a
claimed supplier or a globally impossible machine algorithm.
