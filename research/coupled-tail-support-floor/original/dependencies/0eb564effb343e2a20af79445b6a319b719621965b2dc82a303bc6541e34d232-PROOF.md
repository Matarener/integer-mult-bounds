## 2. Minimum net saving and useful integral targets

    P_b(H) = sum_r r H_r [(m/r)^b - 1].

Multiplying (1) by m gives the exact equivalent condition

    D(H) := mn-mass(H) > P_b(H).                            (3)

If S is the net native rank saving from the admitted mass mn+4, (3) is

    S > 4 + P_b(H).                                        (4)

P_b(H)>0 for a nonempty proper-child profile, S must exceed four. Thus at
least five integer arity units are necessary if arbitrary integer ranks are

## 4. The coupled final-a cut cannot pay for a deficit with full scans

The newly supplied exact `../coupled-tail-escape/PROOF.md` is preserved as
`inputs/coupled-tail-PROOF.md`, authenticated at SHA256
`5b4300fb15364dbdb17974d6d0bb7d387a189ff814fd0293057ea2a47ac58587`;
the accompanying script and exact witness are authenticated in `SCREEN.json`.
This source fixes the q2 prefix through event68 at mass66, with H2=33.
The replacement suffix must act on all six arbitrary incoming banks, with
any extra arbitrary dirty banks restored. Its outputbank1/inputbank3 block
is a single Clifford C=WR of B-rank6f, with exact Gaussian selected-native
adapters. Therefore every row of that coefficient has 2^(6f) nonzeros.

Let R_f be the sum of actual native arities of any replacement, and S_f the
number of scalar binary ADD/COPY accumulations. Initial coefficient rows on
all original and dirty inputs have support one. Physical monomials, scalars
and permutations do not increase the maximum support; a native rank-r child
multiplies it by at most 2^r and a binary ADD by at most two. Cancellation
cannot increase it. The required coefficient proves the pointwise inequality

    R_f + S_f >= 6f.                                       (7)

The local source expressly prices each counted ADD as a literal scan of the
same whole bank with V_f=2^(12f) complex payload records. More generally it
suffices that each scan touches at least c V_f records for fixed c>0 and
requires at least one elementary step per touched record. These are explicit
implementation premises. They give

    T_scan(f) >= c V_f S_f.                                (8)

If R_f <= (6-delta)f for fixed delta>0, (7)-(8) give

    T_scan(f) >= c delta V_f f.

For every fixed lambda<1 this is not O(V_f f^lambda), since after cancelling
V_f the ratio is at least c delta f^(1-lambda), which diverges. In particular
it cannot meet O(2^(12f) f^(1-b)) for any b>0. The six-unit saving target
would require local mass at most4, hence S_f>=2f and scan work at least
2 V_f f in the source's unit full-scan model.

This argument assumes no payload value, field cardinality or precision
bound. It only uses a lower bound of one elementary step per visited record.
Greater precision cannot make that lower bound cheaper. If a claimed upper
budget is instead O(V_f f^lambda P(f)), the conclusion requires
P(f)=o(f^(1-lambda)); an unrestricted multiplier may not be dropped. For a
fixed field/precision, or uniformly subpower P(f), the exclusion follows.
No substitution of p, overflow/range estimate or practical cutoff is supplied.

The exact retained sources phrase the root layer target as O(V d^lambda')
with lambda'<1, after their explicit log(p), compact bit, precision and outer
inequalities. They use f=floor(e/12). At a root active width e comparable to
d, f is comparable to d, and the lower bound is Omega(Vd). Before absorption,
the displayed layer estimate includes `(log p)^3`; the same comparison is
valid whenever the actual complete upper budget is o(Vd), for example when
that multiplier is subpower in d and the remaining exponent has a fixed gap
below one. This note does not independently prove the source/precision
relation for the changed caller. The unconditional statement within (7)-(8)
is the scan lower bound; its contradiction is against the specified sublinear
normalized step budget, not against a budget with arbitrary hidden factors.

There is also a useful native/scan dichotomy. Any asserted sublinear full-scan
budget forces S_f=o(f), so (7) gives R_f>=6f-o(f). Concavity/subadditivity of
x^(1-b) then makes the limiting best possible equal-volume native moment at
least

    33 (1/6)^(1-b) + (1/2)^(1-b) > 33/6+1/2 = 6.           (9)

Thus even a single concentrated cleanup child at the support floor cannot
make the component moment favorable. The exact positive gaps at b=.002,
.003,.005 are enclosed in `SCREEN.json`. This rules out a favorable completion
of this fixed current-input cut in the explicit full-bank scan model; it does
not prove local optimality at mass6 or10, nor a global caller obstruction.
