## 2. Strict improvement for the entire concave range

Define the target-minus-paid moment at exponent 0 < t < 1 by

    F_t(T,P) = Σ_k T_k k^t − Σ_r P_r r^t.

Then the construction has the exact gain

    F_t(T_new,P_new) − F_t(T_old,P_old)
      = 72(1 + 3^t − 4^t) > 0.                       (1)

**Proof.** Each local replacement deletes four H₁ and four H₃ children and debits four H₄ original helper jobs. Eighteen distinct local copies give 72 of each term. Strict subadditivity gives 4^t = (1+3)^t < 1 + 3^t. No erased helper receives productive credit, and no temporary is declared an independent target. ∎

The rank mass of the changed terms balances exactly: 1+3=4. Thus (1) is a genuine concavity gain with no invented rank surplus. It does not come from extra digits of a previous root, verification speed or different hardware.

The old proper partial-helper source contacts are already all-column physical identities. The new program omits an unobserved owned return tail after each last protected rank-4 read; it introduces no new nonzero native map, rational address projector or Gaussian child. Initialization, erasure, header changes, local ordinary cost and owned-bank reservation remain positive costs.

## 3. What the proof does not conclude

A larger mixed moment at each fixed t does not identify a new ordinary BIT exponent without the corresponding finite ordinary admission proof. To conclude an ordinary saving B > 0.0035 one must prove the new complete moment at a suitable t = 1−C with C > B, retain positive completion/splitting and mirror reserves, and supply the changed owned stock, native fees, delayed cover, backend and precision thresholds. The old atom 0.004 and old cutoff certificate are not substituted for those facts.

The Gaussian side needs its own compatible profitable complete supplier above 0.0035. The old selected ordinary saving 0.0034 and selected Gaussian construction do not automatically meet that target. The query-fusion theorem, endpoint reconstruction and global retirement compiler in the companion notes are positive sufficient algorithms on their stated domains; their supplied source instantiations do not close that Gaussian supplier.

No complete κ ≥ 0.0035 certificate is assembled in this release. In particular, no value from a hypothetical removed Gaussian H4 is added to the profiles above: that proposed deletion had a live inserted cache reader and was withdrawn before publication.
