# Discovery scope

The search treats each ADD/COPY as a common-frame vertex. Consecutive
touches on a public field impose increasing-subspace edges; owned fields
impose decreasing-subspace edges. Public endpoint frames are fixed.
SCALE commutes with transport and keeps its scalar chronology. The objective
is the sum of `abs(dim(F)-dim(G))^(1-.00304117)` over actual transport edges.

Single-vertex relabels and whole equal-frame-component relabels did not
improve this source. A coupled expansion to one proposed frame allows an
arbitrary subset of vertices to move together. For each edge this is a
binary submodular cost: finite costs satisfy the triangle inequality for
the rank-distance power; illegal subspace orders are forbidden. A minimum
cut proposes the subset. The first sweep found the two changes retained
here; a second sweep over this fixed candidate list found no further gain.

The candidate list contained the 1,027 source frames and 96 additional
join/intersection extrema of equal-frame components. Both successful moves
use original frames17 and207. No new general optimization algorithm,
global optimum or barrier to other frame/scalar/caller changes is claimed.
Floating arithmetic selected candidates only. The submitted witness is
reconstructed directly from six exact source sites, then checked with exact
scalar/native/phase arithmetic and three rational moment engines.

The reusable result here is the explicit changed itinerary and complete
conditional price, not an assertion that this bounded search exhausts P104.
