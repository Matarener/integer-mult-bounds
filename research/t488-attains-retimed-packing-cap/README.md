## What this does not establish

- **No new κ.** The selected record is unchanged; this package reports an exact
  identity and a scoped saturation consequence.
- **The optimality is inherited.** The 2,551-pattern dual, its 2,539 strictly
  positive reduced costs, 12 symbolic zeros and coefficient hash are PR #3642's.
  I re-executed its verifier on the pinned bytes and reproduced its receipt; I did
  not re-derive the dual basis or the pattern enumeration.
- **No construction replay.** No word, operator, owner journal, native provider,
  admission stage or fresh-bootstrap wrapper is replayed. Nothing here admits a
  supplier or establishes chronology or physical-family attainment, which the
  certificate's own scope excludes.
- **One grid step.** The published headroom rational and the difference of the two
  published decimal floors differ by exactly `10^-24`; the package checks the
  agreement to that grid step and reports both values rather than choosing one.
