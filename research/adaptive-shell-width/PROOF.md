# Fixed-ratio semigroup shells and adaptive chronological width

## 1. New shell lemma

Fix a finite set A of positive integers and an integer M >= 2. Let Gamma be
the multiplicative semigroup generated by A union {M}, including 1. Let Pi
be its finite prime support, V the rational span of the prime-valuation
vectors of the generators, and r=dim V. Since M is a generator, r>=1.
For every integer ell>=0 there is an effective constant K, depending only
on A and M, such that

    |Gamma intersect [M^ell, M^(ell+1))| <= K (ell+1)^(r-1).       (1)

This also holds for all positive integers supported on Pi whose valuation
vectors belong to V, a possibly larger set than Gamma. No freeness or unique
factorization into the given generators is assumed; ordinary prime
factorization is used.

Here is an explicit constant. Write m_i=v_(p_i)(M), choose p_0 dividing M,
and put q=m_0>0 and h=ceil(log_2 M). For each i != 0 define the integer form

    F_i(x) = q*x_i - m_i*x_0.

The common kernel of all these forms in Q^Pi is exactly Q*m, where m is the
valuation vector of M. Since m belongs to V, their restriction to V has
rank r-1. Choose a subset I of r-1 forms independent on V. The restricted
map F_I has kernel exactly Q*m: its kernel has dimension one and contains m.
The empty set I is allowed when r=1. Set

    K = q * product_(i in I) (h*(q+m_i)+1).                    (2)

For a product u in the shell, its nonnegative integer valuation coordinates
obey x_i <= h*(ell+1). Thus the integer F_i(x) lies in the inclusive interval
[-m_i*h*(ell+1), q*h*(ell+1)]. There are at most
(h*(q+m_i)+1)*(ell+1) possibilities for this coordinate. The number of
possible F_I values is at most the product of these r-1 bounds.

Fix one such value. For any two vectors x,y in its fiber, x-y=t*m for a
rational t. Their associated positive integers u,v satisfy u/v=M^t, since
prime factorization gives this equality over the positive reals. Both lie
in the half-open interval [M^ell,M^(ell+1)), so -1<t<1. More precisely the
whole fiber's x_0 coordinates lie in one half-open real interval of length
q: log_M(u) is an affine function of x_0 with slope 1/q. Consequently this
fiber contains at most q distinct integer x_0 values. Each such value
determines x uniquely because the remaining difference t*m has t*q=0.
Multiplying by the number of F_I values proves (1)-(2).

The q factor cannot generally be dropped: with A={2}, M=4, r=1, both 1
and 2 are in the first shell, and every shell contains exactly two powers
of 2. Adjoining M before computing r is essential: A={6}, M=2 gives an
augmented rank of two, not one. The shell then contains
2^ell and 6*2^(ell-2) for ell>=2, with further distinct powers of 6 as ell
grows. A fixed bound obtained by omitting the M direction is false.

## 2. Exact compiler hypotheses and source boundary

Apply the lemma to the **unchanged** adaptive chronological compiler of
codyw912's PR4638, `research/adaptive-chronological-batching/batching.py`,
`compile_widths`, and its `WIDTH.md` sections 1-4. The following are retained
hypotheses/results of that package, not independently reproved here.

* Fixed positive integer target multiplicities W_k and paid multiplicities
  P_j, with 2<=k0=min(k)<=k<=m=max(k), 1<=j<=jmax<m, and a paid rank-one
  position. Put W=sum W_k and P=sum P_j.
* Fixed integers M>=2, g>=1 and ordinary cutoff n0>=m. The named executable
  adaptive interface uses g=a+b with positive a,b, hence g>=2, and fixes
  n0=m. The cases g=1 and arbitrary n0>=m belong to the inherited written
  theorem, not this executable mode. Widths below n0 equal one.
* At n>=n0, C_n=floor(n/m), ell(n)=floor(log_M C_n), G_n=g*ell(n).
  All chronological classes are retained, including descendants of empty
  parent classes. Sources are nonempty. Every upfront complementary batch
  and every separate exit cut is retained; repeated cuts cannot pool counts.
* With source count S_n and those batches (j,b), the actual recurrence is
  a_n=lcm_(j,b)(B_j/gcd(B_j,b)) and B_n=S_n*a_n. Children strictly decrease.
  Their integer decrement is at least min(C_n,k0^(G_n+1)).

Let Gamma be generated by all target ranks, all paid ranks and M. Source
counts are constant on its multiplicative plateaus; only their locations
are counted by (1). Their prime factors need not be in Pi and are never
discarded. At level ell, 1<=S(gamma)<=W*(W+P)^(g*ell).

For N>=n0 let Lambda=floor(log_M floor(N/m)), and set

    lo_ell = max(n0, m*M^ell)
    hi_ell = min(N, m*M^(ell+1)-1)
    delta_ell = min(M^ell, k0^(g*ell+1))
    t_ell = 0                                      if hi_ell < lo_ell,
            1+floor((hi_ell-lo_ell)/delta_ell)       otherwise.

The inherited all-prime divisor is

    B_n divides Q_N=product_(ell=0..Lambda) L_ell^t_ell,  0<=n<=N, (3)

where L_ell is the LCM of all S(gamma) with M^ell<=gamma<M^(ell+1) and
gamma<=floor(N/m). It arises by bounding, separately for every prime, the
sum of source valuations along a decreasing child chain. Different primes
may choose different chains. No common maximizing chain is required.

The new result changes only the bound on the number J_ell of these plateau
starts, replacing the predecessor's full prime box by

    J_ell <= K*(ell+1)^(r-1).

Here r is the augmented valuation rank from section 1. This directly
answers Nick Petalas's PR5962 Open next question 1, which left the adaptive
shell count and this level-LCM use open.

## 3. Effective bit envelope and growth

Let w=ceil(log_2 W) and v=ceil(log_2(W+P)). Equations (1)-(3) give the exact
integer envelope

    log_2 B_n <= K*sum_(ell=0..Lambda)
                    t_ell*(ell+1)^(r-1)*(w+g*ell*v),  n<=N.     (4)

Indeed log_2 L_ell is at most the sum of the logarithms of the source counts
at its J_ell starts. This is a statement about the same B_n; no batching,
source count, leaf count, selected frontier or compiler program is changed.

Put z=max(0,1-g*log_M k0). If k0^g>=M, delta_ell=M^ell and
 t_ell<=m*(M-1). Each summand in (4) is O((ell+1)^r), so

    log B_N = O((1+log N)^(r+1))                    when z=0.    (5)

If k0^g<M, set rho=M/k0^g>1, so z=log_M rho belongs to (0,1).
The inherited integer level bound gives t_ell<=A+B*rho^ell for fixed A,B.
Since rho^ell>=1, this is at most (A+B)*rho^ell. Thus, for fixed r>=1,

    sum_(ell=0..Lambda) rho^ell*(ell+1)^r
       <= (Lambda+1)^r * rho^(Lambda+1)/(rho-1).

As rho^Lambda<=N^z and Lambda=O(log N), equation (4) yields

    log B_N = O(N^z*(1+log N)^r)                    when z>0.    (6)

All constants depend only on the fixed rule and height parameters. These
statements are not uniform in varying M, g or rank alphabets. At z=0 the
geometric estimate cannot be used with rho=1; retaining (5) avoids a false
extra logarithmic saving at that boundary. The bounds need not be tight and
the conservative K can be numerically large.

## 4. Source-bound p16 consequence

The immutable `rank-convergence/BENCHMARKS.json` p16 entry supplies M=1024,
g=a+b=2 and names `compact-joint-compiler/arithmetic/RESULT.json`. Its exact
histograms have m=145, k0=4, W=10928, P=64620 and paid rank one. The
augmented prime support is {2,3,5,7,11,13,17,19,29,41,73}; its rational
valuation rank is r=11 (the checker independently verifies a full-rank
minor). Since 1024^2=4^(5*2), z=3/5 exactly.

Consequently, under PR4638's retained recurrence hypotheses, this **named
p16 adaptive compiler width** has the improved upper bound

    log B_N = O(N^(3/5)*(1+log N)^11),

in place of PR4638's O(N^(3/5)*(1+log N)^12). This is a bound on the same
width sequence, not evidence of a smaller actual width or runtime. The
scientific benchmark is historical and is not the selected PR6030 default
strictified compiler. No new exponent of multiplication, native theorem,
physical-memory claim or selected compiler-assumption discharge follows.

## 5. Evidence and remaining assumptions

The written argument proves the quantified shell lemma. Finite independent
semigroup enumerations, exact fiber counts, independent rank checks and
bounded applications of the actual `compile_widths` corroborate its bindings.
They do not prove the inherited all-size native, ownership or compiler
correctness assumptions. Both PR4638 and PR5962 were archived as partially
reviewed; their publication is not independent scientific acceptance.

Native arbitrary-input semantics, whole-job adapters, chronological flow
correctness, owned erasure, physical allocation/routing/precision/tape costs,
outer transfer and exact integer recovery remain outside this result. No
full selected/historical replay or proof assistant is used.
