# Common-cut23 bound closes one historical C5 caller

For ONE common set of at most 23 live P112 sites, the directions whose
remaining support has binary rank at most seven occupy at most THREE
projective lines, over every characteristic-zero field, using the standard
E8 Weyl-chamber theorem. This excludes the explicit independent-piece caller
`2,3,4^2,7^6` at code-intersection dimension at least six. Its four small
pieces cover at most 22 sites, but its quotient requires six independent
directions. [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives the complete argument and scope.

This is the requested C5 application audit following workflow repair
[#3885](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/3885) and
[#3163](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/3163)'s slice.
The audit found an actual caller in PR3289. Its small support already forces
root normals, so the global normal-plane question is not needed to close
this gap. The new result instead combines a root-restricted seven-flat
capacity with a four-root off-support overlap bound. Full C5, the global
two-six-flat/common-four bound, uniform normal-plane exclusion, decoder
cost, physical suppliers, kappa and the selected record remain outside scope.

## Exact evidence

- All 43,435 binary seven-spaces have at most 36 live labels. Each of the
  120 root normals meets each seven-space in at most 32 of its off-sites:
  5,212,200 exact tests. Live normals need at least 21 deletions; frame
  normals need 24 and cannot qualify here.
- Every one of 8,214,570 four-distinct-root-normal intersections has at
  most 14 live off-sites. A common cut of 23 would require at least
  `4*21 - 3*23 = 15` such sites for four qualifying directions.
- The primary uses the exact authenticated predecessor geometry. A separate
  implementation imports no primary routines: it reconstructs the doubled
  E8 lattice shell, solves labels over Q, enumerates RREF annihilators, and
  joins root pairs. The complete flat and fourfold histograms agree.
- A literal fixture has two independent directions with residual ranks
  seven under the same 22-site cut. Attainment of three is unclaimed.
- Controls reject eight actual optimized CLI source/receipt corruptions,
  four invalid proof budgets, and the substitution of separate cuts for
  a common one. Four independent normals each have a valid 21-site cut,
  but those cuts' union has 66 sites.

PR3289's fixed inventory covers 65/69 historical `(6,4)` rows. Adding the
explicit conditional caller contracts of PR3322 and PR3405 accounts for
67/69. The new lemma raises THIS NAMED INVENTORY to 68/69; it still does not
certify `4,5^3,6^6`. This is not a global public unresolved-row count. The two
successor theorems and their inherited dependencies are reused as written
conditional contracts; their scientific censuses were not freshly replayed.
The PR3289 registered bounded composition replay was separately run and passed.

## Reproduction

Standard-library CPython 3.12; no native compiler or external library is needed
for this package. From the repository root:

```sh
python3.12 -W error -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/p112-common-cut23-caller
```

The three registered commands are `verify.py`, `independent.py`, and
`test_controls.py`. All declared inputs are exact canonical-main bytes, pinned
by commit, Git blob and SHA256 in SOURCES.json. The primary compiles only the
authenticated geometry source, bypassing bytecode imports. The second reads
its literal simple system through AST and executes no predecessor code.
Controls use private byte copies under TMPDIR. Default verification does not
write package or input files. The explicit `--write-result NEW_PATH` option
only generates a receipt and refuses to overwrite an existing path.

CHECKS.json records the actual environment, timings, export checks and unrun
obligations. A metadata/pin PASS authenticates the package, not its mathematics.
The written proof and two finite implementations are same-session AI-assisted
evidence, not independent human or proof-kernel review. No full selected cold
replay, original FINITE suite, upstream P112 manual workflow, Lean or Docker
execution is claimed.
