# Actual marked invoices at d = 1

Prepared by conorholds with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. Apache-2.0.
This deduction uses the supplement theorem in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md), together
with explicitly identified geometric and actual-basis lemmas from the earlier
marked-invoice package. It is a written proof, separate from the finite checks
of the supplement's core.

## Statement and exact scope

Use the E8/P112 code over an arbitrary characteristic-zero field K. There are
eight orthogonal frame roots f_i, 112 live root lines P, their leading-one
binary labels ell_s, and excluded frame labels z_i. Write

    C_h(s) = (r_s,h),       C = {C_h : h in K^8},
    U(q) = span_F2 {ell_s : q(s) != 0},       w(q) = dim U(q).

Let S contain C, dim S = 8+t, and let F=(q_j) be an ACTUAL basis of S.
For its actual dual basis phi_j write

    phi_j(C_h) = (x_j,h),       C_h = sum_j (x_j,h)q_j.

Assume all x_j are nonzero. A member is corrected when x_j is proportional
to a frame root f_i and z_i belongs to U(q_j). Let n_c count these actual
members, and put

    I = sum_F w(q) - n_c,
    T = span_K {q in S : w(q) <= 7},       d = dim(C intersect T).

Assume d=1 and that some actual index k has

    x_k = gamma r_v,       gamma != 0,       v in P.          (M)

For **either t=2 or t=3, I>=65**.

Consequently I-epsilon>=64 for either conclusion and every epsilon in
{0,1}, including epsilon=[ell_v in U(q_k)]. The marked source's additional
witness g, with g|O_v=0 and phi_k(g)!=0, is unnecessary here. These statements
do not assume N8, distinct corrected low frame indices, a minimum actual
basis, or that F intersect T spans T. No cost-65 sharpness claim is made.

Without (M), both values of t satisfy the **unmarked floor I>=64**.
Section 7 proves this separately. Section 8 consequently extends the
actual singleton-pivot consumer to t in {2,3},d=1 under its stated actual rank
and nonparallel hypotheses.

The t=3 cost_w(T)=17 boundary is genuine at the T-space level:
the supplement's [7,6,3,1] witness has cost 17 and d=1. It is not an actual
marked-decoder counterexample. Section 6 handles its actual correction
budget, rather than incorrectly excluding that T-space.

## 1. Actual surplus bookkeeping

We prove the stronger common budgeted lemma: if d=1 and sum_F w<=72 for
t=2, or <=73 for t=3, then I>=65. For contradiction assume I<=64 under
that budget. The mark is not used inside this lemma. It will only establish
the budget when assembling the marked theorem.
The dimension inequality dim T<=t+d follows from C+T contained in S.
The labels are nine-bit, so every piece has w<=9. An actual member
outside T cannot have weight<=7, and therefore has weight eight or nine.
The basis-free peeling inequality used here is

    cost_w(S) >= cost_w(T) + 8(dim S-dim T).

Indeed the threshold spaces S_(<=j), j<=7, all lie in T and equal T's
corresponding threshold spaces. A greedy minimum basis therefore consists
of a minimum T-basis and dim S-dim T additional vectors of weight at least
eight. This compares minimum costs before making any assertion about F.

If dim T=t+1, then S=C+T. Write a=dim T, f=|F intersect T|, r=a-f,
and s=cost_w(T)-8. Let c9 count actual members OUTSIDE T of weight nine,
and define

    eta = sum_(q in F intersect T) w(q) + 8r - cost_w(T).

The actual low members are independent and can be completed to a T-basis
by r words of weight at most seven, because those words span T. Therefore
eta>=r, even when a member of F intersect T itself has weight eight.
Direct counting of the 7+r outside members gives the exact identity

    Delta := sum_F w - 64 = s + eta + c9.                  (1)

Thus I<=64 implies Delta<=n_c. In the cases below this leaves eta+c9<=1,
or eta=c9=0. This is what controls the actual basis; no adaptation is
assumed.

For t=2, dim T=3 follows automatically. Indeed dim T cannot be one:
a one-dimensional space generated by weight-at-most-seven words cannot
contain a nonzero codeword. If dim T=2, a minimum low basis whose span
contains a codeword has cost at least14: a total at most13 either has support below53,
or consists of a rank-seven part and parts of combined rank at most six,
contradicting the supplement. Peeling then gives

    cost_w(S) >= 8(10-2)+14 = 78 > 72.

Consequently dim T=3 and cost_w(T)<=72-8*7=16. This step uses neither the
source's x=0 normalization nor its finite survivor list.

For t=3 the same argument gives dim T=4 and cost_w(T)<=17. More explicitly,
any low space containing a nonzero codeword has minimum cost at least14:
at total cost at most13, if a weight-seven basis member occurs, all others
have combined rank at most six and the supplement excludes their cover;
if none occurs, the largest support-capacity sum is `20+20+1=41<53`.
Thus dim T<=3 would give cost_w(S)>=8(11-3)+14=78>73. Dimension four then
gives cost_w(T)<=73-8*7=17. Section 6 handles the remaining cost17 case.

## 2. Complete minimum-profile exhaustion through cost sixteen

Take a minimum basis of T. Its weights are positive and at most seven.
Its support covers a nonzero codeword and therefore at least53 sites.
Use the rank capacities above; if the total support bound is below70, the
covered codeword is a root/frame direction, and each seven-flat then has
the stronger target capacity32.

For three pieces of total weight at most16:

* If all weights are at most six, the largest support bound is
  `g(6)+g(6)+g(4)=48`.
* If the largest is seven and the second at most four, the bound is at
  most `36+8+8=52`.
* If the second is five, only [7,5,4] can reach53; its crude bound56 forces
  a root/frame target, whose bound becomes `32+12+8=52`.
* If the second is six, the third is at most three. The supplement excludes
  all [7,6,c], c<=3, since the third support has at most four sites.
* The remaining possibilities are precisely **[7,7,1] and [7,7,2]**.

For four pieces of total weight at most16:

* If all weights are at most six, the largest support bound is
  `g(6)+g(6)+g(3)+g(1)=45`.
* If the largest is seven and the second at most three, the bound is at
  most `36+4+4+4=48`.
* With second weight four, only [7,4,4,1] can reach53; target32 reduces its
  bound to49. With second weight five, only [7,5,3,1] can reach53, and its
  target bound is likewise49.
* With second weight six, only [7,6,1,1] and [7,6,2,1] need consideration.
  The supplement excludes both: their small supports total at most three.
* The remaining possibility is precisely **[7,7,1,1]**.

This exhausts all positive integer profiles in the stated ranges, without
the source's LP, N8, Pencil or Lemma N. Their costs and slacks are

| t | Minimum T profile | cost_w(T) | s |
|---|---|---:|---:|
|2|[7,7,1]|15|7|
|2|[7,7,2]|16|8|
|3|[7,7,1,1]|16|8|

For a minimum weighted basis the numbers of vectors at each weight
threshold are fixed by the dimensions of T_(<=w) (the matroid greedy

## 3. The complete non-adapted boundary

If r>0, equation(1), eta>=r and the budget force r=eta=1,c9=0.
The t=2 minimum profile must be [7,7,1]; [7,7,2] has s=8 and no surplus
available. Completing F intersect T by one weight-at-most-seven vector
must attain the minimum, so the ACTUAL inside profile is [7,1]. For t=3
it is [7,1,1]. Let L span these actual inside members. Its support has
at most37 or38 sites, respectively, so C intersect L=0. Dimensions give

    S = C direct-sum L.

There are exactly eight actual outside members, all of weight eight.
Their high restricted code duals form a basis. Write their code normals
as h_l and their duals as mu_l; the actual coefficient map is

    q_l=C_(h_l)+A(h_l),       A(h)=-sum_(b in F intersect T) (x_b,h)b.
                                                               (2)

All corrected high frame indices are distinct. If J is their set, a
corrected high matched to i has h_l supported on its own frame axis i and
the unmatched frame axes, with nonzero coefficient at i. These assertions
come from actual duality, not projected normalization.

For t=2, Delta=8 and I<=64 force n_c>=8. At most one actual low is
corrected, so at least seven weight-eight highs are corrected. If all
eight are corrected, their normals are frame axes. If seven are corrected,
each normal is in span(f_i,f_j0), where j0 is the one unmatched axis.
For a non-axis such normal, put H=U(q_l). Equation(2) forces

    Off(h_l) minus H subset supp(b_7) union {u},

where u is the unit low's site. After deleting u those labels have rank
at most seven, contradicting G4. Hence at least seven corrected high
normals are frame axes. Each needs at least24 cancellations from the

so `7*24 <= 4*37`, a contradiction. This is exactly the actual-(7,1)

For t=3, Delta=9 forces n_c>=9. Only the weight-seven low can be corrected,

For a frame axis different from the corrected low's index, equation(2)

## 4. Actual adapted bases: all surplus possibilities

Now r=0, so F intersect T is an actual T-basis and there are seven outside
members. The allowed actual inside profiles are exactly as follows:

| Minimum profile | eta | Possible actual inside profile |
|---|---:|---|
|[7,7,1]|0|[7,7,1]|
|[7,7,1]|1|[8,7,1]|
|[7,7,2]|0|[7,7,2]|
|[7,7,1,1]|0|[7,7,1,1]|
|[7,7,1,1]|1|[8,7,1,1] or [7,7,2,1]|

To verify exhaustiveness, the minimum [7,7,1] profile has dim T_(<=6)=1,
generated by its unique unit direction; an actual weight-two replacement
in that line still has weight one. At most one surplus unit can therefore
only raise one seven to eight. For [7,7,1,1], T_(<=6) is exactly the span
of its two unit vectors, so a surplus unit either raises one seven to
eight or changes a unit-space basis from weights [1,1] to [2,1]. Raising
both units gives surplus two. For [7,7,2] no surplus is available. The
threshold-dimension formula rules out every profile with fewer than two
members of weight at least seven. Also c9 can be one only when eta=0.

Let C_h span C intersect T. Corrected high duals are independent and
annihilate h, so they are distinct frame axes perpendicular to h.
For independence, the seven high coordinate functionals form a basis of
Ann(T) in S*. Restriction Ann(T) to C* has kernel Ann(C+T)=Ann(S)=0,
since dim T=t+1 gives C+T=S. This proves independence of their actual
restricted duals directly.

The minimum profiles cover Off(h) by two seven-flats and at most two
additional sites, so |Off(h)|<=74. Every row of the actual-profile table
has at most two potentially corrected inside members, since its other
weights are at most two. The actual ledger requires n_c>=Delta>=s>=7;
hence at least five independent corrected high frame axes annihilate h.
The normal h is therefore supported on at most three frame axes. The
restricted-axis count above excludes every non-axis direction with at
most74 nonzeros. Thus h must be a frame direction. This argument counts
corrected weight-nine highs too; none is silently discarded.

## 5. The frame-direction case, including its equality obstruction

It remains that h=f_0 up to a scalar. Let

    W0 = z_0^perp_beta,       N0 = N(f_0).

The live root labels in W0 are exactly N0. In any minimum T-basis from
Section 2, each of the two seven-flats lies INSIDE W0. Otherwise its
intersection with W0 has rank at most six and meets N0 in at most20
sites; the other seven-flat meets N0 in at most32 and the small pieces
add at most two, totaling54<56. Thus

    supp(T) subset N0 union E,       |E|<=2.                (3)

No frame label lies in W0, since beta(z_0,z_i)=1 for every frame index i.
Every weight-seven actual member can be included with a basis of the
small subspace in a minimum T-basis, by threshold dimensions. Hence its
label span lies in W0 and it is uncorrected. All weight-one/two members
are also uncorrected. In the table only a weight-eight inside member can
be corrected, so

    n_T <= eta.                                           (4)

Let n8 count corrected weight-eight outside members. We prove n8<=6.
Otherwise all seven outside members have weight eight and are corrected.
Their independent duals are precisely the seven frame directions f_i,
i!=0. The actual expansion evaluated at f_i then gives

    q_i = lambda_i C_(f_i) + tau_i,       tau_i in T,
    lambda_i != 0.                                        (5)

Use the eight-dimensional polar model B on root residues from PROOF.md.
For i!=0 the full32-root section

    A_i = {q=1 : B(f_i,x)=1, B(f_0,x)=0}

contains no frame root and has leading-one label rank seven. Off its at
most two sites in E, (3)--(5) put every A_i coordinate into supp(q_i).
These at least30 sites still span the same seven-flat: a rank-six flat
has at most20 roots. Since q_i carries z_i, its rank-eight span is the
unique hyperplane obtained by adjoining z_i to that seven-flat, namely

    { (a,x) : B(f_0,x)=0 }.

Indeed A_i's span has equations B(f_i,x)=a, B(f_0,x)=0,
whereas z_i violates the first and satisfies the second. This hyperplane
contains no live site of N0, whose residues have B(f_0,x)=1. Therefore
q_i vanishes on N0 and (5) yields

    tau_i|N0 = -lambda_i C_(f_i)|N0       for all seven i!=0.

These seven restricted frame codewords are independent: N0 spans K^8.
For an explicit reason, all sign patterns of every four-axis incidence
block containing index 0 occur among its neighbors; their span contains
the four corresponding axes. Such blocks cover all eight axes (G5's
S(3,4,8) incidence), so their union spans K^8 over characteristic zero.
Thus dim(T|N0)>=7, contradicting dim T<=4. This proves n8<=6.

Finally let c9_corr count corrected outside weight-nine members. Separating
actual low, corrected weight-eight and corrected weight-nine terms in
the exact ledger gives

    I = 64+s+eta-n_T-n8+(c9-c9_corr)
      >= 64+s-6 >=65,                                    (6)

using (4) and c9_corr<=c9. This exhausts the frame case, both offsets of
the possible actual surplus, and every weight-nine status. It contradicts
I<=64 and completes the cost-at-most16 part of the budgeted lemma.

## 6. The t3 cost-seventeen boundary

Suppose cost_w(T)=17 at t=3. Here s=9. The raw weight budget sum_Fw<=73,
I<=64 and equation(1) force

    r=eta=c9=0,       Delta=9,       n_c>=9.                (7)

Thus the ACTUAL inside basis is a minimum T-basis and all seven outside
members have weight eight. This adaptation follows from equality.

The profile exhaustion of Section 2 extends as follows. With largest
weight at most six, the maximum capacity sum at total17 is
`20+20+8+1=49<53`. With largest seven, the new profiles with capacity
sum at least53 are [7,4,4,2], [7,5,3,2], [7,5,4,1], [7,6,2,2],
[7,6,3,1], [7,7,2,1]. The first two have crude support at most54;
root/frame target32 reduces their capacities to50, excluding them. The
supplement excludes [7,6,2,2], whose small supports total at most four.
Only three profiles remain.

**[7,5,4,1].** At most three inside members are corrected, so n_c>=9
requires at least six independent corrected high frame axes perpendicular
to h. Thus h uses at most two frame axes. Its crude capacity57 and the
restricted-axis count force a frame direction. But its frame target
cannot fit: `32+12+8+1=53<56`, a contradiction.

**[7,6,3,1].** Again at most three inside members are corrected, so at
least six corrected high frame axes annihilate h. Its crude capacity61
and the restricted-axis count force a frame normal f_0. Both the
seven-flat and six-flat lie inside W0. Otherwise the former
would give `20+20+4+1=45<56`, or the latter would give
`32+12+4+1=49<56`. The two large inside members are therefore uncorrected;
only the rank-three member can be corrected. Thus n_c<=7+1=8<9. The
five-site T-space witness remains valid while failing the actual budget.

**[7,7,2,1].** The small members cannot be corrected. Equation(7) would
require both inside sevens and all seven outside members corrected.
The independent high duals are seven frame axes perpendicular to h,
so h is their remaining frame axis f_0. Both inside seven-flats must
lie in W0, since otherwise `20+32+2+1=55<56`. They are then uncorrected,
a contradiction. No direction classification through75 sites is used.

This excludes all cost17 cases under I<=64. Together with Sections 1--5
it proves the common budgeted lemma.

## 7. The unmarked floors

First, for any actual basis in this setting with m<16, without (M),

    n_c <= m-1.                                           (8)

If all members were corrected, their restricted duals would lie in the
eight frame directions. These duals span the full eight-dimensional normal
space: a normal annihilated by every x_j would have C_h=0 by the actual
expansion, hence h=0. Thus all eight frame groups are nonempty. Evaluating
the expansion at a frame f_i uses only its own group. A singleton group
would force its sole q_j to be a nonzero scalar multiple of C_(f_i),
whose label span excludes z_i; it would be uncorrected. Therefore every
group has at least two members, requiring m>=16, a contradiction. This
proves (8), without normalization or any bound on repeated low indices.

For the marked theorem, suppose I<=64. The raw marked cap n_c<=m-2 gives
sum_Fw<=64+8=72 for t=2 and <=64+9=73 for t=3. The common budgeted lemma
then gives I>=65, a contradiction. This proves the claimed marked bound.

For the unmarked theorem, suppose I<=63. Bound (8) gives sum_Fw<=63+9=72
for t=2 and <=63+10=73 for t=3. The SAME common budgeted lemma contradicts
I<=63, proving I>=64 without a root mark. In the unmarked [7,1] case one
can also avoid G4: I<=63 requires n_c>=9, hence a corrected seven-low and
all eight corrected frame-axis highs; away from that low's frame index
the coefficient map only uses the unit, contradicting the matched24 cut.
The common proof retains G4 because the marked boundary can have only
seven corrected highs.

## 8. Complete actual singleton-pivot d1 consumer

This is the consumer of PR3455 CONSUMER.md with its actual hypotheses,
and with d=1 replacing d=0. Let

    N0(s,t) = (r_s,r_t)/2 = sum_j p_j(s)q_j(t),

be an exact factorization with 9+t terms and actual ranks
(rank P,rank Q)=(9,8+t), for t in {2,3},
all p_j nonzero and pairwise nonparallel, and a singleton pivot
p_pi=lambda delta_v, lambda!=0, at a live root v. Rescale the pivot pair
to p_pi=delta_v. Let S span all q_j and assume

    dim(C intersect S_(<=7)) = 1.

Then the actual binary decoder cost is at least64. As in the earlier
consumer, cost is the sum of the ranks of beta restricted to the actual
row-label and column-label support spans. No global reduction to this
rank/pivot class is asserted.

Here is the actual data recovery, which does not use d=0. The column
space of N0 is C. Its distance excludes delta_v from C, so the actual
rank-nine condition implies span{p_j}=C direct-sum K delta_v. Therefore
every nonpivot row has a unique representation

    p_j = (1/2)C_(a_j) + omega_j delta_v.

Nonparallelism with the pivot forces a_j!=0. Project exactness onto C and
onto its singleton complement. These two projections give

    C_h = sum_(j!=pi) (a_j,h)q_j,
    q_pi = -sum_(j!=pi) omega_j q_j.                       (9)

Thus the 8+t nonpivot columns span S and, because rank Q=8+t, form its
ACTUAL basis. Their restricted duals are x_j=a_j, all nonzero, precisely
as required by the invoice theorems. The projected one-sided cost is
I=sum_(j!=pi)w(q_j)-n_c, by the dense pairing's elementary rank-loss
formula for the frame-row hyperplanes.

The row-edit geometry in PR3455 CONSUMER.md Section 3 is independent of
d=0: changing a codeword only at v can reduce its binary term cost only
when its normal is proportional to v and its own v entry is lost.
Deleting one site from a frame word preserves its rank-eight span; a
different root retains its root coloop and rank-eight nonself remainder;
the one-site root-only lemma excludes any other normal whose span drops.
All such losing actual rows are proportional, so there is at most one
by the actual nonparallel hypothesis. If present, call it k. Its projected
span is full nine and its actual span is W_v; its unique possible rebate
is epsilon=[ell_v in U(q_k)]. If c_pi is the pivot's cost, either zero or
one, the exact support comparison therefore gives

    cost(D) >= I-epsilon+c_pi,                            (10)

with epsilon=0 if there is no losing row. These cited row-edit inputs,
including the one-site root-only lemma and no-coloop bounds, are the same
geometric inputs as the earlier consumer; they are not an assumption
that the positive-d space has an adapted basis.

If no losing row exists, the unmarked I>=64 proves cost(D)>=64. If a
losing row k exists, a_k=gamma r_v, gamma!=0, supplies (M), so the marked
I>=65 proves the same conclusion even after its unit rebate, regardless
of whether the pivot is paid. This covers every actual row-edit case.

For precision, in the free-pivot/losing-row case the earlier source's
additional witness is also recovered, without d=0. Loss of the v entry
forces omega_k=-gamma, because C_v(v)=2. Equation(9) then gives
phi_k(q_pi)=gamma!=0. Pivot cost zero means q_pi vanishes wherever
beta(ell_v,ell_s)=1, namely O_v={v} union {s:(r_s,r_v)=0}. Thus g=q_pi is
the actual source witness. The present stronger invoice does not require
it. If q_pi=0, equation(9) and the nonpivot basis force all omega_j=0, so
no losing row exists. This harmless edge case is covered as well.

## Consequences and remaining boundary

The tight actual marked t2/t3,d=1 branches are closed at I-epsilon>=64, for the
precise basis and root-mark hypotheses above; it is not a closure of all
positive d or of every physical decoder. The separate unmarked floor
and Section 8 settle both row-edit branches of the stated tight t2/t3,d1
actual singleton-pivot consumer. The rank, nonparallel, singleton and
d=1 hypotheses remain explicit; no global branch normalization is claimed.

The t3 cost17/slack-nine T-space remains feasible, but it cannot supply the
corrections needed for an actual invoice below these floors. Positive
d>=2 is outside this d=1 proof; [D2.md](D2.md) supplies the complete
actual d=2 extension. Actual d>=3, branch normalization and all other
physical rank/pivot classes remain outside the package result. No normalization or global C1 conclusion is
inferred from the nine literal-list exclusions or the d=1 invoice theorem.
