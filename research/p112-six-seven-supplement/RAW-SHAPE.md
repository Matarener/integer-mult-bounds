# No raw threshold-dimension defect in a putative bad invoice

Prepared by conorholds with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. Apache-2.0.
Definitions, characteristic-zero setting and predecessor credits are those
of D1.md and D2.md. This is a written theorem, not a full Lean theorem.

## Statement

Let S contain C, dim S=8+t, t in {2,3}. Let F be an actual basis, with
every restricted dual x_j nonzero. Put T=span{q in S:w(q)<=7},
d=dim(C intersect T)>0, a=dim T, and I=sum_F w-n_c. Assume

    sum_F w <=72 for t2, or <=73 for t3, and I<=64.

Then **a=d+t**, for every 1<=d<=8. For d<=6 the raw weight budget alone
suffices: the I and nonzero-dual assumptions are not used.

The marked contradiction regime I<=64 implies these raw budgets by
PR3455's raw correction cap; the unmarked regime I<=63 also implies
them. Thus no positive-d invoice search in these regimes needs a
lower-dimensional T branch. This does not force F intersect T to span T,
bound repeated corrected indices, prove N8, or settle the d>=3 invoices.
Global rank/pivot normalization remains separate.

## 1. Peeling and small residual spans

Choose a minimum-weight basis of T, all of whose weights are <=7. Write
f=|F intersect T|, r=a-f, c9 for the number of actual outside weight-nine
members, and eta=sum_(F intersect T)w+8r-cost(T). Completing the inside
independent family with r low generators gives eta>=r. Exactly

    sum_F w = cost(T)+8(8+t-a)+eta+c9.                 (1)

Consequently cost(T)<=8a-8 at t2, and <=8a-15 at t3. Dimension gives
d<=a<=d+t. The case a=d is impossible: T would be contained in C, which
has no nonzero words of weight <=7, whereas T is generated by such words.

We use three established inputs: every representation of a nonzero
codeword by pieces of weight at most seven has total covering weight
at least14; ranks6 and7 alone cannot cover a nonzero codeword;
and the four-six, intrinsic7755 and intrinsic7764 exclusions proved in
CALLERS.md, D2-PROFILE.md and RIGIDITY.md. Deleting k basis coordinates
from a d-dimensional code intersection leaves dimension at least d-k.

For t2 and a<=d+1, delete the d-1 heaviest coordinates. A nonzero
codeword remains at covering weight at most

    floor((a-d+1)cost(T)/a).

At a=d this is <=7. At a=d+1 it is at most
floor(16-16/(d+1)), which is <=13 through d=6. This contradicts14 and
proves a=d+2 for those d.

For t3, d=1 is immediate: a<=3 would give cost(T)<=9<14. For d>=2,
the case a=d+1 is also impossible. Delete the d-2 heaviest coordinates,
leaving a two-dimensional code intersection in the three smallest
pieces. Every one of their weights must be seven: otherwise killing
another coordinate covers a nonzero word by ranks6 and7. Hence every
minimum weight is seven, so 7a<=8a-15 would require a>=15, whereas a<=9.

For the remaining t3 defect a=d+2, delete d-2 heaviest coordinates.
The four smallest weights support a two-dimensional code intersection.
They must dominate one of the necessary lower profiles

    7666, 7765, 7771.                                (2)

Indeed four ranks<=6 are excluded. With exactly one seven, killing it
forces the other three weights to be666, since20+20+12<53. With at least
two sevens, the intrinsic7755 and7764 exclusions leave either a third
seven or a six followed by at least five. No feasibility of (2) is claimed.

The first two alternatives force total minimum cost >=7a-3; the third
forces >=7a-6. Comparison with8a-15 excludes a<=8. This proves the t3
raw-dimension statement through d=6. It leaves only the exact boundaries
listed in Sections 3--4.

## 2. Two actual boundary-profile lemmas

First suppose t2, F consists of eight weight-seven members and two
weight-eight members, L spans the eight sevens, and dim(C intersect L)=7.
Then **I>=65**.

Let D=C intersect L. Both outside duals are nonzero multiples of a normal
h spanning Ann(D). The eight inside coordinate restrictions on D are
nonzero and pairwise independent. To see this, write D=ker lambda in L.
Every coordinate of lambda is nonzero: otherwise its basis vector lies
in C, impossible at weight seven. A relation involving at most two
coordinate restrictions cannot be a multiple of this full-support lambda.
Corrected inside frame indices are therefore distinct.

If h is not a frame direction, neither outside member is corrected.
Assuming n_c>=8 forces all eight sevens corrected, one per frame axis.
Choose two active coordinates i,j of h. The two-axis normal
u=(h,f_j)f_i-(h,f_i)f_j is nonzero and perpendicular to h. Its codeword
belongs to D and uses only the two corresponding seven-pieces. It has
at most72 sites, contrary to the retained two-axis minimum76.

If h=f_0, no inside corrected row has index0. Under n_c>=8, at least
six of the eight sevens are corrected. Seven corrected rows leave one
uncorrected piece A. Any other frame word is covered by its matched
seven, of target capacity20, and A, of frame-target capacity32. This
would give56<=52.

Six corrected rows leave two uncorrected pieces A,B and one missing
frame index b!=0. The word C_(f_b) uses only A,B. Both their label flats
lie inside W_b: otherwise their frame-target capacities total at most
20+32<56. Thus both pieces vanish outside N(f_b). For any corrected
index i, its piece must contain all32 sites N(f_i) minus N(f_b).
Those labels span rank seven and exclude z_i (the two orthogonal-frame
completion equations). Its entire rank-seven span is that span, so it
cannot carry z_i. This contradiction proves n_c<=7 and I>=65.

Second suppose t3, F consists of eight sevens, one unit piece and two
eights, and L spans the nine inside pieces with dim(C intersect L)=7.
Again **I>=65**. The outside normals lie in Kh=Ann(C intersect L).
If h is not frame, n_c<=8 because the unit cannot be corrected.
Otherwise h=f_0 and n_c>=9 forces at least seven corrected sevens.
Inside corrected indices may repeat. The argument counts their groups
and uses a singleton group; it does not require pairwise distinctness.

Every other frame index needs a corrected group. An absent group would
leave its frame word supported by at most one uncorrected seven plus
the unit, of target capacity<=33. Seven corrected sevens therefore give
seven singleton groups; each frame word is covered by its matched
seven, the uncorrected seven and the unit, with capacity20+32+1=53<56.
With eight corrected sevens there is still a singleton group among the
seven required indices. Its frame word has capacity20+1<56. These are
the only cases, so n_c<=8 and I>=65.

## 3. The t2 boundary d=7 or8

At d=7 a defect has a=8. Killing the six largest minimum coordinates
leaves two pieces covering a nonzero word, so their weights total at
least14. Both are <=7; hence all eight minimum weights are seven.
Thus cost(T)=56 and (1) forces r=eta=c9=0 and the actual profile7^8,8^2.
Its eight-low span is T and meets C in dimension seven. The first
boundary lemma excludes I<=64.

At d=8, a=8 is already impossible. At a=9 the same tail argument forces
nine minimum weights seven, cost63, and eta+c9<=1. Adaptation r=0
would give an outside actual dual vanishing on C, since C is contained
in T. Hence r=eta=1,c9=0. The actual inside sum is56, so it consists
of eight sevens; all nonzero T-words have weight at least seven here.
The two outside members are eights. Their inside span L meets C in
dimension at least seven, and cannot meet it in dimension eight:
otherwise C is contained in L and the outside duals vanish on C.
The first boundary lemma again applies. This completes t2.

## 4. The t3 boundary d=7 or8

At d=7 the only defect is a=9. The lower bounds in (2) and cost<=57
force minimum profile1,7^8 with cost57. Equation(1) forces actual
adaptation and the same inside minimum profile, followed by two eights.
The second boundary lemma excludes I<=64.

At d=8 the only defect is a=10. Minimum cost is <=65, whereas the first
two alternatives in (2) require at least67. Thus only the minimum
profiles1,7^9 (cost64) or2,7^9 (cost65) remain. The latter forces r=0,
impossible because C is contained in T and an outside dual would vanish.
In the former, eta+c9<=1 and r>=1, so r=eta=1,c9=0. The actual inside
sum is57. The threshold space through weight six is the unit line;
therefore its nine independent actual inside members are one unit and
eight sevens. Their span L has code intersection exactly seven by the
same dimension and nonzero-outside-dual argument. The two outside members
are eights. The second boundary lemma excludes I<=64 and completes t3.

The new common-profile arguments use actual coordinate expansions and
corrections. They do not assert that T-level deletion preserves an
invoice. All geometric premises are the retained support capacities,
two-axis support minimum and orthogonal-frame completion bounds already
identified in D1.md and PROOF.md. No normalized source list is needed.

## 5. Actual d=3 defect branches closed by transfer

Retain the statement's hypotheses and put d=3. Then every putative bad
invoice has either **r=0**, or **r=1 and eta>1**. In particular all r>=2
branches, and every nonadapted branch eta=r, are excluded. This is a
partial actual invoice result, not the complete d3 invoice.

First, the intrinsic low-family bounds give cost(T)>=29 at t2 and >=30
at t3. For t2, five pieces of ranks<=6 cannot have code intersection
three: delete one coordinate and apply four-six. Otherwise delete a
seven. The remaining four pieces have code intersection at least two
and minimum total weight22, with unique equality profile7771. Thus
cost29 forces minimum profile77771.

For t3, suppose the six-piece cost is<=30 and all weights are<=6.
Deleting a heaviest coordinate leaves five pieces with cost<=25 and
code intersection at least two. The intrinsic five-piece exhaustion in
D2.md excludes all such profiles with weights<=6 (four-six-plus-small,
or55555). Hence a seven exists. Deleting it uses the five-piece lower
bound23, with unique equality profile77711. This proves cost>=30,
with equality profile777711.

Equation(1) now gives eta+c9<=3, so r<=3. At r=3 equality forces
eta=3,c9=0 and the two displayed minimum profiles. Actual inside cost
is8 at t2 and9 at t3. Their threshold filtrations force actual inside
profiles71 and711 respectively: below rank seven the spaces are the
unit line and the two-unit plane. The actual inside span L is disjoint
from C by support distance53, and S=C direct-sum L. All eight outside
members have weight eight. The actual71 argument of D1 Section3
(reused in D2's r2 case) excludes n_c>=8. For711, n_c>=9 forces all
eight highs and the seven-low corrected; every other frame axis has an
actual expansion whose low contribution uses only the two unit lows,
contrary to G2's required24 cancellations. This excludes r=3.

For eta=r>0, complete the actual inside family to T by r low generators.
Their cost is at most7r, whereas eta=r gives

    cost(T)=sum_inside w+7r.

Thus the completion attains the minimum and all new generators have
weight seven. Replacing the inside family by a minimum basis of L while
keeping those completion vectors still gives a basis of T, so

    cost(L) <= sum_inside w = cost(T)-7r <= cost(L).

Hence the actual inside family is a minimum basis of L. At r=1, L has
dimension4/cost<=24 at t2, or dimension5/cost<=25 at t3; its code
intersection has dimension exactly two. At r=2 these are dimension3/
cost<=16 or dimension4/cost<=17, with code intersection exactly one.
The lower bounds just proved, the four-piece bound22 and the two-piece
covering-weight bound14 rule out larger intersections by deletion.
In each case C+L=S. Hence the actual outside restricted duals are
independent in the appropriate annihilator. The adapted eta0 arguments
of D2 (r=1) and D1 (r=2) apply to this explicit L and exclude the bad
invoice. This reuses their actual-profile proofs, not their theorem
statements with L substituted for the global threshold space.

It remains to exclude r=2,eta=3. The budget forces c9=0 and cost(T)=29/
30. The same threshold filtrations give actual inside profile871 at
t2, or8711/7721 at t3. Their code intersection is exactly one: it is
at least one by dimension, and killing one large coordinate in a
putative two-dimensional intersection leaves a support below53.
Thus C+L=S and all seven actual outside members have weight eight.
These are precisely the actual-L configurations excluded by D2's
nonadapted871 argument at t2 and8711/7721 argument at t3. Those proofs
use the displayed actual coefficients, independent outside duals and
n_c>=8/9, all available here; they require neither minimum L nor L=T.
This completes the stated defect-branch exclusion.

The remaining adapted d3 cases and r=1 with eta>1 need new invoice
arguments. For example, minimum77771 with r=1,eta=2 permits actual
inside8771; one cannot infer a7771 minimum basis of that L. Also the
three-dimensional code intersection can contain three independent
frame lines, so the d2 root-plane contradiction does not transfer.
