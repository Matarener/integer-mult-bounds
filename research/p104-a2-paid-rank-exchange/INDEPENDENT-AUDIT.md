full phase. Use z_r for a register's value in the common reference coordinates.
The physical field in chart f is Φ_f z_r. At a coframed scalar gate,

    Φ_f z_r + q Φ_f z_s = Φ_f (z_r+q z_s).

At a paid change a→b, the supplied native map is Φ_b Φ_a⁻¹, so it sends
Φ_a z_r to Φ_b z_r. These two exact identities prove the frame invariant by
induction through the chronological program. The proof never commutes physical

with sums of tensors. Owned operations retain their existing checked contracts.
