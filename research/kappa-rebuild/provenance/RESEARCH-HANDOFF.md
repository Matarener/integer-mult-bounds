## Direction 1: replace the local response with a composition-friendly involution

### Algebraic construction

Split the 112 local ports into 104 ordinary ports U and eight selected ports A. The authenticated pre-reset geometry had operators K and L with `LK=13I`. Its pruned response was

\[
J=\begin{pmatrix}I-KL&-K\\-L&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Completing the selected response gives

\[
H=\begin{pmatrix}I-KL&-K\\11L&12I\end{pmatrix}
  =I+BC,
\qquad B=\binom{-K}{11I},\quad C=(L\ I).
\]

Since `CB=-2I`, `H^2=I`. This is a useful interface change: a second application undoes the first, and the discrepancy from the identity is carried by eight state functions. It is an actual changed data operation; callers must be synthesized for H rather than pretending it still performs the old identity response.

## Direction 2: productive helpers change the recursive family

The old family placed every borrowed helper inside an idle segment of an existing main-data register. Even a fractional idealization of its historical repaired-identity packing missed `C=0.001745`; the best tested p=4 allowance was about `-1.5625611533`. Searching that packing family more finely cannot supply the missing gain under the same constraints.

The new proposal adds registers whose job is a complete transform of their own arbitrary input. They host existing cleaned helper blocks on successive unfinished axes and finish the remaining axes afterward. Their full output credit is legitimate only once the whole transform and all boundary interfaces are proved. A cache ending with `d+v` is not such a productive output.

### The boundary lemma that makes the proposal plausible

An exactly cleaned native word has physical operator

\[
\operatorname{diag}(D,T_1,\ldots,T_{408}),
\qquad T_i=F_{i,\mathrm{end}}F_{i,\mathrm{start}}^{-1}.
\]

Suppose each actual `T_i` has a certified allowed monomial normal form `T_i=L_i F_{r_i} R_i`. Applying `R_i^-1` before and `L_i^-1` after the **whole helper block** changes that block to `F_{r_i}` while preserving D and all zero cross terms. Internal ADDs and contacts stay intact. This avoids a spurious requirement to separately prepare all canonical helper entrance frames, which would otherwise add 371 rank units in the one-pair proposal.

The lemma is elementary block algebra. Its application still needs each actual relative transform, both allowed monomials, and the absolute phase checked, including the backward conjugate convention. The missing native `clifford_exact.lower_child` machinery should provide these checks after source recovery. Independent retagging of internal helper frames would not be a substitute.

### A useful positive from changing the contact location

The relay probes tested optional exchanges, small signed additive relays, and repeated cache-bank assignments. Those finite-field menus did not close the larger controllers. Then the exchange was moved to the target's entrance, where the required future query may be accessible before its frame changes.

For p=1, a constant-chart exact integer example restores one arbitrary cache bank and exposes the missing correction. There were 66 modular closing variants in the 864-case entrance-exchange menu, but that count is only a finite-field screening result. The selected endpoint is independently checked as an integer matrix. It is not a physical supplier.

The stronger operator identity does not require equal or commuting local H operators. Let `QE=I`, `T=I-EQ`, and begin with `Y0=Y+H0 X`. Exchange `QY0` with arbitrary P=d using insertion E. This produces `Y1=T Y0+Ed`. Apply the backward word only to X: `X1=X-H1 Y1`. Repeat the **same query exchange** on Y. Then exactly `Y2=Y0` and `P=d`. The final forward word and signed swap give

\[
X_{out}=Y_0+H_2X_1,\qquad Y_{out}=-X_1,\qquad P_{out}=d.
\]

The fresh relay receipt checks a noncommuting matrix instance. The result keeps arbitrary borrowed data without assuming all owner charts coincide. Native access to both Q and its right inverse E at both contacts, their actual transports, the cache path, and correction of the main outputs remain required. Cheap query access alone does not imply a cheap insertion or decoder.

Using different queries silently is invalid. If the second query is C2 and the first is C1 with `C1 E1=I`, the bank returns

\[
P_{out}=C_2(I-E_1C_1)Y_0+C_2E_1d.
\]

Requiring `P_out=d` for every input forces `C2=C1` after actual transport into a common chart. The proposed remedy is the same future query at both pre-Y contacts, not an assumption that different native queries happen to match.

### Catalytic quarter-turn identity

When `CB=-2I`, the three shears `U(B/2) L(C) U(B/2)` send `(Cx,d)` to `(-d,Cx)`. Squaring this word and negating d implements `(x,d)->((I+BC)x,d)`. After adjacent upper shears merge, it needs two C queries and three B scatters. This is an exact useful identity over dyadic coefficients; its timed native contacts and complete recursive invoice are still open. The source includes negative controls for an omitted final sign and a wrong CB value.
