# Full-identity supplier with no external cleanup bank

## What is established

The restored full-identity ancestor and the later scheduled cleanup can be
composed directly. Their emitted native word passes exact replay in both
directions for all four providers:

\[
F_I:(X,Y,h)\mapsto(X,Y+X,h),\qquad
B_I:(X,Y,h)\mapsto(X-Y,Y,h).
\]

All 632 persistent inputs are arbitrary and independent: 224 data fields and
408 helper fields. Every helper returns autonomously in its logical reference,
with no data-to-helper or helper-to-data defect. The helper boundary certificate
then gives the actual standard partial Fourier transform, including absolute
phase, on each helper output. There is no external cleanup bank. The eight
sequential center computations use one reused owned C0 field, explicitly
cleared before each use and on every return.

This is a complete **ideal local supplier**. It does not by itself establish a
whole recursive recipe, a rounded induction, or a new multiplication kappa.

## Why the composition is valid

The full-identity ancestor and the later zero-bank clean-J canonical differ
in exactly seven fields: `centers`, `center_frames`, `cut`, `compensation`,
`cleanup`, `local_profile`, and `zero_buffers`. Every data and helper frame,
ordered port label, A and B instruction, native warp, entrance and terminal
reference agrees.

The new generator keeps the ancestor's full compensation and all eight center
cuts. It substitutes only the later scheduled `cleanup` and empty
`zero_buffers` list. It then emits the actual full word, maps its sequential
center namespaces to C0, and executes that resulting word on all arbitrary
input columns. Thus it does not infer compatibility merely from similar
profiles or from a historical clean-J receipt.

For each center, the generator chooses a three-dimensional subspace of its
actual eight-dimensional canonical frame. Forward descends 8 to 3 to 0;
backward ascends 0 to 3 to 8 in the proper conjugate orientation. The exact
Clifford compiler supplies only rank-5 and rank-3 children, and a separate
decode checks their complete phase and product. The unchanged native edges
retain authenticated predecessor evidence.

All helper references match the already regenerated all-408 boundary
certificate, and the new endpoint replay proves the logical helper block is
still identity. These two facts together justify its reuse for this supplier.

Deleting one center response from the actual new word is rejected. This
negative control demonstrates that the claimed full identity is not an
unchecked clean-J output or an artifact of dropping the center contribution.
