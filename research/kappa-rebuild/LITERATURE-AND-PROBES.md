# Literature, exact probes, and changes of direction

Apache-2.0. Prepared with substantial OpenAI assistance. This is the research
record for the October 21 reconstruction, preserving the previous cumulative
work. It distinguishes a paper's theorem, a useful analogy, and an actual
native implementation in this repository.

## The accounting question that guided the search

The objective is to increase kappa by reducing the complete recursive bill.
We count the actual transform delivered on each independent arbitrary input,
all paid recursive calls, every preparation and restoration, the permitted
scalar alphabet, storage, precision, row routing, and the outer multiplication
transfer. A partial positive balance is an allowance for unfinished work.
It cannot be reported as a kappa improvement on its own.

The most consequential change in this round is to retain different sizes
of productive output jobs and optimize their whole controller. The finite
compiler then completes the complementary axes and turns the mixed rule
into a single ordinary uniform supplier. On the BIT side, its entire finite
program is assembled before one cover and one common weight are introduced.
These are changes to the recursive family and the interface, beyond a local
operation-count search.

## Older structural precedents that informed the redesign

[Schonhage, *Partial and Total Matrix Multiplication* (1981)](https://doi.org/10.1137/0210032)
and [Peter Vrana, *Partial and weighted matrix multiplication* (2024)](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.15728v1)
are pertinent precedents for converting useful partial work into a larger
complete computation. Their theorems concern bilinear/tensor problems. We
use them as motivation, not as a black-box proof for this native transform
model. The actual transfer here is the integer source/exit recursion, with
every input's complementary axes paid explicitly.

The relevant representation-theoretic viewpoint is concrete: a change of
coordinate chart can preserve a completed job while changing its native
appearance. For BIT, routing the whole cover fiber `g -> gS` matches equal-rank
projectors before lowering to weighted leaves. For complex jobs, a monomial
address map must also retain the absolute quadratic phase. We test those
identities rather than relying on a geometric analogy.

## Iteration record

1. **Reconstruct the useful lost pieces.** The original base and all 3,214
   cumulative paths were authenticated. Complex H, dirty logging, complete
   helper boundaries, and the ordinary identity response were freshly rebuilt.
   The PR589 BIT word was regenerated byte-for-byte. New reconstructions have
   new provenance; missing historical files are not silently declared recovered.

2. **Make the cheap local primitives legal.** Power-of-two balancing reduced
   complex H's incremental scalar allowance from 4,336 to 3,504 while retaining
   eight rank-one calls. The embedded logger uses eight already present helper
   outputs and adds no recursive child, but leaves declared dirty outputs.
   A repeated identical future-query exchange restores 120 arbitrary fields in
   its finite check. Its main endpoint debt remains visible.

3. **Change what the outer unit earns.** Productive helpers carry their own
   independent input data. The first complete p2 identity cover has a mixed
   saving in `[0.001772952527592114765550198802,
   0.001772952527592114765550198803]`. This is a complete finite tariff; its
   asymptotic transfer was a separate task.

4. **Optimize the full controller.** A p2 partial-prefix menu reached about
   0.002013296. The p3 controller then retained four causal idle windows and
   discarded two others through a checked boundary factorization. Its complete
   complex mixed root is about 0.002305090195. The physical main contacts,
   helper owners, endpoint phases and scalar bill are independently audited.

5. **Try to escape the new local optimum.** Paired menus through p6, 64 p3
   single-field masks, and 123,904 commuting stage allocations were evaluated.
   The tested larger path controllers and asymmetric variants did not improve
   the selected complete p3 tariff. These are finite menu results, not global
   impossibility theorems.

6. **Close the changed recursion's interface.** Exact integer batches, causal
   parent/child ownership, source complements and owned-center lifetimes were
   implemented and independently challenged. A strict superpotential gives
   explicit compressed choices of the winning finite ranks. Complex semantic
   precision remains linear with newly derived constants. BIT delays its cover
   and normalization until internal jobs have all been compiled.

7. **Reprice the ordinary BIT conversion and outer computation.** The atom
   exponent was changed to 0.003, the generic-cover prime bound replaced, and
   new stock formulas supplied. The complete ordinary BIT saving exceeds 0.0027.
   The exact outer point at kappa 0.0023 has strict slack in every declared
   cost row. The written outer support audit retains its analytic and native
   hypotheses rather than treating this arithmetic as an unconditional theorem.

8. **Continue with a larger structural change.** The actual PR589 center
   producer/scatter matrices yield an F2 involution completion, replacing nine
   rank-seven calls by nine rank-one calls. Its full local native word passes
   both directions on all 661 arbitrary columns. Independently, the complex H
   branch found a required signed monomial correction to its owner maps.
   A further minimum invertible completion reduces the BIT correction to three
   rank-one calls. Direct whole-controller closure nevertheless has three
   endpoint obligations; the cheapest BIT completion still has rank-one
   deficit 21. The complex direct closure costs 672 rank-one requests.
   Subsequent coefficient-span and fresh-axis leakage probes reject two
   generic cancellation ideas, while leaving geometry-aware coding open.

## Negative results retained because they guide the next experiments

The actual PR589 register-sharing probe screened 96,141 helper pairs against
their full native frame timelines. Only five were even monotone candidates;
two introduce an invalid self-gate and the other three fail the source
response. The nine center producer functions and their consumer map each
have exact binary rank nine, excluding same-cut compression to eight in that
specific representation. The resulting decision was to change the local
response to an involution and resynthesize the caller.

For the complex H maps, preserving the polar form did not preserve the
Gaussian phase. A translation does not repair this discrepancy. A signed
monomial can, and the actual sign has to appear in both the native contact
lowering and the endpoint proof. This is a useful example of why a favorable
rank count cannot substitute for the emitted physical word.

## Reproducible evidence

The authoritative numeric values are the source-bound JSON receipts, not
rounded figures in this narrative. The new scope contains the reconstruction
sources, finite menus, negative witnesses, native records, exact moment
enclosures, independent phase/ownership/cover audits, and the written universal
arguments. `replay_critical.py` reruns the critical construction and audit chain
in a separately materialized checkout; cumulative byte application is checked
independently by the retained overlay tool.

No new source located in this search is evidence of global novelty or of an
official record. The proposed conditional construction requires external
mathematical review. It also uses enormous fixed templates; its exponent
improvement is an asymptotic claim, not a practical runtime benchmark.
