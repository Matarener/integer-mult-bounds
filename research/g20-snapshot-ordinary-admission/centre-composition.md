# A source-bound projected-input service at the last D25 stage

Dugongue, with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. This note gives a concrete informal construction using an actually supplied D25 forward service, the retained moving section and a paid late centre advance. It does not supply a funded complete Gaussian caller or κ ≥ 0.0035. No numerical evaluation, executable mathematics, solver, Lean check, replay or network search was performed.

## Actual predecessor chosen

Use the joined service with reader/service pair (s,t) = (0,2) from `research/centre-code-services`. Write

    G = diag(I_P,q_R),
    A = A_0,2,
    A_P = L_0 E_2,       A_R = (E_2)_R.

The literal supplied interface is

    (F1 x, F1 G w) → (F2 x, F2 G(w + A x)),

where F1 is FULL on the first seventeen axes and F2 on the first twenty-five axes. The old reader label does not change the input's actual physical progress. The native profile is

    p8 = 1392 H1 + 64 H2 + 48 H3 + 16 H8.

All 224 data inputs and the 416 outside values are arbitrary. This is the reviewed joined forward interface, not an assumed D25 lift of the different coalesced reciprocal local word. Its regular-cover barrier, DATA views, late query, inherited helper host and source all-width/phase conditions are retained.

Fix an old base-nine-axis response E0 = I + UV with B = VU invertible, for which the actual source moving section is supplied. One may use the retained rank-16 or rank-30 source with its own matrices and its own k-field centre. These arities are alternatives, not interchangeable readers. Let Q0 be its actual old diagonal line operator. Put

    J = Q0² U,
    D = B⁻¹ V Q0²,
    Π = I − JD.

Then DJ = I and Π² = Π, DΠ = 0. The construction uses the chosen old factor's actual matrices. B⁻¹ and every fixed-odd coefficient scan are supplied and paid by that source's ordinary coefficient model; B⁻¹ is not an unknown inverse oracle.

Q0² consists of simultaneous old-base Pauli translations. Scalar U,V and these translations act only on the completed old coordinates and field roles. They therefore commute with the last eight fresh-axis advance. They are ordinary at the declared old completed-frame cut. No current-stage routed matrix is transported backward through an incomplete frame by assumption.

## Construction and exact endpoint

Allocate k owned centre fields z, erase their entire payloads at entry, and give the whole zero fields the actual F1 header. They are not productive targets. A zero field needs no seventeen-axis Gaussian computation to acquire this header.

At the F1 cut, apply the supplied moving section

    S(J,D)(a,z) = ((I−JD)a + Jz, Da)

to the SOURCE bank and this centre. Since z = 0, the physical states become

    SOURCE = F1 Πx,
    centre = F1 Dx.

The source section is lossless on the joint state. The removed SOURCE mode is stored in the centre, rather than discarded or counted as a new independent input.

Run the actual joined (0,2) forward service on this projected SOURCE and the original arbitrary receiver bank. It gives

    SOURCE = F2 Πx,
    receiver = F2 G(w + AΠx).

Every child still receives the actual current function at its original native cut. Arbitrary-input correctness of the joined service applies to Πx, even though it is correlated with the centre. The entire regular cover completes before its stated late query, exactly as in the predecessor.

Advance each centre through the last eight fresh coordinates, using one H8 child per centre. Its physical state becomes F2 Dx. These requests are paid. This uses the fact that the old section commutes with this fresh advance; it does not ask a past record to pass through sixteen uncompleted coordinates for free.

Now apply the same moving section at the F2 cut. It sends the SOURCE/centre pair to

    SOURCE = F2 x,
    centre = 0.

Indeed the SOURCE component is Π²x + JDx = Πx + JDx = x, and the centre component is DΠx = 0. The receiver is untouched by this final section. Thus the complete new service is

    (F1 x, F1 G w; owned zero)
      → (F2 x, F2 G(w + AΠx); owned zero).             (1)

All original data information survives. On the retained data, (1) is an invertible triangular shear with its stated physical frames. Its response AΠ need not be invertible; invertibility of the complete SOURCE/receiver map does not require invertibility of its shear coefficient. This is an altered service, not a claim that AΠ equals A or the canonical controller coefficient.

## Literal implementation and full local price

The section has the explicit chronological implementation

    a += Jz;
    z -= Da;       using the updated a;
    a += Jz;
    z = −z.

The identity DJ = I proves this implements S(J,D) on arbitrary centre baselines. Both occurrences in the construction above therefore have an explicit finite ordinary implementation at the stated common frames. Every translation, real coefficient operation, fixed-odd scan and complete field pass is charged. For the rank-30 fixed-reader variant, the prior explicit recoder K = I + J(D_moving−D_fixed), together with its four borrowed-centre shears, can convert an existing fixed entry to this moving entry. Those ordinary recodings add no Gaussian request, but their primitive costs are included.

The complete recursive profile of (1) is exactly

    p8 + k H8.

For the sixteen-coordinate choice this adds sixteen H8 children; for the thirty-coordinate choice it adds thirty. There is no productive target credit for the centre. The k owned arrays, their initial erasures, final zero return, any conservative teardown scans and suspended-instance reserves are additional ordinary/storage costs. Their zero return follows from the displayed joint endpoint, not from assuming that a nonzero centre can be freely reheadered.

No part of the actual joined service's p8 invoice is removed. In particular, its original sixteen selected H8 carrier jobs remain paid unless a separate chronological proof deletes them. The additional k centre children are distinct from those existing selected data jobs. The chosen old section need not be the sixteen selected L_0 reader itself, and the different thirty-row fixed-reader inverse is never substituted for L_0.

## Relevance to a complete decoder

Equation (1) gives a concrete way to suppress an old centre-mode component from a later native coupling while restoring the complete SOURCE state and all new work. It replaces a historical carry by an entry/exit section pair and one fresh-eight-axis advance per mode. The modified coupling AΠ, its native price and its physical input/output types are explicit, so a proposed controller can use this service without an unspecified projection oracle.

This does not finish the controller. To replace a particular full-call occurrence, its caller must be shown to want AΠ or must pay the missing term AJD. A correction for three ordered stages cannot discard the other two residual factors. A final ordinary output query through F2 does not by itself supply an entry query through F1 for the current routed E2. The old section is available here because it is based on already completed old coordinates; this argument does not grant the same access to a new current-stage section.

For the reciprocal three-call route, the previously quoted 160 rank units after its 64-H1 subtotal cannot be assigned automatically to this construction. That subtotal belongs to a different local response and conditional route. The source-bound construction here uses the joined p8 forward, changes its coupling to AΠ and pays kH8; it has not been assembled into a complete independent-target histogram with positive moment at 0.0035.

There is also a concrete limit to endpoint prediction. After the first section has moved an arbitrary main mode into the centre, the centre contains independent information not recoverable from the projected SOURCE alone. In the final swap the known zero centre equation is DΠx = 0; its centre-to-centre diagonal is zero. Hence that equation does not itself permit deleting the k paid centre advances. This observation is used only to keep the positive construction's invoice honest.
