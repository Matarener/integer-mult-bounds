**Proof.** A monomial Clifford on either side preserves Gaussian rank. Common conjugation also preserves every actual scalar contact, copied value, owned zero and native physical warp. ## 3. Exact Pauli correction weakens literal occurrence matching

A bit pair need not produce the same logical native operator literally at every occurrence if their discrepancy can be corrected ordinarily. In particular, equality of the full signed operator is stronger than necessary when the symplectic actions already agree.

**Theorem 3. Pauli-corrected occurrence compiler.** Fix a physical source orientation and any specified right frames. For a physical native occurrence e, let its unchanged ordinary physical event be E_e, and let its source and target right frames be H_in,H_out. Its currently induced logical operator is

    L_e = H_out⁻¹ E_e H_in.

Choose C_j from a specified source-admitted dyadic literal Clifford reference occurrence for each positive formal native generator, using its literal inverse if the chosen reference is negative. The right frames are likewise source-admitted dyadic literal Clifford words. Suppose every positive occurrence of generator j has the same binary symplectic action as C_j, and every negative occurrence has that of C_j⁻¹. Then there are explicitly determined ordinary Pauli corrections at the occurrences making their logical actions exactly C_j and C_j⁻¹. The original universal constant endpoint is preserved under this corrected assignment, provided the matched scalar and boundary conditions also hold.

**Proof.** Write C_e for C_j or C_j⁻¹ according to the sign. The Clifford operator P_e=L_e C_e⁻¹ has identity symplectic action. It is therefore a scalar Pauli, including its actual phase. The source-admitted dyadic word hypothesis keeps this scalar in the admitted fourth roots of unity: a dyadic Gaussian rational of modulus one is one of 1, i, −1 and −i. No arbitrary Clifford global phase is introduced. Define the corrected physical event by

    E'_e = (H_out P_e⁻¹ H_out⁻¹) E_e.

Conjugation by any Clifford takes a Pauli to a Pauli. Thus the parenthesized factor is an ordinary translation/sign/phase operation on the complete field, even if H_out itself contains a Gaussian. Its explicit Pauli data are obtained from the specified Clifford conjugation; it is not executed as three separately charged operators. Direct substitution gives

    H_out⁻¹ E'_e H_in = P_e⁻¹ L_e = C_e.

All occurrences now have one exact consistent invertible assignment, including the inverse relation at negative occurrences. Apply the original free-word identity. This proof also retains the scalar phase omitted by a projective symplectic comparison. The finite ordinary correction and its address movement are charged. Exact one-column Pauli identities lift with their prescribed phase once per column. ∎

This theorem does not identify arbitrary different bit pairs. If their symplectic actions differ, a Pauli correction cannot repair that difference. It is useful for genuine phase or Pauli discrepancies, inverse orientation choices and representatives R versus R⁻¹ that have the same symplectic action. No actual nontrivial full-source colour assignment is asserted solely from this relaxation.
