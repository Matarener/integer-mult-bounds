## 4. Consequence for a proposed profitable projection

The construction closes the omitted AJD term exactly and fixes its available physical interface and full price. It does not give a saving relative to running the original joined forward once: that original service already costs p8, whereas the completed projection route costs p8+kH8+96H1 before any proven pruning.

A projection may still be useful if it makes actual requests in the native prefix unnecessary. For example a source-specific whole-zero relation could permit a literal query deletion, or an observer-free source-version identity could permit a proved fusion. Those changes must be established on the altered physical chronology. A lower scalar rank of AΠ, or the existence of a Woodbury formula, does not delete a native event. This note has not found such a deletion large enough to fund the extra requests.

The reciprocal three-call rank subtotal and its 64-H1 Ptot decoder are not an invoice for this service. The completed F2 code words used above do not supply earlier F1 current-code precoders. These distinctions remain part of the exact operator derivation.
