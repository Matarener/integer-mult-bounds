# Target-preserving snapshot fusion with a protected reader

## Scope

This is an informal, source-specific BIT construction. It proves seven additional query fusions in the retained G20 word, with arbitrary input data on every retained helper. It does not establish an ordinary exponent above 0.0035 or a new whole-package κ. The new mechanism is cancellation of a protected reader's cold-baseline contribution, followed by fusion using an already available SOURCE value.

The frozen public source is revision `5e0052ca0aa3036a3afa8b0438927377c8d97d53`, principally `research/bit-g20-nine-owned-relations/vendor/g20/provider/data/FORWARD.json.gz`. All record positions below refer to that literal forward word; the replacement program runs its inverse. The earlier [four partial retirements](partial-retirement.md) and [two simple snapshot fusions](snapshot-fusion.md) are predecessors, not seven additional discoveries attributed to this note. The ordinary component admission for the combined profile is supplied separately in [analytic funding](analytic-funding.md).

## Frame convention and the direct fusion identity

Work over the source BIT coefficient field, of characteristic two. Fix the source's actual maps

`A : ground → L`, `B : L → U`, `C = BA : ground → U`,

of ranks 1, 3, and 4 respectively. Here the notation means the concrete Gaussian transport in the source frame, rather than an arbitrary free linear isomorphism. Its physical/ROW realization must use the actual finite ordinary matcher for `C`; no cross-chart ordinary transport is granted for free.

Suppose the forward word contains `h ← A h`, then `h += x_L`, then `h ← B h`, with no external reader or other change to `h` between the line injection and the last operation. Its inverse sends

`h_U ↦ A⁻¹(B⁻¹ h_U + x_L) = C⁻¹(h_U + x_U)`,

where `x_U = B x_L`. Thus, in the inverse, snapshot the actual SOURCE in frame `U` before that SOURCE leaves `U`; replace the helper's old rank-3 return by an addition of that snapshot and one rank-4 return. Omit the old line addition and old rank-1 return. The helper rejoins its exact former ground value. This identity holds for arbitrary `h_U`; its productive target is retained. The snapshot is stored in a fresh owned field and cleared when its value has been copied. It supplies no productive target credit.

## Protected-reader cancellation theorem

The direct identity extends to a reader which would otherwise prohibit fusion. Let the relevant forward operations have the following order:

1. At ground, `r += h`.
2. On `h`, the rank-1 transport to `L`, followed by `h += x_L`.
3. On `r`, its existing rank-1 transport to the same `L`, followed by `r += h`.
4. On `h`, its existing rank-3 transport to `U`.

Assume that the two displayed additions are the only uses or changes of `r` between their cuts, apart from its displayed transport, and that `h` has no intervening outgoing read after its line injection except the displayed read by `r`. The actual source checks below establish these conditions. Other operations on other fields are unrestricted.

Let the initial ground values be `h₀` and `r₀`. After the first three steps, the recipient is

`A(r₀ + h₀) + (A h₀ + x_L) = A r₀ + x_L`.

Consequently the two reads of the helper may both be deleted and replaced by the single addition `r += x_L` after the recipient's existing transport. The helper remains arbitrary and is now unobserved within its rank-1/rank-3 window. Apply the direct fusion identity to that helper.

For clarity, the inverse implementation needs only one new snapshot, at `U`. At the old helper rank-3 return, add the saved `x_U` and take the rank-4 return. The old proper recipient/helper addition and the old cold ground addition are omitted. Keep the recipient at `L` until the old SOURCE returns to `L`, add its current `x_L`, and then perform the recipient's old rank-1 return. The recipient has no intermediate observer, so delaying this one existing transport is legal. If the SOURCE already has frame `L` at the former proper read, no delay is needed. All SOURCE instructions retain their old order and values.

Equivalently, with inverse inputs `f` on the recipient line and `h_U` on the helper,

`A⁻¹(f + B⁻¹ h_U) + A⁻¹(B⁻¹ h_U + x_L) = A⁻¹(f + x_L)`.

The rewritten word therefore rejoins the complete former state at the cold-read boundary, for every input and every physical width. This is not a zero-input retirement and does not debit a full helper target. It is a two-read cancellation using the actual characteristic-two source grammar.

## Physical admission and simultaneous composition

The source's finite matchers already include the ground-to-upper rank-4 geometries at 506, 508, and 509, used in the earlier partial-helper requests. Frame 507 is the actual existing rank-4 request of G20 helper 464, not a newly assumed common-frame isomorphism. The same matcher and its ordinary finite coefficient/ROW realization are reused. Each fused request is a genuine rank-4 child, charged at its full physical tariff. Snapshot addition and clearing are ordinary finite operations at a common physical frame; they are not Gaussian oracle calls.

All seven new snapshots are kept in separate owned fields. Their lifetimes can overlap. The proof uses seven fields rather than claiming a single reusable slot. The helper/recipient pairs are disjoint. Where one changed helper later interacts with another at `U`, the inverse performs that interaction before either fused return; the old value is restored before every earlier reader. Every SOURCE remains unchanged. Therefore the exact join identities compose with one another, with the earlier two simple fusions, and with the four previously retired partial helpers. In particular, the nine existing COPY captures on owners 469–477 retain their exact former operands.

The arbitrary full-helper baselines are essential: the new six cancellation windows remove their unwanted contribution from the recipient by a pair of opposite occurrences, rather than by declaring that contribution zero. Their full productive targets remain in the target histogram. The existing partial retirements have a different contract and retain their previously charged target debit.

## Exact complete profile change

Write `H_k` for one paid or productive rank-`k` occurrence as appropriate, and set `g(C)=1+3^(1−C)−4^(1−C)`. Each additional fusion changes the paid histogram by `−H₁−H₃+H₄` and leaves the productive histogram unchanged.

Combining the seven new fusions with the preceding two snapshot fusions and four partial retirements gives the following complete 18-call profile relative to PR 5512:

`P_new = P_5512 − 234 H₁ − 234 H₃ + 162 H₄`,

`T_new = T_5512 − 72 H₄`.

Thus the changed paid coefficients are `P₁ = 24993`, `P₃ = 1152`, and `P₄ = 882`; every other paid coefficient is unchanged. Productive rank 4 is removed; all other productive coefficients are unchanged. The original-field count is 11166. The conservative local owned bank has 24 fields: the prior 11, the four retired partial helpers, and nine distinct SOURCE snapshots. No snapshot is counted as a productive original.

For `t=1−C`, the exact controller moment improvement is

`F_new(C) − F_5512(C) = 234 g(C)`.

For every `0<C<1`, this is strictly positive: concavity gives `4^t−3^t < t`, hence `g(C)>C>0`. This proves a genuine source-specific paid improvement, rather than an assumption about a future gadget. It does not by itself certify a root above 0.0035: that requires a sufficient analytic controller inequality and the full ordinary admission with the increased owned bank. No newly evaluated decimal bound is asserted here.
