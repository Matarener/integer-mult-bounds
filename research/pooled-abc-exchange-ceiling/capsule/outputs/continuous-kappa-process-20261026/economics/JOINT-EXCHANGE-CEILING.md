of their rank inventories across all 18 calls:

    A: H1 + H5 -> H6,
    B: 2H2 -> H1 + H3,

A is the independently reviewed PR5545 change; B and C are the two new
PR5551 changes above. This is an optimistic accounting relaxation: there is
no assertion that all requested contacts exist, are compatible, preserve
physical chronology or can be implemented free of restoration. We grant
them anyway. We even allow reverse exchanges and arbitrary sequencing.
First let integers a,b,c denote their signed counts for one local invoice;
then relax these to real averages and optimize the complete pooled invoice.
The final histogram must be nonnegative. Any further recursive restoration debit or target loss
would decrease the raw margin and is set to zero in this optimistic bound.

    p1 = 2428-a+b+c,

    a <= 107+c,    b <= floor((557-c)/2),    c <= 112.

Thus newly created H5 jobs can be reused by A. Bounding the three edit

## 3. Exact optimal inventories throughout 0<C<1

Let the positive savings per exchange be

    gA = 1 + H5 - H6,
    gB = 2H2 - 1 - H3,
    gC = H2 + H4 - 1 - H5.

Strict concavity of x^t gives gA,gB,gC>0. For fixed c, the relaxed savings
are at most

    F(c) = (107+c)gA + floor((557-c)/2)gB + c gC.

This is valid even when a,b,c are signed, because the three gains are
positive and the displayed inventory inequalities bound their coefficients.
As integer c increases by one, F gains either gA+gC or gA+gC-gB. Both are
strictly positive. The first equals H2+H4-H6. For the second,

    gA+gC-gB = 1+H3+H4-H2-H6 > 0:

the decreasing derivative of x^t gives H3-H1 > H6-H4, and 2H1>H2.
Consequently F is increasing all the way to c=112, and the unique optimal
integer counts for a single local invoice are

    (a,b,c) = (219,222,112).

They are feasible in the histogram relaxation: apply all 112 C exchanges,
then all 222 B exchanges, then all 219 A exchanges. There is enough H1 for
the last stage. The optimal local bill is

    P* = 2543H1 + H2 + 611H3 + 236H6 + 13H7 + H9.

It has 3,405 jobs and the unchanged local rank mass 5,894. Starting from
PR5551, one B and one C have already occurred; the remaining optimal counts
are (219,221,111) and lead to exactly the same P*. The optimum is over the
defined single-invoice integer family only, with every physical constraint
relaxed. Applying it independently to each of 18 calls leaves 18 H2 jobs.

Now grant free pooling between all 18 calls, or more generally real-valued
averaged counts. The constraints still read a<=107+c, b<=(557-c)/2, c<=112.
The objective after saturating the first two bounds has derivative
gA+gC-gB/2>0 with respect to c, by the stronger inequality already proved.
Its exact optimum is therefore

    (a,b,c) = (219,445/2,112).

The half is only an averaged accounting relaxation, not a fractional
physical field. Across the actual 18 calls the optimal total counts are
the integers

    (A,B,C) = (3942,4005,2016).

These are feasible in the pooled histogram relaxation: apply 2016 C, then
4005 B, then 3942 A. Relative to the separate integer-local optimum, pooling
allows exactly 9 extra B exchanges. The averaged optimal local profile is

    (5087/2)H1 + (1223/2)H3 +236H6 +13H7 +H9.

It has the same job count 3405 and rank mass 5894. No construction of a
pooled word is asserted. This stronger relaxation also covers heterogeneous
choices between calls and rational mixtures; it is the one used below.

## 5. A rational target-point certificate without a price-engine run

The retained qualified artifact's `C_probe` is `33/10000`, its
`reviewed_head` is `18968423711fd902aaee1099979497d55603e1dc`, and its
`current_moment_at_target.upper_decimal_outward` is the exact rational decimal
`-64.2349800489874753468185`. Let M0(t) denote the PR5545 complete moment
at power t, so the retained interval gives M0(1-33/10000)<-64. This uses its inspectable
signed-moment certificate, not an inference from its claimed kappa. Also

    M*(1-C) = M0(1-C) +18(219gA(C)+(445/2)gB(C)+112gC(C)).

Each g is concave as a function of C on [0,1] and vanishes at zero. For gA,
the second derivative is 5^t(log5)^2-6^t(log6)^2<0. For gB it is
2*2^t(log2)^2-3^t(log3)^2<0, since (log3)^2>2(log2)^2. For gC it is
2^t(log2)^2+4^t(log4)^2-5^t(log5)^2<0, since
(log5)^2>5(log2)^2 and 2^t<=4^t<=5^t. The elementary comparisons follow
already from log2<.7, log3>1 and log5>1.6.

Thus each gain is bounded above by its tangent at zero. The standard rational
log enclosures

    .6931 < log2 < .6932,
    1.0986 < log3 < 1.0987,
    1.6094 < log5 < 1.6095

give

    gA(C) <= C(6log6-5log5) < (2705/1000)C,
    gB(C) <= C(3log3-4log2) < (524/1000)C,
    gC(C) <= C(5log5-10log2) < (1117/1000)C.

For a finite exact-rational way to check these log brackets without importing
an engine, put z=(n-1)/(n+1) and use

    L_N(n)=2 sum[j=0..N-1] z^(2j+1)/(2j+1),
    L_N(n) < log n < L_N(n)+2z^(2N+1)/((2N+1)(1-z^2)).

N=32 is more than sufficient for n=2,3,5 and the coarse displayed brackets.
The separate reviewer has checked these finite sums/tails and brackets using
exact rational arithmetic without a source or price-engine import. No new
interval execution occurred in this design lane.

Using only rational arithmetic after those bounds,

    M*(1-33/10000)
      < -64 + 18*(33/10000)*(219*2705+(445/2)*524+112*1117)/1000
      = -64 + 495448866/10000000
      = -144551134/10000000
      = -14.4551134 < 0.

For comparison, the separate-integer-local upper bound is -14.4706762.
Granting the nine extra pooled B exchanges relaxes it by at most .0155628.

This is deliberately a conservative analytic upper bound, not a newly
computed narrow enclosure or raw-crossing approximation. Even granting all
contacts and every restoration for free, the family misses the necessary
raw margin by more than 14.45 H1 price units at C=.0033. Nonnegative
compiler reserve, extra paid work or lost targets cannot repair it within
the retained architecture.

## 6. Larger-C exclusion and uniqueness

Let N(t)=M*(t)/9^t, for 0<=t<=1. Among the low-rank positive terms, only
ranks 4,5,7,8 have negative derivative after this normalization. Their total
adverse derivative magnitude is less than

    (918+648+198+2196)log9 = 3960log9.

The negative rank-one term alone contributes at least

    (47343/9)log9 > 3960log9.

All other negative low-rank terms have nonnegative derivative; the rank-nine
term is constant. For each high pair, the normalized derivative is
x^t log x evaluated at x=(r+1)/9 minus its value at x=r/9. It is positive
because r>=16 and x^t log x is increasing for x>1, t>=0. The rank145 term
also has positive derivative. Hence N is strictly increasing throughout
[0,1]. Its endpoints are

    N(0) = 12814-65970 = -53156,
    N(1) = 760/9 > 0.

There is a unique raw crossing C*=1-t*. Section 5 shows C*<.0033, and the
moment remains negative for every .0033<=C<1. Every other member of the
defined family has no larger moment at each C. No numerical value for C*
is claimed. This is a scoped raw ceiling, not a P104 or physical optimum.

2. The inventory optimization, including H5 recycling, signed reverse

**Stop:** a .0033 nomination whose complete changed bill is still in this
A/B/C family, with no new productive targets or changed nonlocal lines,
fails before physical construction. A real smaller funded improvement may
still be useful and should be labelled by its own threshold.

**Pivot:** require one actual source-bound paid-path change outside the
three-generator family. The pooled relaxed optimum exhausts H2, H4 and H5;
it leaves 11,007 H3 jobs across the caller, or 611.5 per-call average. A proposed method that changes that H3
inventory, changes higher-rank paths, introduces independently productive
targets or changes the caller is outside the bound. A histogram pairing of
independent H3 inputs does not implement a Gaussian coalescence. Before any
next scan or construction, name the literal role(s), event interval, all
protected scalar reads, legal common frames, restored exits and complete
charged delta. Bind the intervention to source ownership and test exactly
that chronological identity, including all helper inputs, before assigning
it an aggregate saving. No such new physical object is nominated in this
note, and no search of the source author's active contacts was performed.

The previously checked unrestricted rank-mass relaxation has positive raw
budget at .0033, so this result does not justify abandoning P104. It provides
the narrower decision: arbitrary repetition or combination of the currently
demonstrated three economic exchanges is insufficient for this target.
