## 2. The minimum is a ratio, over all positive n

Define D_n=E_(n+1)-E_n and J_n=nE_(n+1)-(n+1)E_n.
The local nL cancels identically in J_n. Thus compatible local changes
cannot change J's minimizer at fixed exponent and path geometry.

PR5554 section2 proves D_(n+1)>D_n for every integer n>=0, including
the n2/n3 junction. Its section3 therefore gives

    J_(n+1)-J_n=(n+1)(D_(n+1)-D_n)>0.

Furthermore E_(n+1)/(n+1)-E_n/n=J_n/[n(n+1)] for n>=1.
Consequently strict J16<0<J17 selects n17 as the unique ratio minimum
over all positive n. The exact checker confirms those signs separately at

    B(C)=min(n>=1)E_n/n

## 3. One isolated nested tail, with lost target credit charged

Suppose exactly one helper's old monotone terminal climb is shortened.

new final f and old final b. No other productive target, paid path, controller

The ideal local gain before any new holder/cleanup/restoration charge is

    G=H_(b-a)-H_(f-a)-H_(b-e)+H_(f-e).

    G<=(b-f)-H_n+(f-e)=n-H_n<=9-H9.

## 4. Scope and evidence boundary

This is a two-point exclusion. No larger-C theorem for the changed family
is established. Several jointly changed tails, changed starts/intermediate
paths, new productive targets, correlated helpers, other fusions or a new
controller law require their own complete signed bill and argument.
No multi-tail count, physical candidate inventory or compatibility theorem
is part of this package.

The original normal/optimized pair and complete output equality are retained.
The eleven finite controls have the distinct strengths described in REVIEW:
five source/accounting mutations, two neighboring-count predicate checks,
four objective/sign sanity checks. They do not replace analytic proof or
provide comprehensive mutant interval engines.

Native all-width/phase semantics, arbitrary-input endpoints, owned lifetime
legality, ordinary work and coefficient/precision/tape accounting, compiler
strictification and all54 strict plus4 functional outer conditions remain
outside this result. The source BIT budget .0034 is not a funding proof.
The source's5894 growth description versus6013 paid-rank census remains
an explained gap, not an asserted disproof of its growth theorem.

The contribution credits the existing PR5554 frontier and all source
predecessors listed in README. Independent agent review is included; human
or formal acceptance is not claimed. No selected kappa changes.
