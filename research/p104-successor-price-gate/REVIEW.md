`14f28d0358c11d55683984f1037c23d58f7d8c9d69b8e23503545b0519246ab4`.

- `ln_unit` uses the first24 positive terms of the atanh series and bounds

  is [0,1/3]. Integer rank normalization reduces log r to this domain plus
  an integer multiple of the similarly enclosed log2.
- The degree11 and12 partial sums for exp(-x) enclose it from below and
  above for0<=x<1, by the alternating-series theorem. The engine checks that
  domain. To enclose r^(1-C), it uses the upper log endpoint for the lower
  exponential bound and the lower log endpoint for the upper bound.
- `evaluate` selects the outward endpoint separately for positive and
  negative integer coefficients. Dividing by positive n preserves order.
  Exact Fraction arithmetic accepts all signs; Decimal is used only to
  print floor/ceiling-rounded interval endpoints.

No float tolerance or sampled approximation enters acceptance. The largest
rank needed by the gate is145, and the actual tested inputs are within the
checked exponential domain. The numerical enclosures are much narrower than
the signs used, but their narrowness is not a substitute for these domain
and rounding arguments.
