The source entry is the literal chronological request together with its current

must be executed. Numerical admission cannot move a dirty acquisition to a
later prefix or turn a COPY SOURCE observation into equality of all DIRTY rows.

There are different quantities here:

* 49,347 and 1,123,650 are cancellation-free integer row coefficients; the
  reverse change and +3 local ADDs are reused from #4067/#4147.
* The 192 and 512 bounds are integer prefix/finite-invoice envelopes. Their
  slack is proved by exact inequalities below. They are not a new rounding
  grid, floating-point error analysis, or conditioning bound.
* `w` is the original root's address/ring precision. Ring arithmetic and unit

  a descendant's width. Existing precision/analytic interfaces remain explicit.
* `Q` is the source-code field width, which affects actual serialized Programs,
  raw fields, scans, parking, and storage. It is distinct from row coefficients.
* Compatible row stock and logical live symbols are different from total

## 3. Per-request bad-class admission, including correlated histories

Take any fresh MOVE/COPY/IDLE request of rank 1<=r<=42. The compiler establishes
a dyadic idempotent projector P0 with characteristic polynomial
t^(8-r)(t-1)^r for each eight-dimensional block, and an invertible actual
chart with dyadic entries and determinant ±1. Reducing the polynomial identity
modulo any odd q proves that each projector has the prescribed rank, since an idempotent
is diagonalizable with distinct roots 0 and 1. Over the local ring Z/q^w its
image and kernel are finite free modules (direct summands of the free module),
so an invertible splitting basis exists at that parameter. We do not claim a
single rational rank minor is a unit for all odd primes; such a minor can have
an odd numerator. The code checks idempotence and characteristic polynomial
over Q, and uses the actual runtime split basis at the compiled parameter.
IDLE bases are coordinate projectors on their actual distinct axes (including
ranks 14 and 42), so their rank/idempotence proof is immediate from those axes;
the eight-dimensional characteristic-polynomial test covers local frame bases.
The ambient 64-dimensional request rank is the resulting r. For COPY the actual chart is the
already aligned one: applying the external owner chart twice is invalid.

For a complete GL64 low cover, multiply g by that fixed invertible chart and
split basis. This is a bijection on GL64(F_q) and on the high lifts. In these
coordinates the projector is Q=G E_r G^-1. The numerical context may conjugate
Q by an arbitrary diagonal unit matrix H obtained from its exact ancestry.
For every principal index set I,

    det((H Q H^-1)[I,I]) = det(Q[I,I]).

This matches the actual source: `Matrices.prepare` forms the unweighted
projector from `(low+q*high)*fixed_preparation`; `selected_page` selects its
fixed low branch; `Page.coefficient` decorates head/shear coefficients by ratios
of incoming units, leaves tail coefficients unchanged, and uses inverse units
for cross coefficients. `Callback.weight` constructs those units from root
units, good pivots, or fallback unit diagonals and checked ratios. Thus its
good/fallback classification is unchanged by the ancestral coefficients,
including correlated coefficients and repeated prefixes. It depends only on the
low G, never on a particular high lift, frozen ordinal or payload bit.

For I={0,...,k-1}, 1<=k<=r, complementary minors give the polynomial identity

    det(G) det(Q[I,I])
      = sum_(J subset [r], |J|=k) (-1)^(sum I + sum J)
           det G[I,J] det G[I^c,J^c] = F_k(G).

Each F_k has degree <=64 and F_k(I_64)=1 in every residue field. Their product
is nonzero, with degree <=64r. The finite-field polynomial zero bound and
Pr(GL64)>1-1/(q-1) give

    delta_r <= min(1, 64*r*(q-1)/(q*(q-2))).

This applies to both reflected MOVE orientations, each actual COPY and every
pooled IDLE, by their respective source-bound split witnesses. There is no
assumption that one matrix is good for every label. We sum each edge's cost
with its own deterministic low-class fraction; linearity of this finite sum
needs no independence between requests or ancestral weights.

For q>2^80 and r<=42, delta_r<2689/q<10^-16. The retained exact primitive
fallback ceiling 6*128*127+3*128+6*127=98,682 is below 32*64^2=131,072.
(The completed caller's 49,911 count is an even smaller inherited ceiling;
we do not take a second saving.) Every one of the 903,456 requests retains
131,072 rank-one fallback allowances, total 118,417,784,832. Therefore the
old surcharge epsilon*131072*E has its actual per-edge coverage premise for
this freshly compiled word **under the completed fallback/weighted-child
contracts**, rather than because E happened not to change. General source
refinement, unit-weight preservation and completed fallback execution remain
explicit inherited premises. A q=3 physical fixture cannot establish this
q>2^80 numerical admission and the numerical guard rejects it.
