## Remaining whole-construction inequality

Write R_X for the displayed recursive tariff sum, S for the scalar tariff,
Pi_X for complete source/coefficient/child preparation and returned-output
work, G_X for physical gather/route/scatter and primitive lowering, C_X for
checkpoint/continuation/disposal work, and F_X for finite table setup and stock
charges not already in those terms. A usable same-contract improvement needs

    R_C - R_D > 864*S + (Pi_D-Pi_C) + (G_D-G_C)
                         + (C_D-C_C) + (F_D-F_C),

with the same original-root numerical and consumer contract on both sides.
The local executed differences and coefficientwise reserve results instantiate
specific pieces of this bill; they do not assign zero to the rest or subtract
unrelated upper bounds. In particular, fixed-tape metadata/allocator lowering,
absolute pooled routing/setup, full residual execution and lifetime backing
remain inherited or unresolved as stated. This package targets a substantial
**enabling integration**, not a demonstrated global paid physical speedup.
The selected supplier remains complex-bound; no selected kappa or outer transfer
is repriced.
