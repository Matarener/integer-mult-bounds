Apache-2.0. Substantial OpenAI Codex assistance.

The main arithmetic finding is conditional: deleting one rank-seven COPY
query in each of the 288 local invocations leaves more than
`4.977907425421188143929582511024` units of additional query moment per
invocation at the necessary primitive endpoint for a 10% κ improvement.
The existing coded-source word, with every deleted helper's owner
obligation returned, leaves more than
`58.382679011994524685794411232083` units per invocation. Those numbers
include the inherited child surcharge.

The latter is an allowance for a **paid** decoder and all additional
recursive children, conditional on the stated owner relaxation. It is not
a zero-price decoder, an attained chronological caller, or a new κ.
The currently selected complex endpoint still precludes the 10% target.

All displayed decimals denote exact terminating rationals. The complete
rational endpoints, adjacent signs and source hashes are in `BUDGET.json`.

## 5. Priced decoder budgets and the limits of rank mass

For extra recursive children of ranks `1<=r<=63`, an arbitrary
nonnegative histogram `D` of total rank mass `L=sum rD(r)` obeys

\[
\sum_rD(r)\chi_{c_*}(r)
\le L\,64^{c_*}\left(1+\frac{32\cdot64^2}{10^{16}}\right)
=L\,\chi_{c_*}(1). \tag{12}
\]

Indeed `Phi(r)/r <= 64^c` and `sum D(r)<=L`. Thus (12) gives a
shape-independent sufficient decoder allowance. The exact adjacent
integer checks give:

| Displayed candidate | Arbitrary extra rank mass per local call | Maximum extra children per call at rank 7 | At rank 14 | At rank 28 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| One uniform COPY7 deletion | 4 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Coded 429 full helpers | 57 | 8 | 4 | 2 |
| Conditional coded 424 full + 5 partial | 58 | 8 | 4 | 2 |

For the 429-helper route, 58 rank-one extra queries per call fail the
displayed-primal screen, but a differently shaped histogram of mass 58
may pass. An actual decoder must be evaluated by its full histogram.
If a decoder occurs only once per replica rather than once per local
call, the same *global* budget allows 521 extra rank-one children per
replica for (8), or 523 for conditional (10). These are maximum counts
in the declared cost model, not physical decoder constructions.

For pure deletion with no added children, the saved moment per rank unit
decreases with the removed rank. Across ranks 1 through 63, the
charged baseline deficit therefore yields:

\[
586.092811777934209119699677053780
<\frac{B_{G_0}(c_*)-64W}{\chi_{c_*}(1)}
<586.092811777934209119699677053781,
\]

\[
590.586005037430702253498243696120
<\frac{B_{G_0}(c_*)-64W}{\chi_{c_*}(63)/63}
<590.586005037430702253498243696121.
\]

So 587 units is the minimum deleted rank mass in the most favorable
pure-deletion shape (587 rank-one children), while 591 units suffice
for every pure-deletion shape of ranks at most 63. COPY7-only deletion
is quantized to 85 children and 595 mass. These facts are planning
thresholds; arbitrary rank-one queries are not assumed deletable.

There is **no equivalent criterion based solely on net mass** when a
proposal both removes and adds children. Equal mass can have different
moments. Use (5), (8), or (10) with the complete added histogram.

## 6. Complete consequence of the direct-reader COPY mass floor

The separate construction audit supplies the following premise for its
declared direct source-query/reader factorization class: there are at
least nine paid COPY children, each rank belongs to `{6,7,8}`, and their
total rank mass is at least 63. The historical P111 label-decoder
63-floor is due to Russell Seligmann (PR757, head `e2ec7d7`) and Cody
Harris (PR761, head `2877587`), as credited in the preserved
`inputs/DIRECT-FLOOR-PRIOR-README.md`. We do not claim that mass floor
as new or extend its operator scope. This section gives its complete
caller-moment consequence under the stated premise.

For every `0<b<1`, the least possible tariff in the larger histogram
class `n>=9`, ranks in `{6,7,8}`, mass at least 63, is

\[
4\chi_b(6)+\chi_b(7)+4\chi_b(8). \tag{13}
\]

Here is a written proof covering every child count. For `n=9` or 10,
an optimizer has mass exactly 63: otherwise a child above rank six can
be reduced by one until that mass is reached. Strict concavity gives
`Phi(6)+Phi(8)<2Phi(7)`, so the number of rank-seven children is
minimized, giving `4e6+e7+4e8` for nine children and `8e6+e7+e8`
for ten. The ten-child raw tariff exceeds (13) by
`4Phi(6)-3Phi(8)>0`, because `(8/6)^(1-b)<4/3`.
It also has one extra surcharge.

For `n>=11`, its raw tariff is at least `11Phi(6)`. Since
`Phi(7)<(7/6)Phi(6)` and `Phi(8)<(8/6)Phi(6)`,
the right side of (13), without surcharge, is less than
`(4+39/6)Phi(6)=(21/2)Phi(6)<11Phi(6)`.
The surcharge comparison again favors nine children. This proves (13)
throughout `0<b<1`. The checker additionally enumerates every feasible
nine- and ten-child histogram at `c_*` and checks the infinite
`n>=11` tail bound.

Replacing the original nine rank-seven COPY children with the
optimistic profile in (13) changes each local histogram by

\[
4e_6-8e_7+4e_8.
\]

It preserves rank mass and child count. Its raw saving per local call
at `c_*` lies in

\[
(0.001059310845595607293914001593,\
 0.001059310845595607293914001594).
\]

Even after applying that favorable change to all 288 calls and using
the optimal raw owner relaxation, the raw target excess lies in

\[
(590.298056111490115249043858634130,\
 590.298056111490115249043858634131).
\]

Granting every exceptional packet free leaves an excess greater than
`590.298056111308193819238247485118`. Therefore this entire direct
factorization class cannot reach the 10% target under the fixed helper,
public-bin and stock premises, even with no new decoder or other costs.
This obstruction is conditional on that class; it does not exclude a
changed factory, source endpoint, common cut, helper contract or caller.

The receipt includes separate charged and free-bad root brackets.
The free-bad upper endpoint is the favorable necessary ceiling; a
charged displayed-primal root is not mistaken for a universally optimal
charged LP value.
