# Independent review of the actual COPY path

The postscatter obstruction is valid in its stated model. It is stronger than
the finite cut search: any finite collection of independent, exact copies of
one unchanged actual retained total, with closed nested private MOVE walks and
monotone public propagation, has total query rank at least seven. The original
COPY7 already has that rank. The coded R429 word changes the endpoint; direct
substitution in the nine-stage native caller has an exact public TAIL defect.
Neither result excludes a changed factory, mixed new query totals, or a caller
with additional paid native operations.

This review separates three kinds of evidence:

- `review_check.py` independently checks finite source facts, exact witnesses,
  supplied dual separators, and several decisive moment inequalities.
- The dimension argument for arbitrarily many branches, the all-count
  histogram minimization, and the native stage identities are written proofs.
- No new physical compiler, private-MOVE interface, complete changed owner
  schedule, precision bound, or joint multiplication theorem is admitted.

The historical 63-unit direct label-decoder floor is prior work of Russell
Seligmann (PR757) and Cody Harris (PR761), under their stated decoder model.
The present reconstruction binds that fact to the actual retained totals and
prices its complete caller consequence. It is not a new 63-unit floor.

## Independent source reconstruction

The checker executes no producer module and reads no producer receipt as an
oracle. Its inputs are fixed compressed word/geometry bytes and the selected
assembly record, individually authenticated by SHA256. It represents binary
rows by sets and symmetric difference; rational ranks and containment use
primitive integer elimination. The B and late-factory audits propagate only
the specified scalar dual observables, rather than reconstructing the
producer's entire influence span.

It covers all 660 ordinary input columns of the retained word and all 651
columns of the coded word, in both orientations. Helpers are independent
arbitrary inputs. Every private lifetime compares its complete snapshot
against the same source at ERASE. Frame replay separately checks every
monotone MOVE, every common-frame ADD, COPY source immutability, and all
physical endpoint labels.

The actual snapshots are `q_j = R_j x + h_(471+j)`. R, K, and the nine
snapshots have rank nine. Removing the private scatter reads and replaying the
complete word produces exactly `K q` on the targets and zero change on every
other persistent row. Thus the required replacement vector is the actual
column `k_j`; the argument does not infer it from a clean-helper trial.

All 636 supplied frame root sets equal their exact rational closures. The
nine centre frames have dimension seven. For every j, roots of ports in
`support(k_j)` have rank eight, and their projections onto the actual centre
hyperplane have rank seven. These finite facts supply the geometric

The coded endpoint is independently recovered as

## Why the finite-branch theorem is complete in its model

Fix a centre hyperplane F of dimension seven. Branch i copies the same
unchanged q into `G_i subset F` at rank cost
`r_i=7-dim(G_i)`. It carries q through a closed sequence of nested private
frames, reads at common frames, and returns to its original G_i for exact
ERASE. Let m_i be the sum of every upward and downward MOVE rank, and L_i
the intersection of all visited frames.

A downward edge of rank d removes at most d dimensions from the running
intersection. An upward edge removes none. Closure makes the total downward
rank `m_i/2`, so

```
L_i subset F,       dim(L_i) >= 7-r_i-m_i/2.
```

After a private read, the affected public field has a frame containing L_i.
Every subsequent upward MOVE and common-frame ADD preserves this support
condition along each coefficient path. A final target p can therefore receive
a nonzero contribution from branch i only when its cap `u_p^perp` contains
L_i. Cancellation can remove such contributions, but cannot create an output
outside this permitted support.

Projecting those recipient roots onto F confines their span to
`F intersect L_i^perp`, of dimension at most `r_i+m_i/2`. The support of the
sum of all branches is contained in the union of their supports. The actual
required roots project onto all seven dimensions of F. Consequently

```
7 <= sum_i (r_i+m_i/2) <= sum_i (r_i+m_i).
```

Direct reads of the original q at F contribute only roots in `F^perp`, whose
projection onto F is zero. They do not evade the argument. For one branch,
the unprojected rank-eight support forces `L=0`, yielding the stronger
`r+m >= 14-r`.

The proof covers arbitrary real private subspaces and any finite number of
independent branches; it does not depend on the original 636-frame catalogue
or forbid a private promotion merely because the old ABI lacks it. It does
require the fixed logical-q chart, nested MOVE tariff, unchanged copied
source, closed return, common-frame scalar reads, and the absence of new
downstream public COPY/downward-query events. Mixing privately different
query totals, changing the factory, or introducing new paid native exchanges
requires a new argument. The rational/real statement is not a uniform claim
about rank under every odd-characteristic specialization.

For `0<b<1`, the child cost is proportional to `r^(1-b)`, which is increasing
and subadditive. Therefore any positive child ranks with total at least seven
cost at least `Phi_b(7)`. A nonzero implementation also has at least one child
surcharge. Under the unchanged remaining inventory, this class cannot lower
the replaced COPY's complete query moment.

## Finite supplements and their limits

The checker tests all 1,700 cuts and all 660 ordinary receiver fields in the
postscatter B interval: **1,122,000 cut/receiver pairs**. At a cut it grants
early splitting of the next original MOVE after the last use at the old frame.
For each receiver it independently permits any real `G subset F_j` of
dimension at least three, even allowing a different G at each insertion.
Exact frame closure makes the root-set containment tests equivalent to
rational subspace containment. The dimension of `F_j intersect F_next` is
exactly `dim(F_next)` or one less because F_j is a hyperplane.

The nine terminal duals

{106,108}, {106,108}, {106,108}, {107}

annihilate every permitted insertion and detect the corresponding missing

The nine stronger late-factory duals are also independently checked for
replacement COPY rank at most four on all 254 completed-centre cuts before
the first COPY, including the eight
remaining COPYs in each changed suffix. The oracle treats COPY and ERASE

checks cover arbitrary real lower frames of dimension at least three in
the actual centre hyperplane;
the fixed catalogue cases are subcases. They do not enumerate arbitrary
earlier factory modifications or private frame walks across later COPYs.

The full A-acquisition census is source-reviewed, but is not independently
rerun by this checker. Its stated limitation to source-to-helper ADDs at
existing common frames, with the helper network fixed, is essential.
The supplementary fixed high-frame search is also producer evidence. Its
free union has full output rank and admits every required pattern. That

For the credited direct-scatter result, the checker independently verifies

argument and ten or more terms by the minor, giving the stated 63 floor in
the direct `span(R)`/`image(K)` factorization class. It is not a floor for
arbitrary routed or intermediate readers.

## Native R429 caller and repairs

The written native calculation correctly retains

```
B_s = [[F_s A_s N,0],[G_s A_s N,G_s]],
R_s = [[G_s,F_s],[0,G_s A_s^-1]],
F_s = N G_s.
```

A_s commutes with F_s and with G_t for `t != s`; it generally commutes
with neither N nor G_s. On the initial last target alone, stages seven and
eight, the final bridges, and the required full parent exchange give

```
x0=x1=x2=x3=N(A8+A7^-1)y3,     y3=A8 y3.
```

No commutation of A_s through N is used. The actual code edge `A[3,0]=1`
gives an integral witness: input port zero at `HEAD=e59, TAIL=0` produces,
after the required full parent factor, `HEAD=0, TAIL=e3` at port three of
every source bank. The actual root-zero chart sends e59 to e3; the
destination route fixes its own root. Every stage-seven route fixes e59,
so the A7-inverse contribution cannot cancel the TAIL impulse.

This witness survives every odd ADDRESS field. The separate frame-walk
theorem above makes a rational/real claim only.

The terminal free-decoder obstruction is valid for invertible scalar mixing
and HEAD/TAIL-separated routes: these operations and their inverses preserve
TAIL-zero support and induce an invertible map on its quotient. They cannot
erase the nonzero quotient class. This does not prohibit paid N conjugations
or a changed coupled caller.

The two repair bounds have appropriately restricted scope. Nine independent
latent readers with one common-cap completion each require at least nine
rank-seven completions. The 111-row argument applies only when an
N-commuting code must retain the original clean map outside changed physical
rows, with one snapshot per repaired row. The checker supplies an actual
off-diagonal noncommuting edge for every row. Neither bound applies to all
overcomplete, ADDRESS-dependent, shared, or differently factored decoders.

The private-MOVE appendix correctly distinguishes a valid operator from an
implemented interface. In exact physical coordinates, transport from U to V
is `D_V D_U`. The product telescopes on any closed nested walk, without
commuting nonnested frames. The private packet is fresh owned workspace;
the ordinary inputs and spectators remain arbitrary. The original controller
rejects private MOVE destinations, so the proof does not imply that this
changed interface is installed.

The two actual intersection examples also expose a real arithmetic boundary:
one is dyadic, while the other has denominators divisible by three.
The latter cannot simply be admitted under a claim covering every odd
prime. Its large-fixed-prime contract is separately stated. The good-adapter
counts are correct: a rank-seven residual has 9,872f affine slots, while
three rank-one residuals have 25,332f. The apparent three-versus-seven
child-rank saving is therefore not an all-cost saving, even before the
complete target-delivery obstruction.

The actual commutation defect also rejects the proposed free stage wrapper:
`C_s^-1 G_s C_s` is generally not G_s. The exact paid wrappers contain
`N C_s^{±1} N`. The appendix's nine-sweep lower bound is valid as a
**fixed-barrier rewrite result**: keep the chronological order of all
coded calls and their individual code factors, and commute/combine/cancel
the explicit sweeps across those fixed barriers. In each of nine disjoint
intervals the active diagonal bank functional is one; every sweep commuting
through either adjacent barrier has that functional zero. Scalar bank
changes transport the functional and preserve nonzero rank. Each interval
therefore retains at least one bank sweep, giving 999 rank-one requests.

This argument does not establish a minimum when code factors or coded calls
may be resynthesized or reordered, when one uses identities of composite
barrier products, or when partial-port and other routed queries are allowed.
It is a check of one explicit correction word, not a universal obstruction
to shared paid N conjugations.

## Complete caller arithmetic

The ordinary and coded histograms are reconstructed from actual frame
differences, including all COPYs. Returning the nine removed full-helper
obligations costs 72 units of rank mass per call. The net complete saving is
60, not the local 132. The conditional mixed-helper variant must have 424
full helpers and five partial helpers; its compatibility is unproved.

The inherited whole-pattern LP is a relaxation of chronological allocation.
Its resource-regime corollary applies to the displayed inventories.
Surcharges price the displayed primal and are not claimed to preserve LP
optimality. Negative claims use the raw optimum, including the favorable
`1-epsilon` discount; positive planning allowances still require a legal
caller and owner witness.

The independent oracle uses a short rational atanh expansion after binary
range reduction and an exponential expansion, with entire-tail bounds. It
confirms the necessary primitive target

```
9199631083923049 / 4990800368916076951,
```

and the per-call allowances:

- One COPY7 deletion: between 4.977907425421188143 and
  4.977907425421188144.
- Coded 429-full-helper candidate: between 58.382679011994524685 and
  58.382679011994524686.

A rank-five replacement already leaves positive excess even against the raw
LP with free bad packets. Nine rank-seven per-call repairs also fail that
favorable test. These necessary exclusions therefore do not mistake the
charged displayed primal for a globally optimal charged allocation.

The all-count histogram minimization is valid for `n>=9`, child ranks
`{6,7,8}`, mass at least 63, and `0<b<1`. Concavity gives
`4e6+e7+4e8` for nine terms and `8e6+e7+e8` for ten; their tariff difference
is positive. Every `n>=11` costs at least `11 Phi_b(6)`, greater than the
nine-term profile. Its complete caller still rejects the target, including
the free-bad discount.

The selected complex certificate remains an additional joint-transfer
obligation. A successful BIT planning inequality alone is not a 10% κ
improvement.

## Reproduction and status

From any working directory:

```sh
python3 -I -B /absolute/path/to/independent/review_check.py --output /tmp/review.json
python3 -I -B -O /absolute/path/to/independent/review_check.py --output /tmp/review-opt.json
```

The normal and optimized receipts must agree byte for byte. The checker has
no repository-root import or Git dependency. Controls remove an actual
private scatter read, grant a missing influence to invalidate a separator,
and remove the native code edge. Each control must fail its named check;
unexpected acceptance is raised outside the caught-exception path. Reversing
all ADD signs is accepted as an equivalent binary presentation, without
claiming equivalence over the integers.

The retained receipt passed on Python 3.14 in ordinary and optimized modes.
A minimal Git-free copy containing only the checker and its six authenticated
inputs passed on Python 3.12.12 in both modes with identical bytes.
In disposable copies, disabling the endpoint guard and the native-edge guard
made their corresponding negative controls fail by unexpected acceptance;
those controls do not certify themselves by catching that failure.
`VALIDATION.json` records the source and result hashes for this check.

The remaining work is constructive: find a changed useful total, factory, or
coupled caller whose complete physical operation and paid invoice satisfy
the demonstrated budgets. The current package establishes scoped
obstructions and planning thresholds, not a positive improved supplier.
