# Direct predecessors and a targeted literature check

The proofs here use elementary concavity, positive moment potentials and exact
intervals. No external theorem is needed to justify the numerical exclusions.
This is a targeted primary-source check, not a comprehensive literature survey.

## Related potential methodology, with a precise limit on its use

[Christandl, Vrana and Zuiddam, *Universal points in the asymptotic spectrum of tensors*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.07851v3)
studies parameters that are additive on direct sums, multiplicative on tensor
products, and monotone under tensor restriction. It provides a broad context
for additive/multiplicative obstructions to construction closures. This does
**not** prove that our rank-power price is monotone for arbitrary native
operators, coded interfaces or tensor restrictions; that is exactly the missing
hypothesis for an unrestricted architectural barrier.

[Wigderson and Zuiddam, *Asymptotic spectra: Theory, applications and extensions*](https://www.math.ias.edu/~avi/PUBLICATIONS/Wigderson_Zuiddam_2025.pdf),
August 5, 2025, especially sections 3.4, 4.2 and 4.5, develops the relation
between monotone homomorphisms, asymptotic algorithms and barriers. Its lesson
for this investigation is methodological: specify a construction preorder and
prove the potential inequalities for **every** allowed generating operation.
The local closure proof here does so only for its explicitly listed invoice
operations; no general spectral duality theorem is applied to multiplication.

The elementary exchanges and the ratio certificate are proved directly in
PROOF.md. No finite mask enumeration, assignment weight, old fixed-word ceiling,
or external tensor theorem supplies the absent universal lower bound over all
legal native factorizations.
