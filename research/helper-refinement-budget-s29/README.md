# Thirteen H9 refinements cannot fund one added recursive call

**Scoped negative result; no new kappa.** Even granting every integral target
refinement of the thirteen original H9 helpers used by PR5599 for free,
their total extra raw target moment is at most **0.870799206564** per local
invocation throughout `0 <= C <= 17/5000`. An added positive-integral-rank
Gaussian call costs at least one. With every other paid job and productive
target fixed, this mechanism therefore worsens paid-minus-target cost by
more than **0.129200793436** per invocation.

This is a preflight for a specific expanded mechanism: use extra recursive
access/transport to expose finer independent helper cuts. It grants *all*
partitions, including cuts the source's basis-pair search never tried. It
does not repeat that search or claim it was complete. It excludes only a
proposal confined to those thirteen original H9 targets that adds at least
one positive-rank call per invocation without another paid saving.

PR5599 itself adds **zero** recursive calls and remains outside the exclusion.
Its actual thirteen binary splits earn about 0.227168535562 at the interval
endpoint. Its 342 added scalar events are ordinary work, not H1 calls.

recomputed certificate. No source executable is imported.

The proof in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) covers all refinements in the declared
model. The 30 partitions of nine provide finite corroboration and a reusable
table, not a proof of the multiplication theorem. Rational atanh/Taylor
enclosures are independently checked at the decisive rank-nine endpoints by
integer exponentiation. Controls reject missing original-target debits,
extra rank mass, duplicate field credit, changed source bytes and an exponent
outside the licensed interval. Positive controls retain the actual uncharged
split gain and demonstrate that the exclusion fails for fifteen fully refined
sources at the endpoint.

## Exact scope and consequences

| Model at C = 17/5000 | First number of H9 sources whose maximal extra credit exceeds one |
| --- | ---: |
| Arbitrary positive integral partitions | 15 (nine H1 pieces each) |
| Exactly two positive integral pieces each | 48 (H4 + H5 each) |

These are exact *arithmetic relaxation* thresholds at the endpoint. They
do not establish attainable independent channels, compatible frames, stock,
compiler funding or improvements at smaller exponents. Each original target
is surrendered once. Full refinement of thirteen sources creates 117 targets
and requires 104 extra independent public inputs; duplicate outputs are not
credited. The complete eighteen-call relaxation has extra credit less than
15.674385718151, so eighteen added H1 calls lose. A single *global* added call
is not excluded by this bound.

All helper preparation, source access, scalar bases, normalizers, routing,
restoration, ownership and compiler work is granted zero extra cost here;
charging nonnegative extra work cannot repair the raw-moment loss. Paid
removals, other targets, changed rank semantics, nonintegral pieces, a larger
exponent interval or a changed caller are outside the theorem. A worse raw
moment does not by itself preclude an implementation or compiler improvement.
