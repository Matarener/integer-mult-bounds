## Decision

There is no presently funded target-sized complete saving from non-arithmetic
Fraction traversal, formatting, dispatch or custody. The one justified new
information boundary was **cyclic-GC time during the unchanged full qualifier**.
The parent's completed passive diagnostic observed 1.539808223 wall / 1.534307444
CPU seconds across all 3,607 collections. Both values are below the fixed
5.199267809 wall / 5.1705 CPU necessary savings, even with zero replacement cost.
The standalone GC route therefore closes without construction or another run.
The diagnostic passed its instrumentation and preservation checks; the cost
premise failed. Its outer runtime is not a new performance sample.

The combined private-integer/GMP plus custom contribution-key route is logically
open but not funded. The integer-only zero-cost screen does not rule out a sum
with genuinely disjoint costs. Conversely, its 4.855 seconds cannot be treated
as an achievable saving, and the failed 6.47% integer/format measurement cannot
be credited as an admitted increment. No development/procurement is justified
merely by adding those numbers.

## Materially distinct non-arithmetic boundary considered: observation payloads

A concrete source-local alternative is to replace only the payload preparation
inside Observation._call with an exact native tree operation, while retaining
Observation._call/finish/take state transitions and both independent calculations.
The changed objects would be the owned helper implementing `deepcopy(value)` and
the immediate canonical digest of that saved value. The returned deep snapshot,
its digest and all checks would remain the same. The entry's complete ideal and
error/witness equality checks would remain literal and active.

Such a helper could traverse an admitted exact dict/list/tuple/str/int/bool/
Fraction tree once to construct the private clone and prepare canonical bytes.
It must preserve dictionary insertion order, tuple/list distinctions, integer
and Fraction types, copy memo aliasing, shared immutable Fraction behavior,
exact key-to-string conversion and JSON separators/sorting. Unsupported/custom
objects and cycles would take the original path with its exception order. It
must not merge mutable output custody between the candidate and Fraction pass.

All before/after input fingerprints remain. `take()` must still recompute the
saved-output digest to detect drift; the earlier recorded digest cannot replace
that check. Both backend outputs still must exist independently and be compared
in full. Four separate registered custody controls are outside BOTH timed PR5864
arms, so speeding those controls cannot count toward the complete target.

This is distinct from rational arithmetic and the owned-integer design, but its
only identified inclusive cost pools are deepcopy and canonical encoding above.
There is no unprofiled evidence of >5.2 seconds, and fusion cannot delete all
copying, allocation, hashing or drift checks. Its substantial alias/type/source/
exception validation bill makes it an unfunded hypothesis. No C tree helper or
full diagnostic is nominated on this evidence.

The same conclusion applies to combining the outer Fraction traversal and hash
helpers while retaining every original operation. Exact objects affected would
be source-bound bodies of `precision.add`, ideal's iteration/scaling/norm loop,
error_trace's outer event/finalization loop, update_hash and execute/reverse_cut
traversal. `Fraction`, its arithmetic methods, constructors, ordered operands,
signed witnesses and final result types would stay original. `sum(map(abs,...),
F(0))` is already a native summation loop; its arithmetic cost is not removed by
another wrapper. No positive budget is obtained by relabeling its cumulative
time as Python traversal. The previously deferred ideal-loop idea remains
unfunded; failure of other candidates is not evidence in its favor.

## Contribution-key representation: a valid but tight disjoint candidate

The actual trace performs 2,579,750 contribution lookups with 2,252,966 hits.
Current native_cache.c:96-97 already calls the member descriptor's C `tp_descr_get`
directly. It does not call Python `__get__` or PyObject_CallFunctionObjArgs to read
slots. There are four reads per contribution, 10,319,000 actual reads, plus the
two scalar reads once per Event context. The earlier indirect-call premise was
corrected and supplies no optimization budget.

Directly dereferencing a qualified Fraction slot would remove only a small
existing C accessor boundary, while adding CPython layout/descriptor offset,
owner, bounds/alignment, NULL-slot, refcount, malformed-packet and freshness
obligations. Standalone, the target would require roughly half a microsecond
saved per one of those four reads, before paying anything new. No measured
attribution supports that premise. A slot-access micro is not nominated.

A more substantial distinct change is an owned stack/custom-key lookup table
that avoids allocating and hashing a fresh seven-PyLong tuple on every hit.
The exact changed objects would be Cache.dict/ring key storage and contribution's
key construction/lookup/eviction implementation, not Event.apply arithmetic.
Original Fraction miss computation, all three ordered score additions, per-item
F(0), source iteration, row writes and counters must stay unchanged. Exact
canonical seven-component equality, full collision resolution, FIFO behavior,
callback failure before eviction, ownership/GC/reentry and bounded residency
remain mandatory. Pointer identity alone cannot replace numeric key equality.
Prehashing immutable q/token components is permissible only with full equality
and source/type qualification; it cannot change hit/miss/eviction receipts.

A standalone custom-key route needs about two microseconds per actual lookup to
reach the target, while retaining all its other work. The 5.312 profiled native
self seconds include far more than tuple creation, and are not unprofiled
removable seconds. There is no present positive standalone cost screen.

The integer-only ceiling plus this disjoint lookup work is logically different:
under the extremely generous assumption of zero integer-engine cost and zero
added checks, an additional 0.343516 wall / 0.317296 CPU seconds could cross the
necessary thresholds. But a real owner retains arithmetic, witnesses, copying,
conversions and checks. If it retained even one second of that integer work,
lookup savings would need to exceed approximately 1.343516/1.317296 seconds plus
the entire new bill. This is an algebraic requirement, not a predicted saving.
The failed integer candidate's observed 2.14 CPU saving cannot be substituted as
an admitted guaranteed contribution.

A private GMP integer owner could in principle reduce allocation of intermediate
PyLong values while the independent Fraction arithmetic remains Python-based.
No such implementation or paid cost exists. Parent-supplied local metadata says
libgmp10 runtime 6.3.0 exists but development headers/unversioned linker library
do not. Runtime presence does not provide a supported pinned C interface or CI
route. Procurement/build/source/ABI/license/environment, arbitrary-width
conversion, exact dyadic divisions, all event/prefix outputs and finite controls
would need explicit pricing. Do not invent private ABI declarations or treat a
new dependency as free. No procurement or GMP pilot is nominated now.

If new disjoint evidence later reopens this combined route, test the cheap
custom-key premise first with one predeclared frozen cache corpus and exact
FIFO/counter/prefix comparisons, before provisioning GMP. The optimistic screen
must include a stated nonzero residual integer cost and a conservative new bill;
using zero residual cost may only reject, never admit the construction. Tiny
high-hit micro gains are not complete performance measurements, and no favorable
repeat may replace a failed sample.

premise, not a universal runtime bound or proof about all future hosts and

disjoint partition. Any future combined claim needs same-run overlap-aware
attribution. Optional caller provenance may explain which allocation family is
active, but a Python stack containing a function is not by itself exclusive
ownership of the collector's heap-scanning cost.

Even a large GC bill does not make `gc.disable`, threshold changes, gc.freeze,
removing traversal flags or altering callback/finalizer timing semantically free.
The original Fraction allocations and operations remain required. Any prospective
policy needs an explicit stable-private-object domain, full cycle/finalizer/
exception/cleanup/restore controls, no suppressed foreign behavior, and all
postponed collection/cleanup cost paid before the complete command ends. The
negative attribution result supplies no reason to design that policy now.

## What blocks construction and what reopens it

No missing user authorization prevented this source review or the completed
bounded attribution diagnostic. The concrete constraint is the conjunction of
the fixed complete target, retained literal Fraction arithmetic/validation,
cold costs and currently unpriced residual work. All measured target-workload
nominees failed their declared gates; the remaining profile shares do not prove
an implementable disjoint saving. That is an evidence/engineering limitation,
not achievement of the goal and not a universal obstruction.

A construction can reopen only on materially new evidence. The reviewed GC
observation does not supply it. A distinct supported route would need measured
disjoint custom-cache savings plus an honestly priced residual integer/GMP route
and supported pinned dependency environment, or another new cost boundary with
its own complete proof and bill. A separate explicit user change of
scope/target/runtime constraints would define a different task; it must not be
inferred from this goal continuation or from the unrelated matrix success.

The single GC diagnostic failed its necessary cost gate. Its negative receipt
is retained and the route is closed without a timing reroll. No more broadly
implemented Fraction/C mechanism is presently justified by this reassessment.
This bounded report ends here; it does not extend the hypothesis search. The
further PR5864 target remains unfulfilled. This is an evidence/engineering
limitation under the retained contract, not a permission blocker or a claim of
universal impossibility.
