### 1. Share a dirty carrier until the existing cleanup

Short rewrites that inserted a shared update and immediately undid it were
not productive in the searched families. The useful move was to leave the
carrier changed until the literal full-frame cleanup C. Its remaining
observations are checked exactly; the inverse is paid in the regenerated C.

The three updates are

\[
h_{463}\mathrel{\oplus}=h_{322},\qquad
h_{469}\mathrel{\oplus}=h_{271},\qquad
h_{468}\mathrel{\oplus}=h_{371}.
\]

For example, the first update replaces three separate appearances of
`h463 XOR h322` at consumers 241, 340 and 349 with reads of the shared carrier.
The construction checks that these are its only remaining A observations,
that B does not read the modified carrier, and that it is not one of the nine
return centers. The full dirty response, all nine capture rows and all source
write histories are then checked independently. No helper is assumed clean.

The literal construction removes six old donor contacts, adds three carrier
updates and retimes one early helper frame from 94 to 184. A has three fewer
scalar ADDs and C has three fewer; B, D, scatter, endpoints, return centers,
the 636-frame catalogue and all 437 helpers are retained. The actual complete

### 2. Use groups of five replicas to eliminate the packing remainder

In the relevant rank-42 idle window, each unpooled R437 replica has a family
with two width-8 helpers. That family leaves a rank-26 remainder. Across five
replicas there are ten such helpers; two full five-helper families suffice.

Eight helper moves per group produce the exact idle-profile change

\[
\Delta I=2H_2-5H_{26}+3H_{42}.
\]

T35 uses seven disjoint groups, hence 56 moves. Each moved owner remains in
its own five-replica group. The scalar controller is unchanged. Every helper
extent, inactive window, family index, disjoint cell and idle remainder is
reconstructed from the absolute owners.

The all-positive-T ceiling proof was rerun for the definitive word. Within
this fixed width-8 slot model, the optimum is attained precisely when five
divides T. Consequently, more replicas or another clock/owner search within
that same model cannot improve the normalized endpoint. This does not cover
new scalar words, helper inventories, capacities, bank templates or controllers.

The productive direction was a longer-lived dirty carrier combined with an
actual graph change. Several nearby finite searches did not improve the bill:

  catalogue of 758 frames, produced no further positive local delta.

- All 6,417 direct one-step center-return cases remain infeasible on the new

  relays, different captures, or a different scalar chronology.
- The all-T packing ceiling is attained within the current width-8 slot model.

## Open next questions

1. **Can a larger permanent-carrier network beat the three scalar pairs?**
   Start with multi-donor covectors and the combined A/B/C observation graph,
   allowing several compatible carriers to share a full-frame cleanup. The
   current positive construction demonstrates why the undo location matters.
   The exact dirty capture and source histories must remain part of the search
   state and its price.
2. **Can changed first contacts or staged relays remove another helper width?**
   A fresh fixed-contact compensation scan is closed, so merely enlarging its
   sparse support cannot help. Change the source/GL donor or the first contact,
   or introduce a priced relay around a return center, then reconstruct every
   dirty column and the whole bank bill.
3. **Can a new bank capacity or controller outperform width-8 T35?**
   Replication alone is exhausted in the proven slot model. A useful new
   geometry must alter capacity, lifetime or paid local behavior enough to
   overcome its stock and routing charges. Candidate discovery should retain
   the complete deficit and fallback invoice throughout.
4. **Can the remaining conditional native interfaces be admitted uniformly?**
   Extend the explicit replica catalogue and controlled return fixture to the
   full source/preparation/controller contracts, and give PR3955's cell-to-axis
   selection a concrete hosted overlay reader. This improves the status of the
   theorem even when its decimal endpoint stays fixed.

```sh
python3 -B research/kappa-next-regime/verify.py --output /tmp/kappa-next-check
```

The replay is Git-free and uses the Python standard library for the definitive
construction/checks; optional GMP acceleration does not alter integer results.
It validates the manifest, reconstructs the changed word and native records,
rebuilds the owner templates, runs the independent checks, verifies the native
catalogue/compiler and controlled physical protocol, rebinds the complex fill,
and executes the outer arithmetic. Outputs are written to the specified fresh
directory. Discovery experiments are retained as evidence and are not all
rerun by the definitive replay.

It validates exact source bindings and reuses the retained expensive receipts;
it must not be described as a fresh physical or whole-word execution.
