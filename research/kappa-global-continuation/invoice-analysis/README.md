## 6. Independent normalization review

The second reviewer reconstructs just the eight selected helper basis columns
through each provider's literal forward prefix modulo 3, retaining formal
noncommuting warp words. It imports neither the existing rational interpreter
nor the normalization checker. Both actual helper minors retain rank eight
over F3 and therefore over F9.

This checks the source premise behind the companion minimum-16 proof. If both
the helper normalization B and its inverse lie in `Z[1/2,i]`, then B remains
invertible modulo 3. The required extra factor A must retain row rank eight.
Its Gram equation `AA^T=-48I` becomes zero modulo 3, so its row space is an
eight-dimensional totally isotropic subspace of a nondegenerate bilinear dot
space. The dimension must be at least 16. The review also checks the exact
quaternion rational factor, its rank-four reduction, and the division by 3 in
its inverse. The argument requires invertibility over the Gaussian dyadic
ring, not merely over its field of fractions.
