# Changed consumers after PR3734: native closure and coupled helper contracts

**Result: no new κ is established.** This round closes the missing modern native
phase checks in the supplied involution report, constructs and prices an explicit
terminal inverse, and builds two stronger dyadic contracts that encode the actual
data and helper state. The terminal inverse is too expensive. The stronger local
contracts need a changed global controller and physical allocation before their
savings can enter a κ invoice.

The most useful new candidate is a **symmetric Gaussian-dyadic involution on 128
ports**. Both directions have complete finite local words on the actual PR3734
providers, with local rank mass **5134 → 5070**, no replacement native children,
and no division by an odd integer. Its additional ports have zero native labels;
the existing 112-port, norm-one, nine-stage supplier does not automatically
implement their interstage contract.

This package preserves executable words, source pins, exact price intervals,
independent native reviews, and the actual helper-owner trajectories. It is a
research continuation, not a selected certificate or a claimed multiplication
bound.

## 3. The new mechanism: encode the actual helper dependence

The omitted centres depend on more than Rx. Literal replay derives

\[
q=Rx+Hh,\qquad y_{\rm omitted}=y+x-Kq.
\]

H is supported on 106 actual helper roles, all with compatible I entrances and
FULL exits. An invertible dyadic minor uses helper roles

```text
224,225,227,229,240,241,256,257.
```

These are independent helper combinations in the current supplier, not eight
new clean registers. Cleaning away the −KHh term hides a usable part of the
existing transfer. The new construction retains and encodes it.

### 3.1 A dyadic pair with no target repairs

Write z=(x,h), let Ez=x, and set Q=[R,H]. Choose a helper-supported P_e with
QP_e=I, and set K_e=(K,0)ᵀ. Then

\[
D=I+(12P_e-K_e)Q,\quad D^2=I,\quad (E-KQ)D=E.
\]

The forward and inverse-transpose companion are

\[
F_D(z,y)=(Dz,y+EDz),\qquad
B_D(z,y)=(D^Tz-E^Ty,y)=F_D^{-T}(z,y).
\]

The backward post-code is legal on the completed X and helper fields at FULL;
it avoids the data-only J⁻¹ division. Both literal programs have profile

```text
{1:2440, 2:725, 3:224, 4:102, 5:20}, mass 5070.
```

Each uses 168592 charged real scalar primitives. Scalar triangle bounds require
120 forward / 126 backward bits, below the retained 192-bit scalar cap. The
forward changes eight helper roles; the backward changes 106. Their restoration
must be handled by a new complete global contract.

### 3.2 A symmetric Gaussian involution and full 128-port pair

Invertible helper basis operations at the compatible boundaries put H into the
form

\[
H_{\rm eff}=[4I_8,\;8iI_8,\;0].
\]

Let

\[
\widehat R=[R,4I_8,8iI_8].
\]

Using the bilinear transpose, not conjugate transpose,

\[
\widehat R\widehat R^T=56I+16I-64I=8I.
\]

Consequently

\[
D_{\rm sym}=I-\tfrac14\widehat R^T\widehat R,
\qquad D_{\rm sym}^T=D_{\rm sym},\qquad D_{\rm sym}^2=I.
\]

All coefficients are Gaussian dyadic. This is a complex orthogonal identity;
it does not assert that D_sym is Hermitian-unitary or that it preserves every
norm used by the precision analysis.

Promote 16 helper coordinates to the X bank and 16 kernel helper coordinates to
the Y bank. The missing extended-target scalar rows can be supplied at the
common FULL boundary. The resulting actual local programs implement

\[
F(z,w)=(D_{\rm sym}z,w+D_{\rm sym}z),\qquad
B(z,w)=(z-w,D_{\rm sym}w),\qquad z,w\in\mathbb C^{128}.
\]

Both providers and both directions pass all 632 independent input columns,
D_sym²=I and D_symᵀ=D_sym, both local endpoint identities, helper-basis
normalization and inverse, and literal scalar-frame compatibility. The complete scalar allowances are 172250
forward / 181578 backward; the final scalar prefix checks remain below 192 bits.
Independent review checked the Gaussian rather than Hermitian transpose and
requested explicit source pins; those pins were added and the canonical replay
rerun.

This is the strongest changed local contract in the package. The 16 added port
labels are zero: their inherited helper paths are I→FULL, whereas ordinary data
ports use C_q→FULL and I→FULL C_q⁻¹ for norm-one q. A global construction must
handle that distinction. Assigning them ordinary norm-one labels and ignoring
the extra transitions would be a false cost claim.

and [exact receipt](artifact/integer-mult-bounds/research/kappa-consumer-followup/baseline/symmetric-extended-results/RESULT.json).

## 5. What a winning complete construction must satisfy

On the unchanged m73, W322560 invoice, the maximum added
complete cost per replica at the requested target is:

| Local deletion assumption | H1-equivalent allowance | H8-field allowance |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Eight H8 replaced by eight H1 per call | 501.906957252301 | 62.964548980198 |
| Eight H8 removed with no replacement | 573.906957252301 | 71.996995056244 |

The original report's 69.75-field lower bound, under its explicit routed-unitary
premise and assumed H8 tariff, excludes its proposed fresh full-field consumer.
The new zero-repair allowance would accommodate 71 H8 but not 72 H8 **if all
other fields and costs really stayed unchanged**. It is a test for a proposed
construction, not evidence that such a decoder has been built.

The denominator matters as much as new children. Under the precise model that
retains the paid invoice but withdraws correctly productive output fields,
the zero-repair allowance is fewer than 7.921 full C73 families per replica.
Reserving eight such fields already fails. A coupled contract can avoid that
charge only by proving that the relevant outputs still deliver their required
transforms.

For the mixed 128-port/zero-label idea, a separate, explicitly optimistic m81
screen assumes W=1024 per replica, coverage mass 82048, and puts every idle unit
into the largest allowed rank57 child. Even this favourable relaxation gives:

| Hypothetical extra consumer | Optimistic complex root lower endpoint |
| --- | ---: |
| 72 H8 | 0.001657897500848277472216 |
| 71 H8 | 0.001699376924679389803093 |
| 70 H8 | 0.001740857906156611936202 |

Thus the unchanged m73 71-field allowance must not be carried into the enlarged
geometry. The m81 screen needs at most 70 whole H8 fields and sufficiently good
actual packing even before further costs. It supplies neither a native m81
controller nor a feasible allocation. The bit side would also cap a large
complex improvement: the independently repriced retained bit endpoint is
0.001733427039589844234749 before strict outer gaps.

   fresh state, changed allocations or transfers need complete charges.

   enlarged m81 screen requires at most 70. Full-field flattening already loses.

   contract and invoice exist, extend precision, route/tape realization,
   recursive hosting and the modern outer inequalities. Local scalar prefix
   bounds alone do not establish this implication.

The package has not established these missing interfaces as an admitted κ path.
