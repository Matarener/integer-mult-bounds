# Arbitrary-count source reader floor and a fixed three-seed consequence

Scientific source pin: `20ecf2891d80b5acf18d980b2164fb44ea7faa90`.
This is a finite-dimensional binary factorization theorem, with an exact finite
census and a written all-parameter pressure implication. It is not a native
supplier impossibility theorem, a formal-kernel proof, or a new multiplication
exponent.

## Model and source binding

Let the columns index the 111 retained, distinct E8 root lines. Let
`R ∈ F₂^(9×111)` be the actual reader matrix obtained by executing the retained
R438 `D,A` word and restricting its nine retained helper rows to the original
source columns. The saved scalar compensation has factor `KR`, where
`K ∈ F₂^(111×9)` has column rank nine. These matrices are bound to the literal
source, not selected from a convenient isomorphic code without a port map.

Exact reconstruction establishes that `D = rowspace(R)` has dimension nine,
minimum nonzero Hamming weight 48, and support on all 111 coordinates. The
independent audit propagates each of the 111 source coordinates separately
through the source word and then enumerates all 512 binary coefficient vectors.
It compares the reconstructed matrices and all 511 nonzero weights with the
frozen receipts. A left inverse of K exists over F₂, so any reader factorization
of KR induces a reader factorization of R using the same reader rows.

Consider any finite binary factorization

`R = BC`, with `C ∈ F₂^(s×111)`.

Reader rows may lie outside D, and s is not fixed. For each nonzero row C_i,
let d_i be the rational dimension of the span of its supporting E8 root lines.
Assume that materializing this reader is charged at least d_i. The bill studied
here is therefore bounded below by `sum_i d_i`. It is a source-only bill.
It is distinct from the rational source/target pairing tariff used by the cold
ten- and eleven-factor floor56 results.

This tariff premise describes separately materialized binary readers. It does
not cover shared native calls, nonlinear readers, operator-valued coefficients,
or a copied target already in a nonzero frame. Gathering, target delivery,
physical storage, dirty-helper restoration and precision costs are not supplied
by the factorization theorem. A realization must prove and pay those obligations
separately.

## E8 capacities

For a rational root-spanned subspace W of dimension d, let M_d be a universal
upper bound on the number of E8 root lines contained in W. The capacities are

| d | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M_d | 0 | 1 | 3 | 6 | 12 | 20 | 36 | 63 | 120 |

Here is the classical reduction behind the table. For d < 8, choose v in W's
orthogonal complement avoiding the finitely many hyperplanes cut out there by
roots outside W. Then a root is perpendicular to v exactly when it belongs to
W. Move v into a closed Weyl chamber. The roots perpendicular to this chamber
point form the standard parabolic subsystem generated by the simple roots on
which v vanishes. Since W is root-spanned, that subsystem has rank d. At d = 8,
the entire E8 system gives 120 root lines. The displayed maxima are attained by
A1, A2, A3, D4, D5, E6, E7, E8. Puncturing cannot increase a capacity.

The independent audit generates all 240 signed E8 roots in doubled standard
coordinates, converts them exactly to a simple-root basis, checks integral
coordinates and positivity, and counts roots in all 256 standard parabolic
subsets. It recovers the table. The Weyl chamber/parabolic theorem remains a
written classical premise; this enumeration is not a formal proof of it.

## The reader floor

**Theorem.** Every factorization in the declared source reader model satisfies

`sum_i d_i ≥ 53`.

First remove zero and dependent rows of C, retaining a basis of its row span
and adjusting B. This preserves R = BC, does not increase the bill, and leaves
t independent reader rows spanning V with D contained in V. In particular
t ≥ 9. A nonzero reader has rational support rank between one and eight.

For any k between one and nine, choose any `t−9+k` of these rows. Their span U
has dimension `t−9+k`, and

`dim(U ∩ D) ≥ dim(U) + dim(D) − dim(V) = k`.

Choose a k-dimensional binary subspace E inside that intersection. Each active
coordinate of E occurs in exactly `2^(k−1)` of its `2^k−1` nonzero vectors.
Every such vector has weight at least 48. Double-counting ones gives

`|supp(E)| = 2^(1−k) sum_(e∈E\{0}) wt(e)
            ≥ ceil(48(2^k−1)/2^(k−1))`.

At k = 9, E = D, so the exact full support 111 is stronger. Thus we may use

`(b_1,...,b_9) = (48,72,84,90,93,95,96,96,111)`.

The support of E is contained in the union of the chosen reader supports.
Order the reader ranks `d_1 ≤ ... ≤ d_t`. Applying the preceding argument to
the first `t−9+k` readers and then the capacity bound gives the nine necessary
conditions

`sum_(i=1)^(t−9+k) M_(d_i) ≥ b_k`, for `1 ≤ k ≤ 9`.

Suppose the total bill were at most 52. Enumerate all sorted tuples with parts
in {1,...,8} and total at most 52, including the empty tuple. There are exactly
340,260 such tuples. The historical producer enumerates rank multiplicities;
the independent implementation enumerates by exact total and least part, and
checks each exact-total count separately against the coefficients of
`product_(r=1)^8 (1−x^r)^(-1)` through degree 52. Both leave exactly one profile:

`t = 13`, all reader ranks four, bill 52.

For this profile, any six reader rows meet D in dimension at least two. Their
support union must therefore contain at least 72 coordinates. Each support
has size at most M_4 = 12, so equality holds throughout

`72 ≤ |union of six supports| ≤ sum of six support sizes ≤ 6·12 = 72`.

Consequently every six supports are pairwise disjoint and all have size 12.
Any two of the 13 readers extend to a six-element subset, and every reader
belongs to one. Hence all 13 supports are pairwise disjoint, each of size 12.
They would require 156 distinct coordinates in a universe of 111, a
contradiction. This excludes every bill at most 52 and proves the theorem.

The independent census records 12,689 tuples with fewer than nine rows,
327,379 first rejected at k = 1, 191 first rejected at k = 2, and the sole
profile above. Higher-k inequalities are valid and checked, but no additional
tuple in this budget range needs them. No attaining bill-53 factorization is
asserted. The earlier fixed-code basis bill63 is a different, narrower domain
and is inherited from earlier P111 work rather than a new claim here.

## Fixed three-seed pressure implication

Let a complete candidate invoice have nonnegative child multiplicities H[r],
parent arity m > 0, stock W, and only positive child ranks r ≤ m. Put

`D_invoice = mW − sum_r rH[r]`.

The moment test `sum_r H[r](r/m)^(1−a) < W` is equivalent to

`P(a,m) = sum_r rH[r]((m/r)^a−1) < D_invoice`.

Now assume the following fixed model:

1. `L ≥ 3`, `m = 1+7L ≥ 22`, and `a ≥ 1/500`.
2. Three seed calls retain the complete bulk histogram
   `c = {1:1295, 2:1342, 3:79, 4:44, 5:93, 6:11, 7:9}`.
   This is the pinned FULL-target profile after deleting exactly its 111
   rank-one target lifts. Its rank mass is
   `sum_r r c[r] = 4986 = 14·111 + 8·429`.
3. Each seed is repaired in the separate reader model just proved.
4. The complete caller's accounting permits common-axis credit at most
   `2·111 = 222` and charges all three reader bills against that credit, so
   `D_invoice ≤ 222 − sum_(j=1)^3 bill_j ≤ 222 − 3·53 = 63`.
   Subsequent FULL-target fanout supplies no further deficit credit.

The fourth condition is a premise about the **complete** invoice. It is not a
consequence of the binary factorization. Additional stock or changed progress
that raises its right-hand side is outside this implication. Likewise, the
three bulk histograms must actually remain in the complete invoice; they
cannot be replaced by an unrelated histogram with the same mass.

For every bulk rank r ≤ 7, `m/r > 1`. Monotonicity, followed by
`exp(x)−1 ≥ x` for x ≥ 0, yields

`P(a,m) ≥ 3 sum_r r c[r]((m/r)^a−1)
         ≥ (3/500) sum_r r c[r] ln(22/r)`.

The first inequality discards only nonnegative pressure terms. It is valid for
omitted reader, idle, padding, repair and owner calls precisely because their
multiplicities are nonnegative and their ranks are at most m. These facts alone
do not establish the fourth accounting condition; both conditions are needed.

For rational x > 1, let z = (x−1)/(x+1), so 0 < z < 1. The positive series

`ln(x) = 2 sum_(j=0)^∞ z^(2j+1)/(2j+1)`

is strictly larger than its first 32 terms. Exact rational evaluation gives

`(3/500) sum_r r c[r] · 2 sum_(j=0)^31
     ((22−r)/(22+r))^(2j+1)/(2j+1)
 > 72.34417105 > 63`.

The full rational value and its seven per-rank summands are in the independent
receipt. All denominators are positive, and the decimal threshold comparison
uses rational integers; no floating-point rounding decides a sign. A tail
upper bound is unnecessary because this is a strict lower certificate.

Thus every complete invoice satisfying these four conditions fails the moment
test for every L ≥ 3 and a ≥ 1/500. This is an all-L consequence, not a finite
L sweep. The exponent a is the primitive moment exponent; no identification
with a final multiplication κ or unproved outer transfer is made.

The distance-44 sensitivity control changes the partition survivors. Replacing
53 by the older 49 floor raises the deficit ceiling to 75, above this particular
logarithmic witness. That control demonstrates loss of this certificate, not
the existence or feasibility of a changed supplier.
