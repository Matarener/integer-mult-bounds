## A geometric rank cap without numerical ranks

Let E be m-dimensional over the binary frame field with its standard nondegenerate dot product. For a subspace U define the symplectic Lagrangian

    L_U={(x,z): x in U, x+z in U^perp}.

No orthogonal projection onto U is assumed. In particular U and a containing hyperplane may be degenerate for the dot product.

**Lemma 2.** L_U is Lagrangian and

    dim(L_U intersect L_V)
      =dim(U intersect V)+m-dim(U+V),
    d(L_U,L_V)=dim U+dim V-2 dim(U intersect V).

**Proof.** Write a point of L_U as (x,w-x), with x in U and w in U^perp. This is an injective parameterization of dimension m. Two such points have zero symplectic pairing because the two cross dot products vanish and the x dot x' terms cancel. Thus L_U is isotropic of dimension m. In an intersection, x ranges over U intersect V and w ranges independently over (U+V)^perp. The dimension and distance identities follow. QED.

**Corollary 3.** If U,V are contained in one h-dimensional subspace H, then d(L_U,L_V)<=h.

**Proof.** The expression in Lemma 2 equals dim(U+V)-dim(U intersect V), which is at most dim H. QED.

Choose each coframe so its INVERSE computational Lagrangian is L_U. An explicit symplectic basis extension constructs such a coframe; the retained exact Clifford lift supplies its phases. The old source's forward frame Lagrangian must not be silently used as its inverse frame Lagrangian. If a retained coframe uses the opposite convention, invert its symplectic action when building the table.

Consequently a new family whose inverse Lagrangians all come from subspaces of the actual restricted eight-plane has every transport rank at most eight. This remains true at formerly native positions after Theorem 1. At any enclosing parent grade strictly above eight these children pass the rank inequality. Other embedding, all-column, spectator, phase and matcher obligations are still part of the actual program contract.

This corollary constructs a rank-capped family. It does not assert without inspection that every old retained boundary coframe is already in that inverse-Lagrangian family. For an application, either verify that boundary fact or freeze and account for each boundary transition separately. Its exact rank is part of the acceptance certificate.
