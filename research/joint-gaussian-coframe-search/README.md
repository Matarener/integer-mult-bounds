# Joint coframe optimization for Gaussian funding

This handoff supplies constructive mathematical tools for searching whole Gaussian programs, with proofs and exact acceptance certificates. The main result replaces independent frame tweaks by a joint minimum-cut problem, including every changed helper target. A second construction removes native-generator restrictions by specializing the retained universal word and paying the resulting physical transitions. A third theorem turns a candidate's full inventory into rational funding and tail certificates.

These are informal mathematical results and proposed applications. They do not assert that a newly funded whole caller has been found or that multiplication κ ≥ 0.0035 follows. The retained conditional BIT saving 0.003503 is unchanged.

Dugongue, with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance.

## The constructions to use first

1. [coframe-cut.md](coframe-cut.md) proves exact physical re-gauging, the inverse-Lagrangian rank formula, and minimum-cut optimization of a complete binary coframe family. It includes all helper endpoint debits and arbitrary dirty owned storage.
2. [native-specialization.md](native-specialization.md) uses the retained free-group endpoint identity to assign native generators identity while paying their actual Gaussian transitions. Inside a common eight-plane, every such transition has rank at most eight. This enlarges the search beyond frames that avoid native warps.
3. [coupled-labels.md](coupled-labels.md) handles simultaneous helper endpoint changes, common-anchor expansions, and fixed logical Clifford transports. Balanced signed interactions reduce to a cut; a small set of remaining interactions can be handled by finite branching. A spanning-forest gauge concentrates nontrivial transport into cycle edges.
4. [funding-certificate.md](funding-certificate.md) proves an entropy-and-curvature first-moment certificate and a rational certificate for all three source contraction guards. It does not require trusting an optimizer's floating-point output.
5. [handoff.md](handoff.md) gives five bounded implementation and validation jobs, their frozen inputs, required witnesses, exact acceptance criteria and stopping rules. Start with the restricted eight-plane word; then apply the same construction to a specified complete caller.

## Why joint moves matter

A helper upgrade can make a single collapse look bad even when a coordinated group of collapses pays for itself. The cut theorem finds the best joint move in its stated family rather than accepting only individually profitable moves. Native-generator specialization makes more vertices eligible, while charging formerly ordinary warps as Gaussian children. These mechanisms change the search space; neither silently transfers an old histogram.

The certificate theorem separates discovery from proof. A contributor may use substantial computation to nominate a full word and its coframes. The mathematical output is an explicit instruction recipe, complete signed inventory, exact normalizers, and rational inequalities. A candidate is useful even without a claim that its search was globally optimal.
