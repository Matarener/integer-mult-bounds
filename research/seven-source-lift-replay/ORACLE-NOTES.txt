Independent five-source core census and projection necessity

Model: a restored source word has one bit-toggle coordinate per source. At
each pre-gate cut its state is q_a. Available nonzero two-read masks are
q_a XOR q_b. Required targets are every nonempty mask of weight at most4:
30 for five sources,56 for six,98 for seven. The final cut duplicates the
initial zero state and adds no nonzero two-read mask.

This oracle was written/frozen without reading the new primary native lift
implementation, predecessor census.c/verify/run_census, or source CENSUS.json.
It uses no C or other source imports. Its enumeration differs from a canonical
word DFS: choose the four positions of the unique four-use source, perfectly
match the other eight positions, and label all five groups by first appearance.

Completeness/uniqueness: every (4,2,2,2,2) word uniquely determines its four-block
and four unordered two-blocks. Conversely these blocks give exactly one
first-appearance canonical word. Thus there are
choose(12,4)*(8!/(2^4*4!)) =495*105 =51975 words. Equivalently, start with five
choices for the labeled four-use source and 12!/(4!*2!^4) label words, then
divide by the free five-source relabeling action5!: the same51975. The
four-use source is not assumed to be the first label. The executable checks
all five possible first-appearance labels occur, including fully covered
words with nonzero four-use labels.

Each of the51975 words is evaluated twice: all pairs of actual prefix states
and direct toggling along every literal contiguous interval. The nonzero
available mask sets agree exactly. The full histogram and lexicographically
sorted1257 full30 words are retained in ORACLE.json. A control records a word
that covers every weight<=3 target yet misses a weight4 target. An odd-use
unrestored word is rejected. Output writes require explicit --output.

Projection necessity (not sufficiency): deleting any one source from a restored
word projects each prefix onto the remaining coordinates. Deleted steps merely
repeat a projected state, so collapsing them loses no projected prefix state.
Every original two-read mask projects to an available mask in the shorter
word; this is also checked on all full30 five-source words. Therefore a full98
seven-source word with profile(4,4,4,2,2,2,2) must project, after deleting any
four-use source, to a full56 six-source word with profile(4,4,2,2,2,2). Deleting
either remaining four-use source yields a full30 five-source core of the
enumerated profile. Insertion of a fresh four-use label followed by canonical
relabeling can reconstruct every such extension; multiple parent choices can
generate the same canonical child, so a lift computation must deduplicate.

Crucial maximum scope: a reported maximum95 from seven-source lifts of ONLY
full56 six-source words is a conditional filtered maximum, not the global
maximum of the complete seven-source multiplicity profile. A nonfull six-source
projection can cover at most55 of the56 targets avoiding the new source, hence
its seven-source extension can cover at most55+42=97 of the98 targets. Thus a
complete no98 exclusion can combine that projection fact with a complete
filtered lift search, without claiming a global95 bound. Raw lift/insertion
counts are not counts of canonical seven-source words.

This oracle does not run the six/seven-source lift search, accept a length22
witness, prove an arbitrary-length floor, or certify a complete source supplier,
physical cost, precision/outer multiplication theorem or new kappa. Any
profile-specific no98 result needs the separately pinned complete primary lift
enumeration plus its coverage/binding checks. It is not an all-size barrier.
