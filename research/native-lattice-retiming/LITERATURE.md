## Bounded discovery and limitations

Before the new upstream sources appeared, bidirectional coordinate retiming,
75 commuting-ADD combinations (108 removed records), and 1,320 paired
same/complement rank-one idle-projector experiments found no paid improvement.
Those are local-only exploratory results; they are not global barriers.

Starting from #4441's exact native patch, the optional lattice optimizer tries
joining frames with each binary line and intersecting them with each binary
hyperplane, with paired helper-boundary decisions and exact nesting checks.
At the discovery exponent 1−0.00300106 it finds one move: intersect frame 732
with the hyperplane orthogonal to binary vector 2, at phase B scalar 480.
Its floating objective is only a ranking aid. `verify.py` does not run a
solver or trust the objective; it executes the explicit PATCH.json.

Subsequent exploratory expansion over 111,887 distinct old/new candidate
spaces, and the same expansion after scalar folding, found no additional
improving single-label move in that particular program. Simultaneous
multiple-label moves, changed scalar graphs and other providers remain open.
No global or exact search-optimality claim is made.
