## Native BIT product before covering

and chronological paid ranks r_l; let V have target roles j of ranks h_j
and paid ranks s_a. For each V target j, instantiate a distinct U copy at

right-route symbol into one common product ambient space. Its productive

Run the U copies in lockstep through their common scalar/COPY/paid-call

distinct across the copies, though their values may be correlated or belong
to owned center banks. Run one V invocation at column multiplicity r_l on

V's target j returns the required job of rank r_l h_j. Undo its complete
role routes and resume U only after V has finished and erased its own

of one U copy are combined. Every center and source-owned COPY is retained.
Thus the target and paid moments multiply under native composition.

The cover is still formal at this point. No intermediate job has been
