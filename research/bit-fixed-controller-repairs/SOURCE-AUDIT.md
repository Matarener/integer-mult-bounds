## Mathematical control boundaries

The historical closed target is `diag(1,-1,1,-1,...)` over the integral free
group algebra. It becomes identity only in characteristic two. It is
involutive, so repeating the entire forward endpoint is a vacuous inverse
control. Change the direction of one actual inverse code operation instead.
The original compensation, B1-pullback and stale-coded-stage mutations alter
actual words and compare their complete eight-input endpoint coefficients.

`normalize` is the identity on the logical rows in the historical interpreter.
Its omission cannot be detected by endpoint algebra. The two native
normalization sweeps and their tariffs remain separate explicit premises or
invoice checks. Ordinary dirty-helper restoration is likewise a premise of
these abstract local interfaces, not an extra bank silently initialized to zero.

The unused `bit_seeded_star.inverse_event` returns every event unchanged and
comments that all primitives are involutive. That statement does not hold
for `new_F`: its square has target `(1+N)x+y`. Its actual `main` correctly
switches `new_F` to `new_F_inverse`. The misleading unused helper is excluded
from this package's required extraction and must not be reported as verified.

The small ring primitives assume equal row lengths and entries in `0..3`;
`plus` uses `zip` and does not itself validate dimensions. The fixed generated
recipes meet those premises. The new general-B proof and checker must state
and enforce their own dimension and ring-domain contracts.

The early-promotion witness is an actual off-diagonal code entry and a
15-coordinate rational route, also nonzero over the specified odd field.
It does not obstruct every new promoted-input factory or changed controller.
The route function's all-pairs census is a finite geometric calculation;
the fresh independent reviewer must distinguish it from the general written
claim.

Numerical comparison constants such as the old 60-unit local tariff and the
492-unit allowance are literal source premises in the historical receipt.
Reproducing those literals does not independently derive their owner, budget
or native implementation hypotheses.
