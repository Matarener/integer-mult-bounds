The all-parameter results are written algebraic proofs. Finite checker fixtures
independently verify the formulas and constructions on their recorded domains;
they do not establish the universal quantifier by enumeration.
The recipe proof incorporates the cross-review's product-order clarification:
moving an inserted repair to the final cut conjugates it by the fixed suffix.
The cross-review preserves the original pre-clarification source hash.

The three historical receipts are regenerated by their unchanged mathematical
functions and compared in full. The new checkers use separate implementations.
The originals are retained for provenance and discrepancy detection, not as
independent evidence for the new all-B theorems.

Source and container hashes are in [FROZEN-SHA256.json](FROZEN-SHA256.json).
Original locations and any storage encoding are in [ORIGINS.json](ORIGINS.json).
Repository pins are in [SOURCES.json](SOURCES.json). Attribution and comparison
scope are recorded in [RELATED-WORK.json](RELATED-WORK.json).

The registered checks make no selected-frontier change, native precision claim,
unrestricted caller lower bound, physical-tape claim or new kappa claim.
