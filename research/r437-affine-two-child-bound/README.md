# R437: two serial affine COPY children need at least121 ranks

**Two proper reversed children, separated by arbitrary forward affine address
maps, cannot implement the all-class rank-seven COPY contract with a cheap
mixed-width pair. Their total width must be at least121f.** A fixed all-COPY
replacement therefore fails the favorable paid moment: even the hypothetical
58+63 pair gives **21915/18944 >1 at exponent zero**, before routing,
preparation, restoration or storage charges. No improvement of kappa is claimed.

The exact domain is a **single serial source pullback**, with linear part
`L W_a M W_b R`, where L,M,R are invertible lower triangular and arbitrary
affine translations are allowed. Ancestor weights are arbitrary diagonal units.
Combining differently routed copies through address-dependent Boolean masks is
outside this domain. This package gives an exact zero-child masked translation
construction using q^(7fw) branches. Its literal full-scan realization has
exponential scan count, providing a scope escape without a physical tariff.

The proof pairs local-ring coordinate flags with reversible address information
flow. The valid all-class projector on the last seven head axes fixes57f
coordinates at each end. Preserved north-east zero blocks exclude two widths
both<=57f. A unit-minor argument forces both widths>=57f. The middle14f quotient
then requires the excess widths to sum to at least7f, hence total121f.
The written proof covers every admitted local ring and every f.

## Measurable result and full-cost boundary

The PR4010 pooled profile has903456 replica-indexed requests,2592 COPY calls,
rank mass1808352 and deficit10272. A fixed serial affine replacement needs at
least114 extra ranks per COPY, adding295488 and bringing mass to2103840,
which exceeds64W by285216. For an adaptive mechanism, a necessary zero-exponent
condition under the hypothetical replacement invoice is `theta <107/3078`
(about3.48%). No class-frequency bound or attaining construction is established.
The positive-exponent formula and additional costs are in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md).

This resolves the serial affine part of PR4014's mixed-width/translation handoff.
It does **not** extend PR4014's one-child theorem to all dirty masked two-child
circuits. That result covers more payload-role logic but excludes translations;
this result permits translations and two children but requires a serial pullback.
Neither theorem closes the full arbitrary-stream caller.
