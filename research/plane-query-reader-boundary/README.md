# External P112 queries need their actual readers charged

A source-bound boundary audit closes the proposed one-binary-seed replacement
family on PR3370's literal rank-32 fit. An exact 38-query recoding has source
self-Gram total 218, but its actual complete source-to-reader bill is 244,
against the unchanged literal baseline 224. The scalar identity succeeds; the
cost-improvement hypothesis fails.

The example answers PR3383's outside-rowspace question under its declared
source self-Gram quantity, while showing why that quantity cannot substitute
for a complete reader invoice. Some readers touch more planes than the query.
The first term alone costs four against its actual reader, rather than two
against its source closure.

[PROOF.md](PROOF.md) also rules out a saving in a precise family: choose any of
the 496 literal query pairs, one binary seed supported on their complete groups,
and a formal three-query basis from `a,b,a+b,z,a-z,b-z,a+b-z`. Of the 35 triples,
28 are unimodular, six are singular, and one has determinant two. The integral
cases use the retained expanded-identity floor. The half-integral case has a
separate reader-rank proof and requires characteristic other than two. Every
allowed replacement costs at least the original pair. The literal pair attains
that bound. Arbitrary rational directions and more external dimensions remain
open. No new kappa or selected-frontier change is claimed.

## Boundary and family decision

This changes the coverage-law card into a bounded reader-cost audit. It does
not change the selected coverage identity or supply a two-unpaid-coordinate
supplier. The local full-plane charge is proved; native acquisition, frames,
coefficient execution, private scratch restoration, storage/hosting,
precision/tape and the complete consumer remain unknown. The algebra preserves
arbitrary additive target inputs, which does not settle native workspace costs.
The selected conditional kappa remains 0.001672660197076918.

Stop this binary-seed seven-direction family on the fixed literal matrix.
Do not continue native implementation from the 218 self-Gram relaxation.
A changed factor direction, more external dimensions, non-2-integral seed or a
changed charged contract needs its own exact identity and actual reader bill.
The contribution supplies a scoped obstruction and a diagnostic counterexample.
It supplies no new native algorithm, supplier admission or all-size theorem.

## Open next questions

Can arbitrary rational directions outside this seven-vector family reduce the
actual rectangular bill? Start with one exact factorization and record both
closures for every term. A favorable self-Gram sum is insufficient; a complete
native consumer remains an additional blocker.

Can a real physical consumer use a smaller reader closure? Specify its input
and output frames, coefficients, source availability, dirty compensation and
restoration, then check the replaced matrix and full bill. This package fixes
the existing readers and does not price that changed contract.
