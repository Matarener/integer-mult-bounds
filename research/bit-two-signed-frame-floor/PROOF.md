# Two signed added frames still require eight calls

Prepared by apoursh with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. Apache-2.0.

## Model and dependencies

Use Cinna Davis's PR2085/2112 formal register model. Addresses are rational
symmetric 2-by-2 matrices, with

```
A = diag(1,0), B = diag(0,1),
P = [[1,1],[1,1]]/2, Q = [[1,-1],[-1,1]]/2.
A+B = P+Q = I2.
```

Add two arbitrary nonzero symmetric rational rank-one addresses R,S. Either
sign is allowed, including equal addresses, opposite addresses and old-label
duplicates. A real symmetric rank-one matrix has exactly one nonzero real
eigenvalue: it is positive or negative semidefinite, never indefinite.

Let Lambda be the additive lattice generated by A,B,P,R,S. Over F2[Lambda],
a paid factor is D_N(F)=I_w+([F]-1)N, where N is any binary rank-one idempotent
and F is one of the six labels. Arbitrary address-independent GL_w(F2)
maps are free. Every role initially contains arbitrary data. For any finite
w>=4 the required complete endpoint is

```
T_w = diag([A+P],[A+Q],[B+P],[B+Q],1,...,1).
```

Extra roles must be restored exactly; no clean workspace is assumed. The
objective is paid-call count only, with no physical native tariff asserted.

**Theorem.** The exact minimum is eight, even with these two signed additions.

The new result is the sign reduction below. The final positive-case
exclusion is **Josie Elliston's PR6111 theorem**, pinned in SOURCES.json;
the one-added-frame case is **apoursh's PR6079 theorem**, also pinned.
These are explicit theorem dependencies, not new proofs or fresh scientific
replays of those predecessors. The reduction itself is proved below without
relying on a search or on PR6111's finite support classification.

## 1. Necessary invariants

Normalize interspersed free maps into conjugates of paid factors and one
trailing free map. Augmentation [F]->1 sends every paid factor and T_w to
identity, so that trailing free map is identity. Conjugation preserves
rank-one idempotence. This is the normalization retained in PR6089.

Write a,b,p,q,r,s for the six nonnegative label counts, and n=a+b+p+q.
For a hypothetical word of at most seven calls using both added labels,
r,s>=1 and n<=5. The rank-one determinant identity (PR2910) gives

```
rR+sS = M = 4I2 - aA - bB - pP - qQ.                 (1)
```

Indeed det D_N(F)=[F], and all free binary invertible maps have determinant
one. The target determinant is [4I2]; monomials in this torsion-free group
algebra are distinct.

Expanding each paid factor as (I+N)+[F]N shows that every target exponent
H in {A+P,A+Q,B+P,B+Q} must be a subset sum of the paid labels, with each
occurrence used at most once. Cancellation can remove support; it cannot
create an exponent absent from every subset. This is necessary, not sufficient.

For an additive character chi:Lambda->F2, substitute [F]->1+chi(F)e,
where e^2=0. The first-order equation implies

```
number of calls detected by chi >= number of targets detected by chi. (2)
```

This follows from rank subadditivity for the sum of the detected N's, and
holds at every restored width. These determinant, support and character
methods retain their predecessor attribution.

## 2. Positive-definite residuals force positive summands

If X,Y are nonzero real symmetric rank-one matrices and X+Y is positive
definite, both X and Y are positive semidefinite. Take nonzero v in ker Y.
Then v^T X v=v^T(X+Y)v>0, so the unique nonzero eigenvalue of X is positive.
Exchange X and Y. Apply this to X=rR,Y=sS; their positive integer
multiplicities preserve signs.

Thus only non-positive-definite M could allow a negative added frame.

## 3. Every indefinite residual fails target support

Put d=a-b, e=p-q. Direct calculation gives

```
2M = [[8-n-d,-e],[-e,8-n+d]],
4 det M = (8-n)^2-d^2-e^2,
|d|+|e| <= n,   d+e = n (mod 2).                    (3)
```

If n<=4, each old address is an orthogonal projector bounded above by I2,
so M>= (4-n)I2>=0. An indefinite M therefore requires n=5. Equation (3)
then requires d^2+e^2>9. Since |d|+|e| is odd and at most five, it must be
five: if it were at most three, d^2+e^2<=9. Equality with n says there is
no cancellation within either opposite pair. Hence all five old calls use
at most one E from {A,B} and at most one G from {P,Q}. If a pair is absent,
choose either of its members. Also r=s=1.

The matrices E,G,I2 are linearly independent over Q. Define the rational
linear functional ell by ell(E)=ell(G)=0, ell(I2)=1. The four target
exponents have ell-values {0,1,1,2}, since the other two old frames are
I2-E and I2-G. Meanwhile (1) gives ell(R)+ell(S)=4.
All old calls have ell-value zero, and each of R,S occurs once. Therefore
every subset sum has ell-value in

```
{0, rho, 4-rho, 4}, where rho=ell(R).
```

This set cannot contain both one and two: containing one forces rho=1
or rho=3, and then two is absent. At least one required target lacks
support. This excludes the indefinite case even without the rank-one
assumption on R,S. There are twenty such count profiles; enumeration is
only a corroboration of this argument.

## 4. Every rank-one residual is also excluded

Since tr M=8-n>=3, a singular residual is nonzero positive rank one.
If nonzero symmetric rank-one X,Y have rank(X+Y)=1, their image lines
coincide. Write X=c uu^T,Y=t vv^T with c,t nonzero; then

```
det(X+Y)=ct (u1*v2-u2*v1)^2.
```

Thus R,S lie on the whole rational line of M, regardless of their signs.

Equation (3) and |d|+|e|<=n imply n>=4. At n=4 or n=5 the circles
d^2+e^2=16 or 9 have only axial integer solutions. Up to old-alphabet
symmetry, the possible old-call multisets are exactly:

| Old calls | Residual | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| 4E | 4Eperp | Span |
| 4E+Eperp | 3Eperp | Span |
| 3E+G+Gperp | 3Eperp | Character |

Here Eperp=I2-E, and {G,Gperp} is the other opposite pair.
The first row permits r+s<=3; the other rows force r=s=1.

In the first two rows every available label lies in span_Q(E,Eperp).
Each target combines one frame from each pair and lies outside that span,
so target support fails.

In the third row choose an integer-valued linear functional on the actual
lattice: 2*offdiag when E belongs to {A,B}, or first diagonal minus second
diagonal when E belongs to {P,Q}. It vanishes on the **entire rational**
line of Eperp as well as on E, and takes values +/-1 on G,Gperp. Thus its
reduction modulo two is well-defined even if R,S have even denominators.
It detects only the two G,Gperp calls, but all four targets. Equation (2)
gives the contradiction 2>=4.

This excludes every rank-one residual. The finite count check has twenty
full profiles: sixteen span cases and four character cases.

## 5. Conclusion and sharpness

Every residual has positive trace, so the three cases above are exhaustive.
Any hypothetical <=7-call word using both R,S must have a positive-definite
residual, which forces both added frames positive. PR6111 excludes that
case for all directions, scales and finite restored widths.

For words omitting one or both added labels, restrict to the subgroup
generated by the labels actually used and the target labels. Its group
algebra injects into F2[Lambda], so the equality also holds in that smaller
algebra. This restriction matters: unused fractional frames need not allow
old-lattice characters to extend to the original larger Lambda.

If only one extra label occurs, PR6079 already proves the eight-call floor
for arbitrary signed scaling. If neither occurs, the two old-lattice
characters chi_AB and chi_PQ separately require a+b>=4 and p+q>=4, hence
at least eight calls. Assigning the two displayed summands of each H to
its own coordinate gives the familiar eight-call word, proving sharpness.

The signed premise is meaningful at longer lengths: replace one A in this
direct word by 2A,-A on the same coordinate projector. This gives an exact
nine-call word using both a positive and a negative added frame. Thus the
reduction is a short-word statement, not an all-length positivity theorem.

## 6. Independent exact corroboration

independent.py separately enumerates all 462 six-count profiles, without
any sign or PSD filter. In basis (A,B,P), with X=rR and S=(M-X)/s, a
target subset with added multiplicities i,j satisfies

```
(s*i-r*j) X = r*s*(H-old_subset) - r*j*M.
```

A nonzero coefficient pins X; a zero coefficient gives a free solution
exactly when its right side is zero. Candidate pins are filtered only for
nonzero symmetric rank one, admitting both signs. Intersecting the four
target sets gives 435 empty profiles, seven free families, and 32 splits
across twenty other profiles. All pinned splits turn out positive, and
each free residual is positive definite. This agrees with the sign
reduction through an independent classification route. None of these
necessary-condition survivors is asserted to be a realizable word.

Finite fixtures and mutation controls are implementation evidence. The
unbounded direction, denominator and width quantifiers come from the
written reduction and explicitly credited predecessor theorems. No
independent human review or formal proof-kernel acceptance is claimed.
