## What the field proof establishes

A finite group is generated by the eight reflections because it acts faithfully
on240 signed roots. Maximizing a checked linear functional moves each real
normal into the closed chamber. Checked simple-coordinate signs identify its
zero roots with a face. Rational matrix rank and kernel extension then transfer
that precise statement to any characteristic-zero K, without an embedding of K
into R or C. A fixed coefficient column's nonempty singleton class has a rational
projective slope, so the all-fiber incidence bound applies to arbitrary K-valued
pieces, including overlaps and cancellations.

These statements discharge the specifically audited global/A2/chamber/field
premises on this exact model. They do not audit all other reductions or prove
full C5, a global decoder floor, native/physical/restoration/consumer/supplier
admission, a multiplication theorem or a new kappa. No selected record changes.
