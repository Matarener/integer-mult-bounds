# Arbitrary-plane temporal frame rails cannot improve the fixed PR1094 word

This contribution is produced by Nick Petalas. It gives a scoped proof and a
finite exhaustive obstruction, with no new kappa.

Cinna Davis / 1337isnot1337's [PR1996](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/1996)
left arbitrary binary two-plane injections open after a coordinate-plane search.
A new backward safety lemma computes the exact admissible injection subspace
at every seed, over any field. Applying it to the pinned historical word closes
the two-plane question:

- The domain has 43,435 planes and 2,525 seeds, or 109,673,375 pairs.
- There are 919,876 legal pairs, including 718,317 no-ops.
- All 201,559 changed legal pairs are propagated, yielding 45 paid profiles.
- The baseline is the unique best distinct profile. Every other profile's
  ceiling is strictly below its lower root endpoint, 1690054109/10^12.

The [proof](PROOF.md) defines the boundary, proves the backward criterion and
explains the complete invoice. [RESULT.json](RESULT.json) is generated by the
verifier's computation and binds the implementation and exact input word.
This extends the domain; it does not audit the predecessor result.

## Scope and sources

This is a historical finite-word side result. The scalar program and all
specified internal and terminal frames remain fixed. The cost comparison uses
the historical optimistic ceiling model, with all induced climb ranks and idle
mass charged. It does not transfer a ceiling to the current selected P104 word.
No native supplier, physical frame adapter, all-size theorem or new conditional
saving is claimed. Independent human and formal proof-kernel review are unclaimed.

Credit Cinna Davis's question and temporal-rail contract in PR1996, the PR1094
provider and retained gauge44 lineage, the frame-coalescence work of PR699,
PR1429 and PR1517, and Douglas Colkitt's exact historical ceiling engine.
The source notices remain unchanged. New files are Apache-2.0.

Python allocates dynamically. The finite bound is 512 address vectors, 6,237
events, 704 registers, 2,525 seeds and at most 45 aggregated profiles. Pending
closure entries are bounded by two per event; no candidate schedules are retained.
The inherited interval engine uses cached recursive logarithms with argument at
most 73 and terminating rational series. It is pinned unchanged; its numerical
proof is inherited. New code has no recursion. This records the bounded Python
allocation and inherited-engine deviations from Power of Ten.

## Open next questions

1. Does a profitable closure on a different current word use the same backward
   safety restriction? First derive its exact event/barrier contract and complete
   signed bill. The historical ceiling here cannot decide that question.
2. Can multiple coordinated injections improve a fixed word even when every
   single injection loses? Start with two overlapping future cones and charge
   every induced climb. This package neither enumerates pairs of injections nor
   proves a universal frame-inflation obstruction.
