## Frozen inputs and adaptations

`FROZEN-SHA256.json` binds every retained original byte and the newly generated
`NATIVE-REMAINDERS.json`. `ORIGINS.json` records exact original local paths or
Git commit/path expressions. These absolute paths are provenance, not runtime
dependencies. The raw R438 word appears both at its source path and inside the
original PR2653 vendored tree because the retained source checker authenticates
both locations; neither copy is silently substituted.

`frozen/old/recursive/` retains the original owner/controller programs, their
full receipts, the old local audit receipt and two proof notes. The imported
`bit_cap_copy_local.py` supplies utility functions to the owner script; its
standalone six-copy cap audit is not an executed stage. The full generic
controller performs its own actual native root/chart checks.

`frozen/upstream/` contains the exact compiler/geometry fragments, original
manifests, placement and candidate invoice, raw words and full 120-root data.
The original projector verifier's `source_check` remains active. Only its
named pure AST fragments are evaluated from upstream global/hosting/geometry
files; their full native entrypoints and optimizers are not invoked.

`frozen/reviews/owner/` and `frozen/reviews/sections/` contain two separate
implementations and their complete expected outputs. Neither independent
checker imports the historical producer. The owner checker inherits the local
operator identity; the section checker requires only the two raw geometry/word
inputs and reconstructs its own binary and rational matrices.

`ADAPTATIONS.json` lists unique string replacements. They relocate paths,
route complete comparisons through type-preserving equality, retain assertions
in AST compilation, and compile the exact authenticated source bytes. No
coefficient, domain, loop bound, expected mathematical value or negative-control
condition is changed. Every patch has a single source occurrence or replay fails.

## Validation distinctions

The registered package replay checks these local claims. Committed-byte/source-
history preflight checks provenance. The Git-free export check establishes
portability. Hosted automatic CI is reported separately in the PR after its
exact source head is read back. None is independent human acceptance, a
formal-kernel proof, an all-size machine construction or a new kappa certificate.
