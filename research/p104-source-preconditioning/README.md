# P104 source preconditioning: a rank-four obstruction

I prove that an invertible scalar source preconditioner U must satisfy
**rank(U-I)>=4** if it completes the fixed P104 channel through the retained
pooled monotone correction model. I derive a path-compression lemma for the
complete pool of 2,309 compatible endpoint pairs, then exhibit a forbidden
4-by-4 block of G/2 with determinant 1/16. See [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) for the
contract and argument.

I also implement a one-H2 source shear that removes the older pooled one-hop
cut at (71,102). I verify its exact native phase and conditional identity;
the stronger support certificate excludes its required correction. This
separates a valid source-boundary implementation from a complete correction.

Under the narrower tariff of one separately charged H2 per rank-one source
update, four updates give moment greater than 1.000088868784313991 at the
pinned selected complex saving. I stop that construction route under its
retained optimistic price model. Rank four alone supplies no price for joint
or fused preparation, and I construct no new multiplication supplier or kappa.
