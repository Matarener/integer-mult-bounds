# A joint bound for additional G20 entry captures

Use the exact source in INPUTS.json. Quotient the nine existing capture
relations before this calculation. Keep every original main field and all
four partial helpers independent. An additional entry kernel R must lie
in the common full-rank8 entrance frame. Its dimension d requires at least
d newly owned helper fields, so it removes at least d rank8 target credits.
All costs to prepare, hold and clear these fields are granted for free below.
This is a favorable relaxation of any such physical construction.

A paid query disappears only if its entire normalized source row lies in R.
The source census finds 2327 eligible queries, 1733 distinct rows and rank
mass 3640. A row with a term in a different entrance frame cannot lie in R.
A nonzero row on retained main inputs alone cannot lie in a feasible R.
The original nine capture queries are already zero and give no new credit.

Give each rank-r query 4r identical tokens. PARTITION.json.gz divides all
14560 tokens into 30 independent sets over F2. Each independent set meets
a d-dimensional subspace in at most d vectors. Thus the sum of the ranks
of every query killed by R is at most 30d/4 = 15d/2. This statement covers
all subspaces at once, including relations absent from the query list,
all choices of pivots, and relations that only become useful in combination.

Put t=1-C. For 0<C<=1/100, r^t<=r and 8^t>=8^(99/100)>15/2.
The last strict inequality is checked by integer powers. The saved
recursive moment is at most 15d/2, while the lost target credit is at least
d*8^t. Every d>0 loses. The unchanged caller repeats this local difference;
nonnegative setup fees cannot reverse it. In particular this covers the
PR5586 ordinary BIT target C=0.0037 and the PR5512 source value.

This certificate is the best bound on the chosen quarter-unit grid:
DENSITY-LOWER.json.gz has 12094 distinct tokens in rank 417. Hence 29
independent sets cannot cover the full token multiset. The exact fractional
capacity lies in [6047/834,15/2]; its exact value is not needed for the
strict rejection. A failed partition search is not used as proof.

## Partial-helper epoch controls

The four partial helpers are 462,463,466,468. Their source queries have
normalized rows h+x and h, at ranks 3 and 1 respectively; x is original
main input 47,52,27,92 respectively. Owning each h costs one rank4 target.
The early-zero variant removes four rank1 queries. Clearing each h again
just before its first source read removes four rank3 queries instead.
Both variants preserve all retained RAW columns and erase every owned
DIRTY column. The literal paired HEAD/TAIL replay checks them modulo 101
and 101^2. Both lose for every t>0: 4*3^t<4*4^t, and 4<4*4^t.

These controls do not classify all possible partial-helper completions,
new capture epochs, changed scalar words or native operations. They show
why moving this particular capture point does not fund a larger supplier.
The static entry-kernel theorem is separate and has the precise full8
scope above. No global BIT lower bound or new kappa follows.
