and independent, including all required dirty inputs. Preserve PR3609's
distinct roles, writable/restored Y access, actual initial and final frames,
and owned private-zero obligations. A **uniform guard** means one common
fixed-point unit for every stored field throughout the specified program.

## 2. Representation refinement preserves the numerical input

Write an initial physical component as $x=2^{-f}m$, $m\in\mathbb Z$.
At guard $g$, store $n=2^gm$ with unit $2^{-(f+g)}$. Then

$$2^{-(f+g)}n=2^{-f}m=x.$$

This is a change of integer representation, not multiplication of the
mathematical input by $2^g$. Keeping the old unit while multiplying mantissas
would instead scale the numerical problem and would require a different
input/output intertwiner. Appending low zero bits is an operation or a
producer-format obligation; it is not automatically free.

The refined execution computes the source's ideal Gaussian word exactly. It
is not asserted to equal a separate execution of the signed-floor program on
the unrefined f-grid with arbitrary inputs outside its exactness domain.

Put $R=\mathbb Z[i]$, $\pi=1+i$, and
$B_9=\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}1&0\\1&\pi\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)^{\otimes9}R^{512}$.
The Newton criterion says a function belongs to $B_9$ exactly when its
Möbius coefficient at subset $S$ is divisible by $\pi^{|S|}$. Consequently
$\pi^{13}R^{512}\subseteq\pi^4B_9=4B_9$, since every Möbius coefficient of
such a vector is divisible by $\pi^{13}$. Also $128$ is a unit times
$\pi^{14}$, so

$$128R^{512}\subseteq4B_9\subseteq2B_9\subseteq B_9.$$

At guard seven, every initial mantissa therefore satisfies PR3609's common
sufficient domain: 104 Y fields in $4B_9$, 78 N fields in $2B_9$, and 176
other fields in $B_9$. The pinned PR3382 module theorem, PR3416 native
covariance, and PR3609's source-bound scalar induction give exact execution
of the entire combined word, including all eight literal private erasures.
This reuses those theorems; it does not re-certify their historical suites.

## 3. Six bits suffice for the standalone entrance

Each nonzero entry of a coordinate C gate or its inverse is a Gaussian unit
times $\pi/2$. A monomial contributes only a permutation and powers of $i$.
Every matrix entry of a product with $t$ such C factors is therefore in

$$ (\pi/2)^t R\ \subseteq\ 2^{-\lceil t/2\rceil}R.$$

The argument is termwise over matrix-product paths: summing terms preserves
their common Gaussian ideal. It does not assume that separated C factors
commute. A prefix of $r$ actual coordinate gates has at most $r$ factors;
its two native wrappers add none.

At a scalar cut, the actual physical value has the usual source-bound form

$$\sum_s c_s J_HJ_{I_s}^{-1}x_s,$$

where $H$ is the current frame, $I_s$ the actual entrance frame, and $c_s$
the exactly traced rational scalar coefficient. At a native internal prefix,
replace $J_H$ by that prefix times its entrance chart. A sufficient guard for
the term is

$$\left\lceil\frac{\dim H+\dim I_s+r}{2}\right\rceil-v_2(c_s),$$

using the full native rank $r$ to bound every earlier native stage. At scalar
cuts set $r=0$. The new checker applies this test separately to every stored
row and every scalar product before an ADD or source half/double.

In the actual entrance, dirty-X coefficients remain integers. Their entrance
charts have dimension one; the full finish contributes at most nine factors.
Y coefficients are half-integers, their entrance frame is empty, and finish
prefixes contribute at most nine factors. The other 41 dirty banks have empty
entrance charts and integer coefficients. The authenticated per-term audit
has maximum guard six. In particular, all 887 unit additions, 103 temporary
source halves and 103 restoring doublings have integral increments on this
grid; no cancellation with the target is used to excuse a fractional donor.

Evaluate the ideal program first. All before/after C-pair values and all
scalar increments lie on the six-bit grid. For a C pair, integral ideal
outputs imply equality with the credited signed-floor lift. Some internal
midpoint divisions of that lift are floors, not separately exact divisions;
their compensating formula is what equals the ideal C pair. This distinction
prevents assuming that arbitrary rounded execution is linear. The induction
also covers negative mantissas and values partly written within a pair.

## 4. Source-bound lower witnesses

Set the physical input $Y2=2^{-f}\delta_0$ and every other initial public
field to zero. In the entrance, the actual scalar program gives the pivot
$X0$ coefficient $-Y2/2$, restores Y2, and applies the new full-frame recipe.
The retained projection never reads or writes X0. Hence its required final
coordinate zero is

$$X0_{\rm final}(0)=2^{-f}\frac{-1+i}{64}.$$

The checker derives the coefficient from the serialized entrance, runs the
actual new rank-nine recipe at guards five, six and seven, and compares the
complete 512-entry column with the direct tensor formula. Guard six returns
integer coordinate $(-1,1)$; guard five rejects a nonintegral C output. The
odd six-bit mantissa proves that both the standalone module and the full
program's exact final output format require at least six uniform bits.

For the full execution, original event 2142 transports D18 into the full
frame. It survives as combined event 3000. Its exact Y2 coefficient is
$-1/4$, so

$$D18(0)=2^{-f}\frac{-1+i}{128}.$$

Backward dependence extraction selects 36 retained events and nine initial
roles: D18 and Y0, Y2, Y44, Y51, Y71, Y79, Y97, Y98. Before executing that
cone, the new scalar/frame audit checks that all nine are restored to their
initial value rows and frames after all 1,238 entrance operations. This is
the explicit justification for projecting the witness through the new prefix.

At guard seven, the actual cone completes 2,304 literal/ideal pairs in four
active natives and returns the complete expected D18 column, with integer
coordinate $(-1,1)$. Twenty-four zero natives use their exact zero action.
At guard six, the first 35 cone events and 2,048 active pairs complete. At
event 2142, bit zero and address zero, the pair inputs are $i$ and $0$;
the twice-ideal outputs are $(-1+i,1+i)$. They are not Gaussian integers
after division by two. This is an actual literal obstruction, not a rejection
merely for failing the stronger $4B_9$ sufficient entrance.

The cone is not claimed as a numerical replay of all preceding events.
Even if some other six-bit path failed sooner, this required ideal value
already prevents a complete exact six-bit execution. Seven-bit sufficiency
comes from Section 2's all-input theorem, not extrapolation of the witness.

## 5. Every final output admits six bits

The exact combined scalar trace checks that every final coefficient has
denominator one or two. It also checks all 358 final frames, maximum initial
frame dimension one, and maximum final dimension nine. Thus each relative
endpoint chart $J_FJ_I^{-1}$ has at most ten C factors. Section 3's elementary
Gaussian-ideal argument puts its entries in $2^{-5}R$; multiplication by a
half-integer puts every output in $2^{-(f+6)}R$.

All final mantissas of a seven-bit execution are consequently even. One exact
halving per real component, together with changing the unit from
$2^{-(f+7)}$ to $2^{-(f+6)}$, preserves the output value. This does not permit
discarding all seven fractional bits. Required frames remain those of the
source: in particular all 104 final Y frames have dimension eight. Logical
Y restoration in the full word is not a return in the empty physical frame.
The standalone entrance instead returns every Y in its original empty frame.

## 6. Magnitude, scratch and signed integer capacity

Let $H$ bound the absolute value of **each initial real and imaginary
component**. Each initial field has real Euclidean norm at most
$\sqrt{1024}H=32H$. Chart maps and native prefixes are unitary. The exact
combined scalar trace has maximum row $\ell_1$ norm 103, first attained at
entrance operation 929 on X17; its separately checked scalar-product maximum
is 30. Thus every complete field and every complete native stage has real
Euclidean norm at most

$$M=103\cdot32H=3296H.$$

The same component bound holds during partially written stages: a component
comes from its preceding or completed pair. Completed disjoint native-pair
updates preserve the field norm. The four-term numerator of any real C output
has coefficient-vector Euclidean norm two, so its magnitude is at most
$2M=6592H$. This uses a **whole-field norm**, not the false inference that
four arbitrary components individually bounded by M sum to at most 2M.

The signed-floor implementation also fits this scratch bound. Its differences
and quarter-turn copies are bounded by 2M; its midpoint is a floor of the
average of two integer components and is bounded by M. Completed C outputs
are bounded by M. Integer-ADD results are covered by their traced rows;
source halves and restoring doubles are explicitly checked. Basic monomial
permutations and phases preserve magnitudes; a simple arithmetic SWAP's sum
temporary is bounded by 2M. This is a sufficient bound for these declared
finite scalar schedules. An unrelated recursive native implementation still
needs its own internal-work proof and bill.

For a concrete convention, let each original-grid signed mantissa have at
most $b\ge1$ magnitude bits, so its absolute value is at most $2^b-1$ and
its usual signed width is $b+1$. With guard seven, sufficient signed widths
are **$b+20$ for fields and $b+21$ for literal scratch**. The corresponding
standalone guard-six widths are $b+19$ and $b+20$. These bounds include sign,
fractional refinement and magnitude growth. They follow from
$3296<2^{12}$ and $6592<2^{13}$; fractional guard bits alone do not supply
integer headroom. Zero input is trivial. No minimal magnitude-width claim
is made, and Python's unbounded integers are not a physical-capacity proof.
