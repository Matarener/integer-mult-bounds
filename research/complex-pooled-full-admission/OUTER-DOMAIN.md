
The exact positive margins are

```

```

Every positive child rank is less than 73. Thus the complete moment at
`sigma` is strictly smaller than the already certified moment at `1-C`.
This includes every restoration and idle child; no child is removed to
obtain the inequality. The physical global checker also evaluates that
moment directly.

The ordering `tau<sigma` matters. At `sigma=1-C`, it fails: the inequality
used in PR3599's internal-node proof reverses. One may prove a different
two-case bound, but it is unnecessary. The choice above permits literal
reuse of that stopped-tree proof. At an internal node `e>=g^eta`,

```
e^tau <= g^(-eta(sigma-tau)) e^sigma.
```

The weighted-node sum pays all internal work by
`O(V g^(sigma-eta(sigma-tau)) (log p)^tau)`. Frontier weights and the actual
elementary leaves pay `O(V g^(sigma+eta(1-sigma)))`. The exact gaps below
`alpha_root` are respectively

```

965399/20000000000000.
```

The unchanged reserved-leaf power `.998` has gap `27/100000`.
For failed root guards, `500(1-alpha_root)=173/200<1`, so the complete
literal root also fits `O(V g^alpha_root log p)`. Fixed constants may be
large and depend on the source, stock and moment slack. No practical
threshold or source-independent constant is asserted.

Retain `d_root=g`, `K=floor(g^(1/500))`, the encoded stopping power `10^6`,
the full-depth stock, and `g<=floor(p^.997)`. The new family count changes

## 2. Complete Fourier, Gaussian and CRT tariffs

The support caller preserves the complete sparse-router rate `tau`. Its
completed invoice is

```
O(V [g^alpha_root log p + (g+1)^tau (log p)^(1+tau)]).
```

The synthetic Fourier caller batches at the actual cap `p^.997`. For
`D=Theta(p^.997)`, `ell=Theta(p^.003)` and total address width `Theta(p)`,
all rounds, signed diagonals and endpoint reversal therefore cost

```
O(V p^Phi log p),   Phi = 1-.997(1-alpha_root) = .99827519.
```

The cap is retained. Replacing it by one would discard a real outer loss.
The complete full-support router has

```
beta = 41544341/41616125,
Phi-beta = 3273151/33292900000000 > 0.
```

Its additional `log^tau p` is absorbed by this particular positive gap.
It is not charged against the much larger Gaussian allowance.

Each complete physical chirp costs three full Fourier calls plus
`O(Nc(ell+1)p^(8/5)+2^ell p^3+poly(p))`, where `Nc` is its coefficient
count. Since `V=Theta(Nc p)`, its extra online power is `.603`.
The physical Gaussian tensor additionally pays all short-field moves,
halos, catalogue scans and coefficient setup. Its Gaussian payload power
is at most `.9981`, and the four canonical gathers use `beta`. Both are
strictly below `Phi`. The producer's `poly(t_max,p)` cost is retained:
`t_max=2^Theta(p^.003)` makes it `2^o(p)`, which the complete tensor volume
`2^Theta(p)` pays. Catalogue construction is not repeated per input line.

Each CRT conversion has the full invoice

```
O(V p^beta (log p)^(1+tau) + D V + C T(n_address+R)
  + C D T poly(n_address)/t_min + poly(p)) + F_q + F_source.
```

Here `n_address,R=Theta(p)` and `t_min=2^Theta(p^.003)`. Thus padding costs
`O(V)`, the offset term is `o(V)`, and `D V` has power `.997<beta`.
The integer construction uses three conversions, each with its own complete
router invoice and both padding passes. A fixed collection of native owners
is compiled once; it is not reconstructed for each conversion.

The complete integer schedule has three physical Gaussian calls (and hence
nine full synthetic Fourier calls), three complete CRT conversions, and
`O(S)` actual scalar operations at `O(p)` width. The exact Karatsuba word
contract bounds the latter, including pointwise products and `S^2` scaling,
by `O(S p^(8/5))=O(V p^.6)`. Linear copying, packing and carries remain paid.
The number of persistent complete packet owners is fixed; their total
high-water storage over this schedule is `O(V)+F_q+F_source`.

Finally

```
(1-1/580)-Phi = 1949/2900000000 > 0.
```

This gap absorbs the remaining `log p` when `p=Theta(log n)` and `V=Theta(n)`.
The old physical root exponent `.99827101` would give
`Phi_old=.99827619697>1-1/580`; it does not certify this target. The new
complex saving is used in the changed root bound.

## 3. The production parameter domain is nonempty

Use the exact packed-box construction in OUTER-JOIN, with
`p=6 ceil(log2 n)`. It has actual scalar-axis count
`D=Theta(p^.997)`, target exponent `ell=Theta(p^.003)`, and target lengths
in `{2^ell,2^(ell-1)}`, with a final `2^ell` axis. Its constructor arguments
are `axes=D-1`, `dimensions=D`, and `d=D+1`. The latter is the Gaussian
parameter, not the root's selected width `d_root=g`.

Retain the source-prime interval

```
(1-1/(2d)) t_i < s_i < (1-1/(4d)) t_i.
```

It gives the exact strict production ratio
`1/(4d)<(t_i-s_i)/s_i<1/(2d-1)`. The product satisfies
`S/T>(1-1/(2d))^D>=1-D/(2d)>1/2`. The retained Baker-Harman-Pintz
prime-interval theorem and the source's packing argument supply `D` distinct
primes once `12d^2<t_min^(19/40)` and its fixed threshold hold. This eventually
holds because `log t_min=Theta(p^.003)`, while `log d=O(log p)`.
The intervals for the two target sizes are disjoint. Trial division over
the full intervals costs at most
`O(D t_max^(3/2) poly(p))=2^o(p)`. The cited prime theorem is a mathematical
input, not established by this package's finite checks.

Choose integer `a=floor(p^.001)` and `u=a^2=Theta(p^.002)`.
The exact production policy is

```

H=4096p^3, L=2^ceil(log2(p^8)),
w=ceil(sqrt(ceil(16p/u)))+2.
```

Every required production guard holds eventually:

| `p>=101`, `a>=2`, `u<p` | Positive powers and exponent `.002<1` |
| `u>=ceil(log2(8p))` | `p^.002` exceeds `log p` |
| `u d<=p` | `u d=Theta(p^.999)=o(p)` |
