## Two corrected draft reuse gaps

The separate adversarial context found two failures in early versions of this
successor, before publication. These are not attributed to PR4088 or PR4165.

First, an existing preflight's successful source reconstruction did not by
itself authenticate the cached admission driver. The final gate requires the
exact inherited engine SHA256
`e69184d235d8790a3c520977dd44dcfb73a99d56989825485f922ff61059c8b1`,
the exact 676-file source inventory, adapter bindings, three review stages and
all 95 retained engine/helper/native-cache inputs. A changed engine is rejected
before the source adaptation can consume it.

Second, a native source hash and successful stage flags did not commit the
produced operators. The phase mutation changed a warp's left phase modulo 4
and refreshed recipe references. The source's exact recipe checker rejected
the resulting operator. The final reuse gate compares every scientific product
to fixed commitments, including when all internal references are coherently
updated. It never derives an expected commitment from the supplied cache.

The regression includes the latter mutation, missing-stage and endpoint
controls. The clean release records the actual native executions and inherited
controls separately; a regression success is not substituted for those runs.

  independent reviews and the extended inherited admission.
* **Separate AI checks:** an independent event/span accounting method and an
  adversarial implementation review. These are not independent human review.
