## 2. The finite bridge preserves complete jobs and actual ownership

The reviewed native precompilation theorem is
[`../architecture/FINITE-PRECOMPILATION.md`](../architecture/FINITE-PRECOMPILATION.md).

the independent chronological reconstruction and challenge cases are in
[`AUDIT.md`](AUDIT.md) and `FINITE-DAG-AUDIT.json`.

At a fixed ambient rank `n`, every productive source is a complete arbitrary
array on all `n` coordinates. Its designated rank-`s` job is supplemented
by a paid complementary rank-`n-s` job. The complement is run first.
The completed mixed word then supplies the designated endpoint, and the
product is the full transform. This uses autonomy of the completed endpoint;
it does not require the complement to commute with intermediate operations
of the mixed circuit.

All normalized source counts and all occurrences of lower-rank batches are
cleared by one explicit finite integer multiplier. An ordered batch uses
distinct physical fields. Each recursive demand occurs strictly after its
input exists and before its parent continues; every recursive rank is
smaller. The independent graph audit rejects aliased input slots, incomplete
batch clearing and cycles. It includes paid demands on owned fields.

A parent-owned copied center can be passed as one arbitrary input to a
lower-rank unit. Correlation with another array is harmless: the child
identity holds for every assignment of its distinct physical input slots.
The parent copy remains present until the actual call returns, and its
eventual erasure is legal only after the prescribed restoration. A fresh
child center has a separate owner tag. No copied center becomes another
productive source, and simultaneous suspended centers are not merged into
one bank merely because they have the same recursion depth. Conservatively
allocate one full center per finite mixed-unit instance and charge every
initialization, pass and erasure. These are fixed constants of the compiled
uniform word.

### Explicit termination and one specified winning rank

Let `L(n)=P_n/B_n` be the exact average number of rank-one leaves per
productive full-rank source, including complements and owned-field calls.
For the complex case the strict mixed test is at saving `0.002305`; for BIT
it is at `0.002769`. Put `t=1-C_test`. Cancellation in the finite flow gives
an inequality, not an assumed equality:

```
sum_e x_e*e^t <= sum_s w_s*s^t,
L(n) = sum_s w_s*L(n-s) + sum_e x_e*L(e),
sum_s w_s = 1.
```

The separate positive rank-one surplus gives `L(n)<=n` by induction. With
source ranks `n/m<s<=n`, exit ranks obey
`E(n)<=r_max*(n/m)^theta`, where `theta=log(r_max)/log(k_min)<1`.
Use `theta_bar=9/10` for complex and `15/16` for BIT. These upper bounds are
proved by the exact integer comparisons `5^10<6^9` and `7^16<8^15`.
Writing `h=1-theta_bar` gives `m*E(n)/n<16*n^-h`.

For the desired uniform exponent `z`, set `z0=(t+z)/2` and
`delta=z0-t>0`. Concavity shows that the source complements leave at least
`eta*sum_s w_s*s^z0`, where `eta=z0*m^(z0-1)>1/4`. Choose

```
H0 = ceil((3+4*delta)/(h*delta)),
n0 = 2^H0,
A  = n0^(1-z0).
```

Then the exit coefficient is at most `1/8` for every `n>=n0`. The base
bound `L(n)<=n` covers `n<=n0`; strong induction gives `L(n)<=A*n^z0`
for every rank. Finally

```
H1 = floor(H0*(1-z0)/(z-z0))+1,
N  = 2^H1
```

ensures `L(N)<N^z`. Independent directed rational logarithm/exponential
enclosures reproduce both strict mixed inequalities and the exact ranks:

| Family | `H0` | `H1` |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Complex | 60,000,040 | 276,540,184,361 |
| BIT | 10,666,731 | 6,552,928,412 |

This is an effective specification of one uniform word, without relying on
an unperformed search for its first success. Its finite multiplicities and
instruction count are computed by the specified integer recursion. The
asymptotic algorithm fixes this word before its input length varies.

## 4. BIT uses one delayed ambient cover and one common weight

The argument reviewed is
[`../bit/DELAYED-COVER-BRIDGE.md`](../bit/DELAYED-COVER-BRIDGE.md).
`BIT-BRIDGE-AUDIT.json` supplies a separate exact implementation audit.
The essential order is to precompile the entire finite projector program
first, and only then apply a single whole-ambient `GL_N` cover and external
weight. Internal jobs are not prematurely lowered to differently weighted
canonical children.

For a rational idempotent `P` in ambient dimension `N`, write

```
D_P = [[I-P, P*C_N], [C_N*P, I-C_N*P*C_N]],
Q_S = diag(S, C_N*S*C_N).
```

Direct multiplication gives `Q_S D_Q Q_S^-1=D_(SQS^-1)` and
`D_P D_(I-P)=D_I`, including nonsymmetric projectors. Equal-rank rational
idempotents admit a fixed rational matcher `S` by matching image and kernel
bases. After the finite program is fixed, choose an odd prime avoiding all
its finitely many denominators, determinants and rank witnesses. All lifts
use these same rational matrices, so this choice is independent of the
eventual column multiplicity.

Use the full cover `g in GL_N(Z/q^w Z)`. To match a parent request `P` to a
child endpoint `Q`, route that child's complete role fiber by `g -> gS`,
execute the entire child, and undo the route. Its autonomous endpoint is
exactly `D_(gPg^-1)`. Separate target roles may use separate matchers: each
route is a bijection of a full fiber carrying arbitrary data. Native port
charts and local helper ownership remain inside the schematic program;
they are not silently discarded when the ambient dimension grows.

This is a paired whole-job construction. It does not implement arbitrary
isolated dense address maps by renaming rows, and it does not assume a
generic RAM-to-tape simulation.

The inherited weight `A` is common to all fresh roles and independent of
the current complete cover field. Consequently `Lambda_A=diag(A,I)`
commutes with every internal role route and every pointwise scalar/COPY
operation. The completed source/complement endpoint is therefore the
required weighted full interchange. Only final rank-one leaves are lowered
to weighted canonical children. Their pivot weights depend on this parent
cover row, which is ancestor data when the child's fresh fields are exposed.
Different leaves can have different weights without violating the common
weight requirement inside any one child.

At `f` columns the inherited reversed pairing is essential. The actual
lift uses `P tensor I_f` with full tail reversal `C_(Nf)`, or equivalently
conjugates the straightforward tensor lift by the blockwise `C_f` reversal.
The independent audit checks this on nonsymmetric rational projectors and
rejects dropping that reversal. It also checks arbitrary common external
weights depending on axes and columns. Current-cover-dependent weights
remain forbidden.

The independent native replay covers all 636 rational frames, 2,386 nested
MOVE pairs, both 661-column local words, 111 ports and 666 embedded charts,
3,073 helper owners, and all 63 paid COPY-center calls. The rank-50 full-job
example has `W=3739`, `P=186725` and surplus `50*3739-186725=225`.
Additional checks include nonsymmetric rational matchers, a genuine

tests retain all q-adic digits of the matcher; dropping its higher digits

### Paid cover, atom conversion, and ordinary endpoint

Right multiplication on `g=g0+qG1` is a finite low-type permutation plus a
fixed affine high-matrix map with its correct carry. The retained native
cover-routing lemma charges it as `O(V*w^(1-a_old))`. The finite number of
routers changes the constant, not this exponent. All bad generic classes
receive their actual fallback. With the explicit choice

```

```

and the finite denominator exclusions, the complete covered moment is at
most `400001/400002<1`. This includes the full fallback bill without
subtracting the good-class calls or assuming bad classes are independent.

Use atom width `w=ceil(e^0.003)` and the retained old ordinary saving
`a_old=384599/10^10`. The resulting saving is

```
(1-0.003)*0.002750 + 0.003*a_old
    = 27418653797/10^13
    > 0.0027.
```

The linear adapters are paid because `0.003` exceeds this saving. The
borrowed row stock satisfies `log S(e)=O(e^0.003 log e)` and is lower order
because `0.003<1-a_BIT`. Its complete borrowed field is restored. Padding
of full rows is performed once for the whole invocation and removed after
the completed endpoint. Numeric padding to atom lengths costs only a
fixed `q`-dependent factor.

The ordinary wrapper uses two completed reversed calls and three charged
bank additions, in chronological order:

```
D <- D-H; F_n; D <- D+H; F_n; D <- H-D.
```

For `(H,D)` this gives successively `(H,D-H)`, `(C(D-H),CH)`,
`(C(D-H),CD)`, `(D,D-H)`, `(D,H)`. Thus it really supplies the ordinary
interchange, at constant multiplicative overhead and `O(Vn)` extra work.
No unshown batch or free half-swap is required.

## 5. The changed row reserve fits the support-local outer wrapper

This is an essential functional obligation, not another numerical moment
test. The applicable retained wrapper is the global support-local wrapper
in `vendor/pr1673/capped-phase-fusion/SUPPLIER-SUPPORT-AUDIT.md` and its
`SUPPORT-ADVERSARIAL-AUDIT.md`, under the current outer package. Its completed
supplier calls cover the **whole global dyadic tensor**. Calling separately
inside fixed coarse-label microboxes would not satisfy the interface.

For a phase with `h` selected bits, take `G=4*ceil(log2 p)+6` and complete
neighborhoods of width `K=128G`, one inside each selected original axis.
The axis length grows like `p^(1-epsilon)`, so every fixed multiple of
`log p` fits eventually. Exposing the complete neighborhoods moves at most
`2hK` bit positions. Native binary matrices have fixed rank, so their
column lifts factor into finitely many parallel row additions on these
selected bits. Unit phases have fixed formulas of the same selected-bit
support. They do not introduce dependence on unrelated coarse labels.

Reserve whole selected chunks for the two front controls, back control and
row prefix. **Do not first transform their selected bits.** The completed
active invocation is identity on every reserved coordinate for arbitrary
contents; those selected-axis kernels can be completed in later shrinking
phases. The finite guard condition used by the retained router is directly
satisfied by `K=128G`; no claim is made that this constant ratio satisfies
the older, stronger wording `K/log p -> infinity`.

The new BIT stock cannot simply be inserted into the old statement
`O(log h)` for all nested reserves. Its own ordinary full-field interface
supplies its rows internally. Alternatively, the following conservative
external product bound explicitly covers simultaneous stock. A BIT call
has width at most `C*h*(log p)^2`. Its logarithmic stock is
`O(h^0.003*(log p)^(2*0.003+1))`. Including a further `O(log h)` nesting
factor and the complex stock gives, after enlarging one fixed constant,

```
q0(h,p) <= C*(log2 h + h^0.003*(log2 p)^3 + 1)
```

reserved complete chunks. This bound retains the **product** of stock
requirements; using their maximum would be invalid. Since `0.003<1`, there
is an explicit fixed polylogarithmic threshold `H(p)` above which
`q0(h,p)+O(1)<=h/64`. For example one may enlarge a fixed multiple of
`[1024*C*(log2 p)^3]^(1/(1-0.003))` to cover the logarithmic term and all
finite-template base thresholds. Consequently

```
R(h) <= 3h/128 + q0(h,p) + O(1) <= h/16
```

for all sufficiently large active phases. The remaining `h<=H(p)` bits are
handled individually in `O(V*polylog p)` time. Thus successive active
widths shrink by at least a factor 16, and their sublinear costs sum to a
constant multiple of the first phase's cost.

Each phase pads its complete row count once by a factor below two. Its
completed endpoint preserves the row coordinate, so appended zero rows
can be deleted before inverse exposure and before the next phase. This
prevents an accumulating factor of two per phase. The exposure is restored
before any next diagonal depending on coarse labels is applied.

The support router's additional control reservoirs occupy
`O(h*(log p)^2)` positions and fit the full `Theta(p)`-bit tensor because
`group_beta<1`. A complete untouched `log^2 p` suffix is chosen and placed
first for short-record grouping; all subsequent reservoirs and routers
stay in its complement. The retained full-span route handles the bounded
regime where this gap argument fails. A single original axis is never
asked to hold all of the `hG` control bits.
