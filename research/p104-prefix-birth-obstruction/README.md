# A paid-deletion requirement for birth-lowered P104 markets

Claim type: scoped accounting proof with finite continuation certificates.
No new kappa, supplier admission or selected-frontier change.

For the PR6030 geometry retained in DreamingOfClouds's PR6170 toolkit, lowering
a market helper's birth into its associated hub cannot improve the recursive
moment if every old paid MOVE and all other productive endpoints remain.
This holds even with arbitrary scalar resynthesis. Each changed helper loses
at least

    18 [4^t + 3^t - 7^t] > 0,  for every 0<t<1.

The lower bound is sharp for the inherited recursive ledger. A single
three-rank ascent attains it. Thus a coupled proposal needs an old paid
transition deletion or another justified target/boundary gain. Merely moving
births downward and using scalar coupling does not pay.

The [proof](PROOF.md) gives the exact contract and the general deficit
`18[s^t+d^t-(s+d)^t]`. Classical concavity is credited background. The new
contribution is the changed-boundary screen, its sharp P104 bill and its
explicit continuation family. It addresses a subfamily of PR6170 Lemma 11's
untested bottom-to-market opening. It does not audit that lemma or settle the
whole opening. The initial claim's construction search stops at this negative
budget gate.

geometry and new splice identities; it does not exhaust scalar programs.
The source pricing rules and arbitrary-input identity are cited premises.
Sharpness concerns the recursive bill, not a complete physical cost optimum.
The word is the selected PR6030 snapshot. Later supplier proposals need their
own source binding, even when the general inequality applies.

Lean, Docker or proof-kernel verification is claimed. The five selected
framework assumptions remain. Independent human and formal proof-kernel review
are unclaimed. A candidate that removes or replaces an old MOVE, changes another
productive endpoint or obtains new independent target credit needs a new bill.

fixture is released before the next; no unbounded search or recursive routine
