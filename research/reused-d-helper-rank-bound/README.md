# Shared-D controllers lose after productive helper reuse is charged

This scoped proof rules out a favorable raw power moment for one exact
characteristic-two FULL/THIN alphabet built from the retained compact BIT
source. It permits arbitrary scalar coupling, every call order, and reuse of
full-rank productive helpers. It does not use a fixed query simplex or a finite
search exclusion. No new kappa or supplier is constructed.

The required endpoint is QD on n fields with a single shared D. If f FULL calls
have v source main pairs each, s is the total number of reused H_(a+1) helpers,
and h is the funded thin H1 count, the proof gives

```text
M=2vf+s >= n,
h >= 2n-2vf-2s,
Delta_1 <= (a-1)(n-M)-delta*f < 0.
```

Here delta is the source's paid rank mass minus its helper mass and 2va.
The integer a is distinct from the formal nilpotent d.
The FULL coefficient conditions and permitted scalar bases are explicit in
[PROOF.md](PROOF.md). For the retained source, v=111, a=7 and delta=63.
The four partial H4 helpers are retained exactly. For every `0<=t<=1`,

```text
Delta_t <= -47*f*8^(t-1) < 0.
```

This is the complete target-minus-paid moment in the stated model, including
every reused helper debit. It cannot justify an improved multiplication
exponent. The retained positive source caller uses distinct fresh D blocks
and is outside the theorem; its rank surplus 753 is checked as a control.
