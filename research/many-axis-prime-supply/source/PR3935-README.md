# Complete physical Gaussian tensor transform

I connect the retained Gaussian expansion, complete chirp and principal-core
compression into one source-box transform. I include fixed-cut padding, four
canonical full-support gathers/scatters, short mixed-radix moves, periodic
halos, original-axis coefficient pages, source-core assembly and exact
normalization restoration. The caller preserves its borrowed data and pages
and restores a fixed inventory of owned tapes.

Under the stated production and inherited transfer contracts, I derive

\[
\|\widetilde{\mathscr F}x-RF_{\boldsymbol s}x\|_\infty
<2^\gamma(Dp^2+4\sqrt2M+22+2D)2^{-p},\qquad
\gamma=D(2u+\lceil\log_2(32p)\rceil+1).
\]

I use `D=d-1` actual resampled axes and retain the source's `R F_s`
convention. With the caller-family growth laws and complete charges in
[PROOF.md](PROOF.md), I obtain the conditional `O(V p^.99827619697 log p)`
bound, plus fixed native compilation. I retain the exact B1/sparse/Fourier
premises, polynomial coefficient setup and the production locality domain.
I leave the selected frontier unchanged.

For the explicit finite case I generated the actual p48/P80 catalogue for
two source7/target8 axes. I compared the complete49-input,64-middle,
49-output forward path against a separate natural-coordinate staged model.
All4900 output bits matched, with12 nonzero restored outputs. I also checked
changed-axis rejection, the half-disk entrance, positive reuse after failure
and protected output. The opposite-direction consumer also matched its
complete staged reference. [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md) separates those author
checks from the final committed-export and hosted receipts.

The finite parameters use reduced halos and establish staged conformance.
They do not meet the production locality domain or establish the production
Fourier-error guarantee. I have not executed the production-prime supplier,
implemented the CRT/integer recovery boundary, or obtained independent human
or formal-kernel review.

## Verification boundary

I retain the actual generated coefficient return as an immutable archive.
The producer check regenerates it and compares the entire packet byte for
byte. The numerical checks authenticate that packet before passing it to
the actual tensor consumer. I split the checks at this existing public page
port after a combined attempt reached600 seconds; I keep both setup and
consumer costs in the verification budget. See [CATALOGUE.md](CATALOGUE.md)
for parameters, hashes, provenance and invalidation.

The checker in `tensor_model.py` uses natural coordinates, reads the emitted
factors and preserves rounding after each axis. It makes no physical
numerical calls. The physical implementation uses encoded metadata and
payload tapes under sealed constructors and a no-decoding harness.

I also check dense-support permutations, unequal mixed-radix fields, seams,
overlaps, core assembly, production policy metadata and arithmetic boundaries.
[ARITHMETIC.md](ARITHMETIC.md) describes the exact fixed-digit leaves,
streamed setup division, encoded small-order radicals and guarded
scalar-width adaptation. I also use bounded loops for signed records up to
4096 bits, preserving the wider-record fallback. I retain
the general-order producer and asymptotic word bounds. The p48 metadata
specialization proves three exact fixed inequalities and changes no numerical
coefficient or child result.

For the six registered checks, use:

```sh
python3.12 -B scripts/research_package.py --package research/physical-gaussian-tensor
```

I bound each command by at most 400 seconds and their declared total by 1200
seconds. I record measured stages in [EVIDENCE.md](EVIDENCE.md) and exact
publication receipts in the linked investigation; runtime depends on the host.
