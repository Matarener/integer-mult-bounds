# The actual nonadapted d=3 invoice, t=2

Prepared by conorholds with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. Apache-2.0.

## Contract and retained inputs

Use the actual-basis contract in D1: C is the eight-dimensional P112 root code,
S contains C with dimension ten, F is an actual basis, every restricted dual
x_j is nonzero, and a member is corrected precisely when x_j is proportional
to a frame root f_i and its binary support span contains the excluded label
z_i. Put T=span of all S-words of binary support rank at most seven, assume
dim(C intersect T)=3 and sum_F w<=72, and write I=sum_F w-n_c.

The inherited RAW-SHAPE reduction gives dim T=5, and closes all r>=2
and all eta=r>0. Here r=dim T-|F intersect T| and
eta=sum_inside w+8r-cost(T). We close the remaining r=1, eta>1 case.

The following retained inputs are used, with no new finite classification:

* Global support capacities through rank seven are 1,2,4,8,12,20,36;
  code distance is 53; a rank-one/two live span contains no frame label.
* A seven-flat meets a frame neighborhood in at most 32 sites. Unless its
  span lies in the target neighborhood hyperplane W_i, its intersection has
  rank at most six and contains at most 20 target sites. Every W_i contains
  no frame label.
* A matched rank-eight hyperplane H containing z_i misses at least 24 of
  N_i=N(f_i), hence contains at most 32 N_i sites (D1 G2).
* For a normal h with two nonzero frame coordinates a,b, every hyperplane
  H containing z_a has rank-eight label span on Off(h) minus H minus any
  single live site (D1 G4, including generic coefficient ratios).
* A nonframe normal on at most three axes has at least 76 live nonzeros.
  A normal on at most four axes with at most 75 live nonzeros is root/frame
  (D2 restricted four-axis rigidity). A nonframe root has exactly four axes.
* Distinct orthogonal frame neighborhoods have common set of size 24 and
  exclusive set of size 32. On their common set the ratio of their code
  values has exactly two classes +1,-1, each of size 12 (D2-PROFILE).

For binary labels ell=(tau,x), put b_i(ell)=B(f_i,x). Then
W_i=ker(b_i+tau), and on live roots b_i=1 means membership in N_i.
For distinct i,j the 32 labels N_j minus N_i span

    V_ji=ker b_i intersect ker(b_j+tau),  dim V_ji=7.

Indeed they lie in the displayed rank-seven space and their cardinality
exceeds the rank-six capacity 20. Deleting one site still leaves 31>20,
so the remaining labels have the same span. The two displayed equations
are independent; this also follows from the existence of the exclusive
and common frame-neighborhood sites. Every frame label satisfies b_i(z_j)=0,
and tau(z_j)=1.

## Exhaustion and actual-L reduction

The intrinsic lower bound is cost(T)>=29. Since the outside count is six,

    sum_inside w=cost(T)-8+eta,
    sum_F w = sum_inside w+48+c9=cost(T)+40+eta+c9,

so the exact residual budget is

    cost(T)+eta+c9<=32.

Here c9 counts actual outside rank-nine members. Thus eta is two or three
and cost(T) is 29 or 30. At cost 29 the unique minimum T profile is 77771.
At cost 30 it is 77772: some seven must occur (otherwise deleting any
coordinate leaves a four-six family with code intersection at least two),
and deleting a seven leaves a four-piece d>=2 family of cost 23. D2's
intrinsic four-piece exhaustion leaves only 7771 or 7772 at cost<=23;
cost 23 forces 7772.

At minimum profile 77771, the weight<=6 threshold space is the unit line.
An independent actual four-member inside family of cost 23 or 24 has exactly
one member on that line and three members of weight at least seven.
Therefore the possibilities are 8771 (eta2) and 9771/8871 (eta3).
At minimum profile 77772 the corresponding threshold is a rank-two line;
eta2 gives the actual profile 8772. These statements use the threshold
filtration of T and never assert that the actual inside family minimizes L.

Let L=span(F intersect T), dimension four, and D=C intersect L. Dimension
gives dim D>=2. If dim D>=3, killing the two largest actual coordinates
leaves a nonzero codeword supported on the last seven and the rank-one/two
member, at most 38 sites. Thus dim D=2, and C+L=S. Consequently the six
outside restricted duals are linearly independent: a combination vanishing
on C also vanishes on L and therefore on S. Corrected outside frame indices
are distinct. If J is their set, D has at most 8-|J| active frame axes.

## Profiles 9771 and 8772

Both have actual inside sum 24, c9=0 and raw sum 72. A bad invoice I<=64
requires n_c>=8. At most three inside members are corrected, so |J|>=5
and D has at most three active axes.

Kill the rank-nine/eight inside coordinate. Its kernel on D contains a
nonzero normal h represented by the other two sevens plus the small member,
at most 74 live sites. The coordinate is not identically zero on D:
otherwise killing either seven in a two-dimensional intersection leaves
seven+small, below distance 53. The kernel is therefore a line, and h is
a frame normal by the restricted-axis lower bound. Each seven-flat must
lie in W_h: otherwise its frame-target capacity is at most 20 and the
other seven plus small contributes at most 32+2, totaling 54<56.
Both sevens are uncorrected. Thus n_c<=6+1=7, contradiction.

## Profile 8771

Write its inside members Q8,A7,B7,u1. A bad invoice requires n_c>=7+c9.
At most three inside members can be corrected, so |J|>=4+c9 and D has
at most four active axes. The Q-coordinate kernel is a nonzero line
(by the same seven+unit distance argument), represented by A+B+u and
having at most 73 live sites. Four-axis rigidity makes its normal h
root/frame.

If h were nonframe, it would use all four possible active D axes. Q could
not be corrected: its nonzero coordinate on D would then be a frame
coordinate vanishing on h, impossible on those four axes. With Q
uncorrected there are at most two inside corrections, forcing |J|>=5+c9
and at most three active axes, a contradiction. Hence h=f0 is a frame.

The 56-site cover by A+B+u forces both seven-flats into W0, since otherwise
20+32+1<56. They are uncorrected. Thus n_c<=7; the bad-invoice lower bound
forces c9=0, all six outside members corrected, and Q corrected. Their
six distinct indices leave exactly a two-frame plane D=span(f0,f1).
Q is corrected at index 1: its coordinate kernel is K f0 and its coordinate
on D is nonzero.

Put H=U(Q). In the actual expansion of C_f1, all 32 sites
X=N1 minus N0 outside the possible unit site must lie in H, because A,B
are supported in N0. These at least 31 labels span V_10 as above. The
correction adds z1, which is not in V_10 (b1(z1)=0 while tau(z1)=1),
but is in ker b0. Since dim H=8,

    H=ker b0.

Therefore Q vanishes on N0. If u were in N0, the expansion outside N0
would identify Q with a nonzero scalar multiple of C_f1 restricted to X,
whose label span has rank seven. This contradicts w(Q)=8. Hence u is
outside N0.

Restricting the actual expansions of C_f0 and C_f1 to N0 now shows that
A,B span both C_f0|N0 and C_f1|N0. Those two functions are independent:
the first is nonzero on the 32 sites N0 minus N1 and the second vanishes
there while being nonzero on the common 24 sites. Thus A,B form a basis
of this two-plane. At least one is a combination

    alpha C_f0|N0 + beta C_f1|N0,  alpha!=0.

It is nonzero at all 32 exclusive sites and at least 12 common sites,
because the two common ratio classes each have size 12. It has at least
44 support sites. But A,B lie in W0 and have rank seven, so each has at
most 32 N0 sites. Contradiction.

## Profile 8871

Write Q8,R8,B7,u1. Here raw sum is 72, c9=0 and n_c>=8 is required.
The restrictions of Q and R coordinates on D are nonzero, and their joint
kernel is zero: killing both leaves B+u, below distance 53. In particular,
every corrected one of Q,R has a matched axis active on D.

First suppose all three large inside members are corrected. Then |J|>=5,
so D has at most three active axes. Kill the corrected Q coordinate.
Its nonzero kernel normal h has at most two active axes, and

    C_h=alpha R+beta B+gamma u.

The R coefficient is nonzero, since B+u cannot support a codeword. Hence
the corrected R index is active in h. If h has two axes, G4 for U(R)
forces the labels outside U(R) and the unit site to have rank eight,
although they lie in U(B), rank seven. If h is a frame axis, R is matched
to it, while B either is matched to the same axis or has zero coefficient.
The matched target cover has capacity at most 32+20+1=53<56. Both cases
are impossible. Thus at most two inside members are corrected.

A bad invoice now forces exactly two inside corrections and all six
outside corrections, so D is the full coordinate plane on two frame axes.

If the two corrected members are Q and R, their indices are distinct:
otherwise their common coordinate kernel on D would leave a nonzero
word in B+u. Name their indices a,b. The actual frame expansions are

    C_fa=alpha Q+beta B+gamma u,
    C_fb=alpha' R+beta' B+gamma' u.

The matched cut for Q requires at least 24 N_a sites outside U(Q),
covered by B+u. In particular beta!=0 and B has at least 23 N_a sites.
If U(B) were not inside W_a, it would have at most 20 N_a sites; thus
U(B) lies inside W_a. The same argument gives U(B) inside W_b.
Therefore B is supported on the common 24-site neighborhood.

Each missing set, after deleting the unit, has at least 23 sites within
this common 24-set. Their intersection has at least 22 sites. On it,

    C_fa=beta B,     C_fb=beta' B,

so their ratio is constant. But each fixed ratio class in the common
neighborhood has at most 12 sites. Contradiction.

Finally suppose the two corrected members are Q8 and B7. Choose a nonzero
normal h in the kernel of the uncorrected R coordinate on D. Then

    C_h=alpha Q+beta B+gamma u,

with alpha!=0 because B+u cannot support a codeword. Thus the corrected
Q index is active in h. If h has two axes, G4 for U(Q) contradicts
w(B)=7 after deleting the unit. If h is frame, Q is matched to it;
B either is also matched to it or has zero coefficient. Again the
target capacity is at most 32+20+1=53<56. This is the final contradiction.

Together with RAW-SHAPE's already proved branches, every actual t=2, d=3
nonadapted invoice satisfying the raw sum<=72 has I>=65. The argument does
not assert a minimum actual L, adaptation, N8, distinct inside correction
indices, or completeness of a normalized source list.
