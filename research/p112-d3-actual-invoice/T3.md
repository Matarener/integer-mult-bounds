## Common actual reduction

Write L=span(F intersect T). There are five independent actual inside
members and six actual outside members. Let Sigma be the inside weight
sum and c9 the number of outside weight-nine members. The exact ledger is

    Sigma=cost(T)-8+eta,
    sum_F w=Sigma+48+c9.

Only cost(T)=30, eta=2 or3, or cost(T)=31, eta=2 remain. Thus Sigma is24
or25. If Sigma=24, a bad invoice I<=64 requires n_c>=8+c9. If Sigma=25,
the raw budget forces c9=0 and a bad invoice requires n_c>=9.

Put D=C intersect L. Dimension gives dim D>=2. It is exactly two. If it
were at least three, kill the two largest actual coordinates in D. The
remaining nonzero word would be supported on the three smallest actual
members. Their support capacity is at most 41: write their weights as
a>=b>=c; then 3a+b+c<=25. If a=7, b+c<=4 and the capacity is at most
36+4+1=41; if a=6 it is at most 20+20+1=41; if a<=5 it is at most 36.
The case a>=8 already has 3a+b+c>=26. This contradicts code distance53.
Consequently C+L=S.

The six outside coordinate functionals vanish on L; restriction to C is
injective on Ann(L) because C+L=S. Hence the six outside restricted duals
are independent. Corrected outside indices are therefore DISTINCT frame
axes. If J is their set, every normal in D is supported on the remaining
8-|J| frame axes. This conclusion includes corrected weight-nine members.

The retained geometric facts used below are those explicitly recorded in
D1/D2: capacities g(1..7)=(1,2,4,8,12,20,36); code distance53; every normal
with fewer than 70 live nonzeros is a root/frame; every nonroot/frame normal
on at most four frame axes has at least 76 live nonzeros; frame words have
56 sites, and each nonframe root has exactly four active frame axes;
a seven-flat meets a frame target in at most 32 sites, or at most 20
if it carries that target's excluded frame label. A rank-six flat carrying
the matched frame label has frame-target capacity at most 12; a matched
rank-three flat has capacity at most 2. These follow by intersecting its
label span with the frame support hyperplane, which excludes that frame
label. A corrected rank-eight frame word plus an error on fewer than 24
sites is excluded by matched-cut G2. Rank-one/two members are uncorrected.

Equivalently, at every threshold its number of members below that threshold
is at most the dimension of T's threshold span. This applies to actual
members of weight eight/nine as well as low members.

For minimum 777711, the threshold through weight six is exactly the plane
spanned by its two unit vectors. Those vectors occupy two distinct sites,

## Sigma25:77731

The three seven-coordinate restrictions on D are nonzero and have
distinct kernel lines: a common kernel of two would leave a codeword
in one seven, the rank-three member, and the unit, of capacity at most
36+4+1=41<53. If the rank-three member is uncorrected, all three sevens
and all six outside members must be corrected. Then D is a two-frame
plane, and the same singleton-group argument gives a frame word in one

Suppose the rank-three member is corrected. It has EXACTLY three support
sites by the leading-one affine slice argument in D1/G1. At least five
outside members are corrected, so D has k=2 or3 active frame axes.

If k=2, at most two sevens can be corrected: their nonzero coordinate
restrictions have distinct kernel lines, so their matched axes must be
distinct and active. Thus n_c>=9 forces exactly two corrected sevens,
the corrected three-piece, and all six outside members corrected.
Let a,b be the two active axes and A the uncorrected seven. The
three-piece's matched frame row is perpendicular to at least one of
f_a,f_b, say f_a. In the actual expansion of C_(f_a), that three-piece
and the seven corrected at b both have coefficient zero. The remaining
target cover is A, the seven corrected at a, and the unit, of capacity
32+20+1=53<56. Contradiction.

If k=3, all three sevens, the three-piece, and exactly five outside
members must be corrected. The three seven rows match the three distinct
active axes. Each of their kernel lines kills its own axis. None is a
frame line: a frame line in a two-plane with three active coordinates
would be the common kernel of the other two active coordinate
functionals, contradicting their distinct seven-kernel lines.

Each kernel normal is therefore a genuine two-axis normal with support
at most 36+36+3+1=76, and the retained two-axis count forces exactly 76
sites. Equality in each cover makes both included seven supports full

supports pairwise disjoint. Considering all three kernels makes all five

Every included coefficient in every76-site kernel word is nonzero:
omitting a seven gives at most 40 sites, omitting the three-piece gives 73,

The three-axis incidence count G5 has eight live roots supported on a

site the 112-site disjoint partition has exactly one nonzero inside
piece. The actual code expansion would force that piece's coordinate

nonvanishing. This is the D2 actual-profile equality argument, independent
of any minimum basis of L or any assertion that L equals global T.
