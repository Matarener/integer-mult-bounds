# Binding status of the ownership implication

Source checkpoint: `de5ec2e7a6074068452f45b1a191b5c72832df4a`; new P104 word: PR5811 commit `883ab9ca680906a0df88fb74ff51d634868af363`. Read-only independent review, 2026-10-26/27. This note does not alter any repository assumption or selected result.

## Judgment

**The current evidence supports a concrete ownership-reduction theorem and actual raw owner/lifetime bindings. It does not yet justify changing the project's five assumptions to four.**

The safe statement is:

> For each fixed finite supplier satisfying the explicitly checked raw typing and capture-avoiding compiler grammar, if its isolated primitive realizations have the specified local time and visited-space bounds and its recursive row/fiber interface satisfies the explicit geometry contract, the segment simulation and bridge dispatcher construct global private allocation, suspension, return and erasure without assuming those properties of recursive children.

This is more than a finite test: the implication is a written all-size argument with explicit physical mechanisms. But the source application must establish both P and G, not rename an owned-child ABI as a remaining assumption. At present the complete delayed-cover BIT-to-ordinary interface and its use inside compact movement have not been fully instantiated into that dispatcher. The obstacle is a missing source-bound interface/space derivation, not a counterexample to the proposed theorem.

`fresh_names_controls.py` passes a legal nested private-field borrowing example and rejects six mistakes: duplicate child arguments, bank capture, child erasure of a borrowed parent, one bank per depth, repeated missing source and early parent erasure. ### Generic physical implication

`ALLOCATION-THEOREM.md` explicitly confines all primitive ports using coded virtual symbols, including virtual blanks; supplies a finite return-PC/descriptor; transfers child input before descent; preserves all parent segments; saves all actual child outputs before pop; reinstalls them on the parent owner; and erases bridges and private segments. That resolves the structural circle in the old wording of `FINITE-PRECOMPILATION.md` Section 1 item 4. We use Sections 3–4 for the finite combinatorial recipe and prove ownership independently. ## Exact sources for the local space argument

1. **Elementary macros:** `upstream/build/sections/02-streams.tex`, Lemmas `elementary-streams` and `ordered-affine-streams`, prove O(V) *time* for stream split/merge, fixed pointwise maps, digit moves, ordered controlled rotations and fixed rational scalings. The stated tariff includes shape preparation, counters, rewinds and returning/clearing the visited portions of work tapes. Because heads move at most one cell per step, each such isolated invocation has O(V) visited space. The final paragraph gives linear-bit-time exact fixed-point arithmetic and reversals under the guard-bit premise. No global owned scheduler is used in this inference.
2. **Compact rotations and repair:** `notes/compact-control-movement.tex`, controlled-rotation proof, uses two piece tapes, output and fixed arithmetic tapes; pieces have total O(V) length. The exceptional repair keeps one full stream with marked holes, at most M extracted complete records, O(A)-bit keys and a fixed number of radix-sort streams. Their length is O(V+MA+poly(A)); with A=O(p) and superpolynomial record R, this is O(V). Radix passes reuse those tapes. This is an additional space derivation from the explicit procedure, not from the larger complete time tariff.
3. **The missing recursive submacro:** The same compact-control proposition invokes arbitrary-width chunk-swap up to twelve times. Its O(V e^tau) running time does **not** alone imply O(V) space. For a fixed ordinary BIT supplier, local O(V) macros plus complete V/W child geometry and the new allocator yield that space theorem. The retained ordinary BIT realization must first be bound to those premises.
4. **Existing structural schedule:** `upstream/build/sections/04-swap.tex`, “A fixed-tape depth-first schedule,” specifies parking inactive streams, child I/O, finite PC/descriptor, returning outputs and local cleanup. `notes/batched-bit-rows.tex`, “A finite integer depth bound” and “Fixed tapes and descriptors,” proves V_child=V_parent/W, the complete row divisibility and polynomial descriptor work absorbed by child volume. These are precise sources for a fixed-width supplier. Quoting their global cleanup assertion in place of a source-bound lowering would be circular for the intended new ownership discharge.
5. **Old ordinary helper:** `notes/copied-centers-bit.tex`, “Physical list and exact bit moment,” identifies the old saving 384599/10^10, ambient 575, productive width 188181929 and strictly smaller child widths. `notes/copied-centers-lemma.tex` describes each retained center as a complete copy/transform/read/discard episode and explicitly retains its V/W volume. It is a good candidate for a first noncircular application of the allocator, using a structural typing argument for the generative word. We have not supplied a full literal old-caller binding in this work.

The new template's large fixed constants must replace old values throughout. The sources' raw 10^9 cap or a raw count 626/129 is not a substitute for expanded O, work tapes, record/route counts or storage constants.

## The exact unresolved application edge

The selected BIT interface is not merely the plain fixed-W interchange lemma. `research/query-thinned-controllers/PROOF.md` Section 4 retains the **delayed GL cover**, its ordinary atom wrapper, exceptional local-ring fallback and old atom-interchange supplier. `research/kappa-rebuild/bit/DELAYED-COVER-BRIDGE.md` Sections 3–7 and `notes/stopped-product-interface.tex` describe that composition.

The delayed cover depends on atom width w. Its cover fiber and effective row-stock factor W(w) vary with the input, although N, the raw role list and the prime are fixed. The generic dispatcher presently fixes a number W=B_N of role ports and uses a literal V/W child split. It cannot simply interpret every role/cover index as a distinct fixed physical tape, nor silently substitute variable W(w) for a finite-control loop limit. Likewise, replacing W(w) by B_N without deriving what the cover coordinate becomes would omit an interface step.

This is plausibly repairable: the delayed-cover source says the high matrix entries are streamed by finitely many fixed low types, and fixed right routes permute a whole cover fiber rather than allocating a field for each g. A correct application could represent variable cover coordinates as an address field within each of finitely many bank streams. But it must explicitly show:

* which literal stream is a bank and which part of its address is the variable cover/row coordinate;
* how each leaf's complete input and output fiber maps to the child's fresh cover and common external-weight descriptor;
* that the independently typed private lifetime still occupies its complete required fiber and no cover slice is silently dropped or credited twice;
* the fixed physical port count, input/output return procedure and descriptor size with variable w;
* a local visited-space bound for all fallback, routing and old-interchange calls; and
* the resulting active-chain/stock inequality with that actual contraction, rather than assuming the fixed-W formula applies verbatim.

The retained common-weight theorem establishes an operator identity and the cover-route price; it explicitly says cover routing does not repair an aliased or cyclic flow. It is not a complete source serialization/allocator proof. The PR589 raw lifetime census addresses its finite circuit but not this all-w ordinary-wrapper mapping.

There is therefore an unresolved dependency chain:

```text
old ordinary BIT local typing + complete-row geometry + local stream space
    -> old ordinary BIT physical owned-return/linear-space theorem
    -> delayed-cover routes/fallback plus variable-cover dispatcher mapping
    -> retained 17/5000 ordinary BIT physical owned-return/linear-space theorem
    -> compact chunk-swap local-space contract for complex movement
    -> complete complex supplier allocation theorem
```

The root's proposed generic theorem and current evidence close major mechanisms on this chain, but do not by themselves constitute all source bindings on every arrow. Leaving those arrows as an explicit part of `precision_tape` is an honest conditional reduction; claiming the whole `owned_erasure` label has disappeared would overstate what was established.

## Recommended final claim

Publish a scoped theorem and artifact: source-bound complex/BIT raw owner typing, capture-avoiding closure of the full finite compiler grammar, and a fixed-tape recursive allocator/return construction conditional on explicit isolated-macro and row/fiber premises. State that it removes the need for a **separate recursive-child ownership postcondition in that theorem**, and identifies the exact remaining source binding needed to discharge the project's label. Retain all five project assumptions until the delayed-cover/ordinary-macro mapping and the rest of the declared scope are completed and reviewed. This preserves the distinction between a substantial proof bridge and a completed multiplication theorem.
