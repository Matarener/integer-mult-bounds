# Sharp integer inertia certificates for sunflower downsets

This contribution was produced by Nick Petalas. Apache-2.0.

Claim type: **scoped mathematical proof with exact finite corroboration**.
Every downset generated by a finite sunflower of maximal sets has a
nonsingular, symmetric, nonnegative integer matrix supported on disjoint
pairs whose number of positive eigenvalues equals its largest star size.
The construction also gives the exact determinant. The new case has three
or more maximal sets. It resolves the disjoint-petal first case left in
Nick Petalas's [#5247](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5247).

This proves the sunflower subclass of Ellis, Filmus and Friedgut's
[spectral Conjecture I, section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28404v1#S4),
recorded as C070 in David Ganz's
[atlas #2849](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/2849).
The general downset conjecture remains open here. Global literature
priority, independent human review and formal proof-kernel review are
unclaimed. No new kappa or selected-frontier change is claimed.

## Statement and construction

Let C,A_0,...,A_(r-1) be pairwise disjoint finite sets, with

```text
r >= 3,  a_0 >= a_1 >= ... >= a_(r-1) >= 1,  a_i = |A_i|,  c = |C|,
D = union over i of 2^(C union A_i),
L = 1 + sum_i (2^a_i - 1),  N = 2^c L.
```

These are precisely the downsets whose three or more maximal sets form a
sunflower. One or two maximal sets are covered by the cited predecessor.
Choose injections phi_i:A_i -> A_0 for i>=1, and let phi_0 be the identity.
The injections define weights only. They do not identify actual ground
elements from different petals.

First take c=0. List every subset of A_0, then the nonempty subsets of
each other petal. This lists D once. Define a symmetric base matrix K.
Within one petal, use ordinary disjointness entries. Between subsets S,T
from different petals, put k=|phi_i(S) intersect phi_j(T)| and use

```text
                        k = 0           k >= 1
one petal is A_0:          1              2^k - 1
neither is A_0:            1              2^(k+1) - 3.
```

Every cross-petal pair is disjoint in the actual ground set. All displayed
weights are positive integers. Within a petal, the only nonzero diagonal
entry is at the empty set, and it is 1.

For general c, write a set uniquely as R union U, with R subset C and U
a base label, and set

```text
M((R,U),(R',V)) = 1[R intersect R' is empty] K(U,V).
```

Then M is nonnegative, integral, symmetric and zero whenever its two
indexing sets intersect. Let s denote the largest star size. The formulas
are

```text
s = 2^(a_0-1)  if c=0;       s = N/2  if c>=1,
e = 2^c sum_(i=1)^(r-1) (2^(a_i-1)-1),
inertia(M) = (s, N-s, 0),
det(M) = (-1)^(N-s) 3^e.
```

Thus the optimal number of nonnegative eigenvalues is exactly s, even
when the competing matrices may have arbitrary real supported entries.

## Proof

The Boolean zeta congruence and the dominant-to-one-petal block below are
the credited ingredients of #5247. The new step chooses all the other
cross blocks simultaneously and cancels every off-diagonal Schur block.
The argument is written in full to specify that new construction; no
predecessor executable is imported or replayed.

For the cube on a set A, put

```text
Z_A(S,T) = 1[T subset S],
J_A(T,T) = (-1)^|T|,
D_A(S,T) = 1[S intersect T is empty].
```

In an inclusion-compatible order Z_A is unit lower triangular, and
`D_A = Z_A J_A Z_A^T`, since the entry is the alternating subset sum
over S intersect T. For each i>=1, let Z_i' and J_i' be the restrictions
to nonempty subsets of A_i, and put u_i(T)=(-1)^(|T|+1). Then

```text
Z_i' u_i = 1,
D_A_i[nonempty,nonempty] = Z_i' (J_i' + u_i u_i^T) Z_i'^T.
```

Let e_0 be the empty-subset coordinate of the full A_0 cube. Define E_i
with columns indexed by nonempty T subset A_i. Its column is the standard
coordinate vector at phi_i(T) when |T| is even, and zero when |T| is odd.
Injectivity gives

```text
J_A_0 E_i = E_i,  e_0^T E_i = 0,
E_i^T E_i = diag(1[|T| is positive and even]).
```

Set H_i=e_0 u_i^T+2E_i. Under congruence by
`Z=diag(Z_A_0,Z_1',...,Z_(r-1)')`, K has the blocks

```text
B_00 = J_A_0,
B_0i = H_i,
B_ii = J_i' + u_i u_i^T,
B_ij = u_i u_j^T + 4 E_i^T E_j     for distinct i,j >= 1,
K = Z B Z^T.
```

To check the explicit weights, Z_A_0 E_i Z_i'^T counts nonempty even
subsets of S intersect phi_i(T). The analogous cross expression
`Z_i' E_i^T E_j Z_j'^T` counts nonempty even subsets of
phi_i(S) intersect phi_j(T). If this intersection has size k, the count
is zero at k=0 and `2^(k-1)-1` at k>=1. Adding the all-ones term and
multiplying that count by 2 or 4 gives exactly the two weight formulas.

Eliminate B_00. It is its own inverse. For every i,j>=1,

```text
H_i^T J_A_0 H_j = u_i u_j^T + 4 E_i^T E_j.
```

Therefore all off-diagonal Schur blocks vanish, and diagonal block i is

```text
J_i' - 4 E_i^T E_i
    = diag(-1 on odd subsets, -3 on nonempty even subsets).
```

This is negative definite, independently of how many petals there are or
how their injected images overlap in A_0. Sylvester's law of inertia
gives exactly `2^(a_0-1)` positive eigenvalues. Every other eigenvalue is
negative. The zeta and Schur congruence factors have determinant one.
There are `sum_(i>=1)(2^(a_i-1)-1)` entries -3, which proves the base
determinant's absolute value and its sign from the negative eigenvalues.

For c>=1, `M=D_C tensor K`. The invertible symmetric D_C has equally
many positive and negative eigenvalues, by its zeta congruence. Hence
M has N/2 of each sign. Also |det D_C|=1, so
`|det M|=|det K|^(2^c)`. This proves both general formulas.

When c=0, an element of A_i belongs to exactly `2^(a_i-1)` sets. When
c>=1, an element of C belongs to N/2 sets. A petal element belongs to
`2^c 2^(a_i-1)<=N/2` sets because L>=2^a_i. These counts give s above.

Finally a star of size s indexes a zero principal block in every real
symmetric disjointness-supported matrix. Its quadratic form vanishes on
that s-dimensional coordinate subspace. If there were fewer than s
nonnegative eigenvalues, the negative eigenspace would have dimension
greater than N-s and intersect this subspace nontrivially. That would
contradict negative definiteness. This proves the asserted optimality.

## First new example and finite evidence

For three disjoint two-element facets, N=10 and s=2. The construction has
inertia `(2,8,0)` and determinant 9. Its weights between the full facets
are 3,3,5. Keeping the two dominant cross weights 3 but changing the last
weight to 1 gives inertia `(3,7,0)` and determinant -7. Changing that last
weight to 3 retains sharp inertia but changes the determinant to 5. This
control distinguishes exact Schur cancellation from mere sharp inertia.
Weakening the coupling coefficient 2 to 1 gives `(2,6,2)`, with four
nonnegative eigenvalues, so zero eigenvalues must be counted.

The standalone standard-library verifier enumerates all 50 nonincreasing
positive petal-size/core tuples with r>=3 and
`c+sum_i a_i<=7`. Matrices have order at most 64. It builds actual disjoint
ground-set labels, checks all 23,333 support entries and the largest stars,
and independently computes exact inertia and determinant by rational
symmetric elimination with both one- and two-dimensional pivots. It also
checks the integer zeta congruence and all 785 leaf Schur entries across
these cases. Thirteen named controls include the new example, changed
weights, null-eigenvalue counting, zero-diagonal pivots and invalid inputs.
No two-facet predecessor cases are replayed.

The universal result rests on the written proof. These finite checks
corroborate the construction and test its failure modes. JSON stdout is
generated from the actual checks and binds all five package files by
SHA256. No receipt is handwritten or committed. The verifier writes no
files and does not fetch or import predecessor sources.

## Implementation bounds and limitations

Every loop has a finite bound from seven ground coordinates, seven petals,
matrix order 64 or the fixed five-file inventory. The parameter iterator
examines at most 1,716 tuples for each of five petal counts; only 50 pass
the stated domain. Symmetric elimination removes at least one coordinate
per iteration and uses at most 64 iterations. No recursive calls, dynamic
code evaluation, subprocesses or external runtime dependencies occur.
All functions fit within sixty physical lines. Explicit exception checks
remain active under optimization.

Power-of-Ten rule 3 has a managed-runtime deviation in verify.py: CPython
allocates lists and exact integers/rationals during execution. Fixed input
and matrix capacities bound live work; there are no external parameters or
unbounded streams. A future parameterized checker must enforce size and
bit-length budgets before lifting these capacities. Rule 9 has six fixed
local rejection callbacks, used immediately and retaining no state. Their
only targets are the literal-matrix validator and the label validator.
Warning-strict normal and optimized execution and AST inspection supply
available diagnostics; a standalone linter is not installed in this
interpreter. No shared build configuration is changed.

General overlapping-facet downsets, a weighted-Hoffman certificate,
physical preparation, recursive callers, precision, stock and supplier
admission remain outside scope. Lean, Docker and the selected cold replay
were not run. This is not a complete multiplication theorem.

## Open next questions

1. Can three maximal sets with different nonempty pairwise intersections
   admit a sharp integer certificate? Start with facets {1,2}, {2,3} and
   {1,3}. Cross-facet pairs may now intersect, so freely prescribing all
   Schur-cancelling entries may violate the support restriction. This is
   the first obstruction to extending the present sunflower proof.
2. Can a smaller weight alphabet give a uniform sharp certificate for
   arbitrary sunflowers? The three-two-element-petal control has a sharp
   alternative determinant 5. Start by classifying its allowed cross
   weights. Exact cancellation and minimum entry size are different
   objectives; no general minimum-weight claim is made here.
