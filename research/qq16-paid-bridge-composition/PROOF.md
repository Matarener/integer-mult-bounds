The companion X leg is unchanged. The selected provider in that template is

## Ordered operator sufficiency

Let D_p be the actual chart-1 owner action. Define

    a_k = sum_p R[k,p] D_p Y_p,
    (K_U a)_p = (1/4) sum_k R[k,p] D_p^-1 a_k,
    h_p = Y_p - (K_U a)_p,                   p in U.

For the conserved B1 equations, a is the old selected-T query ell_1 x1 and
h=y1_U. These source identities are inherited from the pinned QQ16 note.
For this composition only the displayed identities on arbitrary current Y
are needed. They imply, with all operator products ordered,

    B_p - h_p - (K_U a)_p = B_p - Y_p.       (1)

Thus (h,a), comprising 104 full-cover values, suffices for this bridge leg.
No commutation of distinct D_p is used. The verifier checks the complete
operator over 208 independent source/target fields, not just constants or
random values. Its coefficients carry the full 73-column owner matrices.

The eight-component augmentation is a constructive choice, not a lower bound
of eight new physical families. It may be streamed or hosted differently in
a different schedule. This construction explicitly allocates eight owned
query output arrays and pays their clears/copies/return.

## Legal augmented producer

The retained 1,410-event PR3952 WORD is authenticated by exact head, Git blob
and SHA256 and independently regenerated from R and the port partition. Its
112 owned W arrays are cleared from arbitrary dirt, copied from borrowed Y,
normalized by C_qp, and aligned into common F17. All eight query rows are
formed in owner-private F fields before changing any ordinary W.

The augmentation allocates eight owned Q_k outputs, clears them in F17, and
copies each completed F_k into Q_k inside that same owner body. This adds
eight full-cover CLEARs and eight unit ADDs. The original producer then
subtracts K_U a, returns ordinary W to its original owner coordinates, clears
the sixteen selected W temporaries, and clears its private fields before
moving to another owner. At the new boundary W_U=h in F17 and Q=a in F17.
All borrowed Y values/frames are untouched; no early record or X read is used.

## Legal consumer and cleanup

For every ordinary p, apply the source-derived affine map M_p to W_p, where

    M_p F17 = F17 D_p.

At the common frame, add R[k,p] Q_k/4 for each nonzero row coefficient.
Then apply M_p^-1. The result is logical Y_p in F17. Apply C_qp^-1 to return
that owned value to the target's frame F17 C_qp^-1, and subtract it from B_p.
Clear the consumed W_p and every Q_k to zero in identity frame.

Every ADD is between equal complete modulo-four phase polynomials. A map
uses the actual involutive owner matrix and the unique derived even-linear
translation; its inverse transports that translation by the owner action.
Omitting the shift or inverse line is rejected at a scalar frame boundary.
The native positive line is a wrapped H1 with a basis beginning at q_p.
The inverse line uses a q_p translation in the left wrapper. All 112 positive
and 96 negative signed kernels are checked on all 512 local basis columns:
106,496 exact Gaussian columns, with common denominator two. Tensor products
and the full phase identity extend the statement to all positive tensor
depths under the inherited recursive H1 contract. This does not establish
physical tape execution or a precision guarantee.

The 120 full-cover workspace fields are 112 W plus eight Q, all exclusively
owned and initially arbitrary. All finish zero/I. The eight F fields exist
only during one owner-private body, finish zero/I there, and are never used
across an owner move. Peak private workspace is eight. No borrowed field is
cleared or used as an output alias. Allocation and movement are not free.

## Exact fusion and what really disappears

Within the serial composition the producer subtracts K_U a and the consumer
adds exactly K_U a back. The ordinary producer exit map and consumer entry
map are inverse; no intervening operation uses the earlier owner position.
After eliminating this cancellation, the query outputs and their computation
are dead for a bridge-only demand. The sixteen selected source copies are
then dead too. The remaining positive/negative C_qp and inverse map pairs on
ordinary copies cancel. Copy propagation leaves precisely the 96 direct
bridge ADDs already present in the source schedule.

For an exact external-state comparison the checker also retains all 120
allocated workspace fields: the fused word is 96 direct ADDs plus 120 CLEARs,
scalar allowance 1,008. It compares every final value and frame, including
all initially arbitrary workspace, with the serial word. If those unused
fields are never allocated, their cleanup disappears and the source bridge
bill is 768. This avoids treating discarded arbitrary owned dirt as zero.

Fusion removes the added encode/decode work; it does not remove any of the
96 actual source Y bridge ADDs. Therefore this serial repair closes the
missing-information boundary but supplies no new saving for this one
bridge-only continuation. That is the bounded stop result. It is not a
universal lower bound on bridge implementations, shared queries, a changed
producer, or a continuation which independently needs h and a elsewhere.
