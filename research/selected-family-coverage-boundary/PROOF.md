Clear1nsight with substantial OpenAI assistance. Apache-2.0.

## Claim and scope

This rebinds PR1012's family coverage identity to the actual selected #1094
archive and answers the contract bridge in PR3754's pooling question. It does
not show that every possible changed decoder has at most one unpaid coordinate,
and does not dispute the arithmetic fact that pooling can make an average
integer. No new histogram optimum, native supplier or kappa is claimed.

PR1012's original identity is family-by-family:

    8 k_f + h_f + i_f + 1 = 73.

Here k_f counts actual data-register incidences, h_f sums helper widths hosted
on physical family f, and i_f is its actual idle rank. The plus-one belongs to
the family, not to one normalised call. The new audit tests this identity on
all selected rows and distinguishes aggregate mass from the local endpoint.

## Pooling and telescoping

For any partition of physical families into groups G_j, summing gives

    sum_(f in G_j) [73 - 8k_f - h_f - i_f] = |G_j|.

Repartitioning or averaging by calls preserves total W; it cannot create 2W.
This is additive conservation, NOT a per-call integer constraint. Refining a
paid advance into several pieces, or deferring a helper restoration into its
idle continuation, preserves total mass when the original endpoint remains
satisfied. The deferral mechanism retains PR1012's credit.

Aggregate mass is weaker than these rows. Transfer one idle unit from f to g.
The global mass is unchanged, but their remainders become (2,0) instead of
(1,1). A histogram-only check cannot see the defect. The source verifier tests
this mutant, deletion of an idle unit, and simply claiming two credits on the
unchanged ledger. These reject for the expected family mismatch.

formula would demand

    idle_2 = total_2 - 144*5,586 = 222,688.

222,688=4,366*51+22 and per-call average13,918/9 are credited to PR3754 and the

Thus the unchanged selected contract has one credit per physical family.
A different decoder or endpoint could supply a different law, but that needs

integrality premise merely from normalisation.

## Distinct centre and ordinary resources

Deleting all private centres removes9,216 rank units, not14,336 ordinary
coordinate advances. At the mass level, centre-free one-unpaid mass72W exceeds
the two-unpaid/unchanged-centre target71W+64N by5,120. The same saving cannot
silently be booked as both a deleted centre and a second unpaid ordinary
coordinate. This is not a complete moment comparison: ranks, routing and
additional charges still matter, and no realised net saving is asserted.

## Verification and stopping decision

The source-dependent check authenticates the original archive and three exact
members, then reconstructs local frame ranks, both emitted orientation invoices,
every call owner, all matching helper slots, and every idle child. It binds the
entire14,336-row ledger in the result hash. A subtractive second calculation
must give the same all-ones remainder. Groupings modulo1,9,16,73 test additivity,
not proposed physical pooling schedules.

Eight synthetic controls cover exact rows, aggregation hiding local failure,
mass-neutral deferral, a legitimately changed endpoint, typed data, missing
and corrupt input, and equal-dimensional but different binary subspaces.
Normal and optimised runs are registered. Synthetic local evidence is distinct
from actual archive-dependent hosted replay, reported on the PR.
