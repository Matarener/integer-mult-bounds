## Execution limits

The astronomical GL64/O73 covers are specified mathematically, not executed.
No literal complete transition table or new Lean theorem is supplied. The
published Lean baseline proves a different, smaller exponent.

The older physical BIT simulations did not all finish within registered
CPython3.12 timeouts. A3.14 frame diagnostic and one physical macro passed;
other diagnostic runs timed out, and one faulthandler run exited139. No
passing result is inferred from those incomplete runs. The fixed-tape time
bounds here are the sequential-scan and recursion proofs in BIT.md, not
extrapolations from simulator wall time.

These limits concern implementation/formal verification and independent
review. They are not additional hypotheses of the proposed written theorem.
Review should challenge a specific all-size implication if its argument is
insufficient; a finite pass cannot settle that question.
