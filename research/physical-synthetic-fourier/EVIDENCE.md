input/descriptor bytes and Count tapes after each successful packet, require
closed child owners and no live outer leases, and inspect erased outer bodies
with origin heads. I observed at most 41 outer scratch leases, below the
written 128-role allowance and the compiled 256-role pool. The endpoint-only
fixture has its own declared checker schedule; I do not count it as a complete
64-coefficient Fourier execution.

using exact integer sums and rational division by M. I also compare an
independent stage model when selecting the premature-rounding witness. I
disable inherited numerical decoding helpers during physical packet execution.
The runtime does not call either checker oracle.
