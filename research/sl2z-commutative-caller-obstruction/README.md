# The integer-shear target vanishes in every commutative algebra carrier

**Scoped caller obstruction; no new supplier or kappa.** The four-call formal
minimum in [PR3905](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/3905)
remains valid. Its target becomes the identity under every unital algebra map
into a commutative carrier, including encodings by arbitrary finite polynomials.
This resolves one caller-transfer obligation before further word searches.

## Exact object and transfer contract

Use the predecessor's unchanged matrices and group:

\[
X=\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
Y=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
G=\langle X,Y\rangle\leq\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb Z),\quad
B=X^2Y^2=\begin{pmatrix}5&2\\2&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The scalar carrier is \(\mathbb F_2[G]\), paid labels are
\(X^{\pm1},Y^{\pm1}\), and the two-role target is
\(T=\operatorname{diag}([B],1)\). The source proves a four-call minimum for
factors \(I+([g]+1)N\), with binary rank-one idempotent \(N\), on arbitrary
dirty inputs. We replay its unchanged verifier; we do not search for a word.

The transfer considered here is a **unital \(\mathbb F_2\)-algebra homomorphism**
\(\Phi:\mathbb F_2[G]\to C\), applied entrywise to register operators.
Initially only commutativity of \(C\) is required. It need not be a domain,
reduced, finite, or generated by images of single basis labels. The map
preserves addition, multiplication and identity. An arbitrary linear/nonlinear
encoding or a map into a matrix algebra over \(C\) is a different contract.

## Proof: the target always collapses

Exact integer multiplication gives

\[
XY^{-1}X=Y^{-1}XY^{-1}=S
=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad S^4=I.
\]

Set \(U=\Phi([X])\), \(V=\Phi([Y])\). These are units, with inverses the
images of the inverse group labels. Commutativity turns the first relation
into \(U^2V^{-1}=UV^{-2}\). Multiply by \(U^{-1}V^2\) to obtain \(UV=1\).
Consequently

\[
\boxed{\Phi([B])=U^2V^2=1,\qquad \Phi(T)=I_2.}
\]

This uses a relation of the concrete group, not an assumed presentation or
finite search. With any finite number of spectators,
\(\operatorname{diag}([B],1,\ldots,1)\) still maps to identity. Restoring
spectators cannot recover the target distinction already lost by this map.
No such encoding is faithful on the distinction between \(T\) and identity.
This does not make a noncommutative implementation impossible, and does not
invalidate the source's formal minimum.

## Corollary: every paid label collapses in the rational additive carrier

Let \(C=\mathbb F_2[A]\), where \(A=(M_3(\mathbb Q),+)\), the carrier in
the main-branch three-register model. This is a commutative domain: the finite
supports of two nonzero elements generate a finitely generated torsion-free
abelian subgroup, hence a free abelian group. Their product lies in a Laurent
polynomial domain. This is the source's section 2 argument; its computer-assisted
nine-call theorem is not used.

Since \(V=U^{-1}\), the image of \(S\) is \(U^3\). From \(S^4=I\) we get
\(U^{12}=1\). In characteristic two,
\((U^3+1)^4=U^{12}+1=0\). The domain has no nonzero nilpotents, so \(U^3=1\).
Then

\[
0=U^3+1=(U+1)(U^2+U+1).
\]

The augmentation \(\epsilon:C\to\mathbb F_2\) sends each basis label to 1.
Since \(U\) is a unit, \(\epsilon(U)=1\). Thus
\(\epsilon(U^2+U+1)=1\), so the second factor is nonzero. The domain property
forces \(U=1\), hence \(V=1\). Since \(X,Y\) generate \(G\), every group
label maps to 1: the only map is augmentation followed by scalar inclusion.

Every paid factor therefore maps to identity, on any finite number of roles.
Interleaved constant scalar maps would remain; their product is not asserted
to be identity without its own endpoint normalization. The argument also
works for any torsion-free abelian address group in place of \(M_3(\mathbb Q)\).
It is not a universal statement about native callers or larger-register algorithms.

## Evidence and hypothesis controls

The written arguments establish the universal claims. The standard-library
verifier checks exact relations and boundary examples, not the infinite theorem
by sampling:

- Exact integer matrices, inverse labels, both sides of the relation,
  \(S^4=I\), \(B\ne I\), and Laurent-exponent unit cancellation.
- An invalid same-sign commuting assignment fails the relation.
- A genuine character through the 24-element \(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb F_3)\)
  into \(\mathbb F_4^\times\) preserves nontrivial generator images but kills
  \(B\). All quotient products are checked; satisfying two relations alone
  would not establish a character of the actual group.
- A character through \(\mathrm{SL}_2(\mathbb F_2)\) into the dual numbers
  \(\mathbb F_2[e]/(e^2)\) retains generators as \(1+e\), despite having
  augmentation. This shows why dropping the domain premise changes the corollary.
- The noncommutative permutation representation on eight nonzero vectors of
  \(\mathbb F_3^2\) preserves \(B\), moving all eight points. The permutation
  matrices have entries in \(\mathbb F_2\); the matrix algebra is noncommutative.
  This supplies neither native costs nor a faithful representation of the infinite group.
- The unchanged predecessor verifier checks all 85 words of length at most
  three and the four-call formal endpoint, including dirty-role restoration
  and omitted/wrong-label/order controls.
