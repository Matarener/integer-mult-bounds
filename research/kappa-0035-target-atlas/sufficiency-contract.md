## The requested conclusion

Let K₀ = 7/2000 = 0.0035. In this atlas, “reaches K₀” means the same integer-multiplication exponent and computational model used by the selected repository record: a complete construction supporting

    T(n) = O(n (log n)^(1−κ))

with some κ ≥ K₀, under the five explicitly retained framework assumptions below. It does not mean an unrelated Gaussian exponent, a raw moment crossing, a BIT component exponent, or the exponent of a Word RAM computation whose bit cost is uncharged.

The five inherited assumptions concern: native all-column operator identities; owned allocation and erasure; uniform finite mixed-target compilation; precision/storage and fixed-tape movement; and analytic decomposition, prime avoidance and exact integer recovery. These are hypotheses of the selected conditional result, not newly proved facts. An unconditional multiplication theorem would also require their complete discharge. A question in this atlas is not permission to assume an additional unknown decoder or oracle.

For planning headroom, fix

    C★ = 9/2500 = 0.0036,       t★ = 1−C★,
    B★ = 37/10000 = 0.0037.

C★ is a Gaussian supply target; B★ is an ordinary BIT supply target. They are deliberately above K₀. Existence of raw favorable moments alone does not establish the complete conclusion: actual interfaces, ordinary work, finite compilation and the outer join belong to the full statement.

## Two open companions, stated openly

**B★ companion.** There is a deterministic ordinary BIT supplier, with the actual arbitrary-function/ROW/spectator and source/receiver ABI required by the selected outer construction, whose admitted saving is at least B★. Its finite native word, helper credit/debits, leases, owned entry/exit, real routing, coefficient/precision work, completion/splitting, stock and backend fallback are all included. It works at every positive legal width, with effective fixed finite descriptions and the inherited uniformity conventions. The atom saving 1/250 may be retained, but that atom is not itself an ordinary BIT certificate. PR5512 is a starting precedent, not this result.

**G★ companion.** There is a complete source-compatible Gaussian supplier at saving at least C★, with strictly funded finite compiler reserves, the actual reciprocal orientations and independent target operators, and every nonrecursive/native cost included. The concrete Q64 route below is one sufficient instance of this companion. G★ is not established by the previous characteristic-direction decoder, smaller whole-K geometry, sector-sharing example or any raw headline alone.

Neither companion is assumed to be known. Gaussian-focused entries ask for their concrete core **together with B★**; BIT-focused entries ask for their concrete core **together with G★**. The full statement of every numbered X includes that companion and the compatibility clause below. Thus each full X is sufficient on its own under the five inherited framework assumptions. Its shorter core, taken alone, usually is not. These are alternative complete research packages sharing a bottleneck, not a claim that 20 isolated single lemmas would each suffice.

## Compatibility clause A

For each proposed finite construction, its proof must also supply the following source-bound facts.

1. The literal physical input/output operator, including absolute phase, current data versions, address spectators and each independent original input; original productive fields cannot be silently owned, identified or discarded.
2. The complete paid and productive inventories. Retired helpers are debited. Every query, repair, park/restore, route, boundary and recursive child is counted exactly once. Ordinary finite scalar/address work is bounded on the chosen computational model.
3. A uniform implementation at every legal width, with effective coefficient data, actual reciprocal directions when required, initialized owned scratch and its prescribed return. No unproved free code inverse or role-dependent address chart is admitted.
4. A finite mixed-target compiler instantiation with positive reserves, critical/mirror majorants, completion and splitting tails, actual demand/stock envelopes and source-specific precision and movement bounds.
5. An explicit exact parameter choice for the selected new outer endpoint satisfying all its applicable strict and functional inequalities at κ = K₀. For the retained new outer engine, these are the full 54 strict and four functional predicates named in the PR5413 source closure, not the historical balanced-transfer conversion. Reprove any predicate whose hypothesis the proposal changes.

A is a completion requirement of the conjectured construction. It is not a claim that all new finite words are automatically admitted. The intended open problem is to produce the specified construction with its full proof, not to prove one local identity and insert A as an assumed unknown subroutine.

For readers who want the compact exact logical form, a Gaussian entry means

    X_i := [its precisely stated Gaussian construction]
           AND [the B★ companion]
           AND [their source-bound compatibility A].

A BIT entry instead means its stated BIT construction, G★ and A. A genuinely joint alternative must state both sides directly. Each entry's source and attainable-first discussion refer primarily to its core; the shared companion remains open work.

## Q64: an explicit conservative sufficient Gaussian budget

**Q64 core contract.** Replace the local standardized PR5413 offer in every one of its 18 positions, preserving its full physical main/helper ABI and all independent target operators, so that its Gaussian paid histogram is bounded componentwise by the old local histogram minus 64 H₁. All other changed recursive work is already included in that bound. Ordinary work, precision and source width must satisfy A. An equivalent whole-caller construction with the same T and at least 1152 units of net paid-moment reduction at C★ is also a Q64 core.

This is a deliberately conservative sufficient budget, not an optimized threshold or a conjecture that 64 is necessary. Here is the accounting behind using it. The selected source funds a positive F at a saving strictly above 3/1000. On the interval [3/1000,C★], the unfavorable target part of F's derivative is bounded below by −5 Σ_k k T_k, since all target ranks are at most 145 and log 145 < 5; the paid derivative is nonnegative. The displayed T satisfies Σ_k k T_k < 250000. Thus passing from 3/1000 to C★ can lose less than 750 raw moment units. Q64 adds 18·64 = 1152, leaving strict positive raw headroom. These elementary accounting implications specify why a Q64 construction would be useful; they are not an attempted proof that such a construction exists.

The compiler and outer proofs in A still have to fit into that headroom. In particular Q64 alone does not imply κ ≥ K₀ while the ordinary BIT side is fixed below K₀. Each corresponding full X includes B★ and A explicitly.

More refined entries may instead use their exact Δ(C★), or build a wholly different provider with strictly positive full moment at C★. A question whose headline merely says “cheaper” is not complete until its stated quantitative budget and A are met.

## Why the BIT companion cannot be silently dropped

The selected package's ordinary BIT admitted saving is 17/5000 = 0.0034. The locally retained newer PR5512 advertises 0.003483969976, an ordinary component improvement which is still below K₀. Its atom saving 1/250 = 0.004 does not remove its charged ordinary stock conversion. Neither a Gaussian decoder nor a faster inverse-code implementation automatically raises that admitted BIT exponent.

Similarly, the historical formula κ = a/(1+a) is not used as the conversion for this selected mixed-target construction. The source itself warns that its old balanced-transfer tables are historical. All numbered targets use the actual new outer predicates through A; a changed Word RAM target must supply its own explicit complete outer theorem and model comparison.
