# Three BIT-side targets for the shared bottleneck

These are proposals, not proof claims or tested candidates. Use [the common contract](sufficiency-contract.md) for K₀ = 0.0035, C★ = 0.0036, B★ = 0.0037 and the compatibility clause A. Each **full** X below includes the explicitly open G★ Gaussian companion; its BIT core alone does not imply the whole multiplication result. No conjecture below was proved, disproved, searched or numerically evaluated in preparing this list.

The retained PR5512 source is a useful nearby starting point: 649 independent local inputs, 11 owned arrays, nine captured relations, 432 full and four partial helpers before the nine full-helper retirements, and admitted ordinary BIT saving 0.003483969976. Its atom saving is 0.004. These facts are not a certificate for B★.

## B1. Multi-epoch source-compatible capture kernels

**Full target X_B1.** There exists a changed finite native G20-type producer and its inverse which expose two or more chronological capture epochs on the actual SOURCE versions. At each epoch the captured forms have explicit independent helper pivots in the same physical entrance frame as their source terms. Retire those pivots as owned fields, prepare their actual whole-function relations and eliminate the corresponding whole-zero QUERY/COPY requests. The word preserves every retained arbitrary input and every remaining full or partial helper operator, and initializes and returns all owned data according to the source contract. Across a complete caller, let m₁ be the number of removed rank-1 calls, m₇ the number of removed rank-7 calls, and h₈ the number of retired full rank-8 helpers; let P_extra and T_extra describe every other changed paid and productive job. Its actual signed moment is

    F_new^BIT(D) = F_old^BIT(D)
                  + m₁ + m₇·7^(1−D) − h₈·8^(1−D)
                  + Σ T_extra,k k^(1−D) − Σ P_extra,r r^(1−D).

The construction has F_new^BIT(D) > 0 for some D with B★ < D < 1 and a complete ordinary BIT admission at B★, including all finite backend and stock losses. The full target includes a compatible G★ Gaussian supplier and A at K₀.

**Why this would suffice.** Its requested output is exactly the B★ companion, rather than a new raw moment alone; the G★ companion and A supply the other half of the common sufficient contract.

**Why prioritize it.** PR5421's capture-relation mechanism and PR5512's actual G20 application already connect whole-zero native forms to removed requests. The changed premise is multiple actual epochs and source versions, with their own prepared pivots. It is not an assertion that the nine fixed COPY slots secretly have a tenth independent relation, or that the same owned buffer can serve incompatible live epochs.

**First research object to formulate later.** One two-epoch word with its actual frame-labelled capture forms, leases and complete helper debit. No attempt to construct it is made here.

**Precedents.** Ali Romman PR5421; ai-nanachi PR5512 and its `research/bit-g20-nine-owned-relations/PROOF.md`; the compact productive controller PR4760/PR4814; DreamingOfClouds PR589 and eumemic PR555; Cinna Davis's query-thinned/source-compatible caller; zxv and Jacob Alan Sussman's compilation lineage.

**Relative priority:** first BIT core to investigate, because the missing object extends a working physical relation protocol. Achieving the quantitative B★ budget is additional open work, not guaranteed by finding one more relation.

## B2. Joint partial-helper production with retained complements

**Full target X_B2.** There is a changed source-compatible BIT producer in the compact partial-helper family which has an explicit finite list of productive helper projectors U_j with ranks r_j, including a larger useful partial-helper subsystem than the four retained rank-4 jobs. Each helper begins on its own arbitrary independent original function; its stated physical target is the actual paired-address involution D_(U_j), with a charged whole-job matcher. If a decomposition needs a complementary function component, that component is retained and returned with its own explicit operator and cost, rather than erased or counted twice. Joint native factorization of these projector jobs and the unchanged main SOURCE/receiver interface yields a complete paid histogram P' and productive histogram T' satisfying

    Σ_k T'_k k^(1−D) − Σ_r P'_r r^(1−D) > 0

for some D with B★ < D < 1, and the full ordinary compiler/cover/backend proof admits saving B★. The complete X_B2 includes G★ and A at K₀.

**Why this would suffice.** The stated ordinary admission meets B★; it does not infer an ordinary saving from the target ranks by themselves. Together with G★ and A it reaches the common endpoint.

**Why prioritize it.** The compact436 lineage has actual arbitrary-idempotent cover machinery and four working partial helpers. The desired changed object is joint production of a larger partial subsystem with all complementary data accounted for. This changes native contacts and outputs; it is not the fixed-source trick of adding fresh commuting axes to old rank-8 helper paths covered by PR5528's loss result.

**First research object to formulate later.** A pair of physical partial projectors whose actual combined native path costs less than their separately completed paths, with both source/receiver interfaces preserved. A pair alone would be progress, not the complete X_B2.

**Precedents.** ai-nanachi's compact436 PR4326 and productive-controller PR4760; completed ordinary PR4814 and G20 PR5512; `research/bit-compact-productive-controller/README.md`; Ali Romman's fixed-source price-gate PR5528; Cinna Davis, DreamingOfClouds, eumemic, zxv, Jacob Alan Sussman and the retained projector/ROW/native lineage.

**Relative priority:** second BIT core; it has concrete existing partial-projector and matcher precedents, but asks for a new joint native factorization and its complete ordinary admission.

## B3. Non-permutation output codes consumed across BIT calls

**Full target X_B3.** There exist explicitly given invertible binary owner codes C_in and C_out, a native BIT producer with the retained arbitrary-function and whole-ROW contracts, and a complete chronological consumer which accepts the producer's actual coded outputs without independently converting every call back to a permutation code. Each inter-call contact is performed in a genuinely common physical frame, and the code is retained as state only while its actual independent information remains available. At the final boundary the original independent target operators are restored, with every code application, deferred correction, owned field and normalization paid. General owner codes are permitted: C_out need not be a permutation.

Let T_code and P_code be the actual final independent targets and all recursive requests of this combined caller. Its full moment is strictly positive at some D > B★, and its fixed finite word has an ordinary BIT admission at B★. The full X_B3 includes G★ and A at K₀.

**Why this would suffice.** It supplies the ordinary B★ companion through a new producer/consumer contract; the required final target and admission prevent a coded intermediate matrix from being priced as an independent standard output. G★ and A then complete the implication.

**Why prioritize it.** PR5528 separates the permutation-only correction-rank floor from a retained arbitrary-invertible rank-three completion. General owner mixing is therefore a concrete changed premise, with a predecessor code to examine. The conjecture is not that the known rank-three code is a free native decoder; it asks for the complete code-resident caller and its strict total budget.

**First research object to formulate later.** One two-call physical owner-code contact in which input and output codes genuinely telescope and all original functions survive. No attempt to find or validate it is made here.

**Precedents.** `research/bit-fixed-source-price-gates/PROOF.md` and its permutation-rank appendix, Ali Romman PR5528; the retained PR589/PR555 code and compact-caller lineage; ai-nanachi PR4760/PR4814/PR5512; mixed-target recursion and ordinary-cover predecessors. The linked TI/symplectic transporter notes motivate explicit intertwiners; they do not supply a free native owner-code realization.

**Relative priority:** third BIT core, because an actual low-rank code precedent exists, but the missing consumer changes more of the caller's state contract than B1 or B2.
