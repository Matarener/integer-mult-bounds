# 27 research targets for conditional κ ≥ 0.0035

Prepared for Dugongue with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance from one coordinator and three workers. This is a question atlas for other researchers, not a new κ certificate. No proposed construction was proved, disproved, searched, simulated or numerically evaluated. No web search, remote PR fetch, Lean check or mathematical source replay was performed.

**Each full numbered target fills the requested blank:** if its stated construction exists with its explicitly included companion and compatibility proof, then the selected-model conditional integer-multiplication construction supports κ ≥ 7/2000 = 0.0035. The same five framework assumptions as the selected record remain explicit. A Gaussian-only core does not silently establish the full conclusion.

There are 27 numbered targets: eight native-provider questions P1–P8, eight controller/interface questions C1–C8, eight structural questions S1–S8, and three additional ordinary-BIT questions B1–B3. Related formulations are cross-referenced below; their number is not a claim of 27 independent mechanisms or 27 new mathematical discoveries.

## Read this qualification first

The retained selected ordinary BIT saving is 0.0034; newer PR5512 advertises 0.003483969976. Both are below 0.0035. An improved Gaussian provider alone therefore does not establish the requested conclusion under that unchanged outer contract. Its atom saving 0.004 is a different interface.

Accordingly, every Gaussian-focused full X explicitly includes an **open ordinary-BIT companion B★ at 0.0037**. The four BIT-focused full targets B1, B2, B3 and S8 explicitly include an **open complete Gaussian companion G★ at 0.0036**. Every target also includes the exact physical, compiler, precision, movement and outer compatibility clause A. These are included parts of the question, not granted results or assumed hidden gadgets. Read [sufficiency-contract.md](sufficiency-contract.md) for the precise definitions and complete implication budget.

This format preserves what is actually sufficient. A single core such as “find one reusable query” can be a useful first step, but it is not advertised as a theorem that alone raises κ to 0.0035. The atlas asks for complete alternative research packages sharing a real bottleneck.

## Quantitative content rather than “some improvement”

The common contract fixes Gaussian target C★ = 0.0036 and ordinary BIT target B★ = 0.0037, leaving margin above 0.0035. The full current PR5413 paid and independent target histograms are reproduced there. For an identical-ABI local substitution, a reduction of 64 rank-1 requests per local occurrence, with unchanged productive operators, is a conservative sufficient Gaussian budget. Across the 18-call caller this is 1152 units of raw moment improvement. P1–P5 use that explicit budget; P6–P8 give their own precisely stated equivalent or stronger budget.

Other entries have complete inequalities of the form

    Σ independent_target_count · rank^(1−C★)
      > Σ total_paid_count · rank^(1−C★),

using their own actual changed targets and paid requests. Their inequalities are clauses to establish, not signs that were evaluated here. Every entry also requires the positive finite compiler and outer reserves in A. Local debt, a code's scalar rank, mode dimension, a faster verification script or a larger uncompleted raw root is not substituted for this complete accounting.

## Overlap map: avoid duplicating another worker's question

- P1, P4, P8 and C1 concern related query-sharing ideas but different witness contracts: local three-term relations; an already completed independent helper; a whole thin-query state realization; and cross-cut observations. Share extracted source-version data before opening separate construction campaigns.
- P2 and C8 belong to the same sector-multiplexing family. P2 specifies 64 local pairs; C8 specifies general complete-caller sector factorizations. They are two precise alternative questions, not claimed independent mechanisms.
- P3 and B1 both use causal captured relations. Their Gaussian and characteristic-two native frames, arithmetic and helper debts differ; a proof in one does not automatically transfer to the other.
- C6, S3–S5 and B3 retain codes, records or displacement state. Their actual code operators and service types differ, so do not substitute one consumer's bill for another.
- S1, S2 and S7 use different whole-K/E objects. Odd-112, compressed ambient-18 and full-120 data, mode counts and target ranks are kept separate.
- C3 and the previous characteristic-direction decoder use real independent directions. Repeating the same Q cannot earn fresh rank. The prior full-rank H2 repair does not make the old whole-K invoice profitable.
