# Evidence and exact covered domain

This package fixes one 12-port D4 geometry in seven binary ADDRESS dimensions. Each logical field is an arbitrary Gaussian-dyadic array. The OPEN/reverse words have 24 arbitrary source/target fields; the four-helper bus has 28 arbitrary fields. The four helper inputs are independent dirt. Four orientations are distinguished: W, its literal reverse inverse, the same-chronology inverse transpose, and that dual word's literal inverse. A unitary native K dualizes to its entrywise conjugate K, not its adjoint. Neither ZERO frame nor native rank 0 means zero data.

| Evidence | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| PROOF-NATIVE.md and check_native.py | Literal carrier/bus words, all field inputs, complete native paths, actual phase and local scalar/scan bill |
| PROOF-NORMALIZATION.md and check_normalization.py | Split-Lagrangian written lemma, four explicit C-port gauges, both orientations, exact inverse wrappers and literal monomial columns |

| artifacts/NATIVE.json.gz, artifacts/NORMALIZATION.json.gz and artifacts/STARS.json.gz | Complete deterministic records regenerated by the registered commands |
| REVIEW-VERIFICATION.md and controls.py | Frozen-byte execution, complete typed record coverage and actual acceptance controls |
| frozen/history | Unchanged historical construction and receipt context; these files are not executed as the new verification |

The exact compiler is shared source infrastructure, authenticated before execution and compiled from captured bytes with assertions enabled. Separate new checkers were constructed and cross-reviewed in distinct mathematical contexts, not separate independently implemented Clifford compilers. The normalization checker additionally interprets its literal monomial lowering on every ADDRESS column. No finite-character approximation replaces the written tensor lift.

All regenerated record fields are compared, with integer/float/boolean distinctions, list order, duplicate-key and nonfinite-number checks. All three records are required by exact stage coverage. Original inputs and scripts have SHA256 bindings in FROZEN-SHA256.json and ORIGINS.json; regenerated gzip records are compared by their complete decompressed JSON content. Output is external temporary state.

Historical FINAL-FREEZE omitted d4_geometry.py, which source_carrier.py and reverse_carrier.py imported. Its original bytes are preserved here as provenance. The new checkers do not execute historical importlib loaders or depend on the old absolute workspace paths. Historical generation/search receipts are not claimed as fresh discovery runs. Only the four frozen normalizer winners are replayed.

The selected 12-port block is a specific input to this package. The older original 9D/P112 endpoints and source batching are a distinct domain. The full parent tiling includes 120 labels with 8 omitted; there is no uniform P112-wide h7-width assertion. C14's moved-helper bus channels supply context, while its closed-star, one-N and causal-controller claims are separate.

Exact native counts, paid scalar/monomial scan upper bounds, physical tape cost, owner feasibility, prefix/rounded precision and an improved recursive moment are distinct obligations. The last four remain open. AI review and package CI are separate from formal-kernel or independent human acceptance.
