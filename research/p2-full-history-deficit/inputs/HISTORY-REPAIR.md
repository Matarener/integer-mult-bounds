# At least 104 paid returns are needed to repair the frozen caller

At every width f>=72, the frozen PR4948 passive caller needs at least
**104 additional paid field returns** to meet its original target. New
calls may use earlier new returns. Even if each new return costs only one
rank-one job, the complete optimistic invoice fails for every tariff
r^p with 0<p<=1. Thus adding paid repairs cannot make this frozen word
profitable under this invoice and target contract.

This extends the zero-new-call result in [APPLICATION.md](APPLICATION.md).
It also gives an error bound when a repair uses q<104 returns. The history
dimension principle is inherited from Chris R's PR5425/PR5446 and the
[relative-history lemma in PR5449](https://github.com/aliromman/integer-mult-bounds/blob/30ff28bfa7986b5534794f58de0a6359feb474cf/research/paid-history-quotient/README.md).
The new result binds that principle to the actual retained source, adds
a quantitative approximation bound, and closes the optimistic repair budget.

## 1. Exact contract

Use the source pins and the three mandatory proof documents in
[APPLICATION.md](APPLICATION.md). In particular, keep all original paid
input expressions. Grant the decoder all 39 observed banks at the start,
even observations that physically occur later. Grant all original inputs
and all autonomous helper histories as well. There are 448 independent
main inputs and 816 independent helpers. Every original output remains
required. Copies are dependent fields, with no new target credit.

Fix f and let V=(C^(2^17))^(tensor f). One additional paid return is one
V-valued linear row on the independent inputs. It can be any linear map;
it need not be a Clifford map or commute with ordinary operations. It
can depend on all retained rows and earlier new returns. A primitive
with several V-valued outputs counts each new output separately.

Ordinary operations are scalar row combinations and left actions from
the fixed columnwise monomial Clifford algebra. We grant the whole such
algebra, which contains the actual finite code operations. Storage,
COPY, initialization and owned CLEAR may all be free. A lost or cleared
row remains in the conceptual history used for the lower bound. Scalar
coefficients, program length and choices may depend on f. The program
must remain linear in the data. No data-dependent branch is included.

Feedback among new paid calls is allowed. Feedback that changes an
original paid input is excluded: its response would no longer be a
member of the frozen observation list. Extra free observations, native
internal prefixes, larger field capacity, noncolumnwise payload maps,
antilinear operations and changed target credits also need a new contract.

For the price conclusion, each additional charged primitive is a positive
rank job on this field, of rank r>=1. A new multi-output supplier must
account for its actual paid primitives. The proof does not charge its
free outputs as extra queries. A supplier with a different ordinary
alphabet, capacity or price rule is outside this statement.

## 2. Relative history dimension at one width

**Reusable form.** Let H be any subgroup of the projective n-axis Clifford
group. Suppose the initial history is in a coefficient span that is
invariant under left H. In each of b independent input columns, let the
required unitary Clifford target T_j have its whole coset H T_j outside
that initial coefficient support. At any width with full-Clifford Gram
lower bound lambda>0, an exact decoder needs at least b additional
same-capacity returns. With q returns its selected block error satisfies
`D^(-f)||E||_HS^2 >= lambda(b-q)_+` and
`||E||_op^2 >= lambda(1-q/b)_+`, where D=2^n.
The targets must occupy distinct diagonal input/output positions. The
ordinary operations, arbitrary new linear returns and phase rules are
those of section 1. The proof below uses b=104 and a common T; replacing
these by b and T_j gives the stated general form. For another caller,
the complete initial history and coset exclusion must be proved anew.

Let H be the projective monomial Clifford group on 17 axes, with h elements.
Choose unitary representatives. Its tensor lifts, including their phase
factors, span a unital algebra A at the fixed width. The pointwise theorem
in [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) shows that dim A=h for f>=72. Multiplication of
projective representatives can introduce a phase. It does not change this
dimension or the algebra closure.

Restrict all input rows to the 104 zero-diagonal ports of original main
bank 0. Set every other independent input to zero. This is a legitimate
restriction of the required endpoint. The autonomous helper rows then
vanish. Let M be the A-module generated by all frozen observation rows
after this restriction.

The source audit gives pure Clifford grades in {0,1,7} in each of these
input columns. Left monomial action preserves each grade. Thus M lies
in the span of these grades, separately in every input column. The
desired first target bank has diagonal coefficient T=D1 Q^-1, of grade 9.
Write t_j for the row with T^(tensor f) in input column j and zero elsewhere.

For each j, the h words H T are projectively distinct. They all have
grade 9. The pointwise theorem shows that the h rows in A t_j are
independent. Different j have different independent input columns.
Consequently the target module L=sum_j A t_j has dimension 104h, and
L intersects M only at zero. This uses the complete finite Clifford
alphabet, not a free-word interpretation of its physical matrices.

Let b_1,...,b_q be the new paid rows, in their actual order. After every
prefix, every available row lies in

    M + A b_1 + ... + A b_q.                              (1)

Ordinary operations preserve this module. One new return adds a cyclic
module A b_i of dimension at most h. This statement needs no restriction
on the paid map and no assumption that A has no zero divisors. Overwrite,
clear and feedback do not invalidate (1). If every t_j is returned,
the final module contains L. Dimension in the quotient by M gives

    104h <= dim((M + sum_i A b_i)/M) <= qh,
    q >= 104.                                            (2)

This is the relative-history argument of PR5449, with its missing
physical source and all-width premises supplied here. Counting nominal
output rows alone would not prove (2): singular target rows can generate
smaller cyclic modules. The invertible unitary T and pointwise
faithfulness supply the full dimension h for each present target.

The count is attained for this isolated target block in the enlarged
arbitrary-linear-oracle model: return each t_j in one call. This is not
a claim that 104 native calls suffice for the complete four-bank caller,
helper restoration or a permitted physical implementation.

## 3. Error when fewer returns are used

Give operator rows the normalized Hilbert--Schmidt inner product, with
normalization D^(-f), D=2^17. Let lambda_f be any lower Gram bound for
the complete projective Clifford alphabet. In this package one can use
lambda_f=1/9 for f>=72, and lambda_f=125/126 for f>=84.

Form the 104h-column frame F whose columns are H t_j. Let Q be the
orthogonal projection onto the complement of M. For a coefficient
vector c and any m in M, the Clifford coefficient supports of F c and
m are disjoint in every input column. The pointwise Gram bound gives

    ||F c - m||^2 >= lambda_f ||c||^2.

Take the infimum over m. Hence (QF)* QF >= lambda_f I. The subspace
N=Q(sum_i A b_i) has dimension at most qh. Let P_N be its orthogonal
projection. A basic rank approximation inequality gives

    ||(I-P_N) QF||_HS^2 >= lambda_f (104-q)_+ h.           (3)

For completeness, if a frame B has B*B>=lambda I_m and P has rank at
most r, choose m-r orthonormal vectors in ker(PB). Their squared image
norms are at least lambda each. Extend them to an orthonormal basis and
sum. This proves ||(I-P)B||_HS^2>=lambda(m-r). It is the elementary
rank bound behind classical low-rank approximation, not a new general
matrix approximation theorem.

Suppose the decoder produces rows y_j, with error e_j=t_j-y_j. Each
H y_j lies in M+sum_i A b_i. Unitary left action preserves each error
norm. Thus the total squared error over the 104h orbit rows is exactly
h sum_j ||e_j||^2. It is at least (3). After division by h, the selected
104-output by 104-input block error E satisfies

    D^(-f) ||E||_HS^2 >= lambda_f (104-q)_+,
    ||E||_op^2 >= lambda_f (1-q/104)_+.                   (4)

The second inequality uses rank(E)<=104 D^f. It is a worst-case error
on the direct sum of the 104 input spaces. It does not assert that 104
individual worst-case errors occur on one common field input. A complete
endpoint has at least this operator norm error, since restriction and
selection are contractions. New paid rows may lie outside the Clifford
span: only their module dimension enters (3).

For example, at q=103 and f>=84 the squared operator norm error is at
least 125/13104. At q=0 and f>=72 it is at least 1/9, as in the original
passive application. Error below the corresponding q=103 threshold
therefore also requires at least 104 returns.

## 4. The complete optimistic invoice is adverse

The authenticated frozen mixed checker supplies these job histograms:

    P = {1:4754, 2:824, 3:1020, 4:254, 5:80, 8:224}
    T = {4:56, 7:14, 8:94, 9:876, 17:224}.

T includes all original helper credits exactly once. The source calls
this an optimistic, unrealized invoice. The histograms are frozen source
declarations; this package does not recount a new physical word. A lower-bound test can grant
every omitted finite cost as zero. By (2), a repair must add at least
104 positive-rank returns. Replace them all by rank-one jobs to obtain
the cheaper paid profile P'=P+104 delta_1. Do not remove an original paid
job, change its input, or add a target credit in this comparison.

For d=P'-T, calculate K(k)=sum_r d_r min(r,k). The exact support knots are:

| k | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 17 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| K(k) | 5996 | 7134 | 7448 | 6742 | 5838 | 3870 | 2900 | 1800 | 8 |

Between support knots K is linear. All its positive integer values are
positive. For any increasing concave tariff phi with phi(0)=0, set
s_k=phi(k)-phi(k-1). Summation by parts gives

    sum_r d_r phi(r)
      = s_17 K(17) + sum_(k=1..16) (s_k-s_(k+1)) K(k) >= 0. (5)

For phi(r)=r^p with 0<p<=1, s_17>0 and K(17)=8. The inequality is strict.
The original rank-mass surplus is 96; the unavoidable repair costs at
least 104. Equation (5) shows the loss at every positive concave power,
not merely at p=1 or at a numerical sample of exponents. No rounding or
root estimate is used. Constant-zero or flat concave tariffs need not
give a strict inequality.

This applies the repository's existing
[exact invoice comparator](../../scripts/compare_recursive_invoices.py),
with its credit to classical concave order, mightyOil and predecessors.
The new [repair invoice](REPAIR-INVOICE.json) binds this arithmetic to
the source histograms and the proved added-return count. The comparator
alone does not establish those physical premises.

There is also an error floor for any profitable approximate repair.
With 96 added rank-one jobs the same hinges remain nonnegative, with
K(17)=0 and the earlier knots positive. Such a repair cannot have a
positive margin for any 0<p<=1. Thus any positive margin requires q<=95.
Equation (4) then gives squared operator error at least 1/104 from
width 72, and at least 125/1456 from width 84.

At the explicit reference tariff p=1-605457647/200000000000, the pinned
source interval engine encloses the unrepaired margin strictly between
36 and 37. This uses the selected kappa value only to choose a reference
tariff; it is not a selected-caller replay or an outer admission result.
Positive margin permits at most 36 rank-one returns. Every such repair
has squared operator error at least 17/234 from width 72, and at least
2125/3276 from width 84. The exact outward-rounded interval is retained
in REPAIR-RESULT.json. All other added costs are still granted zero.

## Verification, attribution and remaining work

[verify_repair.py](verify_repair.py) replays the unchanged source application,
loads the actual target row and invoice through the retained portable
loader, and checks the exact cost certificate. Small physical Clifford
examples test module dimensions, cached targets, feedback, zero divisors,
phase powers and approximation errors with exact Gaussian rationals.
The written proof supplies the general quantifiers. Finite checks are
not a proof kernel or an independent human review.

Dugongue supplies PR4948 and its native source. Chris R / Clear1nsight
supplies the retained passive audit, both clarifications and the
PR5425/PR5446 history arguments. Ali Romman's PR5449 supplies the explicit
relative-history quotient formulation. Carl Eckart and Gale Young's
[1936 paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02288367) is a classical source for
low-rank matrix approximation. This package does not claim those methods
as new. The retained interval engine is zxv's exact outward-rounded
arithmetic from `higher-moment-strictification`, source commit
`20bb93cd990d2ff683e4d0fc56b87a42cd5d3296`; its frozen input is already
declared in SOURCES.json. Ali Romman prepared this extension with substantial OpenAI Codex
assistance. Apache-2.0.

**Open next questions.** A changed original paid input can create a new
grade-9 path and invalidate the frozen-history premise. Bind and price
that changed word separately. A richer ordinary alphabet can also change
the module dimension. For another caller, derive the complete retained
history before applying (2) or (4); an already cached target removes a
charge. Sharper width and spectral constants remain open. There is no
new caller, kappa improvement, selected-frontier change or global barrier.
