# Bind and strengthen the complete p2 repair deficit

The frozen coded p2 caller needs at least **225 new same-capacity linear
returns** at every width f>=72. The previous PR5526 bound used 104 columns.
This package binds all four original banks to the literal observation list,
then uses coupled rows to reach 224 negative targets and one further positive
output obstruction. It also gives a 240-return construction in the enlarged
arbitrary-linear-oracle model. The exact minimum is in [225,240].

No new kappa or physical supplier is claimed. The constructive upper bound is
not a 240-H1 native word. Even the lower bill of 225 added H1 jobs makes the
complete frozen optimistic invoice adverse for every power tariff r^t,
0<t<=1. New costs and lost target credit cannot be omitted from a changed caller.

The [proof](PROOF.md) states the full model and its limits. The new result is
useful because it closes the source-binding handoff from PR5570, identifies
why the eight selected columns in each negative bank also fail, and isolates
the last positive-output repair as the rank of I-C3 C1. A future caller must
change that source contract or pay the missing work. A sampled bank trace or
another numerical root would not establish these facts.

columnwise relaxation and reached all 224 negative targets. The complete
late-history analysis then exposed a further nonzero positive-output
obligation, giving 225. A rank-eight factorization supplies the 240-return
oracle construction and identifies the remaining exact optimization.

contributions for each intermediate bound.

proof. Hosted CI and independent human review are separate from local checks.
