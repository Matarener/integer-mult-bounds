# Entry-frame discovery and a checked positive control

`complete_candidate.py` supplies an exact rational interpreter for the
affected arbitrary helper input, including the chronological free warp
words. Its native frame walk recounts the supplied local word and rejects
a proposed terminal scalar repair when its source or destination is not
FULL. It is a construction aid, not an independent all-column physical
native compiler.

The neighboring `coupled-boundary` package uses this interpreter for its
literal five-helper combinations. Those partial words preserve all
protected outputs but leave the specified FULL donor dirt. Their HPE-ended
source helpers cannot simply be read by a terminal FULL scalar repair.
No completed improving candidate was produced.

## Positive control

From the repository root:

```sh
python3 -B research/kappa-boundary-continuation/entry-retiming/positive_control.py
```

This reverses the already retained helper544 move, checks its two remaining
FULL errors, and appends the two legal scalar repairs. The rebuilt profile
is `2437 H1 + 724 H2 + 226 H3 + 102 H4 + 20 H5 + 8 H8`, with rank mass5135.
The retained word has mass5134. This is a known control, not a new
improvement or an independent full physical admission. The projected
release search correctly rediscovers the old544 move on this control.

## Discovery scripts and limits

`search_odd.py` and `search_odd_cuts.py` search a narrower full-B-map
preservation ansatz. That ansatz misses the known544 positive control:
its negative output cannot exclude the projected release mechanism and
is not used as a barrier or a replay gate in the main report.

`search_frame_lifts.py`, `greedy_frames.py`, and `greedy_raise.py` are
discovery scans of particular monotone frame changes. The greedy passes
use floating-point prices to nominate candidates; their negative results
are observations, not exact global optimality proofs. They did not nominate
an improving complete word. Their inputs now resolve relative to the
retained vendored followup, so the scripts can be inspected or rerun after
relocation. They are intentionally outside the formal replay gates.
