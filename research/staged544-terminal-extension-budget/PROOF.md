## Exact contract and claim

Let k = 1730663236534549309146/10^24, s=1-k, and h(r)=r^s for r>0,
h(0)=0. This is the lower endpoint of the root enclosure claimed in PR3945;
its source moment is sum n_r r(73/r)^k. Comparing sum n_r h(r) is equivalent,
since the common positive factor73^k and the normalizer remain unchanged.
The endpoint is a source pin, not a new root or supplier validation.

Keep either pinned PR3945 word's helper entrances and every paid interior
scalar/native frame unchanged. Enlarge any chosen terminal subspaces by
nonnegative dimensions. They need not be coordinate extensions, uniform
across calls, or equal between providers. Keep the ambient binary dimension9,
helper identities and the bin inventory at T360. Permit an arbitrary new owner
assignment; for the lower bound, drop all time-window and family restrictions,
and even permit fractional packing. Each helper remains whole in one bin and
each unused remainder is paid as one recursive child.

If D>0 is the total number of added helper dimensions over all calls, the new
complete local-plus-idle tariff exceeds the incumbent by more than

    0.006843664113335406486182 D.

Thus terminal-only enlargement cannot improve the complete tariff even with
unrestricted repacking in this relaxed model. If an actual implementation adds
other costs, they cannot repair this deficit. If interior repricing introduces
savings, it must supply more than this amount to beat the incumbent; this
necessary allowance is not a sufficient construction. Mixed shrink/enlarge
moves and changed helper entrances are outside the theorem.

## Local rank function and the full budget

A terminal frame is a subspace of F_2^9. If its last paid climb had dimension
r and the terminal extends by d while the preceding frame is fixed, the new
charge is h(r+d) instead of h(r). The Grassmann rank identity gives this
increase independently of the extension's orientation. Because0<s<1, h is
concave and its discrete increments decrease. For0<=r<r+d<=9,

    h(r+d)-h(r) >= d*(h(9)-h(8)).

This is also checked as nine exact inequalities. Summing all roles gives a
local surcharge at least mD, m=h(9)-h(8). The finite certificate encloses

    0.987736182658129815011783 < q < 0.987736182658129815011784,
    0.994579846771465221497966 < m < 0.994579846771465221497967,
    0.006843664113335406486182 < m-q < 0.006843664113335406486183.

Adding the local and idle inequalities proves the stated complete deficit.
No dirty-state or phase relaxation is used to assert a new working circuit:
this is a necessary-cost rejection of a broader relaxed class, so proving
some candidate semantically valid could only shrink that class.

## Sharper fixed-identity zero-feasibility certificate

Retaining actual owner/time/child/port identities is even more restrictive.
For each role a nonnegative increment d obeys both

    d <= 9-dim(terminal),
    sum_{roles in bin} d <= capacity - occupied width.

Every occupied bin with positive residual capacity has only width9 roles,
whose terminal dimension is9. There are23328 bins with five width9 roles and
slack3, and4320 bins with three width9 roles and slack5. Every other occupied
bin is saturated. The137952 empty rank48 bins contain no retained role.
Consequently every d is zero. This is a rank-capacity certificate, not a
failed enumeration of frame orientations. It covers call-specific increments.
A proposed role reassignment invalidates this sharper premise, but is still
covered by the more permissive tariff theorem above.

## Bounded pivot: the17 direct rank-one refunds cannot finance an extension

There is a concrete terminal-only shrinking option, so we priced it rather
than interpreting the one-sided theorem as an obstruction to every change.
Both frozen providers have exactly17 protected width5 helpers whose last
terminal climb has rank1. Their preceding frame is the unique legal terminal
of one less dimension. The other74 protected terminals have zero last climb,
so cannot shrink with the preceding frames fixed. The unprotected helpers
retain FULL terminals under the inherited cleanup contract; adding new private
buffers is outside this scope.

Extend the credited dual to width4 by pi(4)=1-h(5). The supplementary checker
examines all2551 patterns in widths4..9 at the same exact3945 endpoint. It finds
12 symbolic tight and2539 strictly positive inequalities. The original actual
packing still attains the bound. Therefore every width5->4 refund raises the
best possible idle tariff by at least pi(4)-pi(5)=1, exactly canceling its
saved terminal rank1 child. Such a refund can tie, but cannot pay by itself.

For simultaneous direct refunds on R roles and terminal extensions adding D
other dimensions, the idle change is at least R-qD while the local change is
at least -R+mD. The same positive (m-q)D deficit remains. For D=0 the complete
change is nonnegative. This rejects that concrete mixed pivot before any
relocation search. Equal-dimension frame rotations are tariff-neutral here;
changing interior frames, entrances or the protected-buffer set remains open.
No larger terminal search or unpriced new native module is justified by this
pivot. The supplementary code shares the main exact power routine and is not
claimed as an independent numerical implementation.
