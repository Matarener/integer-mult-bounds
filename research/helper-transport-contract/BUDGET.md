# Exact budget decision for a changed transport or helper boundary

Prepared for apoursh with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. Apache-2.0.
This is a local arithmetic and operator-contract audit, not a new supplier or a
new bound. No source tape or selected record was modified.

## Decision

A paid auxiliary transport cannot be financed by the entire optimistic
twenty-field regrouping allowance if it adds even one positive-rank recursive
job **per LOCAL**. The isolated value-preserving repair of the native line warp
costs H2; even one such repair shared across all eighteen LOCALs costs more than
all eighteen optimistic regrouping allowances combined.

A materially different route is the productive successor hand-off in
[GamingPuzzled's PR5472](https://github.com/CrocSwap/integer-mult-bounds/pull/5472),
pinned here to `ce96fe9747ea0643bd8c30d448c1ba8e2b30ac30`. Splitting a rank-nine
target into rank four and rank five earns a positive moment credit with no
change in paid work. Its author supplies a checked small gadget; this package does not independently
replay or admit that gadget. Its real-supplier crossing and consumer boundary remain unconstructed.
One new H1 per hand-off would outweigh its credit.

The surviving constructive question is therefore a changed native word that
reuses already-paid climbs to create the required contact and preserves the
consumer obligations. The calculations below exclude specific paid repairs;
they do not prove that all changed words, gauges or interfaces are impossible.

## 2. The maximum allowance with retained twenty-field endpoints

The source interval is backward `[9044,9137)`, restricted to the dependency
closure of fields19,300,301. The script derives its twenty fields and85 selected
events from the actual source, checks the frames through the prefix, and extracts
the endpoint invoice. Its paid invoice is 38 H1. The endpoint
rank increments are thirteen line-to-cube increments of 2, three line-to-plane
increments of 1, one zero-to-cube increment of 3, and three zero-to-plane
increments of 2. Thus the most optimistic direct-endpoint invoice is

\[
P_{\mathrm{ideal}}=3H_1+16H_2+H_3.
\]

For \(0<t<1\), nonnegative recursive ranks \(r_1,\ldots,r_m\) paid along one
retained carrier satisfy
\(\sum r_j^t\geq(\sum r_j)^t\geq d^t\), where \(d\) is its required endpoint
dimension increase. This proves the allowance

\[
B_{20}=38-(3+16\,2^t+3^t)
      =35-16\,2^t-3^t
      =0.07703787664069600\ldots .
\]

This endpoint lower bound grants all scalar mixing and any equal-rank warps for
free. The old twenty-field proof shows the ideal cannot be attained under its
fixed monotone, no-auxiliary, no-new-warp model. Here it is deliberately an
optimistic allowance after changing one of those restrictions; it is not a
construction. The bound still requires the same retained carrier endpoint
increments and the same recursive rank semantics. A changed matching of inputs
to outputs, new productive entrance/exit contracts, or a new cost model must
rederive it. In particular, it is not a universal ceiling for every boundary
change.

An elementary bound also makes the exclusion robust:

\[
B_{20}=32(1-2^{-s})+3(1-3^{-s})
\leq s(32\log 2+3\log 3)<25.7s<0.078<1.
\]

## 3. Complete signed accounting and multiplicities

Write \(A\) for removed paid moment, \(C\) for all added paid transport and
restoration, \(L\) for surrendered productive credit, and \(G\) for new,
independently justified productive credit. A necessary raw improvement condition
is

\[
A+G>C+L.
\]

For the retained-endpoint regrouping question, \(A\leq B_{20}\). Every additional
positive-integer-rank job costs at least 1. Losing an entire positive-rank target
also costs at least 1. Neither can be financed locally by \(B_{20}\), even before
charging other work. Unknown restoration and consumer debits cannot be set to
zero.

Charging one event per LOCAL differs from charging one global event. All prices
below have certified outward rational enclosures in `verify_budget.py`; the
decimals are abbreviated for reading.

| Quantity | Moment |
|---|---:|
| Twenty-field optimistic saving \(B_{20}\) | 0.07703787664069600 |
| Eighteen such savings | 1.38668177953252805 |
| One H1 | 1 |
| One H2 | 1.99580768739754101 |
| One H3 | 2.99003912499864780 |
| One isolated H7 exit coefficient | 6.95888540202818528 |
| One productive9→4+5 split | 0.01861244404255384 |
| Eighteen such splits | 0.33502399276596921 |
| Conditional9→1+8 split, not admitted credit | 0.009463658975626097 |
| Eighteen such conditional first-line splits | 0.170345861561269763 |
| \(B_{20}\) plus one split per LOCAL | 0.09565032068324985 |
| All eighteen copies of that optimistic combined allowance | 1.72170577229849726 |

Consequences:

- One added H1 per LOCAL loses even against the combined ideal saving plus one
  target split per LOCAL.
- The eighteen-call ideal saving could pay one once-global H1 as an arithmetic
  matter. This is neither a shared transport construction nor evidence that the
  unattainable ideal saving can be realized.
- One H2 charged only once globally already exceeds all eighteen ideal savings,
  even after also granting one productive9→4+5 split per LOCAL.
- More target splits or other independently proved savings change the budget.
  For example,54 such splits can in principle pay one H1;18 cannot. No statement
  here excludes a broader mechanism with a different complete invoice.

Even a one-unit downgrade of a target of integer rank at most 145 costs at least
\(t145^{t-1}>0.982064895964294826\), by the mean-value theorem. That exceeds the
local allowance. This is a scoped convenience bound for the selected rank
range, not a rule for arbitrary ranks or a reason to ignore compensating credit.

## 4. Independent assessment of the isolated native-warp proposal

The native worker provides exact full-tableau and absolute-phase tests on
dimension2 and on the selected dimension9 lines273 and324. In its convention,
with canonical frame maps \(T_U,T_V\), a legal zero-recursive-rank warp is

\[
W=T_V K T_U^{-1},
\]

where \(K\) is a nontrivial logical permutation. Value-preserving transport is
instead \(T_VT_U^{-1}\). A correction
\(C=T_VK^{-1}T_V^{-1}\) satisfies \(CW=T_VT_U^{-1}\); for the two tested distinct
lines, the compiler gives rank2 for both the correction and the value-preserving
transport. Thus **one H2 plus its monomial adapters** is a sufficient isolated
one-way repair. A return trip could add cost, but is not needed for the losing
budget conclusion.

The scalar-transfer reason is independent of the compiler calculation: warping
only a source, applying ADD, and undoing the source transfers \(Kx\), not \(x\).
Undoing the source does not undo the data already added to the destination.
Putting the same logical gauge on both participants makes scalar ADD commute
with that common gauge. A common invertible binary map preserves intersections
and dimensions; by itself it does not create a meeting of previously unequal
frames. More complicated rewrites involving other fields remain outside this
isolated statement.

This is not semantic impossibility of transport: the exact paid repair exists.
It is a budget exclusion for financing that repair from this specific local
regrouping allowance. Rank-zero operations still have ordinary movement/scalar
cost, which a future complete caller must fund.

## 5. Different interface: productive successor hand-off

The pinned PR5472 mechanism lets an arbitrary independent successor start at a
middle frame. When the conduit and its carried port value meet at that frame,
scalar cancellation leaves the old helper pure and transfers the conduit to
the successor. All above-frame consumers must have had an earlier opportunity
to subtract the successor's still-pure input. Only then may later operations be
renamed without changing paid climbs.

For a9→4+5 hand-off the signed target change is
\(\Delta T=H_4+H_5-H_9\), and the paid change is zero. Its gain is

\[
G_{4,5}=4^t+5^t-9^t=0.0186124440425538449\ldots>0.
\]

Strict concavity makes4+5 the largest credit among single positive-integer
splits of 9. The new local cut control that retires a helper at its first line
would instead suggest the smaller conditional credit
\(1+8^t-9^t=0.009463658975626097\ldots\). Restoring an arbitrary cut coordinate
does not establish this target: the selected helper's cut value is mixed with
original input columns, and the physical entrance/exit basis and all consumers
still need to be handled. The checker labels this credit as unadmitted. A
retirement at the next plane with the successor born at the first line duplicates
one H1; even the larger4+5 credit cannot pay that charge.

The native check examines a specified same-label formal exit shear: each of the
sixteen named Y outputs ends in a rank-eight frame containing line21,
so its specified value-preserving coefficient into the retired line has Fourier
rank7. These are not claimed to be the mandatory coefficients of the true inverse
through the main transform, which remains unconstructed. Pricing just one such
coefficient as a retained Clifford transport costs
\(7^t=6.958885402028185276\ldots\), already far above both the conditional1+8
credit and that credit plus \(B_{20}\). This does not claim sixteen H7 charges,
a lower bound for every joint decoder, or a failure of input rebasing itself.
The missing object is a sufficiently cheap complete exit/consumer realization.

This changes productive ownership and is therefore materially different from
moving an owned scratch field. If the successor is owned zero rather than a
fresh arbitrary productive input, its H5 credit is unavailable. Retiring the
old target at rank4 then surrenders
\(9^t-4^t=4.957085643947558602\ldots\), before any new paid work.

The small PR5472 gadget validates a5→3+2 pattern, not the full selected9→4+5
supplier. Its own screens find no suitable crossing in the retained word. The
native worker's two-field gauge argument also blocks manufacturing such an
isolated clean cancellation by independently warping the conduit and port:
purity of \(P(h+a)-qQa\) for arbitrary \(a\) requires \(P=qQ\). It does not rule
out a new word that creates the contact using existing paid climbs.

Therefore the next constructive contract should demand: one explicit new
contact; an arbitrary productive successor with its entrance/exit frames;
every affected consumer's pre-subtraction; unchanged complete paid histogram;
the new complete target histogram; and exact endpoint rows and native phases.
The owned relations, controller, caller and strict compiler must then be
re-derived before claiming an improved selected bound. This session does not
build or admit that word.

## Reproduction and remaining questions

Run the small source-pinned calculation with:

```sh
python3 -B verify_budget.py --repo /absolute/path/to/integer-mult-bounds-transport --json
python3 -B -O verify_budget.py --repo /absolute/path/to/integer-mult-bounds-transport
```

Both passed with Python3.12.14; the earlier arithmetic-only version was also
checked with Python3.13. The script checks the exact supplier-source hashes
and uses explicit exception checks, so optimized execution keeps all checks.
It imports only the attributed arithmetic engine, not a full caller replay.
An independent rational Taylor-series guard proves coarse logarithm brackets
without importing that engine's log or power functions. Combined with
\(1-x\leq e^{-x}\leq1-x+x^2/2\), it separately certifies the per-LOCAL H1 loss,
the once-global H2 loss against all eighteen combined allowances, and the fact
that a once-global H1 is not excluded by the ideal aggregate allowance.
Four semantic mutations run through the parameter or signed-invoice pipeline:
substituting selected κ for tested saving, charging the once-global H1 per LOCAL,
mistaking a paid split for a target split, and crediting an owned successor as
productive. Every mutation is rejected. The exact runtime inputs are the
funding record, attributed rational arithmetic module, canonical source word,
and selected backward provider word; their hashes are recorded in `the checker’s `--json` output`.

Open next questions:

1. Can one changed native word place a conduit and its carried port at a genuine
   intermediate frame using the existing paid climbs? The first useful artifact
   is exact endpoint rows and a full signed invoice, not a larger census.
2. If one transport is claimed to be shared across LOCALs, what actual common
   boundary implements it, and how many independent input fields does it carry?
   Pricing one shared field does not authorize packing eighteen unrelated ones
   into the same rank charge.
3. If productive credit pays for transport, does the source expose an independent
   arbitrary input and the required Gaussian output for every credited target?
   A rank-zero scratch register or a coupled relation is not that certificate.
