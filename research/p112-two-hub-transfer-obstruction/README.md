# The exceptional two-port transfer fails with two hubs

For marks E,F and ports E+G,F+G, the six-edge K3,2 attachment admits
no marked support transfer to any characteristic-two field, even when all
nine query images may depend on the graph and have arbitrary dimension.
The old field may have any characteristic different from two. This is an
author-written scoped obstruction, with exact finite premise checks.

PR3573 proved that coherent maps fail at this placement while its single
star has a hub-dependent GF4 map. The new result closes its two-hub
arbitrary-map handoff. Both hubs join all three terminals; no edge between
hubs is needed. [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives the all-field contradiction.

The concrete 42-port rational witness also has no characteristic-two exact
completion whose scalar query and reader supports are contained term by
term in its supports. Its query/reader ranks are (5,6), and its nine-term
complete-plane bill is 54 against original 42. It loses by 12. No minimum,
cheaper decoder, universal pair floor, supplier, new kappa or selected
frontier update is claimed.
