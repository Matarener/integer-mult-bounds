# C02 exact-source and production-control review

This is a bounded source/control review of the two-stage P112 paired-endpoint
package. The source and acceptance design are suitable for the stated finite
scope. Final executed-control evidence and exact reviewed bytes are recorded
in `CONTROLS.json`, `CONTROL-VALIDATION.json`, `SOURCE-CLOSURE.json` and
`CORE-SNAPSHOT.json`. Substantial OpenAI Codex assistance; no independent human
or formal-kernel certification is claimed.

## Findings

The endpoint stage checks all 112 ordered P/Q/R contracts, 456 distinct
phase-bearing canonical maps, 224 rank-seven residual children, all 64 entries
of the signed B4 Laurent matrix, and the two stated finite native convolution
models. Its actual global phases, inverse signs and paid translations are
part of acceptance. The canonical residual bridge applies M_E, T_R and
M_U^-1 in that chronological order; it does not count distinct recipes as
execution occurrences.

The geometry stage reconstructs the same full ordered contracts and the
5,824 fixed edges. Every isolated contact has independent arbitrary source,
target and helper inputs. The actual inverse helper transition restores its
physical entrance state. Rank-two and rank-three contacts charge that return
as well as all four scalar ADDs; the rank-one contact uses no helper.
The phase arithmetic covers all 512 characters and both signs at every edge.
Twelve actual compiler lowerings are samples of the separate all-edge
phase/rank argument, not an exhaustive list of every lowered adapter.

For the retained representative rank-two and rank-three contacts, omitting
physical helper return produces the respective differences `-1-i` and `-1+i`
on helper input field 2 at Fourier character 1. These are explicit arbitrary
dirty-input witnesses; deleting only the helper-output comparison admits
the mutated words.

The fixed scheduling recurrence is exact for its stipulated triangular-zero
source-batched model. An admissible sequence is a clique in the compatibility
graph, so the deterministic independent-color-class partition gives a valid
pruning bound. This conclusion is confined to the fixed O and that model.

The alternative-terminal screen has a different contract: one separate helper
per nondirect edge, changed terminal Q_s-perpendicular, and the old
65-dimensional normalization. Its rank-mass lower screen is not added to the
restored-contact invoice, and it is not a shared-helper lower bound.

## Source binding and controls

The package executes the same fresh checker bytes that it hashes. Both
checker-owned compiler loaders use captured authenticated bytes with
`dont_inherit=True, optimize=0`; outer optimization cannot erase assertions.
No historical hard-coded main or importlib loader is executed. The exact
Git/history provenance closure was read directly; gzip adaptations bind both
their container bytes and original decoded bytes.

Both complete scientific records are checked using canonical, type-preserving
JSON. Missing evidence is rejected, not regenerated. The stage coverage gate
requires exactly one declared expected record per stage. A separate production
gate compares all 112 ordered P/Q/R records across stages and recomputes their
canonical digest, before and after the selected stage.

The scientific endpoint receipt contains 125 intended semantic rejections and
one executed phase-check deletion. The geometry receipt contains fourteen
intended rejections and fourteen executions with the corresponding production
guard deleted. Their catches require the intended error, and unexpected
acceptance occurs outside the rejection catch.

The compact review independently exercises actual selected endpoint and
geometry predicates after authenticating their sources. It includes inverse
and bridge Laurent defects, an omitted native-source translation, a global-i
defect, dirty-helper leakage, omitted physical return, wrong delivery signs,
and a positive cancelling ADD pair that preserves the operator but increases
the invoice. It also forces a phase-index collision between distinct exact
512-byte phase arrays. These controls do not rely on generic hash failure.

Complete source, typed-record, nested-omission, stage-coverage, compression,
output-path and paired-contract controls are included separately. The complete
compact receipt is identical between normal and optimized execution apart
from the explicitly reported inner optimization mode.

The final compact runs used bundled CPython 3.12.14 and passed 64 intended
rejections and 14 guard deletions in each inner mode. The normal and optimized
outer drivers produced byte-identical complete receipts. All 23 frozen
entries matched their declared exact Git, historical or current-run source
bytes. Both full scientific records were compared, and the common ordered
contract digest is
`2765fa3074ba08133d8dbf1810c76e6d744127b4024d7933e1fe73d84e86389f`.

## Boundary of this review

I inspected the mathematical representations, acceptance predicates, complete
fixed receipts, source closure and proof arguments. I executed compact actual
predicate probes, not the complete 5,824-edge or native B4 programs. The owning
specialists' full normal/optimized replay records remain separately attributed.
The parent performs repository registration, committed and Git-free replay,
publication and hosted-CI verification.

The result does not supply a compatible simultaneous many-edge caller, paid
canonical/native occurrence schedule, helper ownership, rounding, recursive
stock, physical access, or improved multiplication bound. Detailed reasoning
and source roles are in `SOURCE-AUDIT.md`.
