# Two left cosets cannot create an additional centre-kernel direction

This contribution was produced by Nick Petalas. Apache-2.0.

Claim type: scoped algebraic proof, with a source-bound finite premise and
small exact corroboration. Independent human and formal proof-kernel review
are unclaimed. The theorem is about the historical scalar observation
contract of PR3071/3291/3656/3797/3828, not the current P104 native word.

## 1. Contract and inherited premises

Let G act regularly on its owner labels, let H be a finite subgroup, and let
rho_p be involutions indexed by ports p. Work over Q or C. R_p is a column
of a fixed reader R. For inputs supported on B, retain every later output,
including outputs outside B, and put

```text
Sx(g)  = sum_p R_p x(g,p),
4Tx(z) = sum_p R_p x(z rho_p,p).
```

For D={a,b,c}, suppose the following conditions hold.

1. The right-coset partition of the ports by H rho_p consists of D and
   singleton classes. H is generated by rho_a rho_b and rho_a rho_c.
2. R_D has rank two with kernel generated by u=(1,-1,-1).
3. Every pair of distinct reader columns is linearly independent.
4. For W=span(R_a,R_b), every R_p with p outside D is outside W.

The single-block coset classification, subgroup order 4080, reader rank
eight, and one constant triple direction per left translate are **cited
premises**, from Clear1nsight's PR3828 and Ross Richardson's PR3797.
Sagar Thakrar's PR3291, through PR3071's source reader, supplies the
nonparallel-column premise. These predecessor results are not audited here.
Matarener's PR3656 supplies the original subgroup and constant witness.

Condition 4 is the additional source check. On the authenticated reader,
exact elimination gives rank(R_0,R_6,R_p)=3 for each of the 109 ports outside
{0,6,85}, and rank two for those three ports. The program extracts R from
the archived canonical prefix using the retained query convention. It does
not execute a predecessor verifier or repeat the group/coset census.

## 2. Separation lemma

Let B_1=sH and B_2=tH be distinct left cosets. They are disjoint. At any
fixed later output z, the ports arriving from B_i are either absent, one
singleton port, or exactly D. This follows from the right-coset partition
after left translation by s or t.

There are two additional exclusions.

* If both blocks contribute singleton ports p and q, then p differs from q.
  Indeed, B_1 rho_p and B_2 rho_p are disjoint, since right multiplication
  by rho_p is a bijection.
* Both blocks cannot contribute D at the same z. Their triple output sets
  are B_1 rho_a and B_2 rho_a, which are disjoint by the same bijection.

Now impose Tx(z)=0. If only a singleton arrives, its coefficient is zero.
For two singletons, pairwise independence of R_p,R_q makes both
coefficients zero. For one singleton and one triple, the triple's response
lies in W and the singleton's column lies outside W, so its coefficient
is zero. These exhaust every output equation that contains a singleton.
Every source coordinate on a singleton port appears at its own output
z=g rho_p. Consequently all source coordinates outside D vanish.

This argument allows arbitrary partial intersections of the translated
outgoing sets. It assumes neither normality of H nor that those sets form
one common quotient partition after translation.

## 3. Exact kernel on two blocks and their subblocks

Once all singleton coordinates vanish, the source equation gives

```text
x(g,p) = alpha(g) u_p, p in D.
```

There is no cross-block triple output. Therefore the later equations
reduce separately on B_1 and B_2 to

```text
alpha(g) = alpha(g rho_a rho_b) = alpha(g rho_a rho_c).
```

Both products generate H. The right Cayley graph on each left coset is
connected, so alpha is constant separately on each block. Conversely each
constant triple vector has zero S and T, as in the cited one-block witness.
Their source supports are disjoint, so the vectors are independent. Thus

```text
ker(S on B_1 union B_2) intersect ker(T on B_1 union B_2)
  = span{1_(B_1) tensor u, 1_(B_2) tensor u},
dimension = 2.
```

This holds for **every** pair of distinct left cosets. It is not a finite
enumeration of relative translations.

For any A contained in B_1 union B_2, extend its inputs by zero. The theorem
then says that its kernel has dimension j, where j is the number of these
two cosets contained in A in full. Omitting any owner of one block forces
that block's constant amplitude to vanish. This is an immediate support
corollary, without an additional search or a hypothesis about missing outputs.

For the cited 112-port, rank-eight reader, dim ker(S_A)=104|A|. Hence

```text
rank(T_A restricted to ker(S_A)) = 104|A| - j.
```

This is the exact minimum number of additional **scalar linear**
observations that, together with S, determine the whole T field. A lower
count leaves an ambiguity in ker S outside ker T, even if postprocessing
is nonlinear. A linear quotient by the common kernel attains the count
abstractly. There is no assertion of efficient physical access or decoding.

For two full source cosets, |A|=8160 and j=2, so the minimum is
848638. These are derived counts from the theorem and cited source order,
not a claim to have eliminated the full 913920-variable matrix.
The invisible fraction remains exactly 1/424320 of ker S. Pairing two
blocks cannot improve the single-block information-compression density.

## 4. Executable corroboration and limits

The source check establishes only condition 4 and the distinguished relation.
The other actual-source premises are inherited as stated in section 1.

The new implication is corroborated over Q on G=S3 times C2, with
H=A3 times {0}. The three D routes are the three transpositions with zero
C2 bit. Two singleton routes are (identity,1) and (first transposition,1).
Their columns are e3 and e4, while the D columns are e1+e2,e1,e2.

Exact elimination checks every pair of the four left cosets and every
subblock of one pair. Literal inherited vectors span the computed kernels.
The fixtures contain both possible overlapping class types, singleton with
singleton and singleton with triple. Controls separately place a singleton
inside W, make it parallel to a triple column, duplicate two singleton
columns, and discard later outputs outside the source. Each alteration
admits more than two directions on at least one enumerated two-coset block.
The receipt records the measured dimensions rather than assuming them.

Three or more source cosets can contribute several singleton columns at
one output, permitting dependencies not excluded by this proof. This paper
does not decide that case, other triples' subgroups, or arbitrary owner
sets outside two left cosets. The rank-one-per-observation tariff, physical
acquisition, native phases, dirty restoration, storage, precision, compiler
and outer obligations remain separate. No new kappa or complete
multiplication theorem is claimed.
