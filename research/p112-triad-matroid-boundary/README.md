# Five-term triple replacements cannot lower the rank57 triad bill

Every rank 57 rational fit on the fixed PR2757 whole-triad mask is conjugate
to its canonical fit by invertible changes within physical groups. This
preserves every rank-one term's complete source and reader tariff, including
arbitrary exterior factors and cancellations.

Two consequences follow. A decoder whose augmented query/target or
reader/target matroid has a characteristic-two representation costs at least
334. Separately, replacing any three gauge-adapted canonical terms by at most
five arbitrary rational terms cannot reduce their bill. Disjoint replacements
of this kind retain the global floor 334. No decoder costing296 or less, new
supplier, or improved kappa is supplied.

The new evidence combines an explicit gauge, the I334 transfer, and a complete
eight-element matroid classification for the five-term problem. The catalogue
has 325 rank-three and 940 rank-four types, including loops and parallels.
Exact field, merge and support-transfer certificates leave 135 support
patterns. Their full physical pricing covers all 29,260 target triples and
every target ordering: 6,075 conservative envelope tests and 27,304 actual
class/table pricing jobs establish the floor. Separate implementations agree.

The four-term foundation includes new triad censuses of both non-Fano
exceptions: VVV has 31 equality triples, MMM has 18, and neither saves. The
seven-point classification is inherited from Nick Petalas' PR3513 and rerun
in full. His four-term reduction is retained from PR3538 at exact head
`e63a7635da461ae14e562ed4bb26be26a0fdb5f4`. Its plane census is not reused as
evidence for this different triad geometry.

## Scope and limits

The targets in an arbitrary rank 57 fit are **gauge-adapted** columns, not the
original literal indicator columns. A changed target basis can change an
augmented matroid. Fixed exterior-space restrictions must also be transformed.
The proof identifies the two residual-axis conventions explicitly.

Six-term triples, larger target blocks, arbitrary higher-rank fits, changed
covers and other physical contracts remain open. Native acquisition,
coefficient execution, frames, dirty restoration, hosting, storage, precision,
all-size compilation, both suppliers and outer transfer are not established.
The selected result is unchanged. These exact computations and separate
AI-agent checks are not independent human or formal proof-kernel review.

## Open next questions

Can six or more terms share a triple, or can a larger joint block save?
Begin with an exact candidate and charge every actual reader. Six-term triples
lead to nine augmented elements, outside this complete eight-element census.
One unit saved on each of 19 disjoint triples would only lower 334 to 315;
reaching 296 requires 38 units of compatible savings.

Can a higher-rank fitting matrix meet the 296 gate? Begin with its nonzero
Schur complement and verify the strict endpoint before pricing it. The gauge
theorem requires rank 57. [TASK.json](TASK.json) records the blockers and
first tests. This package does not classify nine-element configurations.
