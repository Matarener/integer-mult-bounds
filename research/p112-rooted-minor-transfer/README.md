# The exceptional support-transfer obstruction survives rooted minors

Marked support transfers descend through deletion and contraction whenever
the two old marks remain independent in the quotient. Consequently every
terminal-rooted K3,2 minor at the exceptional E,F / E+G,F+G placement has
no characteristic-two transfer, even with graph-dependent query images in
arbitrary dimension. This includes every finite subdivision of the six
edges, connected branch replacements and graphs with additional edges.

This closes the minors/subdivisions question explicitly left open by
PR3583. [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) gives the new contraction lemma, the exact
rooted-minor hypothesis and its all-field tree-elimination argument.
The inherited arbitrary-map K3,2 obstruction is retained verbatim in
[predecessor/PROOF.md](predecessor/PROOF.md). ORIGINS.json binds that
actual mathematical dependency and its finite premise certificates to
commit f9db162a408ba978c10b00be265f5ffca321957c, original paths, Git blobs,
SHA256 hashes and predecessor credit. Replay needs no foreign Git objects
or network. Frozen inputs and predecessor notices are unchanged.

The result is a sufficient obstruction to one support-transfer proof
method. It gives no graph classification, edge-minimality, cheaper decoder,
literal tariff minimum, universal pair floor, physical supplier, new kappa
or selected-frontier change. In particular it does not lift the earlier
42-port literal factorization to all graphs. Independent human and formal
proof-kernel review are unclaimed.
