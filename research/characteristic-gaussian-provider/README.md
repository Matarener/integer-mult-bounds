**Main result:** the actual whole-K/E coded negative terminal can be closed to its full independent Gaussian rank with one rank-2 query, replacing the previous two rank-1 repair queries. The construction preserves absolute phase, works at every positive lifted width, gives explicit ordinary XOR-coordinate normalizers, and retains independent inputs, owned-storage cleanup and reciprocal orientations.

**κ status:** no higher complete multiplication κ is proved. The new full-rank repair strictly reduces its own concave tariff, but the supplied complete whole-K/E invoice still does not yield a profitable supplier. The older rank-dropping repair also remains better under the isolated concave-moment comparison. The notes state these distinctions explicitly; none of the mathematical profiles below is advertised as a new selected record.

## Assembly: what can already be used

The full-rank decoder applies immediately to either the original ambient-25 whole-K/E code, retaining its own rank-73 targets, or the newly reconstructed ambient-18 code, whose three fresh stages have rank-52 targets. It replaces only the terminal repair. Replacing the old local bill by the smaller one while retaining rank-73 targets is invalid.

For the compressed case, with v = 112³ and h = 20480, the complete closed provider is

    independent targets: 2v H₅₂,
    Gaussian requests:   3v(2 H₈ + 2 H₉) + 3h H₁₈ + v H₂.

Compared with the previous full-rank repair, its target-minus-paid moment improves by v(2 − 2^t) for every 0 < t < 1. This exact improvement is insufficient to change κ, as D7 proves from the complete invoice. Ordinary code costs, effective coefficient descriptions and field-volume precision guards remain charged. No complexity theorem is inferred from a query profile alone.

The current eight-mode provider has a different interface. Its complete autonomous promotion returns productive profile {4:28, 7:7, 8:47, 9:550} at paid profile {1:2425, 2:412, 3:510, 4:127, 5:40}. This is a proved all-input completion, not a profitable supplier asserted from nominal helper credit. Its selected-mode offers preserve the remaining coupled outputs instead of silently discarding them.

The sector-multiplexing theorem is a complete query-sharing algorithm for its stated operators. It can be used whenever ordinary involution-sector coefficient blocks actually occur. Scalar-only boundaries with identical sector coefficients do not automatically gain from it. No such saving is subtracted from the current provider's bill in this release.
