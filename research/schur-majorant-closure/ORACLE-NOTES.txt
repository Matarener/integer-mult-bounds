New candidate statement corroborated here: for A=I+H, a nonnegative entry
majorant P with every row and column sum <=rho<1 retains that SAME bound
after arbitrary principal restriction and principal Schur elimination.
An entry majorant may dominate either complex modulus or
mu(z)=abs(Re z)+abs(Im z), with the corresponding output interpretation.
This sharpens the predecessor's loose rho+rho^2/(1-rho) bound, which is 1
at rho=1/2. It does not change a precision allocation automatically.

Written argument, separate from the finite computation: put B=retained,
E=eliminated after first dropping other indices. The entry majorant is
Q=P_BB+P_BE(I-P_EE)^(-1)P_EB. The inverse is nonnegative by the convergent
Neumann series. Since P_EB*1 <= rho*1-P_EE*1,
(I-P_EE)^(-1)P_EB*1 <= 1-(1-rho)(I-P_EE)^(-1)*1 <= rho*1.
Consequently Q*1 <= P_BB*1+rho*P_BE*1 <= rho*1. Transpose the argument for
columns. Each side separately needs its corresponding original budget;
neither side alone implies the other. Entrywise majorization of the true
Schur correction follows from submultiplicativity and the inverse's
Neumann series. Gaussian component norm mu and complex modulus both have
the needed triangle/product inequalities. Principal restrictions reduce
nonnegative row/column budgets. Iterated restrictions/eliminations retain
the original eliminated set in the principal-Schur description.

Independent method: each complex Schur entry is the ratio of its bordered
minor determinant to the eliminated principal determinant. There is no
Gaussian-elimination routine or primary-source import. Fractions and pairs
of Fractions are exact; no floating comparisons are used. Majorant Q is
computed independently as I minus the corresponding bordered-minor Schur
complement of I-P. All 2401 two-by-two matrices over seven small Gaussian
rationals are checked, plus 36 bounded dense matrices of dimension3/4 and
four lower triangular Toeplitz dimension4 matrices. Every retained,
eliminated and dropped partition is included. Selected dense cases explore
all one-index elimination/drop histories until one index remains.

Controls: a row-only rho=3/4 three-by-three input gives a Schur correction
with maximum column sum9/8; transpose gives the opposite one-sided failure.
rho=1 admits a singular eliminated block. A nonprincipal submatrix of I2
can be [0], violating near-identity conclusions. A wrong Schur correction
sign fails the exact block-of-inverse identity although both signs satisfy
the norm bound; the finite checker does not pretend that norm testing
alone detects this fault. Diagonal examples with nonempty elimination
attain rho exactly, proving no smaller uniform scalar bound is possible.

Finite evidence is not a formal-kernel proof of the arbitrary-size lemma.
The four Toeplitz examples are generic exact matrices, not a fresh replay
of the predecessor's triangle_plan, phase compiler or rounded precision
argument. Full bounded-generator/caller composition, tape delivery, setup,
online/offline coefficient widths and multiplication/outer theorems remain
outside this oracle. No new kappa or physical speedup is claimed.
