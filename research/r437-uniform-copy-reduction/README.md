## Original caller: uniform semantics and the relative bill

[ORIGINAL-CALLER.md](ORIGINAL-CALLER.md) specializes the existing good/fallback
construction to the actual nine R437 COPY frames and arbitrary complete dirty
functions, with unit ancestor weights and all high lifts. It proves a
rank-specific bad-low-class bound by Cauchy–Binet/Jacobi:

    delta_7 <= min(1,448*(q-1)/(q*(q-2))) <896/q,
    delta_7 <449/q for q>=450.

Every high lift shares the low classification; weighted diagonal conjugation
preserves all leading principal minors. The fraction counts a deterministic
complete cover, not algorithmic failure or a union over labels/prefixes.
At admitted q>2^80 it fits the inherited 10^-16 allowance.

There is one good child of width 7f and at most 49911 fallback children of
width <=f per bad class, including all main and half-reset calls. The complete
relative mean cost over the cover is

    T(7f,w)+delta_7*49911*max_(1<=j<=f)T(j,w)
      + local scans/leaf toll + explicit encoded preparation.

The familiar `T(7f,w)+10^-16*(32*64^2)*max T(j,w)+O_q(n+w^p)` follows
**only under the retained completed-callee, fixed-tape, numerical and canonical
root/history hypotheses**. This new argument does not prove those premises.
There is no factor multiplying the coarse child count by physical prefixes;
all equal or correlated prefix values retain their separate ordinals.
