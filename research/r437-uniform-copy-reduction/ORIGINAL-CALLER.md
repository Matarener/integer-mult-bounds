source preservation and exact private output, conditional on a completed
whole-packet residual with the stated source, spectator and cleanup contracts.

In the actual pinned compiler (`macro_code.py`, COPY branch), the first copy

## 2. All high lifts have the same good/fallback classification

Fix q, r=7 and the rank-seven split basis B_P over F_q. Right multiplication
g0 -> G=g0 B_P is a bijection on GL_m(F_q). Its projector is

    Q=G E_r G^-1,  E_r=diag(I_r,0), m=64.

The source's `compile_specification` tests the successive leading pivots of Q.
Equivalently it tests the leading principal minors Delta_k, k=1,...,r. In
`good_form`, columns of Q are reversed before northeast elimination; its
northeast pivot products are the same leading principal minors of Q, up to
the fixed reversal convention. Every nonzero low pivot lifts to a unit in
Z/q^w. Thus a good g0 stays good for **every** high lift at every precision.

Incoming weights form a diagonal unit matrix H. Each leading principal block
of H Q H^-1 is conjugate by its restricted diagonal H_I, so its determinant
equals Delta_k. Therefore the classification is independent of the actual
ancestor units and of the body/dirty payload. No new bad event is introduced
per column or per high prefix. There is no probabilistic failure of the
algorithm: the construction traverses a fixed complete cover and uses a
completed fallback on every bad class.

## 3. A rank-specific complete low-class count

For I={0,...,k-1}, Cauchy–Binet and Jacobi's complementary-minor identity give

    det(G) Delta_k =
      sum_(J subset {0,...,r-1}, |J|=k)
       (-1)^(sum I+sum J) det G[I,J] det G[I^c,J^c] = F_k(G).

Every term of F_k has total degree k+(m-k)=m. At G=I, F_k=1. Hence F_k is
a nonzero polynomial over **every** residue field, including q dividing r.
The product F=product_(k=1)^r F_k is nonzero and has degree at most rm=448.
This proof uses complementary minors of one invertible matrix, rather than
assigning the full degree of each entry of G^-1 independently.

A nonzero polynomial of total degree d in independent uniform field entries
vanishes on at most a d/q fraction. One proof inducts on the number of
variables: separate the highest power of the final variable, charge zeros of
its nonzero leading coefficient by induction, then use the univariate root
bound on the remaining fibres. The resulting degree contributions add to d.
This bound is harmlessly trivial if d/q>=1.

For the uniform full matrix distribution,

    Pr(GL_m) = product_(i=1)^m (1-q^-i)
             >= 1-sum_(i=1)^m q^-i > 1-1/(q-1).

Conditioning explicitly on invertibility gives

    delta_7 < min(1,448*(q-1)/(q*(q-2)))
            < 896/q                    (q>=3),
    delta_7 < 449/q                     (q>=450).           (A)

The first expression can be used as a non-strict upper bound at the capped
value one; strictness of the uncapped fraction is immaterial. For the admitted
q>2^80, delta_7 <449/2^80<10^-16. The denominator 2^80 here is a monotone
lower bound on q, **not** a claimed prime. This is a deterministic fraction
of low classes; every low class has exactly the same H high-lift multiplicity.
It is not a union over COPY labels, all paid requests, columns, or prefixes.

The exact finite check independently evaluates Jacobi numerators with an
adjugate over complete small GL domains, including 78,336 matrices in GL2(F17)
and 11,232 in GL3(F3). A missing Jacobi sign is detected. These audits support
the displayed polynomial proof; they do not prove its quantified statement.

## 4. Good and fallback children, with every physical call paid

Retain the source's completed normal-form identities and primitive contracts.
A good residual has forward ordered adapters, one weighted reversed child
of width 7f, then backward adapters. All current high fields remain frozen
prefixes and all unused rows/spectators stay complete. The child is outside
the prefix loop. Its [B',G',S'] and compatible fresh-row divisor are the ones
in PROOF.md section 4. Width 7f<n gives the proper-width induction step;
below the cutoff the source executes the completed eight-primitive leaf.

The original good plan emits 420 head, 420 tail and 4096 cross ADD commands:
the first two counts are sum_(k=0)^6(63-k). All 4936 commands have source
physically before target. Its two sweeps therefore pay **9872*f** complete
ordered ADD passes, even if some prepared coefficients are zero. They do not
need the ordinary reverse adapters of the alternate construction.

For a bad class the invertible 128-dimensional residual has a unit-pivot
Gaussian fallback over the same local ring. The retained finite control has
at most 127 row exchanges, 254 initial pair/unit child commands, and 16256
child commands introduced to reverse above-diagonal shears. Each complete
pair command has one main child and at most two same-bank half resets.
The source omits zero-width halves and preserves the middle atom at odd f.
Therefore a safe full bound is

    J_bad = 3*(127+254+16256) = 49911
          < 32*64^2 = 131072                              (B)

coarse child calls per bad low class. Every one has width at most f; the
half resets have width floor(f/2). Preparations, ordered scans, signs,
canonical commits and restoration are additional local work. The terminal
sign is not a recursive child and is included in that work. No child or
private buffer disappears merely because it carries a low/rank tag.

The fallback also has at most 16256 ordered ADD commands and one sign
command per column, hence at most **16257*f** scalar passes, before its child
and preparation costs. `source_audit.py` authenticates and executes the
unmodified original pure plan definitions on actual ambient-64 good and bad
R437 frame-515 conjugates. It checks these command/order bounds and expanded
main/half counts at f=1,2,3,10,64. That is an explicitly **reused-code**
algebra/control probe, separate from the independent engine and from physical
payload execution or generated callback/continuation validation.

## 5. The full relative bound that the original implementation would need

Let T(j,w) be a **uniform per-payload-bit upper bound** over the admitted
descendant programs, complete spectators, compatible stock and generated
unit-weight contexts. Do not replace it by a measurement at one fixture.
Write M_f=max_(1<=j<=f) T(j,w); no unproved runtime monotonicity is assumed.
One family/low packet has v=V_parent/(W N), with N=|GL64(F_q)|.

Let gamma_orig be the total encoded preparation, directory/Count setup,
source-copy, transfer, and successful cleanup work not already covered by
the completed primitive contracts. Before absorption, summing all N low
classes and dividing by their complete total volume N v gives

    C_copy_cover/(N v)
      <= T(7f,w) + delta_7*49911*M_f
           + A_q*(f+leaf_toll(w)+1) + gamma_orig/(N v).     (C)

The dropped (1-delta_7) factor in front of T is an upper bound, not a claim
that bad classes execute a second good child. It is legitimate because T
is nonnegative. Every bad main and half child remains in the second term.
The clone and final guarded disposal, intermediate releases, parent checkpoint,
returned output and source/directory head restoration are in the local bill.
The outer family's gather/route/scatter costs remain additional as appropriate
to the containing node. Summing equal complete volumes is essential: using
the smaller visible seven-coordinate chunk would underprice the spectators.

Under the predecessor's **canonical root-coupling**, encoded preparation,
complete fixed-tape primitive, and numerical/storage arguments, the last
terms have the stated source-specific bound A'_q*(n+w^p). C0 has p=1;
C1 uses p=1-a1 and depends on both reached B1 primitive replacements.
Then (A),(B) imply the inherited safe envelope

    C_copy_cover/(N v)
      <= T(7f,w) + 10^-16*(32*64^2)*M_f
                     + A'_q*(n+w^p).                     (D)

This is the complete rank-seven specialization **relative to those named
contracts**. It covers arbitrary dirty functions and every high lift;
it does not create a new low-class error or an extra per-prefix child factor.
It does not independently prove the required T, gamma absorption or physical
normal-form/refinement premises. Outside the admitted root/history domain,
retain (C) with the actual gamma and encoded widths. Reusing the scalar
endpoint alone would not justify either bound.
