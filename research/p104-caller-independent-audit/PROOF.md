claims become finite validation evidence only if the paired execution passes.

## Exact spectral convention

For binary vector a, the positive native Gaussian G_a has Fourier eigenvalue
i^(a·z mod2). A list of signed axes is represented by a Z4 phase polynomial.
The parity expansion modulo4 has only linear and quadratic terms; a coefficient
2 contributes a translation and no quadratic term. The checker retains the
constant phase separately. All Gaussians, binary address permutations and
translations in this spectral calculation have eigenphase1 at zero frequency.
An exterior scalar coefficient remains outside every digit-column tensor power.

On each actual port q, the nine vectors q,b1,...,b8 from the pinned source form
an orthonormal basis. The stage route swaps b_i with the corresponding fresh
axis e_i and fixes q. It is the product of commuting binary transvections
v_i=b_i+e_i, each of even norm. The emitted recipe specifies these v_i. The
checker reconstructs every column of its 265-dimensional matrix, checks the
inverse on every basis vector, and checks orthogonality on its entire changed
support against all265 columns. The unchanged complement is the identity.

For each contact J=X_t P_R, the complete forward phase identity is checked,
as is the inverse J^-1=P_R X_t=X_(Rt) P_R. Four contacts are emitted per full
site/port. More than checking charts, the physical composite is checked:

    J_exit F_local J_entry.

For a diagonal coefficient this has phase R(D0)+2(t_exit+R(t_entry)); for the
lower cross coefficient replace D0 by D0 Q^-1. Each equals the intended Ds or
Ds Q^-1. This binds the actual entry/exit implementations to the free-Laurent
caller. It retains all-input local independence from productive helpers.

## Helpers, scratch and paid jobs

Each full site receives its own527 fresh arbitrary helper inputs. The local
public735-label injection is emitted for every site. Global helper IDs are

For every helper endpoint U=Phi_final Phi_initial^-1, the retained compiler
lowers U=M_left G_r M_right. The literal native gate circuit is checked using
signed Pauli images and an independently executed exact dense column at input0.
All compared circuits consist of verified Clifford gate types. Equality of
all signed Pauli conjugations implies that their quotient commutes with the
full matrix algebra, hence is a scalar. The nonzero exact column fixes that
scalar, including its absolute phase. The compiler's internal assertions are
not relied on for these external comparisons, which also run under Python -O.

The checker also tests M_left^-1 U M_right^-1=G_r. The right inverse acts on
that fresh helper before its local call, and the left inverse after the call.
This use of arbitrary helper inputs relies on the local all-input ABI: main
outputs have no residual helper contamination and helper jobs are autonomous.
Tensor spectators are identities, not additional productive jobs. Different
port boundary routes can therefore coexist with these autonomous helpers.
The208 intrinsic main adapters are checked by the same native method.

Each thin query emits owned initialization, copy, the q-basis inverse,
one positive rank-one Gaussian, the forward basis, X_q, add and owned clear.
