# Evidence and scope

The package contains one replacement of the PR3605 compensation boundary.
Its comparator, intermediate forest and final two-sided word all use the same
source matrix. The checked final words are in
`artifacts/TWO-SIDED-WORD-{base,extra}.json.gz`.

| Evidence | What it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| [PROOF.md](PROOF.md) | Arbitrary-dirty basis identity, exact signed-forest class minimum, disjoint-helper construction and paid local comparison |
| [PROOF-NUMERICAL.md](PROOF-NUMERICAL.md) | Full logical words, scalar-projection invoice, exact attained range and reachable-grid statements, with explicit physical exclusions |
| `construct.py`, `artifacts/DESIGN.json` | Complete deterministic edge and helper-choice census, frozen recipe, literal words and actual defect witnesses |
| `independent_mst.py`, `artifacts/INDEPENDENT-FOREST.json.gz` | Separate Fraction arithmetic, graph minimum, helper scores, restored basis and arbitrary-input operators |
| `check_word.py`, `artifacts/WHOLE-WORD.json.gz` | Separate integer-dyadic/free-word interpreter, complete source and candidate words, emitted scalar programs and prefix witnesses |
| `price_replay.py`, `artifacts/PRICES.json` | Literal Horner microcircuits and all four panel prices |
| `compare_prior.py`, `artifacts/PR1080-COMPARISON.json` | One frozen prior-source/recipe comparison; no relabeling search or broader obstruction |
| [REVIEW-DESIGN.md](REVIEW-DESIGN.md) | Separate review of the construction proof |
| [REVIEW-NUMERICAL.md](REVIEW-NUMERICAL.md) | Separate review of the logical and scalar numerical models |
| [REVIEW-VERIFICATION.md](REVIEW-VERIFICATION.md) | Source execution, acceptance controls, completeness and package boundary review |

The mathematical checkers do not import one another or the builder. They share
the authenticated source words and candidate bytes. The original transport and
emitter retain their own checks; they are producers and inherited evidence,
not another independent verifier. Their executed source bytes are captured,
authenticated and compiled with assertions enabled.

The 15 compared records comprise all 11 construction outputs, complete price
and prior-comparison records, the independent forest record and the complete
whole-word record. Comparison is typed and covers every JSON field. Gzip
payload equality permits equivalent compression containers for regenerated
outputs; frozen inputs retain exact byte pins.

`FROZEN-SHA256.json` and `ORIGINS.json` enumerate the immutable inputs and
their provenance. The package attributes preserve the two inherited producer
files' original CRLF bytes through Git; no source text is rewritten. Original research files are retained without edits outside
this package. The two proof copies have only the declared filename-reference
adaptations in `ORIGINS.json`. Historical C01 factory screens are provenance
only and have not been rerun.

The scientific source remains PR3605
`3efbb5de06a49ffeea6684482f2d3fe48cdca3fb`. That PR head is not an ancestor of
this publication's base. [SOURCE-HISTORY-ALIASES.json](SOURCE-HISTORY-ALIASES.json)
records byte-for-byte bindings to the retained main copies used by the
repository's source-history gate. Neither the experimental word nor its
scientific pin changes. The prior PR1080 word is authenticated as one exact
member of its pinned source archive.

Finite exact computation, written all-input arguments, AI review, package CI,
native/physical admission and a formal-kernel proof are separate kinds of
evidence. This package supplies the computations, arguments and AI reviews;
the PR records hosted CI separately. Native/physical admission and formal
kernel evidence are not supplied.
