## Suggested first wave

Begin with routes 7 and 12: they have explicit constructors and close contact with current retained copies and asynchronous hubs. In parallel, derive the exact source response matrix for routes 1–3, and actual snapshot relations for route 8. Routes 16–19 support those constructions with stricter complete guards and realistic finite costs. Route 20 is a separate model-aware line; it must not be advertised as a free conversion of the selected Turing-machine theorem.

For the source-independent algebra, the smallest useful witness may be tiny. For an actual κ claim, the decisive witness is the complete unchanged word, both supplier budgets, all three compiler guards, new stock/precision constants and the nine outer rows. Do not assign an unpriced decoder or an unnamed physical delivery gadget to a future theorem while claiming the final result now.

## Handoff contract

Every source instantiation should state its exact head and cut/window; actual original and dirty-owned inputs; literal program and query classes; full output and helper matchers; complete paid/productive changes; peak live and suspended reserves; all-column/phase conditions; and the tested source compiler inequalities. A source construction can be proposed before its expensive replay, but that status must remain visible. The selected frontier is unchanged by this atlas.
