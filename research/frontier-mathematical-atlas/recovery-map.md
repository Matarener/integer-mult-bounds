## Why this is a new handoff

The new atlas does not reproduce all old entries. It develops explicit noncommuting query channels, block causal hubs, source-version/intertwiner caches, distributed reachable-state stabilizers, moving central provider contracts and phase/class-sensitive compiler guards. The source-independent pieces have proofs here; their actual source witnesses remain concrete research questions.

Where another contributor has already implemented an earlier idea, its PR is credited and treated as infrastructure. Where a new theorem narrows the old route, that scope is carried forward. No old numerical bound, restricted ceiling, machine threshold or source census is silently transported into the current P104 construction.
