# Eight source bound conjectures for substantial progress

These eight statements are **unproved campaign conjectures**. They express possible source-bound constructions, not newly discovered open problems in the literature or facts inferred from a favorable rank. Their motivations are structural; this atlas has not searched for or validated their witnesses. The 42 questions inside the volumes give additional, more incremental targets.

For a current-source assertion, freeze the relevant public head from recent-pr-index.json. A positive witness must preserve arbitrary original inputs and complete outputs, charge every Gaussian and ordinary connector, and supply the actual native/spectator, ownership, precision and compiler interfaces. A local witness is not automatically κ ≥ 0.0035.

## C01 An actual two demand spectator bundle exists

**Conjecture.** The retained-copy/asynchronous P104 line through PR5774–PR5795 has an actual response window containing two conditional Gaussian updates that admit G10/R12's same-spectator one-query implementation, with no extra Gaussian connector and an ordinary/owned bill inside a freshly proved source allowance.

**Motivation.** Retained copies create correlated owned state, while conditional operators can leave spectator capacity unused. The concrete algebraic two-to-one construction is proved; its occurrence in this real word is unknown.

**First witness.** Give the two original operators, source versions, literal S/T words, every input/output normalizer and the complete replacement. A scalar role matrix tensor the spectator identity is outside the profitable shape.

## C02 A useful many channel asynchronous hub exists

**Conjecture.** A frozen successor of the current P104 source admits one S09 hub with r>=2 independent channels and causally available lower-triangular Gram correction, whose complete signed gain exceeds that of implementing those same channels as separate single-channel hubs after all access subdivisions, targets and storage are included.

**Motivation.** Existing asynchronous hubs show that collection and injection need not be simultaneous. Block collection may share real access work. Neither rational rank nor r channels at different incompatible pivots proves this claim.

**First witness.** Supply C,D, the unit pivot block, all temporal contact maps and the ordinary triangular correction. Check both original and dirty-owned support and retain the difference-buffer CLEARs.

## C03 A nonidentity cycle transporter pays across a whole window

**Conjecture.** On an actual current native incidence graph, G02's complete cycle/boundary equations admit an explicit ordinary-admissible invertible root transporter whose induced whole window has a strictly better complete signed moment at the same tested exponent than its fixed canonical-chart predecessor.

**Motivation.** The fixed-chart and identity-native theorems constrain particular alphabets. A coherent nonidentity transporter changes a shared representation, rather than another chart seed inside those alphabets.

**First witness.** List independent cycle products, the root matrix and all phase-correct transported maps; emit and price the resulting whole word. An invertible abstract GL solution without its ordinary word is insufficient.

## C04 Distributed reachable graph stabilizers remove paid work

**Conjecture.** There is a current retained-copy window with at least two proved owned graph relations h=Lx and a finite sequence of G15 stabilizers, each with a sparse low-rank parameter, for which the regenerated full window saves Gaussian moment strictly after every changed instruction and target is counted.

**Motivation.** The new instructions may differ off the exact reachable subspaces while agreeing on every actual arbitrary original input. This is genuine ownership information, not an assumption that an original helper is zero.

**First witness.** Supply the reachable relation at every cut, each actual stabilizer word, frozen external endpoints and the complete new instruction/phase bill. Keeping only one observed output is insufficient.

## C05 A source compatible guard portfolio crosses the target

**Conjecture.** At C_star=219/62500, at most three physically compatible Gaussian program variants generated from at least two of the atlas's construction families admit a rational direct-sum/chronological assembly with all three complete compiler guards strictly below one and a fresh finite implementation contract.

**Motivation.** Target growth, paid cost and completion stock need not move together. A07 proves sparse support once a feasible three-guard cone point exists. It does not assert such a source point exists or that mixing today's losing first moments can create one.

**First witness.** Give each complete variant and the actual assembly, exact multiplicities, all setup/connectors and strict rational guards. Whole multiplication additionally needs a compatible admitted BIT budget and the actual outer rows.

## C06 Moving central providers close without an extra repair

**Conjecture.** A changed pair of actual Gaussian providers can realize G25 with Q1D0=D0Q0, where the nonconstant Q0,Q1 have their required positive Gaussian grades and exact ordinary/native matchers, and the completed two-provider caller improves its full paid/productive moment after all new fees.

**Motivation.** The block cancellation proof does not require D0 to commute with Q0. A moving interface could admit producers excluded by a common central-axis demand.

**First witness.** Construct the actual providers and lower their central operators with absolute phase. Naming Q1=D0Q0D0^(-1) is not a provider or a Gaussian-grade proof.

## C07 Cache subspace compression makes a larger helper construction feasible

**Conjecture.** One of the proposed multi-channel P104 constructions has a phase boundary where S13's future-demand intersection compression strictly lowers its necessary retained cache bank without additional Gaussian acquisitions, and the resulting actual live and suspended reserve fits a compiler/precision budget that the uncompressed construction fails.

**Motivation.** Current pool and pebble tools allocate fixed values; S13 changes which linear combinations survive. It can improve stock even when minimum acquisition count is unchanged.

**First witness.** Give the past and future row spaces, a permitted-coefficient basis conversion, actual request order, live intervals and fresh reserve/precision bounds. Basis conversion may temporarily require more space and must be charged.

## C08 A compatible above target BIT admission can be completed

**Conjecture.** The exact G20 helper-split chain through PR5679/PR5708 can be given a compatible admitted BIT saving strictly above .0035 under the same five framework assumptions, with all-column native action, actual independent port targets, ordinary/precision/stock closure and the new Gaussian consumer bindings established.

**Motivation.** The ordinary component proposals already exceed the target, but selected admitted BIT remains .0034. A major complex improvement needs this compatibility step rather than silently taking the ordinary value as admission.

**First witness.** Resolve one named quantified interface in the current exact G20 source, then compose the actual native/compiler/consumer contract. PR5798's finite caller audit and PR5782's native-address work are relevant current predecessors; their stated scope is not automatically the whole conclusion.

## How to use these conjectures

Prefer C01, C02 and C04 for concrete new construction work, with C08 as a necessary parallel component-admission effort for a whole above-target result. C03 and C06 are more structural changed-interface routes. C05 is an assembly target after actual mechanisms exist; C07 can keep those mechanisms within resources. The present proved atlas remains useful if some conjectures fail, because it specifies exactly which model and sufficient construction were tested.
