# Raw finite-field pencil special-linear recognition

Ali Romman, with substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. Apache-2.0.

## Claim and source boundary

Fix a supplied finite field K=F_q with one common element encoding. Let
m,n>0. A tensor is any ordered pair X=(A,B) of m-by-n matrices. The action is

```text
(P,Q,S).X = (P(s11 A+s12 B)Q^T, P(s21 A+s22 B)Q^T).
```

This package gives a deterministic complete label for the orbits of
SL_m(K) x SL_n(K) x SL_2(K), a fixed representative for each label, and
literal determinant-one maps to that representative. It also gives a map
between two equivalent raw inputs. The decision has polynomial cost in the
factor dimensions for fixed q. Map construction has expected polynomial
cost, with exact checked residue norms. No dimension, index, primary degree,
length or multiplicity cap is imposed by the implementation.

The field is common to both inputs. Field automorphisms, changes between
unrelated field encodings and tensors with more than two slices are outside
the claim. Zero pencils, linearly dependent slices, rectangular shapes,
zero minimal indices and pencils with no invertible rational slice are in
scope. The complete kappa calculation does not change.

PR5692 supplies raw strict Kronecker decomposition and its actual maps.
PR5689 supplies the complete fixed-slice determinant subgroup, including
all off-diagonal mixing. Those are credited prerequisites, not new theorems
here. This package supplies their raw SL interface, basis/order determinant
handling, an executable unbounded-degree norm witness, and independent
finite SL-orbit checks. The bounded character code is only a comparison
oracle. It is neither patched nor used as an unbounded implementation.

## Strict canonical coordinates and every determinant

The retained extractor returns blocks, R and Q with

```text
A Q = R A0,       B Q = R B0.
```

Order its individual blocks by their exact type records. Apply the actual
row and column block permutations E and F. Then

```text
g=(E R^-1, F Q^T, I2)
```

maps the raw pencil to the sorted block model C. Thus det(g_row)=det(E)/det(R)
and det(g_col)=det(F)det(Q). The program keeps these matrices and checks both
coefficient equations. It does not discard signs or identify equal block
orders without their actual maps.

The raw extractor writes finite regular blocks as `(Comp(f^ell),I)` and
infinity as `(I,Comp(X^ell))`. The supplied-character source uses the opposite
slice convention. Exchanging both coefficient equations does not change
the fixed-slice stabilizer. The subgroup below therefore applies directly.
Every slice action in the new algorithm is applied to the actual matrices;
no implicit slice swap or uncharged determinant correction occurs.

## Complete fixed-slice character subgroup

For a sorted model C, let H(C) be the image in (K*)^2 of its row/column
stabilizer with the slices fixed. The source theorem gives the subgroup
generated by the following pairs, for every u in K*:

```text
right index e:       (u^e, u^(-e-1)),
left index e:        (u^(-e-1), u^e),
regular length ell:  (u^ell, u^-ell).
```

This includes index zero. The regular residue degree and repeated-copy
count do not replace ell. Repeated identical generators may be omitted.
The source proof uses the full Hom algebra, not a block-diagonal assumption.
Distinct singular types and singular/regular Hom maps are triangular in
an appropriate order. For a regular primary, the residue multiplicity map
of exact length ell appears on ell filtration layers. Its determinant is
Norm(det D)^ell. Off-diagonal/radical maps add no determinant factor.
This proves the upper bound. Varying one block or one multiplicity coordinate
attains each displayed generator, so the bound is exact.

The program closes these generators in the finite group (K*)^2 by breadth
first search. It stores a generator path for each element. There are at
most (q-1)^2 elements. This is an exact finite group calculation, not a scan
of the large matrix stabilizer.

## Complete raw SL label

Enumerate every S in SL_2(K). For each, apply S to X, compute its sorted
strict model C_S and a strict map g_S. The full map from X is
`(g_S_row,g_S_col,S)`. Let d_S be its pair of row and column determinants.
Let l_S be the least pair in the coset d_S H(C_S), using the fixed integer
encoding of K elements. The label is the least pair

```text
(sorted block records of C_S, l_S)
```

over all S, together with m,n. The associated representative is C_S with
its first row multiplied by l_S[0]^-1 and its first column multiplied by
l_S[1]^-1. Positive m,n make these diagonal maps well defined. This label
is relative to the declared common field encoding.

First, it is independent of the extractor's coordinate choices. Two strict
maps to the same model differ by a fixed-slice stabilizer. Their determinant
pairs differ by H(C_S), so their cosets and labels agree.

Next, suppose Y=(U,V,T).X with det U=det V=det T=1. For every slice action
S on Y, the corresponding action on X is ST. The row/column maps U,V have
determinant one. Hence the sets of strict types and determinant cosets are
the same as S ranges over SL_2. Their least labels are equal. This proves
invariance under the entire product group, including slice actions.

Conversely, choose the winning S and d=d_S. Since l is in dH, put
h=l/d componentwise. The stored path supplies a literal fixed-slice
stabilizer (H_row,H_col) with determinants h. Let D_row,D_col have first
diagonal entries l[0]^-1,l[1]^-1 and all other entries one. Then

```text
(D_row H_row g_row, D_col H_col g_col, S)
```

maps X to the stated representative. Its two determinant products are
l^-1 h d=1, and det S=1. Thus equal labels give SL maps to one identical
representative and imply equivalence. The label is complete.

If w_X and w_Y map X and Y to that representative, the transporter from
X to Y is `w_Y^-1 w_X`, component by component. The program checks all three
determinants and both literal coefficient matrices. Different labels return
no transporter. This is a decision for all raw pencils in the stated class,
not a promised-model recognition test.

## Exact norm witnesses without a degree cap

For a finite primary f of degree d, choose a uniform residue polynomial a
with d coefficients in K. For nonzero a, compute

```text
N(a)=a^((q^d-1)/(q-1)) mod f.
```

This is an element of K. Accept exactly when it is the requested nonzero u.
The norm map on the cyclic multiplicative group of F_(q^d) is onto K*.
Each nonzero target has (q^d-1)/(q-1) preimages among all q^d sampled residues.
The success probability is at least 1/q. Independent uniform trials therefore
terminate almost surely with expected trial count at most q. The result is
checked exactly; randomness affects runtime and the selected witness, not
correctness, the orbit label or the representative.

On a length-ell block, use P=a(Comp(f^ell)) and Q=P^-T. The residue unit
lifts to a unit, commutes with the companion matrix and has determinant
N(a)^ell=u^ell. Infinity uses a constant unit u. Right and left blocks use
the literal scalar maps from the displayed character formulas, with empty
local factors allowed at index zero. Insert each generator in the full
block coordinates, multiply along the stored path, and check the full
fixed-slice identity. There is no enumeration of q^d residues in this code.

The default uses SystemRandom. Reproducible verification uses specified
Random seeds. The expected-time theorem assumes independent uniform draws;
it is not a deterministic worst-case bound for a supplied random stream.

## Positive preparation and caller limits

There are q(q^2-1) special-linear slice actions. The code tests fewer than
q^4 candidate two-by-two matrices and invokes the retained polynomial raw
extractor on their transformed coefficients. It keeps a per-input cache of
identical transformed pencils only. Sorting, all row/column maps, determinant
calculations, character closure and all coefficient checks are paid work.
For fixed q their counts and workspace are polynomial in m+n. No discrete
logarithm or unpriced large stabilizer oracle is used.

A norm trial uses polynomial arithmetic modulo f and an exponent with
O(d log q) bits. Verification of irreducibility uses the retained exact
factorization routine. Lifting to length ell and multiplying the full maps
has polynomial matrix cost. Each character path has fewer than (q-1)^2
steps. The witness therefore has expected polynomial preparation cost for
fixed q. The implementation builds explicit finite-field operation tables;
no complexity polynomial in log q is claimed. The polynomial exponent and
large-field constants are not optimized.

This preparation is an algebraic tool for the tensor-atlas interface. A
multiplication mechanism still needs native realizations, a producer and
consumer, physical moves, dirty restoration, storage, precision, repeated
preparation and a complete paid recursive bill. These costs remain unknown,
not zero. No physical caller, supplier admission, kappa improvement or
practical multiplication speedup follows from this package.

## Evidence and attribution

The verifier constructs independent complete small SL-orbit partitions
using elementary transvections on all three tensor factors. It compares
labels on every raw input and checks every returned map by a separate
coefficient sum and small determinant formula. Larger fixtures include
extension fields, degree-four primaries, length four, index six, multiplicity
four, dimension seventeen, zero pencils and full projective-line pencils
with no invertible rational slice. Exact norm tests extend through degree
eight and length-four lifts. The frozen supplied-model program supplies a
separate comparison for its own bounded character domain.
