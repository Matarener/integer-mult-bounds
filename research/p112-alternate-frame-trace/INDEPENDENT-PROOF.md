# Independent completion of the alternate-chart obstruction

## Verdict and exact scope

The interrupted claim is valid. For the fixed asymmetric rank8 anchor on
groups [1,2,27,28], the selected annihilators generated by anchor axes
[6,1,11] admit no rational split satisfying all physical rectangle caps.
Three mandatory self caps, masks34,22,28 in group order [1,2,27,28,3,4],
already contradict one another. This covers every rank2 idempotent in the
four-dimensional remaining chart, including degenerate principal blocks.

Other annihilator choices and other anchors remain outside this theorem.
No decoder saving,334 global floor,296 construction, or selected-bound
improvement is established.

`independent.py` is standalone standard-library code. It
imports no producer algebra or old scratch helper. It reconstructs the
anchor factorization, flags, coordinate change and physical caps from three
hash-pinned inputs (one local fixture and two predecessor geometry files). Its sparse polynomial calculations are exact
identities in sixteen indeterminates, not coefficient samples. The old
two-cap positive fixture is retained as a control showing that cap28 is an
essential additional premise.

## Complete remaining chart

Write the anchor A=UV, with U twelve by8 and V eight by12. Append a zero ninth
coordinate. For the selected H0,K0, construct X with col(X)=ker(H0), and Y
with YX=I6 and ker(Y)=span(K0). The two individual new-group flags give
covectors a3,a4 and vectors b3,b4 with

    [a3;a4] [b3,b4] = diag(-2,1).

Choose D=[b4, basis(ker(a3) intersect ker(a4)), b3]. Every flag-compatible
rank3 split projector P has first row and column equal to e0, and last row
and column zero, in these coordinates. Consequently, necessarily and
sufficiently,

    D^-1 P D = diag(1,Q,0),  Q^2=Q, rank(Q)=2.

This uses only the nonzero flag pairings and idempotence. It does not require
any entry or principal minor of Q to be nonzero. Conversely every such Q
provides a biorthogonal split of the combined six-dimensional pair frame.
Let L=[U,0]XD and R=D^-1 Y[V;0] be the resulting literal anchor maps.

## Cap34 gives a rank-at-most-one residual

Put B=group2. Direct exact reconstruction gives

    L_B diag(1,Q,0) R_B = T = Q[0:3,0:3] diag(1/2,1,1),
    L_B R_B = diag(0,1,1).

Group4 uses the complementary projector. Eliminating its own I3 block from
the group2/group4 self rectangle leaves

    B_res = I3-L_B(I-P)R_B = E00+T.

The literal physical tariff is4. Hence its cap is exactly rank(B_res)<=1.
For brevity write B_res=B below. No exact-rank-one assumption is made.

## Cap22 forces tr(B)=1

On the group2/group27/new-group3 self rectangle, first eliminate the new
group's own I3. Two explicit invertible row/column subtractions leave two
zero directions and the four by4 matrix

    S4 = [[I3-T, T e0], [e0^t T, -T00]].

The physical tariff is6, so rank(S4)<=3 and det(S4)=0. Its determinant is
identically

    det(I3-T) - det((I3-T)[1:3,1:3]).

Since T=B-E00 and rank(B)<=1, the rank-one determinant formula gives

    det(S4)=1-tr(B).

The checker verifies this identity under B=x y^t with six independent
variables. This parameterization covers every rational rank-at-most-one B,
including zero, and introduces no division. Therefore tr(B)=1 follows even
on every boundary stratum; in particular B=0 cannot satisfy this cap.

## Idempotence forces two particular diagonal entries

Every rank2 idempotent on Q4 has trace2 and complement rank2. Thus the
upper3 principal minor of I4-Q vanishes. In terms of B,

    Q[0:3,0:3]=(B-E00)diag(2,1,1),
    det(I3-Q[0:3,0:3])=3-2B00-3(B11+B22)=0.

Combining this with B00+B11+B22=1 yields B00=0 and B11+B22=1. Therefore

    Q00=-2, Q11+Q22=1, Q33=3.

These steps require no inverse of Q, its principal block, or I3-T.

## Cap28 requires a contradictory trace

The group27/group28 anchor block is I6. Direct reconstruction gives, on the
five active coordinates of Z=R_CD L_CD,

    Z_active=diag(0,3/2,1,1,0),
    W=diag(3/2,1,1,0).

The omitted sixth coordinate is killed on both sides by P. Factor any rank2
idempotent Q=FG with GF=I2. Such a factorization exists because image and
kernel of an idempotent are complementary; no genericity is assumed.
The image of diag(1,Q,0) is the direct sum of e0 and image(F). Eliminate the
anchor I6 from the group27/group28/new-group3 self rectangle. Its remaining
three by3 block is exactly

    diag(1, I2-GWF).

The literal tariff is7. Since the anchor contributes rank6 and the first
diagonal entry contributes1, the cap forces GWF=I2. Multiplication by F,G
gives QWQ=Q. Equivalently

    Q(W-I4)Q=0.

Cyclic trace and Q^2=Q therefore force

    0=tr((W-I4)Q)=(1/2)Q00-Q33=-4,

a contradiction. This is an exact all-Q argument; neither invertibility of
W nor any source/reader restriction of F,G is needed.
