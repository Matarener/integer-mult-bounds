This argument applies to the actual all-fresh p6/keep25279 native word in

## 1. Actual native product and field ownership

Let native mixed unit U have target roles i of ranks k_i and chronological
paid ranks r_l. Let V have target roles j of ranks h_j and paid ranks s_a.
As in the present word and its products, each ambient rank equals the
maximum target rank, and all its paid ranks fit that ambient space.

For every V target role j, instantiate a separate U copy at column count h_j.
Its productive fields have labels(i,j), all distinct arbitrary complete
arrays. Work in the common product ambient space, completing all unused
coordinates as arbitrary spectators. Give every copy its own owned center
banks. These are initialized and erased, and receive no productive credit.

Run the U copies in lockstep through their common scalar and paid-call list.
At paid position l, pause every copy. Copy j requests a rank r_l*h_j job
on its current physical owner. These owners are distinct across the copies;
some may be owned centers containing correlated values. One V invocation at
column count r_l receives those fields as its j-indexed productive roles.
Wrap this complete invocation with the permitted role-specific whole-job
axis matchers. Its endpoints provide exactly the requested ranks h_j*r_l.
Its own centers have separate identities.

Finish V, undo all its complete-job matchers, and only then resume U.
Never combine different paid positions of the same U copy. This is a finite
acyclic chronological program, and arbitrary-input correctness permits
correlated values in distinct physical input slots. No scalar input is
copied to manufacture additional productive credit.

The final independent(i,j) endpoints have ranks k_i*h_j; the remaining
paid calls have ranks r_l*s_a. Thus target and paid moments multiply.
Iterating v times gives target moment T_tau^v and paid moment P_tau^v,
with minimum target5^v and maximum target105^v. This is substitution of
whole native programs; it never tensors a sum of matrix operators.

The same recipe works at every further positive column multiplicity by
lifting each fixed coordinate/phase generator and matcher. The scalar word
remains unchanged. One owned bank per raw unit instance is a safe finite
allocation, retaining every bank until its actual owner clears it.

## 2. Integer splitting at a rationally specified cutoff

Write tau=1-C with0<C<1. Choose b=19/4 and the integer cutoff

```
J = floor(b^v).
```

It satisfies b^v/2<=J<b^v<5^v. Replace any paid rank R>J by consecutive
jobs on disjoint axes of ranks J,...,J,r, where R=qJ+r and0<=r<J.
Omit the remainder when r=0. Preserve the original whole-job normalizers
around this complete replacement. The disjoint-axis splitting law preserves
the exact endpoint at every column count, without changing productive roles
or rank-one mass.

The replaced job's moment charge is bounded by

```
q*J^tau+r^tau <= R*J^(-C)+J^tau <= 2*R*J^(-C).
```

Summing high ranks and conservatively retaining the original moment for all
lower ranks bounds the new paid moment by

```
P_tau^v + 2*P_1^v/J^C.
```

Define alpha=P_tau/T_tau and gamma=P_1/(b^C*T_tau). Since the floor costs
at most a factor2 in J andC<1, the normalized complete paid moment is

```
<= alpha^v+2^(1+C)*gamma^v < alpha^v+4*gamma^v.
```

Both alpha<1 and gamma<1 are required and separately certified. The second
inequality is a rank-one tail condition; a favorable mixed moment alone
would not justify the construction. Every final paid rank is now below
every productive target rank.
