## 1. Arbitrary physical dirt at a paired boundary

Use a legal exact ADDRESS field and nondegenerate frame projectors. Write

    D_P = [[I-P, P], [P, I-P]].

These are linear ADDRESS involutions and induce permutations of complete
VALUE arrays. Suppose a local word is verified on every independent logical
source, old-target and helper input. Assume helper h has entrance frame E,
terminal frame U, final logical helper row exactly h, and zero coefficient
in every required public output over the BIT VALUE field F2. This is a full
row/column contract, not merely a right eigenvector or a dimension count.

For an arbitrary physical array u, define h=D_E^{-1}u in the proof. This is
a bijective change of variables, not an executed entrance operation. The
verified physical endpoint is then

    u -> D_U D_E^{-1}u,

and all public outputs, **as F2 VALUE arrays**, are independent of u. Signed
integer target coefficients may remain nonzero and even; binary D*S=0 does
not assert their integer vanishing. Exact integer source/helper return and
the binary target shear are distinct obligations. Since the logical theorem holds
for every joint tuple, it also holds when different entering physical arrays
are correlated with each other or with public data. No probabilistic or
clean-input assumption is used. Executing an additional entrance query is
required only if a caller separately demands the predecessor's identification
h=u while changing E. That is a different boundary contract.

If E is contained in U, the orthogonal projectors satisfy
P_E P_U=P_U P_E=P_E. Direct block multiplication gives

    D_U D_E^{-1} = D_(P_U-P_E),
    (P_U-P_E)^2=P_U-P_E,
    rank(P_U-P_E)=dim(U)-dim(E).

Thus the residual is the interchange on U intersect E-perp. For U=FULL,
it has width 8-dim(E). This algebra does not establish that its adapter,
queries or tape implementation are free. It applies only where the exact
projectors and ADDRESS maps are defined.

## 2. Dedicated cover-bank theorem

Fix a finite cover group G acting through doubled charts diag(g,g). It must
contain every adapter used below. Public families and private work families
are separate from the ordinary helper banks. Let m contain each full local
footprint, including spectators, not just its residual rank.

For each role j assigned to a particular bank, let T_j be its certified
paired physical residual. Choose disjoint coordinate blocks I_j and explicit
adapters Pi_j in G satisfying

    Pi_j T_j Pi_j^{-1} = D_(I_j).

Within any one local invocation and bank, require all Pi_j to be distinct.
The physical family label is the complete pair (bank,g); disjoint coordinate
blocks alone do not excuse a repeated family in the same invocation.

At cover invocation d, assign role j to g=d Pi_j^{-1}. For fixed j this is
a bijection of G. For distinct roles in one invocation, distinct Pi_j imply
distinct g, hence no simultaneous-family alias. At fixed physical owner g,
role j occurs at d=g Pi_j and acts by

    d T_j d^{-1} = g D_(I_j) g^{-1}.

Every role therefore reaches that owner exactly once. The D_(I_j) commute
because their coordinate blocks are disjoint. Their product is the swap on
the union. Add and charge a padding query on the complementary coordinates.
The final owner action is g D_(I_m) g^{-1}=D_(I_m), because a full head/tail
swap commutes with every doubled chart diag(g,g).

Execute each complete local call before the next, in an order preserving the
public source chronology. Intermediate helper states are not exposed to
another invocation. Each later invocation accepts the arbitrary array left
by the preceding ones. These premises make the argument chronological; they
do not construct pending windows for recycled public families.

Unused local spectator axes may belong to a different role's later residual
block. The completed current endpoint is the identity on its spectators, and
the next role accepts arbitrary entering arrays. No permanently untouched
global spectator block is needed. Simultaneous reuse or a local theorem that
assumes those spectators clean would invalidate this conclusion.

This is an algebraic finite-cover hosting theorem. Every adapter, source
access, controller, private buffer and native query still requires its paid
implementation. A rational GL chart is not automatically admitted by an
orthogonal or dyadic native alphabet. Centrality of the final full swap does
not authorize arbitrary non-diagonal ADDRESS charts.

## 4. Exact hybrid seed and all-bank rank obstruction

Fix one public port and one common nontrivial involution N. Work formally in
R=F2[N]/(N^2-I), or in a faithful actual representation of it. Set d=N+I;
then d^2=0 and d is nonzero. The checker uses the faithful binary matrices

    N = [[0,1],[1,0]],       d = [[1,1],[1,1]].

The images of 0,I,N,I+N are all distinct. Thus checking the resulting
four-by-four binary block matrices verifies every coefficient of the
two-bank seed; no scalar substitution N=I can hide a coefficient defect.

With epsilon indicating whether the actual target cap was lifted to FULL,
the normalized forward and backward operators are

    F0=[[I,0],[N,I]],        B0=[[I,N],[0,I]],
    F1=[[I,0],[I,N]],        B1=[[N,I],[0,I]].

Exact multiplication gives

    F0 B0 F0 = [[0,N],[N,0]],
    F1 B1 F1 = [[I+N,N],[I,I+N]],
    (F1 B1 F1)^2 = diag(N,N).

The six-call squared seed does not undo a FULL target lift already charged
inside each call. Omitting its actual middle B changes the operator.

Write any of these four calls as A(I+dR_j), with A its invertible constant
matrix at N=I. The respective normalized derivatives are

    R_F0=E21, R_B0=E12, R_F1=E22, R_B1=E11,

all rank one over F2. Embedding the call in any number b of banks preserves
this rank. Arbitrary interleaved **N-independent scalar** bank matrices in
GL_b(F2) may be granted free; they conjugate these derivatives and do not
increase their ranks. No port mixing or operator-valued adapter is admitted
by this statement.

Move every constant factor to the left in a k-call product. Since scalar
bank matrices commute with d and d^2=0, the result is

    A_total [I+d (R'_1+...+R'_k)],

where each R'_j is a conjugate of a rank-one matrix. If the normalized
required endpoint is N on n independent banks and I on any additional
restored banks, its d coefficient has rank n. Faithfulness forces equality
of coefficients. Rank subadditivity therefore gives

    n = rank(sum_j R'_j) <= sum_j rank(R'_j) = k.

This proves the obstruction for every b,n,k, not only the checked two-bank
seed. A fixed nontrivial common N, independent bank inputs and the permitted
scalar adapter alphabet are essential. At N=I the representation is not
faithful. If distinct queries use different noncommuting involutions, the
products of their d terms need not vanish. N-dependent free adapters could
themselves supply the required endpoint. These changed models are excluded.
