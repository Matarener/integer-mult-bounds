# Any-support skew kernel: fixed two-site cleanup tariff

This written rank bound is conditional on the explicit repeating scalar word,
transport and complete inventory below. It removes the two-plane support
hypothesis from the preceding kernel gate. Complete actual source-word
bindings are checked separately at q=2,3 for 36 exact wider-support fixtures,
for every positive tensor multiplicity f. A finite check is not an all-q
source compiler, a global caller obstruction or a multiplication theorem.

Let q>=2, k=2q+1, n=2q+2 and m=2k+2. N_s acts on the two axes of plane s,
N_s^2=-I, and its native arity is 2. The planes s=0,...,k are disjoint.
P_s exchanges planes0 and s and is a physical monomial. Set
F_s=N_s...N_1, F=F_k. J is any actual Gaussian Clifford on all m axes with
J^2=-I, including its whole-field phase. No support or parity assumption on
J is made. Put J_s=F_s J F_s^-1, B_s=P_s N_0^-1 J,
Q=J^-1 B_1 J=J P_1 N_0^-1, and

    D_u=(B_(2q) B_(2q-2) ... B_4) B_3 J.

The product in parentheses uses even indices only. The target on each of
n independent original banks is FJ. Let nu(G) be the binary symplectic
B-block rank of G. It is invariant under inverse and physical monomials
on either side, and is subadditive under products. Ranks are in units of f: pointwise nu/f may be real for a
nonuniform family, so no rank-parity or tensor premise enters this written
inequality. Write r=nu(FJ), d=r-2k and a_s=nu(J_s).

For every chronological s in0,...,k,

    FJ=(F F_s^-1) J_s F_s,
    r <= 2(k-s)+a_s+2s = 2k+a_s.

Thus a_s>=d. This remains valid when d is negative; it is then merely a
weaker lower bound. The retained Q recovery W=F Q^-1 F^-1 satisfies

    W(FJ)=F N_0 P_1.

The right side has native rank m, so w=nu(W) obeys w+r>=m, or w>=2-d.
No commuting of J with a fresh N_s has been used.

The declared two edits replace the query3 source post by U and the last
even source post by V, and delete the whole D_u inverse tail recovery.
For the repeating scalar topology, the first direction and inverse dual
row are isolated in the later bank. Its transport obeys

    T_2=P_4 J_3,
    T_t=P_(2t) J_(2t-1) N_(2t-1) T_(t-1), t>=3.

The current deletion correction is C=F_(2q) D_u^-1 F_(2q)^-1. Hence

    V=L U^-1 R,
    L=C T_q P_3,   R=T_q^-1 P_(2q).

These equalities retain the actual directions, phases and right input gauge.
The full row/column isolation is a premise of this rank theorem; q2/q3
checks establish it from the literal event words, not from selected inputs.

The transport telescopes WITHOUT a support restriction on J:

    F_(2t)^-1 T_t = B_(2t) F_(2t-2)^-1 T_(t-1),
    F_4^-1 T_2 = B_4 F_3^-1.

Indeed P_(2t) commutes with F_(2t-1),
N_(2t)^-1 P_(2t)=P_(2t)N_0^-1, and
F_(2t-1)^-1 N_(2t-1)=F_(2t-2)^-1. No interchange involving J is needed.
Cancelling the even B product in L, and then using
B_3^-1=J^-1 N_0 P_3 and J^-2=-I, gives

    L=F_(2q) J^-1 B_3^-1 F_3^-1 P_3
     =-N_(2q) N_(2q-1) ... N_3.

Thus ell=nu(L)=4q-4. With rho=nu(T_q)=nu(R), subadditivity gives

    rho <= sum_(t=2..q) a_(2t-1) + 2(q-2).

The isolated rank-distance inequality gives
nu(U)+nu(V)>=|ell-rho|>=ell-rho. Therefore the complete two-site charge is
at least 2q minus the displayed sum of q-1 chronological J ranks. This
argument also allows a charged nonmonomial U; taking U physical monomial
is the attaining mechanism in the finite construction, not a necessity
for this lower bound.

After deleting D_u recovery and before the two replacements, the required
remaining inventory is:

* k(n-1)+1 prefix N calls and q-1 reverse N calls, each native rank2;
* two J_0 occurrences;
* one J_(2t) occurrence for each t=1,...,q;
* two J_(2t+1) occurrences for each t=1,...,q;
* one Q recovery of rank w;
* the two changed sites charged at nu(U)+nu(V).

Thus there are 3q+2 J occurrences in total. Each J_(2t-1) used in the
transport estimate (t=2,...,q) occurs among the paid events. Cancelling
one occurrence for each such t leaves (3q+2)-(q-1)=2q+3=n+1 paid J ranks.
The N-only bill is

    A=2[k(n-1)+q]=8q^2+10q+2=n(2k-1).

Consequently the COMPLETE native bill M obeys

    M >= A+2q+(n+1)d+w
      >= n(2k-1)+(n-2)+(n+1)d+(2-d)
       = n(2k+d)=n*r.

This proves no favorable native mass against the optimistic stock n*r
under this complete inventory and isolated two-site interface, for every
q satisfying the stated interface. It also proves no favorable native
moment at exponents0<sigma<=1 under the same equal-volume full-bank
normalization, when every positive child arity is strictly below r:
(a/r)^sigma>=a/r. Heterogeneous physical volumes require their actual
weights and cannot be replaced by this unweighted consequence. Full-arity/nonproper calls do not become valid
recursive children by this argument. Native rank zero scans remain paid.

The finite script tests18 fixed reproducible actual X-character skew

They instantiate the written rank argument; its universality comes from

Changed scalar topology, other cuts or more coupled replacement sites,

## Support-metric extension for separately charged unary implementations

For an arbitrary finite unitary G, define lambda(G) as log2 of the larger
of its maximum row-support size and maximum column-support size. Matrix
multiplication gives lambda(AB)<=lambda(A)+lambda(B); adjoint invariance
gives lambda(G^-1)=lambda(G). Multiplication by an arbitrary monomial on
either side preserves lambda. A native a-axis Fourier/chirp block has
lambda=a. Thus the sum of native arities in a separately charged
implementation of G is at least lambda(G), even when its physical
monomial adapters are nonlinear address permutations.

The same displayed proof uses only those metric properties and the exact
support sizes of F_s, L and F N_0 P_1. Therefore it also gives M>=n*r for
an arbitrary unitary skew J satisfying the identical exact-word premises,
where r=lambda(FJ)/f. For stock interpretation the intended native target
must have support exponent equal to its declared parent arity. Each
retained J_s, W and replacement must be implemented and charged separately
as a composition of native calls and physical monomials, or have another
proved native-charge lower bound at least lambda. A coupled-bank ADD or
dirty-helper implementation with uncounted path additions does not
automatically satisfy that lower bound. Uncharged cross-boundary fusion
would also change the declared inventory premise. This extension does not certify any non-Clifford J circuit,
all-q source realization, different scalar word or different volume stock.
