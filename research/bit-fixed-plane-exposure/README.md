# All-subset exposure bounds for 273 fixed BIT planes

For each of120 root lines from the pinned full E8 list and153 rank-two
frames actually present in the R438 word,
a finite partition certificate proves an inequality for **every subset S**
of its438 helpers:

    dim(E) * nullity_F2(D_S) <= 8 * |exposure_E(S)|.

Here D is the actual111-by438 binary helper-to-target dirty map, and exposure
is the union of target/COPY resources reached by the prescribed causal
common-E promotion. This covers273 specified planes and all helper subsets
for each one. It is not a bound for arbitrary planes, mixed entrances or
changed chronologies, and it does not imply a multiplication exponent.
Scientific pin: `20ecf2891d80b5acf18d980b2164fb44ea7faa90`.

## The fixed model

All438 helper columns are retained in their original order. D is bound to
the complete semantic uncompensated old R438 word, including its nine copied
totals. The calculation uses the full kernel D_S, with no quotient through
cold target rows. Independent source reconstruction distinguishes that
semantic map from a merely syntactic compensation list.

A selected helper is promoted to common E. At a scalar ADD below E, promotion
of either actor forces both to the joined frame. Once an old frame contains
E, the extra promotion stops. The unchanged chronological word determines
these implications. Copied private fields remain ZERO; a reached copied-total
source not containing E exposes one extra COPY rank. A reached rank-seven
target cap not containing E exposes one extra target rank. The120 resources
are111 target caps and nine copied totals. The coefficient8 is an auxiliary
partition capacity, not the actual one-rank charge of a reached resource.

This PRIVATE-ZERO convention differs from the global-E2 word with private
fields at E in the paired-helper package. Public source paths may change
under the promotion; those changes are not counted by this auxiliary
objective. A literal selective supplier and its complete costs are not
constructed here.

## Why a finite partition covers every subset

Make r=dim(E) colored copies of every helper. Partition all438r copies into
r independent D classes, one for each color, and resource classes of at most
8 incident copies each. A copy assigned to a resource must carry that
resource in its actual causal exposure mask; no copy can be assigned twice.

For any helper subset S, each independent color class contains at most
rank(D_S) copies belonging to S. Only resources in exposure_E(S) can contain
those copies, and each holds at most8. Counting all r|S| copies gives
r|S|<=r*rank(D_S)+8*|exposure_E(S)|, proving the displayed inequality.
The certificate does not require trusting the augmentation algorithm or
enumerating2^438 subsets.

All273 saved partitions assign every colored copy. Their auxiliary optimum
is exactly zero because the bound is zero and S=empty attains it.
Eight is the least integer common coefficient for this fixed family: the
actual frame116 with all438 helpers has nullity327 and90 exposures, so
2*327-7*90=24>0. This supplies a real-coefficient lower bound109/15, not
an optimal-real-coefficient theorem. The
historical producer's matching-cut machinery is retained and replayed, but
is not needed to prove this zero optimum.

## Limits and credit

The auxiliary objective r*nullity(D_S)-8*|exposure_E(S)| is **not net deficit**.
Narrower helper residuals lose corresponding progress credit; promoted pivots
and changed source paths can alter query histograms even with zero nullity.
A rate consequence needs an independent complete owner/moment benefit bound
and all source, target, COPY, adapter, private and padding costs. No such
benefit theorem, native selective word, public owner, rounded precision or
physical source/tape implementation is supplied here.

Sagar Thakrar's R438 transport and the eumemic, DreamingOfClouds and Ali Romman
E8/BIT lineage provide the immutable inputs. The causal promotion experiment
and semantic dirty map are retained predecessor work. This contribution
adds the complete fixed-plane partition certificates, their all-subset
interpretation and independent source/causality verification. The generic
partition counting argument is not claimed as a new combinatorial theorem.
Related kernel-basis and paired-boundary work retains the PR2857/2908 and
PR386 ancestry described in the paired-helper contribution.

## Open next questions

Can a changed mixed-E or cold-target quotient model yield a complete useful
word? State its causal resources and native realization before applying a
partition bound; the fixed model here does not cover that change.

Can the auxiliary objective be related to a rigorous complete moment/owner
benefit bound, including source-path changes? Without that bridge, even an
all-subset auxiliary bound is not an all-rate rejection.
