# The asymmetric U9 K-word composes with full H6 compensation

The exact local U9 construction can replace the old K-word in the complete
edge or address-bucket compensation chronology. This closes the specific
source-cut integration gap. It does not give an improved kappa: the retained
bucket network still needs 227,312,640 compensation helpers.

The correct splice is after all compensation helpers capture their sources
at the original source lines, and before the outer word moves those sources
to FULL. The new K-word itself performs their partial U9 and final FULL
climbs. Moving the old outer FULL climbs before the partial gather would be
an illegal ordinary descent; the executable rejects that defect.

At the splice, sources, targets and the 816 K centres may contain arbitrary
values. In the forward orientation the compensation helpers hold d+x and
targets hold y-Ad. The K-word adds Kx and restores its centres, then the
compensation scatter adds A(d+x); each helper subtracts its source at FULL.
The result is y+(K+A)x=y+x with all ordinary dirt restored logically.

In the same-order inverse transpose, sources already contain the earlier
helper dirt and target contribution. The K-word's arbitrary-source contract
accepts those values. Targets remain unchanged during its private gathers;
the later compensation phases cancel the captured dirt and give x-y.
No clean-input assumption is introduced at the internal boundary.

`compose.py` emits and replays every scalar/frame/private episode for the
same 32 actual ports used by the retained U9 fixtures, all 816 K centres,
436 compensation edge helpers and 1,316 independent input columns in each
orientation. All ordinary helpers finish at physical FULL, retaining their
original logical dirt. Six controls reject premature FULL, missing initial
compensation and missing helper undo in both orientations. Normal and `-O`
replay pass. The code retains explicit overflow checks.

The written full-bucket extension uses its existing helper h(source,label)
and broadcasts the same captured source to all targets in that label bucket.
Only the internal K chronology changes. Its full per-stage histogram is

```

R=227312640; private rank mass=16*9+800*17=13744.
```

Every ordinary K centre, source visit and private episode is included.
The rank-mass improvement is 128 per stage and no ordinary helper is
deleted. This is a phasewise full-domain composition proof with a finite

The inherited phase-aware U9 recipes, universal edge-transition theorem and
