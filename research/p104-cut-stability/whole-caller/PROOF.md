## 4. Scalar all-interval and physical chart bounds

Use realified coordinate sup norm: the maximum of all real and imaginary
coordinate magnitudes. All monomial recipes have norm one and introduce
no denominator. A normalized C on one bit has norm at most two and binary
denominator exponent at most one. A complete f-column C direction therefore
has norm at most `2^f=N`, with binary denominator exponent at most f.

The retained all-cut source certificate has exact prefix row sum 131072 and
exact suffix row sum 135415/4. Its additional all-interval upper bounds are
**Gamma=2^296** and **D=54**. These interval bounds are not asserted to be
attained maxima. The inexpensive checker independently reproduces them by
the following O(25310) recurrence on all 743 roles. Start each e,d at zero;
MOVE is logical identity. For ADD (including physical COPY),

```
e_dst = max(e_dst,e_src+ceil(log2(abs(q))))+1
d_dst = max(d_dst,d_src+log2(den(q))).
```

For SCALE use `e=max(0,e+ceil(log2(abs(q))))` and add the coefficient's binary
denominator exponent. INIT/CLEAR reset both to zero. Keeping identity as a
possible restart at every cut dominates every scalar interval. Row-sum
triangle inequality proves the e recurrence, including coefficients below
one; denominator arithmetic proves d. COPY is ADD, so independent owned
residuals are present throughout this bound. Separately, the product of all
per-record `(1+abs(q))` bounds gives Gamma<=2^27646 and D<=17032; this weaker
bound is reproduced as an independent crosscheck.

The source initial public chart dimension is at most five, and every current
chart dimension and native child rank is at most nine. Canonical Phi_F and
its inverse use exactly dim(F) normalized C directions, plus unit monomials.
Semantic local prefixes therefore cost at most `LambdaP*N^(5+9)`, suffixes
at most `LambdaS*N^(9+9)`. A cut inside a complete native C-factor recipe
adds at most nine C directions at each extremal end. Safe local bounds are

```
prefix <=LambdaP*N^23, suffix <=LambdaS*N^27,
any interval <=Gamma*N^36,
interval denominator exponent <=54+36f.
```

This includes the arbitrary dirty inputs and the owned residuals. The bound
is at declared field/native-C-factor cuts, including fully retained spectator
fields. Internal scalar accumulators, literal in-place butterfly lowerings,
movement/tape loops and recursive child internals need their admitted
primitive-prefix contracts. This proof does not silently assign norm one
to an unmaterialized internal implementation.

## 5. Whole caller and error ABI

An arbitrary whole interval intersects at most 18 LOCAL intervals, fifteen
queries and fifteen IDLE events. All individual upper bounds are at least
one, so retaining every event also bounds a truncated interval, prefix or
suffix. The 486 lowered outer ADD layers contribute at most 2^486; the
queries contribute at most `4^15*N^15`; all IDLEs contribute at most N^632.
Hence

```
K(N) = 2^516*Gamma^18*N^(18*36+15+632)
     = 2^5844*N^1295,
Dwhole(f) = 18*54+1295f = 972+1295f.
```

Global whole-job and stage maps are unit monomials; they change neither
bound. The full 265 axes include the central block and all 32 fresh blocks.
There is no forgotten spectator-dimension factor in this componentwise
sup-norm argument. Parallel modules use disjoint raw positions. No sum over
13062 fields, 104 ports or external spectator addresses is needed.

The input/output error ABI is explicit: an arbitrary realified coordinate
residual of sup norm eta at any declared interface is transported to a later
interface with sup norm at most K(N)*eta. For multiple injected residuals,
sum their separately transported contributions. A count of recursive
rounding sites and a bound for primitive interiors must still be supplied
by the retained precision/compiler specialization. K alone is not such a
site count. The weaker product crosscheck is `2^498144*N^1295`, with binary
denominator exponent `306576+1295f`.

There is a sharper corollary using the retained active-fiber Hilbert method
credited to Ross Richardson and Adam Gall in the
`research/p104-retimed-internal-grid` lineage. Fix an outer spectator
address. Use maximum **individual-field** Hilbert norm over the common
265f active coordinates, not joint l2 over all fields. Each canonical chart,
complete native C factor, phase and route is unitary. At a local interval's
endpoints, each block is a scalar coefficient times a product of two
unitaries; its block norm is the coefficient's absolute value. Thus Gamma
alone, without N^36, bounds that local interval in this norm. The scalar ADD
factor is two, each query costs at most four, and whole IDLEs are unitary.
The complete caller's maximum-individual-field Hilbert interval bound is
therefore **2^5844**.

Convert once, at the interval input, from realified coordinate norm to one
active complex fiber's Hilbert norm. Its dimension is `2^(265f)`, so

```
||delta_out||coordinate-infinity
 <=2^5844*sqrt(2*2^(265f))*||delta_in||coordinate-infinity
 <=2^(5844+ceil((265f+1)/2))*||delta_in||coordinate-infinity.
```

No factor from the number of fields or spectator addresses is dropped:
neither occurs in a maximum-individual-field/fixed-spectator-fiber norm.
All 265f active coordinates do occur. A cut inside a real-output accumulator
is not a complete C-factor cut and retains its separate primitive contract.
The independent scoped review is retained as HILBERT-FIBER-REVIEW.md. This
corollary improves the coordinate amplification; the coarse denominator
bound and storage expression below remain valid crosschecks.

For dyadic real/imaginary inputs of magnitude at most 2^b and initial grid
2^-d0, every exact raw intermediate at these interfaces has a common binary
denominator dividing `2^(d0+972+1295f)`. A signed numerator therefore fits

```
b+d0+6818+2590f bits per real or imaginary component.
```

A dense raw reservation of all 13062 complete fields consequently fits
`2*13062*N^265*(b+d0+6818+2590f)` bits, plus fixed descriptors and separately
reserved recursive-child banks. This is a constructive coarse exact-value
range/storage majorant. It neither changes the input precision nor assumes
unbounded constant-size integers. Rounding errors use the same explicit
input/output ABI; a complete rounded multiplier is not constructed here.
