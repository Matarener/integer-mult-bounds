## Why these fields can receive productive credit

The previously tested controller families required every borrowed local helper
to fit inside an idle segment of an existing main-data field. The new proposal
adds fields whose entire job is a complete Fourier transform of the ambient
rank. Their arbitrary contents must be transformed correctly, rather than
erased or treated as zero. Each field can host several already-cleaned native
helper roles and then finish its remaining transform.

There is a useful whole-block interface lemma. Suppose the exact physical
operator of a cleaned native word is

\[
\operatorname{diag}(D,T_1,\ldots,T_h),\qquad
T_i=F_{\mathrm{end},i}F_{\mathrm{start},i}^{-1}.
\]

Here `D` is the data operation. The absence of helper/data and helper/helper
cross terms is essential and follows from full arbitrary-input restoration,
not merely from testing zero helper inputs. If allowed monomial adapters
`L_i,R_i` satisfy

\[
L_iT_iR_i=F_{r_i},
\]

then applying those adapters only at the boundaries gives exactly
`diag(D,F_r1,...,F_rh)`. The internal word and every internal ADD remain
unchanged. A later call can normalize its own complete helper block onto a
disjoint set of unfinished axes of the same field. These transforms compose
to the field's complete ambient Fourier job.

This is different from changing each internal helper frame independently.
That latter shortcut can invalidate same-frame contacts. In particular, a
literal synchronous preparation of all native first-call entrance frames
would add 371 rank units in the p=1 case. The boundary-only block lemma avoids
that preparation if, and only if, the actual native relative transforms have
the stated allowed monomial normal forms with correct phases. The complex
agent identifies the existing `ce.lower_child(T_i)` normal-form routine for
that check; its fresh all-408-role adapter replay is not part of this receipt.

## What is checked and what remains

The standalone script checks every expanded host, stage eligibility,
per-call width inventory, nonnegative remaining path, integer replica lift,
strict child-rank decrease, the total rank identity, and the complete tariff
at two exact rational exponents. Its logarithm and exponential calculations
use rational series bounds, and the JSON intervals are rounded outward.

It does not reconstruct the earlier native source profile from its original
word. That profile was authenticated earlier in this round; the executor
reset interrupted access to those predecessor artifacts. Nor does it verify
fresh monomial normal forms for all408 relative helper transforms, emit a
whole enlarged controller, or admit its outer bilinear use. The scientific
meaning of these receipts is a concrete, fully invoiced productive-helper
interface candidate with explicit remaining obligations.

The native involution completion also uses one sequentially reusable owned
clear field. Its recursive calls are included in the 8H1-per-call invoice,
but this scratch field receives no productive output credit. A complete
controller must supply that actual scratch capacity and retain its clearing
proof. The productive field counts in this note are not the total allocated
register count after adding such owned scratch.

The historical repaired-identity kernel cannot be replaced by the pruned
kernel plus free historical query assembly. The separate query-contract
audit rules out the tested deletions. This note uses the actual changed
involution kernel and keeps its main-data closure outstanding.
