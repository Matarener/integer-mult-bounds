# Scope of the independent bridge certificate

Chris R / Clear1nsight, substantial OpenAI Codex assistance. Apache-2.0.
The construction and its claimed improvement are Dugongue's PR3945; this note
explains the independently recomputed implication and its boundary.

## Relative dirty-state operator identity

Erase frame annotations and identity scalar multiplications from each literal
program. Resolve every native index, including inverse indices, to its exact
permutation of all 512 binary labels. Interleave the B-native events at their
actual cut positions. Keep A, the cut, B/restoration, and cleanup as separate
sequences, so a changed chronological boundary cannot vanish by concatenation.

The old and new token sequences agree exactly for both providers. All other
caller fields, including prelude/corrections, inputs and private-buffer metadata,
agree as data. Each matched scalar operation defines the same rational linear
map, and each matched native token defines the same abstract permutation map.
Their compositions therefore agree on every vector of formal initial register
and private-buffer variables, hence every arbitrary dirty assignment. Inverse
or contragredient maps of equal invertible maps are equal as well. This is a
relative algebraic identity; it does not independently prove the predecessor's
caller theorem, nor identify the physical Gaussian/Clifford phases of a newly
framed native implementation. Those obligations remain external.

## Complete local frame invoice

For each frame, explicitly enumerate its binary linear span by closure under
XOR. Its dimension is the base-two logarithm of its cardinality. At every paid
scalar entrance, require inclusion of the preceding frame in the requested
frame and charge one child of rank equal to the dimension increment if positive.
A native transport must map the full entrance point set to the exit point set;
it cannot silently change dimension. The centre descents occur on private
copies and contribute their prescribed difference charges. Finish with each
literal terminal entrance-to-exit climb.

This recomputes the inherited frame-billing contract without reading the words'
claimed profiles. The old invoice is
`2440 H1 + 725 H2 + 224 H3 + 102 H4 + 20 H5 + 8 H8`.
The new invoice is
`2482 H1 + 712 H2 + 218 H3 + 109 H4 + 20 H5 + 8 H8`.
Each provider adds 26 dimensions and 30 recursive children locally. All 26
changed helper terminal subspaces contain their old terminal as a codimension-one
subspace; every helper entrance is unchanged. Hence exactly 26 helper widths
increase from four to five.

## Literal owner implication

For a physical bin with capacity c, represent occupied coordinates as c bits.
Every old and new role contributes the exact interval at its recorded offset,
with its width derived above. Reject overflow, intersection with earlier occupied
coordinates, and a later chronological role placed before earlier coordinates.
At the end require a compact prefix. This supplies disjointness and exact unused
capacity without assuming that a reconstructed prefix is the submitted offset.

Check every role's time against its source window, every call's distinct physical
families, reused-family window separation, and exact old/new role identities.
These predicates depend on the actual owner, child, port and chronology, not on
aggregate width counts. Every one of the 84,240 changed bins contains exactly one
old width-four role and three width-nine roles, with one old unused coordinate.
The changed role's width becomes five. All submitted offsets obey the above
interval predicates and all other bins retain their occupied coordinates.

There are 3,240 actual calls, each using one of the two checked providers. Both
have the same local invoice and width changes. Consequently the complete paid
delta is the literal identity

`3240*(42 H1 - 13 H2 - 6 H3 + 7 H4) - 84240 H1`

`= 51840 H1 - 42120 H2 - 19440 H3 + 22680 H4`.

Its rank mass is `51840 - 84240 - 58320 + 90720 = 0`. The fresh bitmap audit also
reconstructs the complete old/new profiles independently of this rearrangement.
Thus the retained terminal-to-owner cost bridge is corroborated. Pricing this
profile at a root, admitting the full native/release chain, and discharging
uniform model assumptions are separate claims, not consequences of the bitmap
identity alone.
