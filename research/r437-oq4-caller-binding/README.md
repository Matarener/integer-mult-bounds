## The exact physical boundary

The probe has a **46-bit controlled payload (two 23-bit prefix fibres), controlled child outputs and
stock11**, with residue/index bank capacities131072/1024. Its actual fixed-role parking/CALL/RETURN protocol executes; it
does not execute the full rank26 or rank7 residual body, forward/backward
adapter sweeps, original-width production stock, ordinary wrapper, native
producer or router cohort. Its small payload does not satisfy the volume
encoded by its numerical extents. All63 readers consume the controlled private
result using the actual physical XOR dispatcher, preserving source and private
value through the complete65-event COPY/read/ERASE interval at22014..22078.
The two returned bit transformations are
explicit test stimuli, not an asserted interchange oracle.

For example, the encoded root packet alone would contain
`660*3^4096` payload bits at these extents. A successful return on46 bits
cannot certify that full endpoint. Likewise, `macros=0` in the receipt means
the complete global dispatch word was not run. No complete fixed-q GL64 word
or microtransition table was materialized. [BINDING.md](BINDING.md) separates
the instantiated capsule contract from the remaining whole-caller argument
and retains its full preparation, routing, stock and visited-cell obligations.

This closes the previous **synthetic-ancestor/source-binding gap** at these
real physical gates. It does not close supplier admission or independently
prove PR2809's universal refinement. PR3950's conditional cost bound remains
conditional; finite parking counts here do not set its asymptotic constant.
