    det(G)*det(Q[I,I])
      = sum_(J subset [26], |J|=k) (-1)^(sum I+sum J)
           det G[I,J] * det G[I^c,J^c] = F_k(G).

Each polynomial F_k has degree at most64 and evaluates to1 at G=I, in every
residue field. Their product is nonzero with degree at most64*26=1664.

    delta26 <= min(1,1664*(q-1)/(q*(q-2)))   (q>=3),
