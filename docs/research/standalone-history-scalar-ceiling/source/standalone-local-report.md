## 3. The independent-coordinate history-module lemma

The reviewed finite-feedback proof is `EXACT-FANOUT-MODULE-LOWER-v1.md`, SHA256 `cfa9f3df855f96a3e73bcb8042a750a3aa41d18ec098d6a34268370f3e84d782`. Its independent static review is SHA256 `2b45b3cfa4cc995a8948f5b2a047819aef0b61cb490508d66575abe624e25cf6`. We use its history argument and prove the simpler target-module specialization here. We do **not** reuse its numerical source specialization C=112 or assume the present targets have that source's common eight-column normalizers.

Fix the one-column space V over K=Q(i), with the fixed product geometry included. In this nomination there are 625 binary axis groups per column; this is an amplitude space of dimension 2^625, not dimension 625. Let G be a finite group containing all allowed ordinary one-column address gates; enlarging it to all computational-basis monomial matrices on V with fourth-root entries is permitted. Set R=K[G]. Exterior K scalars act once and are coefficients of R. Let Gamma be a group of actual invertible one-column matrices containing G, all paid operators, and the target operators h_i. Its inclusion of an operator grants no free implementation of that operator.

The same finite straight-line word and the same exterior coefficients must work at every positive width f, or every f in a consecutive infinite tail. For any finitely supported element sum c_j[g_j] in K[Gamma], all-width evaluation is injective. Indeed choose a K-linear functional ell on End(V) such that its finitely many values lambda_j=ell(g_j) are nonzero and pairwise distinct. Over the infinite field K the excluded choices are a finite union of proper hyperplanes. Applying ell tensor-powered f times gives sum c_j lambda_j^f=0. Taking consecutive f=f0,...,f0+s-1 gives a Vandermonde determinant

```
(product_j lambda_j^f0) (product_{a<b}(lambda_b-lambda_a)) != 0.
```

Thus every c_j is zero. Fixed common width-dependent layout conjugations can first be undone. Exact per-column phases stay in g_j; exterior scalar coefficients stay in K. In particular [iI] is not i[I]. A width-one expansion of Q is not an all-width group-algebra relation. A sparse residue class of widths is not a consecutive tail and is not covered.

Let N count all independent initial current fields, including any arbitrary independent dirty ancillas. Known-zero fields supply zero rows. In formal row space K[Gamma]^N, let e_j be the independent initial rows and I=R^N their ordinary span. Adjoin each actual paid output row t_k to the conceptual history, even when later overwritten or cleared:

```
M_m = I + R t_1 + ... + R t_m.
```

Every current row belongs to this module. An ordinary operation takes an R-linear combination. A paid operation may act on any earlier-response-dependent row, but returns only one actual transformed whole-field row and therefore adds at most one R generator. No paid operator is assumed R-linear or allowed to act freely on all of M_m. Hence

```
dim_K M_m <= (N+m)|G|.                                  (UPPER)
```

Suppose k required output rows are h_i e_{v_i}, with distinct independent input coordinates v_i and h_i outside G. Ordinary initial backgrounds may be added and then subtracted inside M_m. Define

```
W_i = R[h_i] e_{v_i}.
```

The map r↦r[h_i]e_{v_i} is injective: [h_i] is a group-algebra **unit**, so right multiplication by [h_i]^-1 cancels it. Thus dim_K W_i=|G|. In coordinate v_i, elements of I have group-basis support in G, whereas W_i has support in G h_i. These sets are disjoint because h_i is nonordinary. The different W_i occupy different input coordinates. Consequently

```
I + W_1 + ... + W_k = I direct-sum W_1 direct-sum ... direct-sum W_k,
dim_K M_m >= (N+k)|G|.                                  (LOWER)
```

The h_i may all be equal or lie in the same coset; distinct source coordinates already give the direct sum. No nonunit of R is cancelled. R may have zero divisors, which can only decrease the dimension of a cyclic history contribution in (UPPER). Since |G| is finite and positive, (UPPER) and (LOWER) imply **m>=k**.

For (CONVERSION), k=120 using the v coordinates. All-width faithfulness promotes the full arbitrary-input endpoint equalities to the formal row equalities needed in this lemma. Therefore the standalone converter requires **at least 120 total new Gaussian field calls**. Positive rank greater than one does not circumvent a one-output-row bound at the fixed capacity.

## 5. Scope that must accompany the stop decision

1. **Named conversion only.** The map is A Jbar A^-1 after the literal ordinary Jbar^-1 entrance and the existing reciprocal source service, in the frozen factorwise rectangular nomination. A different C_J implementation, semantic type, or central/private mixing rule needs its own analysis.
2. **Standalone ordinary initial span.** The converter receives arbitrary independent current fields w and only their ordinary span, plus independent dirt or zero scratch. Earlier Gaussian/native/cached rows, old recipient versions and correlated paid side information are not available for free. The theorem does not prove their absence at the real parent cut.
3. **Fixed capacity and one output.** Every charged call transforms one whole field at the declared common capacity into one new output row. Packing into a larger field, splitting capacities, or a multi-output primitive changes the model.
4. **Finite columnwise grammar.** Fixed finite G, a fixed finite word, fixed exterior coefficients, exact K-linear operations, and all positive widths or a consecutive tail are required. General inter-column operations, width-growing/width-dependent words, nonlinear or approximate algorithms are outside the proof.
5. **Independent full current inputs.** All 120 v coordinates are simultaneously arbitrary. Restricting them to a dependent image, permitting nonordinary initial backgrounds, or demanding fewer outputs changes the target module. The source proof supplies surjectivity but does not remove existing side information.
6. **Complete profile held fixed.** The moment conclusion compares exactly one basic P with its unchanged conditional T and this newly charged standalone conversion. A converter that earns additional genuine independent productive jobs changes T and needs a different complete invoice; conversion intermediates are not granted target credit. The proof does not add 120 per occurrence across a shared caller, prove every type row has this profile, or rule out different weighting of a larger multitype recursion.
7. **Source premises and admission boundary.** Native, helper, absolute phase and all-width source facts are retained premises. The earlier product theorem's ambient=max-target hypothesis does not literally hold for BC ambient25/targetmax9; this report only uses the already nominated local rectangle and supplies no compiler application.

For a concrete countercase to any unrestricted incremental reading, suppose the converter is additionally given correctly versioned rows h_i w_i from previously paid work. It can return those rows with zero new Gaussian calls. This does not assert that such rows exist in the actual source; it shows exactly why the ordinary-initial-module hypothesis cannot be omitted. Likewise, if all 120 w_i were the same field and all h_i the same operator, one paid output followed by copies could suffice. The source's independent-owner argument is essential.
