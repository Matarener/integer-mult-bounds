# Central powers-of-two extension of the standalone history bound

Status: **PROPOSED EXACT SCOPED EXTENSION; PENDING INDEPENDENT STATIC REVIEW.**

This lemma extends only the ordinary scalar grammar of the sealed standalone conversion report, SHA256 `6dc97b31c69d7ca4ff49f649fc55d85c1e01e46cdbf7923f880811220f2e80d9`. It permits a finite word to use exterior factors 2^(k f), where k is an integer fixed for each instruction and f is the fiber width. The earlier report and scalar clarification remain unchanged. The proposed proof below replaces their finite K-dimension argument with a finite dimension argument over a central fraction field. It neither supplies physical divisions by rational functions of 2^f nor transfers a standalone bound to a cut holding paid history.

## 1. Precise statement

Let K=Q(i), let V be a fixed finite-dimensional K-vector space, and let G be a finite subgroup of GL(V,K) containing the ordinary fixed columnwise address operators. For the retained source, G may be conservatively enlarged to all computational-basis monomial matrices with fourth-root entries. A field at width f is V tensor-powered f times, up to a common prescribed layout conjugation.

Fix one finite straight-line program, independently of f. Its ordinary operations are:

* the columnwise lift g^(tensor f) of any fixed g in G;
* whole-field ADD, COPY and CLEAR;
* exterior multiplication by c*2^(k f), with c in K and k in Z fixed independently of f for each instruction.

Finite combinations of these scalars are automatically allowed by ordinary sums. All instruction positions, coefficients c, integer exponents k, field owners, capacities and paid one-column operators are fixed. The word must be exact on arbitrary independent current input fields for every positive integer f, or for every f in a consecutive infinite tail. Arbitrary fixed finite scratch, arbitrary independent dirty inputs and finite feedback from previous paid responses are permitted.

Each paid call applies a fixed invertible one-column operator a in GL(V,K), lifted to a^(tensor f), to one actual current whole-field row and produces one transformed row at the same fixed field capacity. Different calls may use different a. In the application these are the permitted positive-rank Gaussian calls. Including a^-1 in an ambient group does not grant a free inverse call.

Suppose kappa distinct initial coordinates v_1,...,v_kappa have required output rows

```
h_i^(tensor f) w_vi,       i=1,...,kappa,
```

where h_i are fixed invertible one-column operators, and none is of the form 2^n g with n in Z and g in G. An arbitrary background from the ordinary span of initial inputs may be added to each required output and subtracted conceptually. The initial accessible history consists only of that ordinary span, plus independent dirt and zero scratch; no correlated nonordinary observations are supplied for free.

**Claim:** Any such program requires at least kappa paid whole-field calls. No assumption of a single query layer, pairwise different h_i cosets, commutativity of paid operators with G, or an integral-domain ordinary group algebra is needed.

For the source-bound natural canonical conversion, kappa=120. The existing source proof supplies all 120 full-current-input directions and shows that each h_i is nonmonomial. Multiplication by a scalar 2^n cannot change a monomial's support, so those h_i satisfy the stronger exclusion h_i notin <2I>G required here.

## 2. Central scalar group and its exact coefficient representation

Set sigma=2I_V and S=<sigma>. The group S is infinite cyclic: 2^n I=I only when n=0. Every scalar matrix is central in GL(V,K). Since G is finite, S intersects G only at I. Indeed sigma^n in G has some finite order d, so 2^(nd)=1 and n=0. Thus the ordinary matrix group is the internal direct product

```
O = S G = S x G.
```

In particular, existing fourth-root phase matrices cause no intersection problem. A nontrivial power of 2 cannot equal i^j. Phase matrices in G and exterior coefficients in K remain distinct formal data.

Let Gamma be the actual matrix subgroup generated by O, all paid operators and their inverses, and the target h_i and their inverses. It can be infinite. The ambient algebra is the formal group algebra A0=K[Gamma], with basis symbols [a] for actual distinct matrices a in Gamma. Set

```
Lambda = K[tau,tau^-1],        tau maps to [sigma],
R = K[O] = Lambda[G].
```

Lambda embeds centrally into A0. A nonzero Laurent polynomial in [sigma] is nonzero because the matrices sigma^n are distinct group-basis elements. Also the presentation R=Lambda[G] is faithful because sigma^n g has a unique pair (n,g).

Although sigma is the scalar matrix 2I on V, the formal element [sigma] is not the exterior coefficient 2[I]. Its width-f evaluation is 2^f I, whereas 2[I] evaluates as 2I. The ordinary instruction c*2^(n f) is represented by c tau^n; it is not treated as a coefficient sequence external to the algebra.

This distinction is essential. For example [sigma]-tau[I] is identically zero by the definition of tau, while [sigma]-2[I] is a nonzero formal element that happens to evaluate to zero at f=1. No width-one matrix relation is substituted for an all-width formal relation.

## 3. All-width faithfulness survives the enlarged finite-word grammar

For every f>=1 define the K-algebra homomorphism

```
Phi_f : A0 -> End_K(V^(tensor f)),
Phi_f([a]) = a^(tensor f).
```

Products are preserved because tensor powers preserve composition. In particular

```
Phi_f(c tau^n [g]) = c*2^(n f)*g^(tensor f).
```

Every actual row reached by a fixed finite word has finite support in A0 after expanding its finitely many Laurent coefficients. Its exterior coefficients then belong to K and are fixed independently of f. This remains true with negative exponents, overwritten fields and finite feedback.

The family Phi_f, over all f in any consecutive infinite tail, is injective. To prove it, take distinct nonzero matrices a_1,...,a_s and suppose

```
sum_j c_j a_j^(tensor f) = 0       for every integer f>=f0,
```

with fixed c_j in K. Since K is infinite, choose a K-linear functional ell on End_K(V) such that lambda_j=ell(a_j) are nonzero and pairwise distinct. The excluded choices form a finite union of proper hyperplanes. Apply ell^(tensor f) to the equality for f=f0,...,f0+s-1. The resulting Vandermonde determinant is, up to sign,

```
(product_j lambda_j^f0) * product_{a<b}(lambda_b-lambda_a),
```

which is nonzero. Thus all c_j vanish. Scaling some matrices by powers of 2 does not change the argument: they are either the same actual matrix, in which case terms are combined first, or distinct nonzero matrix vectors separated by ell. No finiteness of Gamma is used.

Consequently exact endpoint equalities on independent inputs at all permitted widths become equalities in A0^N before any localization. Fixed common layout conjugations can first be removed. Per-column phases stay inside actual matrix group elements, including [iI], and exterior i remains a coefficient. This proof does not admit arbitrary functions c_j(f): only the finite Laurent representation specified in Section 1 is used.

## 4. Why central localization does not lose target information

Choose one representative r from each coset of S in Gamma. Centrality lets each group element be written uniquely as sigma^n r. Partitioning the formal group basis by these S cosets gives the explicit direct sum

```
A0 = direct-sum_(r in Gamma/S) Lambda [r].
```

Thus A0 is a **free Lambda-module**, even if Gamma is infinite and A0 has other zero divisors. Every nonzero p(tau) in Lambda acts injectively on A0: write any alleged annihilated element in the displayed finite representative expansion and use that Lambda is a domain in every coordinate. The same holds for A0^N and its submodules. This is the torsion check; it is not an assumption that every nonzero element of A0 or R is cancellable.

Let F=K(tau), the fraction field of Lambda, and form the central localization

```
A = F tensor_Lambda A0,
R_F = F tensor_Lambda R = F[G].
```

The map A0->A is injective by the torsion calculation. The same chosen S-coset representatives form an F-basis of A. The ordinary subalgebra R_F embeds and has finite F-dimension |G|; distinct g in G represent distinct S cosets because S intersects G trivially.

All actual group elements [a] remain units in A, with inverse [a^-1]. No unproved cancellation in a group ring is used. R_F itself may have zero divisors—for an involution u, (1-[u])(1+[u])=0—and no part of the proof cancels those factors.

Localization is an algebraic counting device **after** the all-width endpoint identities have been promoted. It is not an operation available to the physical program. For example 1/(tau-2) exists in F but has no evaluation at f=1 under tau=2^f. The proof never evaluates this denominator at width one, never inserts division by 2^f-2 into the program and never changes its all-positive-width requirement. No pole issue arises because only identities in A0 are evaluated physically; A is used for a dimension comparison. If desired, an equality in A can always be compared after clearing a common nonzero Laurent denominator, with injectivity justified above.

## 5. History upper bound, including paid feedback

Let N count the arbitrary independent initial current fields, including independent dirty ancillas. Known-zero scratch adds zero rows. Regard their initial rows e_1,...,e_N as elements of A0^N. Every ordinary operation has coefficients in R. After m paid calls let t_1,...,t_m be their actual output rows, retaining them conceptually after overwrites and CLEARs. The ordinary history module is

```
M_m = R e_1 + ... + R e_N + R t_1 + ... + R t_m.
```

All current rows belong to M_m. Ordinary operations form R-linear combinations. A paid call applies its actual [a] on the left to its actual current input row and creates one new row t_j; that row alone is adjoined. The proof does not assert that [a] commutes with G, that paid action is R-linear, or that an entire earlier history module can be transformed for the charge of one row. Central powers of sigma commute with [a], so localization is compatible, but no broader commutativity is required.

Inside A^N take the corresponding localized span

```
Mhat_m = R_F e_1 + ... + R_F e_N + R_F t_1 + ... + R_F t_m.
```

Each R_F generator contributes at most |G| to F-dimension. Therefore

```
dim_F Mhat_m <= (N+m)|G|.                          (U)
```

The image of each cyclic module can be smaller than |G|, which only strengthens this upper bound. No freeness of M_m or its quotients is needed. Equivalently one may localize the surjection R^(N+m)->M_m; the displayed span and its direct dimension bound avoid needing a separate general exactness assertion.

## 6. The required target modules survive and remain independent

By the all-width endpoint proof, each required target row [h_i]e_vi lies in M_m after subtracting its ordinary initial background. Thus it also lies in Mhat_m. Set

```
I_F = R_F^N,
W_i = R_F [h_i] e_vi.
```

Right multiplication by the unit [h_i] gives an injection R_F->W_i, so dim_F W_i=|G|. Concretely the elements [g h_i]e_vi for g in G are independent because g h_i and g' h_i occupy the same S coset only if g= sigma^n g', which forces n=0 and g=g'.

Their S-coset supports are disjoint from those of I_F in coordinate vi. Otherwise g h_i = sigma^n g' for some g,g' in G, implying h_i in S G=O, contrary to the target assumption. Different W_i occupy different input coordinates, so they are mutually independent even when the h_i are identical or lie in the same O coset. It follows that

```
I_F + sum_i W_i = I_F direct-sum W_1 direct-sum ... direct-sum W_kappa,
dim_F Mhat_m >= (N+kappa)|G|.                       (L)
```

This proves directly that localization has not erased or merged the target modules. Combining (U) and (L), with finite positive |G|, gives m>=kappa. Arbitrary independent dirt raises N on both sides; zero scratch does not raise N. This completes the proposed proof.

## 7. Application to the already nominated conversion, and limits

The sealed source argument identifies the required standalone post-map as T=A Jbar A^-1. On each of the 120 independent v owners, after the fixed role aliases, its one-column operator is a tensor product of nine copies of h=Q J Q^-1, with identity spectators. The actual source equation Phi581=L2 h Q²R1 gives four nonzeros per row for h. Hence each full h_i is nonmonomial and cannot belong to O, all of whose matrices are scalar multiples of monomials. Scalar powers of two cannot alter this support test. No new target classification is needed.

The rectangular geometry is the grid of 625 binary axis groups (u_a,v_b) per fiber column. For the source rank-one demand, the child Gaussian acts on the canonical U row; J acts across the U coordinates within each active V column and overlaps that row. These are not independent Hilbert-space factors on which A and J automatically commute. Applying the child to every U row would change the source demand. This lemma retains exactly the frozen factorwise conversion and supplies no changed physical construction.

Subject to the retained source, ownership, surjectivity and all-width premises, the bound of 120 new standalone Gaussian field calls therefore survives the added finite-word scalars c*2^(k f). The earlier exact signed-profile argument is unchanged: every positive-rank call costs at least 1 for 0<t<1, and the fixed grant obeys T_t-P_t-120<0 throughout that interval. This is an analytic reuse of the sealed symbolic inequality, not a new moment evaluation or a funded κ claim.

The extension does not cover arbitrary width-dependent scalar functions, width-dependent exponents k(f), a word whose length grows with f, a restricted residue class of widths, noncolumnwise gates, changed capacity, nonlinear/approximate computation, free correlated observations, newly granted productive outputs, or different code/semantic families. Some such extensions might admit a separate proof; none is asserted here. In particular the original source's 112-fanout classification is not reclassified or generalized by this note.

The decisive remaining restriction for an actual caller is still its initial history: supplying the already computed target rows from earlier paid work can make the additional call count zero. This lemma only excludes powers of two as an escape from the specified **standalone ordinary-input-history** bound. It does not prove that actual-cut history lacks useful rows, or add the bound independently at repeated recursive cuts.
