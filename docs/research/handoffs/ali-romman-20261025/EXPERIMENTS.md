## File and status decisions

The local archive keeps superseded receipts as history. In particular, do
not use the earlier tensor bill before source binding, the rejected floating
repair candidates, or timed-out geometry searches as positive evidence.
The native-gauge and odd-E8 package drafts are superseded by their merged
PRs. Export-check scripts and publication drafts are verification records,
not new mathematical contributions.

The primitive rank proof in the P112 support finding is inherited. The
new check binds its certificate to the literal source and the stated
puncture. Do not claim a new general parity-projector method.

Border-product, spectral-renormalization, lattice-extension, bit-only
outer, and other unbuilt ideas were considered but not implemented.
They are not completed experiments. A future task must state a changed
premise and a complete cost test before expanding one of these routes.

All scopes are released. No publication or packaging task is left open by
this record. The complete negative E decoder, caller pricing, and all-size
admission remain research obligations. Their absence is not evidence of a
universal obstruction.
